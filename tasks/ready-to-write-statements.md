Venue: [lean-eval](https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval), which depends on Tau Ceti, once its stable Tau Ceti pin includes `liouvilleMeasure` and `ofComplexManifold` (its rc pin does); open lean-eval PR https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval/pull/676 adds the problems. formal-conjectures depends only on Mathlib and is not a fit.
- [2022·196(1)·2] The Birkhoff-Poritsky conjecture for centrally-symmetric billiard tables (Bialy, Mironov), https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2022.196.1.2: #92. Venue: [lean-eval](https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval), which depends on Tau Ceti, once its Tau Ceti pin includes `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/RotationNumber.lean`; formal-conjectures depends only on Mathlib and is not a fit.
