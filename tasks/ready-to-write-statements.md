## Papers

2 papers all of whose listed definitions are here.

- [2024·200(1)·6] On $L^\infty$ estimates for fully non-linear partial differential equations (Guo, Phong), https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2024.200.1.6: #14. Theorem 1 (the `L^∞` bound for `C²` solutions of `f(λ[h_φ]) = e^F` on a compact Hermitian manifold) also needs two notions not in code, both small and definable inline over Tau Ceti: the eigenvalue vector `λ[h_φ]` of `ω + i∂∂̄φ` relative to `ω` as a function on the manifold (its pointwise linear algebra is `LinearMap.IsSymm.relEigenvalues`, `TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RelativeEigenvalues.lean`), and the admissible pair `(f, Γ)`. Merged [ComplexHessianEquations](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ComplexHessianEquations/README.md) specifies both (§3.1 `λ[α]`, §2.4 admissible pairs) ([census 014](../census/014.md), [census 038](../census/038.md)). The rest is `TauCeti.manifoldLeviForm` (`i∂∂̄φ` for `C²` `φ`), `SmoothTwoForm.liouvilleMeasure` and `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.ofComplexManifold`. Venue: [lean-eval](https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval), which depends on Tau Ceti, once its stable Tau Ceti pin includes `liouvilleMeasure` and `ofComplexManifold` (its rc pin does); open lean-eval PR https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval/pull/676 adds the problems. formal-conjectures depends only on Mathlib and is not a fit.
- [2022·196(1)·2] The Birkhoff-Poritsky conjecture for centrally-symmetric billiard tables (Bialy, Mironov), https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2022.196.1.2: #92. The main theorem (a `C²` centrally symmetric table, positively curved, whose phase cylinder between a rotational invariant curve of rotation number 1/4 and the boundary `{δ = 0}` is foliated by continuous rotational invariant curves is an ellipse) is stated on `StrictConvexBilliardTable` with `ContDiff ℝ 2`, with rotational invariant curves as invariant graphs `IsInvariantGraph` and their rotation numbers `IsInvariantGraph.rotationNumber` (the graph form that the third Remark of Definition 92 allows, and Birkhoff's theorem justifies), in the coordinate `v = cos δ`, where `{δ = 0}` is `{v = 1}` ([census 092](../census/092.md)). It also needs three notions not in code, all small and definable inline: central symmetry of the table, the foliation condition (every state between the curve and the boundary lies on an invariant graph) and ellipses as tables. Venue: [lean-eval](https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval), which depends on Tau Ceti, once its Tau Ceti pin includes `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/RotationNumber.lean`; formal-conjectures depends only on Mathlib and is not a fit.
