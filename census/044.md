## Prerequisite chain
1. Lebesgue integration, `L¹_loc`, matrices, entire functions: in Mathlib.
2. Carathéodory linear ODE, bounded measurable coefficients on compact intervals: in Tau Ceti (`Caratheodory.lean`).
3. Extension to `L¹_loc` coefficients on `ℝ₊` (Picard iteration with `exp ∫ p_K` bound) and entire dependence on a parameter (Prop 44.1): unplanned.
4. Real Dirac system, transfer matrix `M(t,z)`, Hermite–Biehler functions `E, Ẽ`: unplanned.
5. Scattering functions `a, b` (Def 44.3) and Prop 44.4 (`det G = 1`, `|a| ≥ 1`, `SU(1,1)` on ℝ): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A roadmap on "Canonical systems, Dirac operators and nonlinear Fourier analysis" would build: linear ODEs with `L¹_loc` coefficients and analytic parameters (extending Tau Ceti's Carathéodory ODE and UnboundedOperators UO-D07's local integral equations), Dirac and canonical (Hamiltonian) systems, transfer matrices and Hermite–Biehler/de Branges spaces, Weyl–Titchmarsh m-functions, the SU(1,1) scattering transform with its Plancherel identity and Krein/Denisov existence of scattering limits, and Hardy/Smirnov/outer-function theory on the upper half-plane (Fatou). Texts: Remling, *Spectral Theory of Canonical Systems* (2018); de Branges, *Hilbert Spaces of Entire Functions*; Tao–Thiele, *Nonlinear Fourier analysis* (lecture notes); Garnett, *Bounded Analytic Functions*. It would sit beside OperatorTheory/UnboundedOperators (merged; its UO-D07 treats Sturm–Liouville operators) and consume the ToeplitzOperators Hardy space work.
