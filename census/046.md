## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED: Schatten classes `S_p` are implemented in Tau Ceti (OperatorTheory/OperatorIdeals, Part B done) and completely bounded maps are a merged CStarAlgebras target, but Schur multipliers on `S_p(L²(Ω))` and the Hilbert–Schmidt kernel isomorphism `L²(Ω×Ω) ≅ S_2` are not targets; NoncommutativeIntegration explicitly puts Schur multipliers outside its scope.

## Mathlib
- Definition absent (Mathlib's only "Schur multiplier" is group-theoretic; `Matrix.hadamard` gives the finite entrywise product).
- Present: `MeasureTheory.Lp`, product measures, `L²` as a Hilbert space, compact operators `IsCompactOperator`, `ContinuousLinearMap` on Hilbert spaces, `Matrix.hadamard`.
- Missing: Schatten classes, Hilbert–Schmidt operators, the kernel isomorphism.

## Tau Ceti code
- Definition absent (no Schur multiplier on operators; grep `SchurMultiplier`: none).
- Present: Schatten family `TauCeti.OperatorIdealFamily.schatten 𝕜 p hp` (`TauCeti/Analysis/OperatorIdeal/Family/Schatten.lean`, `1 ≤ p`, Hilbert spaces), `hilbertSchmidt` family (`Family/HilbertSchmidt.lean`), trace class (`Family/TraceClass.lean`), `UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm.schatten` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/UnitarilyInvariantSeminorm/Schatten.lean`), `TauCeti.Analysis.InnerProductSpace.HilbertSchmidt.Basic`; integral operators with operator-valued kernels and Schur's test on `Lᵖ` (`TauCeti.integralOperatorLp`, `TauCeti/MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/Operator/Integral/Basic.lean`); multiplication operators on `Lᵖ` (`Lp/Operator/Multiplication.lean`).
- Missing: the unitary `L²(Ω×Ω) → S_2(L²(Ω))`, `k ↦ T_k`, and its inverse (kernel of an HS operator).

## Open roadmap PRs
- None (all open-PR hits for "Schur multiplier" are the group-cohomological `H₂(G,ℤ)`; PR #1842 uses Schatten classes for Lie-group representations, unrelated).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `L²(Ω)`, product measure, `L^∞(Ω×Ω)`: in Mathlib.
2. Schatten classes `S_p`, `1 ≤ p ≤ ∞`, Hilbert–Schmidt class, density of finite rank: in Tau Ceti (OperatorIdeals Parts B–C, implemented).
3. Hilbert–Schmidt kernel isomorphism `L²(Ω×Ω,μ⊗μ) ≅ S_2(L²(Ω))` (Prop 46.1): unplanned (Tau Ceti has `integralOperatorLp` for Schur-test kernels, not this).
4. Schur multiplier `S_M` on `S_2` (Def 46.2): unplanned (multiplication operator on `L²(Ω×Ω)` transported through 3).
5. `S_p → S_p` norm and extension by density (Def 46.3): unplanned; needs 2.
6. Completely bounded norm: CStarAlgebras C3.2 (merged, not implemented) plus tensoring with `M_m`; unplanned for multipliers.
7. Transference / Herz–Schur / Fourier multipliers on `Lᵖ(ℝⁿ)`: PDE roadmap owns Euclidean Fourier multipliers and Littlewood–Paley; transference unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A short "Schur multipliers" roadmap beside OperatorTheory/OperatorIdeals and NoncommutativeIntegration: HS kernels, Schur multipliers on `S_p` for general σ-finite `Ω`, duality `p ↔ p'`, interpolation, Grothendieck's characterisation of `S_∞` (equivalently `S_1`) multipliers by factorisation through Hilbert space, Herz–Schur multipliers and transference to Fourier multipliers, Lafforgue–de la Salle discretisation, and the Hörmander–Mikhlin-type `S_p` criteria. Texts: Pisier, *Similarity Problems and Completely Bounded Maps* (Ch. 5) and *Introduction to Operator Space Theory*; Lafforgue–de la Salle (Duke 2011); Conde-Alonso–González-Pérez–Parcet–Tablate (the Annals paper). Prerequisites (Schatten classes, cb maps, noncommutative interpolation) are already roadmapped, so this is a modest extension.
