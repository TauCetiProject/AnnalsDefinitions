# 3. Defect group of a block

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775) specifies the definition as a target: relative traces and the Brauer homomorphism (§6.1), `IsDefectGroup p e P` with its characterization by minimality in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)`, `isDefectGroup_conj`, defect classes, the Sylow defect groups of the principal block, `O_p(G) ≤ D` and the defect-zero criterion (§6.2), and Brauer's first main theorem `brauer_first_main` (Brauer correspondent, §7.1). Merged [ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII/README.md) (PR #2411) adds defect-group theorems on top. The definition (Remark (1) form) is in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.IsDefectGroup R p e P`, a `p`-subgroup maximal with `Br_P(e) ≠ 0`, with existence (`exists_isDefectGroup`), the permutation of defect groups by elements fixing `e` (`IsDefectGroup.conj`), the equality with the subgroups minimal in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)` (Definition 3.3, `IsBlockIdempotent.isDefectGroup_iff_minimal`) and the conjugacy of all defect groups of a block (`IsBlockIdempotent.isDefectGroup_conj`) (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup/Basic.lean`), and the Sylow defect groups of the principal block (`isDefectGroup_principalBlockIdempotent_iff`, `DefectGroup/Principal.lean`), so `D` up to conjugacy, which the papers use, is available, together with the transfer of defect groups along a reduction `R → S` with kernel in the Jacobson radical (Theorem 3.2(3) form), along injective changes of coefficients, and to direct products, the first half of the defect-zero criterion, `O_p(G) ≤ D` (`IsBlockIdempotent.piCore_le_of_isDefectGroup`), the defect `d(B)` with `|D| = p ^ d(B)` (`TauCeti.blockDefect`, `IsBlockIdempotent.card_eq_pow_blockDefect`), the converse over an algebraically closed field (defect zero exactly when the block algebra is `M_n(k)`, `IsBlockIdempotent.isDefectGroup_bot_iff_exists_algEquiv_matrix`), the central character `λ_B` of a block, defect classes (`TauCeti.IsDefectClass`), and the Brauer correspondent idempotent `TauCeti.brauerCorrespondent D e ∈ Z(R[N_G(D)])`, nonzero at a defect group. Martínez's Sylow description of defect groups through a defect class is in code (`TauCeti.IsDefectClass.isDefectGroup_iff`, `GroupAlgebra/Block/Defect/Sylow.lean`). Not in code: the defect-zero criterion over a splitting field that is not algebraically closed and in the form `|G|_p ∣ χ(1)`, and Brauer's first main theorem (the bijection of blocks with defect group `D`; open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/7245 has block induction and Brauer's formula for `Br_P(e_B)`). Of its inputs the block idempotents (#2) and §6.1's relative traces `Tr_Q^P` with the conjugation and Mackey formulas, orbit-sum bases and reduction, trace ideals and the Brauer homomorphism `TauCeti.brauerHom` with its kernel are in code. Depends on #2.
