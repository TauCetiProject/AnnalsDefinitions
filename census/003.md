# 3. Defect group of a block

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775) specifies the definition as a target: relative traces and the Brauer homomorphism (§6.1), `IsDefectGroup p e P` with its characterization by minimality in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)`, `isDefectGroup_conj`, defect classes, the Sylow defect groups of the principal block, `O_p(G) ≤ D` and the defect-zero criterion (§6.2), and Brauer's first main theorem `brauer_first_main` (Brauer correspondent, §7.1). Merged [ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII/README.md) (PR #2411) adds defect-group theorems on top. The definition (Remark (1) form) is in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.IsDefectGroup R p e P`, a `p`-subgroup maximal with `Br_P(e) ≠ 0`, with existence (`exists_isDefectGroup`) and the permutation of defect groups by elements fixing `e` (`IsDefectGroup.conj`) (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup.lean`). Not in code: the equality with the subgroups minimal in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)` (Definition 3.3; only the existence of minimal subgroups and that they are `p`-groups are in code), conjugacy of all defect groups of a block `isDefectGroup_conj`, defect classes and Brauer's first main theorem, so `D` up to conjugacy, which the papers use, is not yet available. Of its inputs the block idempotents (#2) and §6.1's relative traces `Tr_Q^P` with the conjugation and Mackey formulas, orbit-sum bases and reduction, trace ideals and the Brauer homomorphism `TauCeti.brauerHom` with its kernel are in code. Depends on #2.

## Tau Ceti code
- Defect groups (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.2): `TauCeti.IsDefectGroup R p e P`, maximal among the `p`-subgroups `Q` with `Br_Q(e) ≠ 0` (`isDefectGroup_iff`, `IsDefectGroup.isPGroup`, `IsDefectGroup.brauerProjection_ne_zero`), with `IsDefectGroup.conj` (defect groups of a `g`-fixed element are permuted by `g`), `exists_isDefectGroup`, and minimal subgroups `P` with `x ∈ Tr_P^G(R[G]^P)`, which are `p`-groups in characteristic `p` (`exists_minimal_mem_relTraceIdeal`, `isPGroup_of_minimal_mem_relTraceIdeal`) (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup.lean`), on `relTraceIdeal_sylow_eq_top` (`GroupAlgebra/RelativeTrace.lean`). The equality of the two descriptions and `isDefectGroup_conj` are not in code. Open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4237 (§6.2, defect groups are the minimal trace subgroups and form one conjugacy class, `TauCeti.isDefectGroup_iff_minimal`, `isDefectGroup_conj`, `IsBlockIdempotent.isDefectGroup_conj`, `RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup.lean`) and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4255 (§6.2, the defect groups of the principal block are the Sylow `p`-subgroups, `TauCeti.isDefectGroup_iff_sylow_of_augmentation_ne_zero`, `isDefectGroup_principalBlockIdempotent_iff`, `RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup/Principal.lean`).
- Higman's criterion at `H = 1` (§6.2, the "Route without vertices" input to the defect-zero criterion): a representation projective over `k` is projective iff `1_M = ∑ g, g α g⁻¹` for a `k`-linear `α`, `Rep.id_mem_range_norm_linHom_of_projective`, `Rep.projective_iff_id_mem_range_norm_linHom`, `Rep.moduleProjective_iff_id_mem_range_norm_linHom` (`RepresentationTheory/NormSplit/Projective.lean`). The defect-zero criterion itself is not in code.
- Relative traces and the Brauer homomorphism (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.1): the conjugation representation `TauCeti.conjRepresentation` on `R[G]`, the fixed points `TauCeti.conjInvariants R H` (`R[G]^H`, with `conjInvariants_top : R[G]^G = Z(R[G])`), the relative trace `TauCeti.relTrace R Q P : R[G]^Q →ₗ[R] R[G]^P` (the relative norm of the conjugation representation) with `relTrace_apply_eq_sum_of_bijective`, `relTrace_inclusion`, transitivity `relTrace_relTrace` and the product formulas `relTrace_mul`, `mul_relTrace`, and the two-sided trace ideal `TauCeti.relTraceIdeal` (`R[G]^P_Q`) with `relTraceIdeal_mono` (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/RelativeTrace.lean`); the coefficient projection `TauCeti.brauerProjection P : R[G] →ₗ[R] R[C_G(P)]`, `brauerProjection_mul` for a `p`-subgroup in characteristic `p`, and the Brauer homomorphism `TauCeti.brauerHom : R[G]^P →ₐ[R] R[C_G(P)]` with `coeff_brauerProjection_conj_of_mem_conjInvariants`, the `N_G(P)`-invariance of the Brauer projection of an `N_G(P)`-invariant element (`GroupAlgebra/Brauer/Projection.lean`), on `IsPGroup.sum_eq_sum_fixedPoints` (`GroupTheory/PGroup/FixedPointSum.lean`); the kernel `∑_{Q<P} k[G]^P_Q` of `Br_P`, `TauCeti.ker_brauerHom : RingHom.ker (brauerHom hP) = ⨆ (Q) (hQ : Q < P), relTraceIdeal R hQ.le` (Webb, Lemma 12.5.3), with `brauerProjection_relTrace_eq_zero` (`GroupAlgebra/Brauer/Kernel.lean`). Rosenberg's lemma (§6.1) is in code: `IsIdempotentElem.exists_mem_of_mem_iSup` (an idempotent with local corner in a sum of two-sided ideals lies in one of them) and `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent.exists_mem_of_mem_iSup` (Webb's form, for a primitive idempotent of an Artinian ring), `RingTheory/Idempotents/Corner/Local.lean`. The conjugation formula `conjInvariantsEquiv_relTrace` (along `TauCeti.conjInvariantsEquiv g H : R[G]^H ≃ R[G]^{gHg⁻¹}`) and the Mackey formulas `inclusion_relTrace_eq_sum_relTrace_conj`, `inclusion_relTrace_eq_sum_doubleCoset` (§6.1, `GroupAlgebra/RelativeTrace.lean`, on `GroupTheory/DoubleCoset/{Cover,Map}.lean`); orbit-sum bases `TauCeti.conjInvariantsBasis` of `R[G]^H` and the reduction map `conjInvariantsMap` with `conjInvariantsMap_relTrace` and `conjInvariantsMap_surjective` (§6.1, `GroupAlgebra/OrbitSum.lean`). ## Prerequisite chain
1. p-groups, Sylow, normalizers/centralizers: Mathlib.
2. Block idempotents of `k[G]`: Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent`); blocks of `O[G]`: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3 (merged; see #2).
3. Relative trace, Brauer homomorphism: Tau Ceti (`relTrace`, `relTraceIdeal`, `brauerHom`; ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.1, in code); Rosenberg's lemma: Tau Ceti (`IsIdempotentElem.exists_mem_of_mem_iSup`); Mackey and conjugation formulas and orbit-sum bases: Tau Ceti (`inclusion_relTrace_eq_sum_doubleCoset`, `conjInvariantsBasis`); the kernel of `Br_P`: Tau Ceti (`ker_brauerHom`).
4. Defect group and its conjugacy; Sylow case; defect `d(b)` as `log_p |D|`: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.2 (merged; the definition `IsDefectGroup` and minimal trace subgroups in Tau Ceti, `GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup.lean`; the case `H = 1` of Higman's criterion for the defect-zero bullet in Tau Ceti, `Rep.projective_iff_id_mem_range_norm_linHom`; the defect is ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.3, merged). Conjugacy, the equality with minimal trace subgroups and the Sylow case: open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4237, https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4255.
5. Transfer of defect groups between `O[G]` and `k[G]` (Theorem 3.2(3)): via ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3 lifting; explicit statement unplanned but trivial.
6. Brauer correspondent (Brauer's first main theorem): ModularCharactersAndBlocks §7.1 (merged).
7. Green's `S ∩ gS` description: unplanned (not needed by the papers).
