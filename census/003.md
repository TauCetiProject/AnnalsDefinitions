# 3. Defect group of a block

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775) specifies the definition as a target: relative traces and the Brauer homomorphism (§6.1), `IsDefectGroup p e P` with its characterization by minimality in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)`, `isDefectGroup_conj`, defect classes, the Sylow defect groups of the principal block, `O_p(G) ≤ D` and the defect-zero criterion (§6.2), and Brauer's first main theorem `brauer_first_main` (Brauer correspondent, §7.1). ## Prerequisite chain
1. p-groups, Sylow, normalizers/centralizers: Mathlib.
2. Block idempotents of `k[G]`: Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent`); blocks of `O[G]`: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3 (merged; see #2).
3. Relative trace, Brauer homomorphism: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.1 (merged, not implemented), on Tau Ceti's relative norm.
4. Defect group and its conjugacy; Sylow case; defect `d(b)` as `log_p |D|`: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.2 (merged; the defect is ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.3, merged).
5. Transfer of defect groups between `O[G]` and `k[G]` (Theorem 3.2(3)): via ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3 lifting; explicit statement unplanned but trivial.
6. Brauer correspondent (Brauer's first main theorem): ModularCharactersAndBlocks §7.1 (merged).
7. Green's `S ∩ gS` description: unplanned (not needed by the papers).

## Library-scale gap
None beyond merged ModularCharactersAndBlocks and its Part II, which await implementation, for what the papers use (D up to conjugacy, `|G:D|_p`, abelianness, `N_G(D)`, Brauer correspondent). Reference: Webb, *A Course in Finite Group Representation Theory*, Ch. 11–12; Linckelmann, *The Block Theory of Finite Group Algebras*.
