# 3. Defect group of a block

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775) specifies the definition as a target: relative traces and the Brauer homomorphism (§6.1), `IsDefectGroup p e P` with its characterization by minimality in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)`, `isDefectGroup_conj`, defect classes, the Sylow defect groups of the principal block, `O_p(G) ≤ D` and the defect-zero criterion (§6.2), and Brauer's first main theorem `brauer_first_main` (Brauer correspondent, §7.1). Merged [ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII/README.md) (PR #2411) adds defect-group theorems on top. The definition (Remark (1) form) is in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.IsDefectGroup R p e P`, a `p`-subgroup maximal with `Br_P(e) ≠ 0`, with existence (`exists_isDefectGroup`), the permutation of defect groups by elements fixing `e` (`IsDefectGroup.conj`), the equality with the subgroups minimal in `Tr_P^G(k[G]^P)` (Definition 3.3, `IsBlockIdempotent.isDefectGroup_iff_minimal`) and the conjugacy of all defect groups of a block (`IsBlockIdempotent.isDefectGroup_conj`) (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/DefectGroup/Basic.lean`), and the Sylow defect groups of the principal block (`isDefectGroup_principalBlockIdempotent_iff`, `DefectGroup/Principal.lean`), so `D` up to conjugacy, which the papers use, is available, together with the transfer of defect groups along a reduction `R → S` with kernel in the Jacobson radical (Theorem 3.2(3) form), along injective changes of coefficients, and to direct products, and the first half of the defect-zero criterion. Not in code: defect classes, `O_p(G) ≤ D`, the rest of the defect-zero criterion, the defect `d(B)` and Brauer's first main theorem. Of its inputs the block idempotents (#2) and §6.1's relative traces `Tr_Q^P` with the conjugation and Mackey formulas, orbit-sum bases and reduction, trace ideals and the Brauer homomorphism `TauCeti.brauerHom` with its kernel are in code. Depends on #2.
