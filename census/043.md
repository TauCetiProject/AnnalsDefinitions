## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged DiracScatteringAndNonlinearFourierTransform specifies it: the scattering limit `a(∞,·)`, `b(∞,·)`, `ρ(∞,·) = b(∞,·)/a(∞,·)` locally uniformly on `ℂ₊` for square-integrable potentials (§5.1, Prop 43.1 on `ℂ₊`), the non-linear Fourier transform `q⌃ = ρ(∞,·)` with truncations `(q_T)⌃ = b(T,·)/a(T,·)` (§5.2, Def 43.2(b)), its boundary function on `ℝ` (§§5.2–5.3), and the convergence of truncations in `L²(ℝ)` and in `Π`-measure, with `a(T,·) → a(∞,·)`, `b(T,·) → b(∞,·)` in `Π`-measure (§5.5, Prop 43.1 on `ℝ`, Def 43.2(a)). §9.6 states the paper's pointwise convergence theorem only at resonance-free points (its conditional form); that almost every point is resonance-free is not a target, and the roadmap (README l.2410–2422) cites an error report (Mnatsakanyan 2022) against an earlier arXiv version of the paper's proof and Denisov 2026, which poses the almost-everywhere convergence for potentials on `ℝ` truncated to `(−T, T)` as a question. It rests on the scattering functions of #44 (Layer 4) and on the half-plane function theory of its Layer 2, whose §2.2 uses merged AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.5. Of the chain, §§1.1–1.2 (integrable-kernel Grönwall, linear Carathéodory with an integrable coefficient), the propagator of §1.3 with its Dyson expansion and variation of constants, Liouville's formula (§1.4), continuous dependence on the coefficient (§1.5), the uniform bound and the Checks of §1.6, the Poisson and Schwarz integrals of measures and of `L¹(Π)` functions of §2.1 with their covariance, and the Herglotz representation of AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.5 with uniqueness of the triple and its resolvent specialization are in code; nothing of the transform itself is.
