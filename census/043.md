## Prerequisite chain
1. `L²(ℝ₊)`, convergence in measure, finite measure `Π`: in Mathlib.
2. Carathéodory linear ODE: in Tau Ceti for bounded coefficients only; `L¹_loc` with entire parameter dependence unplanned.
3. Scattering functions `a(t,z)`, `b(t,z)` and their properties (#44): unplanned.
4. Truncated transform `b(T,·)/a(T,·)` (Def 43.2(b)): unplanned, immediate from 3.
5. Existence of scattering limits on `ℂ₊` (Denisov) and in `Π`-measure on ℝ (Prop 43.1): unplanned; needs bounded-type / Smirnov class theory on the half-plane.
6. Nonlinear Fourier transform `b(∞,·)/a(∞,·)` (Def 43.2(a)): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
Same roadmap as #44: canonical systems and nonlinear Fourier analysis (Tao–Thiele lecture notes; Remling, *Spectral Theory of Canonical Systems*; Denisov's Krein-system papers), together with Hardy, Nevanlinna and Smirnov classes on the upper half-plane and outer functions (Garnett, *Bounded Analytic Functions*; Koosis, *Introduction to H^p spaces*). The definition itself is short once #44 and Prop 43.1 exist; Prop 43.1 is the real cost.
