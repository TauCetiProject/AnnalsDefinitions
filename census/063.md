## Status
IN-CODE. Definitions 63.1–63.3 are in Tau Ceti: `SymplecticForm.Tames`/`Compatible` pointwise and `SmoothTwoForm.Compatible` on manifolds, the `C^∞` topology `TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.frameTopology` (the subspace topology from `ContMDiffSection.frameTopology` on `End(TM)`, independent of the covering frames for a finite-dimensional model), and `J(M, ω)`, `J_τ(M, ω)` as the subtypes `{J // ω.Compatible J}`, `{J // ω.Tames J}` with the subspace topology (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Topology.lean`, HeegaardFloer §4.1, F2.1). The relative space `J(M, ω; U, J_fix)` of 63.3(b) is the subspace of those agreeing with `J_fix` off `U`, expressible directly. Basic API: Hausdorff, closed embedding into sections, compatible structures closed among tame ones, nonemptiness. Proposition 63.4 is not in code: completeness and the Baire property of `J(M, ω; U, J_fix)`, and contractibility as a statement about the space (the Cayley contraction exists pointwise and as smooth paths, not as a continuous map in this topology). The cited paper's genericity statements ("a comeagre subset", Mathlib `residual`) can be written against these declarations.

## Mathlib
No almost complex structures or symplectic forms on manifolds. Has the compact-open/Fréchet tools only in pieces (`ContMDiffSection`), no C^∞ topology on sections.

## Tau Ceti code
- Linear: `TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure`, `SymplecticForm.Compatible` (`Geometry/Symplectic/AlmostComplex.lean`), `Tames`, `exists_compatible`, `exists_tames` (`Geometry/Symplectic/ExistsCompatible.lean`), operator-norm topology on linear J and openness of tameness (`Geometry/Symplectic/AlmostComplex/Topology.lean`), `ContractibleSpace` of the linear tame and compatible structures (`Geometry/Symplectic/Cayley/Contractible.lean`), `compatibleTangentSpace`.
- Manifold: `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Basic.lean`), `SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_compatible` (`.../AlmostComplex/Polar.lean`), `cayleyPath` (pointwise contraction, `.../AlmostComplex/CayleyPath.lean`), extension of compatible J from a neighbourhood (`.../AlmostComplex/Extension.lean`).
- The space and its topology (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Topology.lean`): `TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.frameTopology` (convergence of every derivative of the frame coefficients, uniformly on compact coordinate sets), `isEmbedding_toEndomorphism_frameTopology`, `isClosedEmbedding_toEndomorphism_frameTopology` (`J² = -1` is closed), `t2Space_frameTopology`, `frameTopology_eq_of_covers` (independence of the covering frames for a locally compact model), `continuous_toEndomorphism_apply_frameTopology`, `tendsto_frameTopology_iff`; for a two-form, `SmoothTwoForm.isClosed_setOf_invariant_frameTopology` and `isClosed_setOf_invariant_tames_frameTopology` (compatible structures are closed among tame ones). Tameness is not asserted open (it is not, on a noncompact manifold).
- Section topology: `ContMDiffSection.frameTopology` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Topology.lean`), a locally convex topology of compact-derivative convergence on smooth sections over any second-countable base, with countable seminorms (`Section/CountableSeminorms.lean`), cover comparison and restriction (`Section/FrameCover.lean`), Hausdorff, continuous evaluation, and completeness (`ContMDiffSection.completeSpace_frameTopology`, `Section/Complete.lean`).
- Invariant compatible J for a compact group action (`SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_compatible_mfderiv_smul_apply_of_compactSpace`, `.../AlmostComplex/Polar.lean`).
- Localized variations of a smooth J at an injective point: compactly supported sections of End(TM) with a positive pairing against a nonzero cokernel covector (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Variation.lean`), the input for transversality by varying J (F2.4).
- Missing: completeness or `BaireSpace` for `J(M, ω)` and `J(M, ω; U, J_fix)` (63.4(b)), closedness of the compatible structures among all smooth J, continuity of the Cayley contraction in `frameTopology` and `ContractibleSpace` for the spaces (63.4(c)), and a named relative space `J(M, ω; U, J_fix)`.
