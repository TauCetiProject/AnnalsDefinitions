## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. HeegaardFloer §4.1 (F2.1) specifies "the spaces of smooth tame and compatible J on a finite-dimensional symplectic manifold" with nonemptiness, a contraction to a chosen J₀, relative extension and C^∞ continuity on compact sources; F0.5 adds finite-C^ℓ parameter spaces. Tau Ceti has the pointwise and global existence theory but no space J(M, ω) with its C^∞ topology.

## Mathlib
No almost complex structures or symplectic forms on manifolds. Has the compact-open/Fréchet tools only in pieces (`ContMDiffSection`), no C^∞ topology on sections.

## Tau Ceti code
- Linear: `TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure`, `SymplecticForm.Compatible` (`Geometry/Symplectic/AlmostComplex.lean`), `Tames`, `exists_compatible`, `exists_tames` (`Geometry/Symplectic/ExistsCompatible.lean`), operator-norm topology on linear J and openness of tameness (`Geometry/Symplectic/AlmostComplex/Topology.lean`), `compatibleTangentSpace`.
- Manifold: `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Basic.lean`), `SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_compatible` (`.../AlmostComplex/Polar.lean`), `cayleyPath` (pointwise contraction, `.../AlmostComplex/CayleyPath.lean`), extension of compatible J from a neighbourhood (`.../AlmostComplex/Extension.lean`).
- Section topology: `ContMDiffSection.frameTopology` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/Topology.lean`), a locally convex topology of compact-derivative convergence on smooth sections over any second-countable base, with countable seminorms (`Section/CountableSeminorms.lean`), cover comparison and restriction (`Section/FrameCover.lean`), Hausdorff, continuous evaluation, and completeness (`ContMDiffSection.completeSpace_frameTopology`, `Section/Complete.lean`, with no compactness or countability hypothesis). The `MontelSpace` instance is not proved.
- Invariant compatible J for a compact group action (`SmoothTwoForm.IsNondegenerate.exists_compatible_mfderiv_smul_apply_of_compactSpace`, `.../AlmostComplex/Polar.lean`).
- Localized variations of a smooth J at an injective point: compactly supported sections of End(TM) with a positive pairing against a nonzero cokernel covector (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Variation.lean`), the input for transversality by varying J (F2.4).
- Missing: the set J(M, ω) as a topological space with the C^∞_loc topology (63.2-63.3), J(M, ω; U, J_fix), contractibility as a statement about that space.

## Prerequisite chain
symplectic manifold, smooth sections [Tau Ceti] -> compatible/tame linear J, existence, Cayley contraction [Tau Ceti] -> smooth compatible J on M, existence, extension [Tau Ceti] -> C^∞ (Whitney/compact-open C^∞) topology on Γ(End TM) (63.2) [HF F2.1 "C-infinity continuity"; ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.3 (K5a.SmoothSectionMontel) Fréchet topology on sections, in code as the complete space `ContMDiffSection.frameTopology` on any second-countable base, Montel property missing] -> J(M, ω), J_τ(M, ω), relative versions (63.3-63.4) [HF F2.1, not implemented] -> contractibility, Baire property for generic J [HF F2.1/F2.6d].

## Library-scale gap
Planned. Implementation mainly needs a reusable C^∞_loc topology on sections of a bundle over a noncompact manifold (ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.3's `ContMDiffSection.frameTopology` already provides this on any second-countable base, as a complete space) and the space-level packaging of existing pointwise results (McDuff-Salamon, *J-holomorphic Curves and Symplectic Topology*, §3.2 and Prop. 4.1.1 of *Introduction to Symplectic Topology*).
