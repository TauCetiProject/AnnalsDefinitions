## Status
IN-CODE. Definitions 63.1–63.3 are in Tau Ceti: `SymplecticForm.Tames`/`Compatible` pointwise and `SmoothTwoForm.Compatible` on manifolds, the `C^∞` topology `TauCeti.SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.frameTopology` (the subspace topology from `ContMDiffSection.frameTopology` on `End(TM)`, independent of the covering frames for a finite-dimensional model), and `J(M, ω)`, `J_τ(M, ω)` as the subtypes `{J // ω.Compatible J}`, `{J // ω.Tames J}` with the subspace topology (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/Topology.lean`, HeegaardFloer §4.1, F2.1). The relative space `J(M, ω; U, J_fix)` of 63.3(b) is the subspace of those agreeing with `J_fix` off `U`, expressible directly. Basic API: Hausdorff, closed embedding into sections, compatible structures closed among tame ones, nonemptiness. Proposition 63.4(b) is in code for `J(M, ω)` and `J_τ(M, ω)` on any Lindelöf manifold with finite-dimensional model, compact or not: tameness is a `G_δ` condition, so both spaces are completely metrizable and Baire (`TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.isCompletelyMetrizableSpace_compatible_frameTopology`, `baireSpace_compatible_frameTopology`, `baireSpace_tames_frameTopology`, `Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/AlmostComplex/CompletelyMetrizable.lean`). Not stated: the Baire property of `J(M, ω; U, J_fix)` (a closed subset of `J(M, ω)`, since agreement with `J_fix` at each point is closed by `continuous_toEndomorphism_apply_frameTopology`), the metric of 63.4(a), and the contractibility of 63.4(c) as a statement about the spaces (the Cayley contraction exists pointwise and as smooth paths and smooth finite-parameter families, not as a continuous map in this topology). The cited paper's genericity statements ("a comeagre subset", Mathlib `residual`) can be written against these declarations.

## Mathlib
No almost complex structures or symplectic forms on manifolds. Has the compact-open/Fréchet tools only in pieces (`ContMDiffSection`), no C^∞ topology on sections.
