## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
The restricted tensor product, the spherical-line bound and Flath's factorization with local components `localFactor` are explicit targets of merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §§3.4–3.5, with the global `(𝔤,K)×G(𝔸_f)` module and `IsAutomorphicRepresentation` in §3.2 and the automorphic-form carrier in merged ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6. Its local and adelic inputs (smooth admissible representations, Hecke algebras, Satake; adelic points, integral models, admissible maximal compact `∏K_v`) are targets of the merged roadmaps SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups and AdelicAlgebraicGroups. The definition is not in code; first pieces of its inputs are: smooth representations and smooth vectors of topological groups (SR.0.1) and the abelian category of smooth representations (SR.0.2), the existence half and the corner part of the finite-corner factorization of §3.4, Lie-group test functions, smooth vectors, the word and Sobolev seminorms on them, smoothness of `G`-finite vectors and the integrated operators of Gårding smoothing (§§0.1a, 2.7), `K`-finite vectors (§2.1), finite generation, coefficient change with its base change and restriction functors, smooth characters, smooth duals and admissibility of smooth representations (SR.0.4), products of `A`-valued Haar measures, integration of `C_c^∞` against them and the open-closed exact sequence of `C_c^∞` (SR.1.1), the Hecke algebras of permutation modules (SR.1.2), compactness of the points of projective schemes over a nonarchimedean local field and local charts on the points of smooth algebras over a complete nontrivially normed field (RG2.0.2), and the discreteness of `G(K)` in the finite adelic points and the triviality of the modular character on compact and central elements (AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.1.3, AA.1.5).

## Library-scale gap
None beyond the merged roadmaps: SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups, AdelicAlgebraicGroups, ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6 and AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups Layers 2–3 lay out the theory (Bump, *Automorphic Forms and Representations* Ch. 3; Flath, Corvallis; Getz–Hahn). The risk is the depth of the stack: RG2.3 has in code only the definitions of smooth and reductive integral models and hyperspecial subgroups, and the merged supplier layers have only their first items in code (SR.0.1, parts of SR.0.4, SR.1.1 and SR.1.2, AFORG §§0.1a, 2.1's `K`-finite vectors, 2.7). The finite-dimensional algebra of §3.4 (`RestrictedTensor`, `finiteCornerFactorization`) needs only Mathlib and is under way.

Related definitions: #72, #73, #76, #90 (nearly isotypic subspace) all depend on #67.
