## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
The restricted tensor product, the spherical-line bound and Flath's factorization with local components `localFactor` are explicit targets of merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §§3.4–3.5, with the global `(𝔤,K)×G(𝔸_f)` module and `IsAutomorphicRepresentation` in §3.2 and the automorphic-form carrier in merged ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6. Its local and adelic inputs (smooth admissible representations, Hecke algebras, Satake; adelic points, integral models, admissible maximal compact `∏K_v`) are targets of the merged roadmaps SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups and AdelicAlgebraicGroups. The definition is not in code; first pieces of its inputs are: smooth representations and smooth vectors of topological groups (SR.0.1), the existence half of the finite-corner factorization of §3.4, and Lie-group test functions and smooth vectors (§§0.1a, 2.7).

## Library-scale gap
None beyond the merged roadmaps: SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups, AdelicAlgebraicGroups, ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6 and AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups Layers 2–3 lay out the theory (Bump, *Automorphic Forms and Representations* Ch. 3; Flath, Corvallis; Getz–Hahn). The risk is the depth of the stack: RG2.3 (hyperspecial models) is untouched and the merged supplier layers have only their first items in code (SR.0.1, AFORG §§0.1a, 2.7). The finite-dimensional algebra of §3.4 (`RestrictedTensor`, `finiteCornerFactorization`) needs only Mathlib and is under way.
