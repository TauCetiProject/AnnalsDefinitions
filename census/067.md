## Library-scale gap
None beyond the merged roadmaps: SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups, AdelicAlgebraicGroups, ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6 and AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups Layers 2–3 lay out the theory (Bump, *Automorphic Forms and Representations* Ch. 3; Flath, Corvallis; Getz–Hahn). The risk is the depth of the stack: RG2.3 has in code only the definitions of smooth and reductive integral models and hyperspecial subgroups, and the merged supplier layers have only their first items in code (SR.0.1, parts of SR.0.4, SR.1.1 and SR.1.2, AFORG §§0.1a, 2.1's `K`-finite vectors, 2.7).
