  - Theorem A (arXiv:1807.07381, p. 2): if `A` is a separable, exact C*-algebra which satisfies the UCT and admits a faithful, amenable trace, then there is a simple, unital AF-algebra `B` with a unique trace and a trace-preserving embedding `A ↪ B`.
