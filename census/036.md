## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED: amenable traces are not a target anywhere, but c.p.c. maps, nuclearity, exactness and quasidiagonality (including the matrix-map criterion) are targets of merged CStarAlgebras (not implemented), Mathlib has completely positive maps, and Tau Ceti has tracial states only implicitly as 0-KMS states.

## Mathlib
- Definition absent: no tracial state, no amenable trace, no hypertrace.
- Present: `CompletelyPositiveMap` / `CompletelyPositiveMapClass`, notation `A₁ →CP A₂` (`Mathlib/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CompletelyPositiveMap.lean`, defined via `CStarMatrix` positivity for all `k`); positive linear maps and functionals `A →P[ℂ] ℂ` (`PositiveLinearMap.lean`, `PositiveLinearFunctional.lean`); GNS (`GelfandNaimarkSegal.lean`); `CStarMatrix`; `Matrix.trace`; C*-algebras (non-unital and unital) classes.
- Nearest missing pieces: a `TracialState` structure (state + `τ(ab)=τ(ba)`), the normalised 2-norm on `M_n`, and the predicate itself (a net of c.p.c. maps into `M_{n(i)}` that are 2-norm asymptotically multiplicative and asymptotically trace-preserving).

## Tau Ceti code
- Definition absent (grep for amenable trace / hypertrace / quasidiagonal / nuclear C*-algebra finds nothing; `Analysis/Normed/Operator/Nuclear.lean` is Banach-space nuclear operators, unrelated).
- Tracial states appear only implicitly: `TauCeti.CStarAction.isKMSState_zero_iff` (`TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/Action/KMS/Basic.lean`) characterises 0-KMS states as norm-one positive functionals with `ω(ab)=ω(ba)`. Also `CStarConditionalExpectation` (`Analysis/CStarAlgebra/ConditionalExpectation/Basic.lean`), GNS (`Analysis/StarAlgebra/GNS/Basic.lean`).

- Prerequisites in `TauCetiRoadmap/CStarAlgebras/README.md` (no STATUS.md, so nothing implemented yet): Layer 3 C3.2 completely bounded maps; Layer 4 C4.1 completely positive maps, Schwarz, multiplicative domain (the trace state on `M_n` is an explicit example there); Layer 5 tensor products and inductive limits (C5.4, for AF algebras); Layer 6 C6.1–C6.2 nuclear maps/nuclear algebras via c.p.c. factorisation through `M_n` (closest analogue of Def 36.3); Layer 7 C7.1 exactness; Layer 8 C8.1–C8.3 quasidiagonality and "the matrix-map criterion" (operator-norm analogue of Def 36.3, i.e. quasidiagonal traces' cousin).

## Prerequisite chain
1. C*-algebras, positive functionals, CP maps, `CStarMatrix`, `Matrix.trace`: in Mathlib.
2. Tracial state (as a structure): implicit in Tau Ceti (`isKMSState_zero_iff`); no named definition; positive tracial functionals are a target of merged OperatorKTheory §1.5 (not implemented).
3. Normalised trace and 2-norm on `M_n`: trivial from Mathlib (`Matrix.trace`, Frobenius norm); unplanned as such.
4. c.p.c. maps into `M_n`: Mathlib `CompletelyPositiveMap` plus contractivity; CStarAlgebras C4.1 (merged, not implemented) for the API.
5. Amenable trace (Def 36.3): unplanned.
6. Equivalences (hypertrace, nuclear ⇒ all traces amenable, exact case via hyperfinite GNS closure): need CStarAlgebras C6 (nuclearity), C7.1 (exactness), C2.1 (state extension), VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 13 (hyperfinite = injective); all merged, not implemented; the equivalences themselves unplanned.
7. Application (Schafhauser): simple unital AF-algebras (C5.4 inductive limits, merged), UCT and KK-theory (not examined here; likely unplanned).

## Library-scale gap
A "Traces on C*-algebras" layer is needed: the tracial state space T(A) (weak* compact convex, Choquet simplex for unital A), faithful/amenable/quasidiagonal traces, hypertraces, Kirchberg's and Brown's equivalences (Brown, *Invariant means and finite representation theory of C*-algebras*, Mem. AMS 2006; Brown–Ozawa, *C*-Algebras and Finite-Dimensional Approximations*, Ch. 6), with nuclear ⇒ amenable and the exact/hyperfinite characterisation. It sits naturally as a new layer of CStarAlgebras after Layer 6–8 (it reuses c.p.c. approximation and quasidiagonality), consuming VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 13.
