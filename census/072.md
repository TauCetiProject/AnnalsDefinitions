## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Definition 72.2 is the complement of the non-CM subset of target R16.4.6 "The non-CM self-twist condition" of merged [GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer/README.md): on the cuspidal `GL₂(𝔸_F)` isomorphism classes (the ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6 carrier), over any number field `F`, non-CM means trivial stabilizer under twisting by all Hecke characters, with `χ² = 1` for self-twists (72.3(a)) and twist invariance (Remark 2). R16.4.6 imports a determinant-twist action without naming its owner: its *Needs* line names only R16.4.1, ALS.F.6 and AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors AL.3. The only roadmap that defines the twist action and central characters is merged AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §3.2 (`twist`, `centralCharacter`), so the census takes §3.2 as the supplier; that `twist` preserves the cuspidal carrier is not a target. Quadratic automorphic induction `AI_{K/F}(θ)` is target R17.4.16 of the same roadmap (with `AI θ ≅ AI θ ⊗ η` and cuspidality iff `θ ≠ θ^σ`), but the Labesse–Langlands converse 72.3(b) (a cuspidal `π` with a nontrivial self-twist is automorphically induced) is not a target there: R17.4.13 (Gelbart–Jacquet) states it in its opening prose and R17.4.5 defers it to R17.4.16, whose API does not contain it. Merged AutomorphicGaloisRepresentations §3.15 defines its own CM predicate on regular Hilbert eigensystems by automorphic induction (the induced form of 72.3(b), totally real `F`). Nothing in code.
