# 90. The π-nearly isotypic cuspidal subspace

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII: roadmap", §4.2a "Near equivalence and the π-nearly isotypic cuspidal subspace", states Def. 90.2–90.3 and Prop. 90.4 essentially verbatim, citing BPLZZ Theorem 1.1. That covers the closure of the sum of irreducible cuspidal subrepresentations nearly equivalent to a not-necessarily-automorphic `π`, the level-`K` part, the spherical criterion, and the strict containment of the `χ_π^T`-eigenpart (Remark 4). No merged roadmap specifies the definition. Its prerequisites are merged targets: cusp forms, `L²_cusp` discreteness and finite multiplicity (ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6), actual cuspidal and discrete occurrences (AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §4.2), restricted tensor factorisation and local components (AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §§3.4–3.5), `G(𝔸_F)` and its quotient measures (AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.1–AA.2), the Satake classification (SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.4), and the spectral adapters of AutomorphicSpectralTheory (§0.50 restricted tensor factorisation of irreducible unitary realizations, §4.4 `L²_disc = L²_cusp ⊕ L²_res`, §4.5 finite multiplicity, §4.6 Hecke and central-character compatibility); of these only the topological groups `G(𝔸_F)` are in code (`TauCeti.AdelicPoints`). Nothing of the definition is implemented.
