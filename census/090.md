# 90. The π-nearly isotypic cuspidal subspace

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII/README.md) §4.2a "Near equivalence and the π-nearly isotypic cuspidal subspace" (README l.1191) states Def. 90.2–90.3 and Prop. 90.4 essentially verbatim, citing BPLZZ Theorem 1.1. That covers the closure of the sum of irreducible cuspidal subrepresentations nearly equivalent to a not-necessarily-automorphic `π`, the level-`K` part, the spherical criterion, and the strict containment of the `χ_π^T`-eigenpart (Remark 4). Its prerequisites are merged targets: cusp forms, `L²_cusp` discreteness and finite multiplicity (ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6), actual cuspidal and discrete occurrences (AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §4.2), restricted tensor factorisation and local components (AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §§3.4–3.5), `G(𝔸_F)` and its quotient measures (AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.1–AA.2), the Satake classification (SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.4), and the spectral adapters of AutomorphicSpectralTheory (§0.50 restricted tensor factorisation of irreducible unitary realizations, §4.4 `L²_disc = L²_cusp ⊕ L²_res`, §4.5 finite multiplicity, §4.6 Hecke and central-character compatibility); of these only the topological groups `G(𝔸_F)` are in code (`TauCeti.AdelicPoints`). Nothing of the definition is implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: Hilbert spaces, closed subspaces, orthogonal projections, `lp`/Hilbert direct sums, `MeasureTheory.Lp`, Haar measure, unitary representations only as group homomorphisms into unitaries.
- Missing: adelic quotients `G(F)\G(𝔸_F)`, `L²` with central character, cusp forms, automorphic representations and their local components.
