# 90. The π-nearly isotypic cuspidal subspace

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII: roadmap", §4.2a "Near equivalence and the π-nearly isotypic cuspidal subspace", states Def. 90.2–90.3 and Prop. 90.4 essentially verbatim, citing BPLZZ Theorem 1.1. That covers the closure of the sum of irreducible cuspidal subrepresentations nearly equivalent to a not-necessarily-automorphic `π`, the level-`K` part, the spherical criterion, and the strict containment of the `χ_π^T`-eigenpart (Remark 4). Its prerequisites (`L²_cusp`, discreteness, finite multiplicity, restricted tensor factorisation) are in open PRs #1037, #925 and #871. Nothing is merged or implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: Hilbert spaces, closed subspaces, orthogonal projections, `lp`/Hilbert direct sums, `MeasureTheory.Lp`, Haar measure, unitary representations only as group homomorphisms into unitaries.
- Missing: adelic quotients `G(F)\G(𝔸_F)`, `L²` with central character, cusp forms, automorphic representations and their local components.

- Restricted products and adeles (Completed/RestrictedProducts), point topology on `G(F_v)` and compact-open integral points, Haar averages on compact groups (`TauCeti.haarProb`, `TauCeti.haarAverage`).
- Missing: everything from `L²(G(F)\G(𝔸_F), ω)` onwards.
