## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.OuterAut G = MulAut G ⧸ MulAut.conj.range` for every group, with `OuterAut.mk` (kernel the inner automorphisms), `mk_eq_mk_iff`, `OuterAut.congr` (transport along an isomorphism, including the inversion `Gᵐᵒᵖ ≃* G` used for fundamental groups) and `mulEquivOfIsMulCommutative` (`GroupTheory/Automorphism/Outer.lean`): Definition 61.1. `TauCeti.ContinuousOut G = ContinuousAut G ⧸ conj.range` (`Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Basic.lean`), isomorphic to `OuterAut` for discrete `G` (`mulEquivOuterAutOfDiscrete`, `ContinuousAut/Discrete.lean`); `outerAction : E ⧸ N →* ContinuousOut N` for group extensions (`.../ContinuousAut/OuterAction.lean`).
- Surface groups as presented groups `TauCeti.SurfaceGroup.Orientable g m` with peripheral elements (`GroupTheory/SurfaceGroup/Basic.lean`), not identified with the topological `π₁`.
- No homeomorphism-to-Out homomorphism for spaces. The only mapping class group is the smooth `TauCeti.MappingClassGroup I M` = π₀ Diff(M, ∂M) of a compact manifold (`Geometry/Diffeomorphism/MappingClassGroup.lean`), with no action on `π₁` and no punctures.
