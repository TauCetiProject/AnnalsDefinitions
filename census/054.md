## Mathlib
No character variety or representation space of a surface group. Has `FundamentalGroup`, group homomorphisms with `Pi` topology (`ContinuousMap`, product topology), quotient topology for group actions (`MulAction.orbitRel.Quotient`), `IsSemisimpleModule` (complete reducibility), `LieAlgebra.killingForm`, `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup`, `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`. No Lie group real forms, no algebraic-group Zariski closure for real groups.

## Tau Ceti code
- Cartan involutions of real Lie algebras: `LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution` (positive definite twisted Killing form), the Cartan decomposition `LieEquiv.isCompl_fixedSubalgebra_cartanP` with the bracket relations and signs of the Killing form (`Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/{Basic,Decomposition}.lean`); the instance for `sp l R` (`Algebra/Lie/Symplectic/Cartan.lean`, `Symplectic/Killing.lean`) and `GLSymplectic` (`LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Symplectic/Cartan.lean`); `TauCeti.IsRealForm` for representations (`RepresentationTheory/RealForm.lean`).
- Cartan involutions of real affine algebraic groups: `TauCeti.twistedRealPoints θ` (the real points `{g ∈ H(ℂ) : g = θ(conj g)}` of the real form twisted by an involution `θ`) and `TauCeti.IsCartanInvolution θ` (they are compact), with inverse transpose on `GLₙ` as the example (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/CartanInvolution.lean`, `Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/GeneralLinear/CartanInvolution.lean`).
- Cartan decompositions of `Sp(2n, ℝ)` and of the realified `GL(n, ℂ)` (`Analysis/Matrix/Cartan/Realify.lean`).
- Surface groups as presented groups: `TauCeti.SurfaceGroup.Orientable g m` with generators `a`, `b` and `peripheral` elements, and the nonorientable groups (`GroupTheory/SurfaceGroup/Basic.lean`, `Closed.lean`), the abelianization of closed surface groups, freeness with boundary, torsion-freeness. No identification with the topological `π₁`.
- `TauCeti.OuterAut G` for every group (`GroupTheory/Automorphism/Outer.lean`), consumed by PR #2430 §1.1.
- No real reductive group datum, no Hom(π₁Σ, G) space, no reductive-representation predicate, no character variety.
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45233 (LieGroups Layer 9 global matrix model: `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.cartan_polar_decomposition` for closed subgroups of `GL(n, 𝕜)` stable under conjugate transpose, the input of LieGroupsPartII §7.1 self-adjoint linear groups).
