# 45. Schrödinger operator −Δ + V on L²(ℝ^d)

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED, for d = 3 only. Merged `TauCetiRoadmap/OperatorTheory/UnboundedOperators/` UO-E06 (§5.7) specifies −Δ with domain H² on L²(ℝ³) and the essential self-adjointness of −Δ + V on `C_c^∞(ℝ³)` for real V ∈ L² + L^∞, and states that it is "an ℝ³ theorem" not extended to ℝⁿ. Merged [QuantumFoundations](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/QuantumFoundations/README.md) §8.2 (merged PR #1771) specifies the free Laplacian `H₀ = −Δ/(2m)` on `H²(ℝⁿ)` for every `n ≥ 1`, self-adjoint with Schwartz and `C_c^∞` cores and `σ(H₀) = σ_ac(H₀) = [0, ∞)`, but no potential. −Δ + V in every dimension is specified only in open PR #2424 (SchrodingerOperatorsAndSpectralTypes) §4.4 defines `H_V = H₀ + M_V` on `H²(ℝ^d)` for real `V ∈ L^∞(ℝ^d)`, `d ≥ 1`, self-adjoint, bounded below by `ess inf V`, essentially self-adjoint on `C_c^∞` and on Schwartz space, with `σ_ess = [0, ∞)` for `V` vanishing at infinity, and §4.5 treats the paper's quasi-periodic trigonometric potentials; it takes the free Laplacian `H₀ = −Δ` from QuantumFoundations §8.2. In Tau Ceti: bounded Kato–Rellich, self-adjoint maximal multiplication operators on `L²` (UO-A07), and the free Laplacian on Lᵖ(ℝ^d) as the heat-semigroup generator `TauCeti.weakLaplacianLp` with `C_c^∞` core and, for real-valued functions and `1 < p < ∞`, domain `W^{2,p}` (`H²` at `p = 2`); its self-adjointness on L²(ℝ^d) is not.

## Mathlib
- Has: Fourier transform on L² (`Analysis/Fourier/LpSpace`), tempered-distribution Fourier multipliers, Bessel potential spaces `TemperedDistribution.MemSobolev` / `BesselPotentialSpace` (`H^{s,p}(E,F)`, so H²(ℝ^d) = H^{2,2}), Laplacian `InnerProductSpace.instLaplacian`, `LinearPMap` and `IsSelfAdjoint`.
- Missing: −Δ as a self-adjoint `LinearPMap` on L²(ℝ^d), Schrödinger operators.

## Tau Ceti code
- Bounded and relatively bounded Kato–Rellich: `IsSelfAdjoint.add_of_isRelativelyBounded` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/KatoRellich.lean`), `Analysis/Normed/Operator/LinearPMap/RelativeBound.lean`, perturbation constructors `LinearAlgebra/LinearPMap/Perturbation.lean` (Prop 45.3).
- Bounded multiplication operator: `multiplicationOperator` (`MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/Multiplication.lean`). Maximal unbounded multiplication operators on `Lᵖ` (UO-A07): `MeasureTheory.Lp.maximalMultiplication`, closed and densely defined, adjoint `MeasureTheory.Lp.adjoint_maximalMultiplication`, self-adjoint for real symbols `MeasureTheory.Lp.isSelfAdjoint_maximalMultiplication`, `MeasureTheory.Lp.existsUnique_maximal_multiplier` (`MeasureTheory/Function/Lp/MaximalMultiplication/Basic.lean`).
- Heat semigroup on Lᵖ(ℝⁿ): `TauCeti.heatSemigroup`, with generator the distributional Laplacian on its maximal domain `{f ∈ Lᵖ : Δf ∈ Lᵖ}`, `1 ≤ p < ∞` (`TauCeti.generator_heatSemigroup_eq_weakLaplacianLp`, `TauCeti.mem_domain_heatSemigroup_iff`, `Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Generator.lean`; the partial map `TauCeti.weakLaplacianLp`, `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Laplacian/WeakLaplacianLp.lean`). Its resolvent is convolution with the Bessel-type kernel `TauCeti.heatResolventKernel`, whose Fourier transform is `(λ + 4π²‖ξ‖²)⁻¹` (`TauCeti.resolvent_heatSemigroup`, `TauCeti.resolvent_generator_heatSemigroup`, `TauCeti.fourier_heatResolventKernel`, `Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Resolvent.lean`). Test functions `C_c^∞` form a core (`TauCeti.generator_heatSemigroup_hasCore_range_ambientToLp`, `TauCeti.weakLaplacianLp_hasCore_range_ambientToLp`, `Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/Core.lean`), and for real-valued functions and `1 < p < ∞` the domain is `W^{2,p}`, equivalently the Bessel potential space `H^{2,p}`, so `H²` at `p = 2` (`TauCeti.mem_domain_heatSemigroup_iff_exists_wkp`, `TauCeti.mem_domain_heatSemigroup_iff_memSobolev`, `Analysis/PDE/HeatKernel/SobolevDomain.lean`). At `p = 2` its negative is the free Laplacian as an operator; its self-adjointness on L²(ℝ^d) is not in code.
- Schrödinger with V ≥ 0 on closed manifolds, form level only (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sobolev/Schrodinger.lean`); not ℝ^d.
- Missing: self-adjointness of −Δ on L²(ℝ^d) (as the Fourier conjugate of the maximal multiplier by `4π²|ξ|²`); −Δ + V.

1. Fourier–Plancherel on L², H^{2,2}(ℝ^d): Mathlib.
2. Self-adjoint maximal multiplication operator by |ξ|²: merged UnboundedOperators UO-A07, in Tau Ceti (`MeasureTheory.Lp.isSelfAdjoint_maximalMultiplication`).
3. Free Laplacian H₀ on L²(ℝ^d), domain H², self-adjoint, σ = [0, ∞), C_c^∞ core: as the heat-semigroup generator with `C_c^∞` core and domain `H²` (real-valued functions), in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.weakLaplacianLp`, `TauCeti.mem_domain_heatSemigroup_iff_memSobolev`, PDE §6.3); self-adjoint: merged UnboundedOperators UO-E06 for d = 3 only; every d in merged QuantumFoundations §8.2 (not implemented), with form domain and essential spectrum in PR #2424 §4.2.
4. Bounded Kato–Rellich: Tau Ceti (`LinearPMap.isSelfAdjoint_perturb_bounded`, SA-C54); lower bounds and cores of the perturbation: PR #2424 §§2.1–2.3.
5. H = −Δ + V for V ∈ L^∞ real, any d: merged UnboundedOperators UO-E06 (d = 3); PR #2424 §4.4 for every d ≥ 1.
6. Spectral measures and σ_ac (needed to state the paper's theorem): definition #35, target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-C03; `σ_ac` of `H_V` by PR #2424 §1.4.
7. Quasi-periodic potentials: PR #2424 §4.5 (elementary).

## Library-scale gap
Specified by PR #2424 Layer 4 on merged QuantumFoundations §8.2: what remains is the review and merging of PR #2424, then implementation on UO-A07 (the free Laplacian as a conjugated multiplier) and the bounded Kato–Rellich theorem, both already in Tau Ceti. The paper's content (Bethe–Sommerfeld, multiscale perturbation theory for quasi-periodic operators) is far beyond, but stating it needs only 45 and 35. Reference: Reed–Simon II, §X.2 and IX.7; Teschl, *Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics*, Ch. 7 and 10.
