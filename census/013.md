## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
The Mumford–Tate group of a finite-dimensional rational Hodge structure is target §P.3a of merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) (README l.189): `MT(V) ⊂ GL(V)` as the subgroup of the supremum `I_h` of the Hopf ideals of `O(GL(V))` killed by `h*` (`TauCeti.HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal`), with geometric connectedness, the sub-Hodge-structure criterion, the scalars in nonzero pure weight and reductivity for polarizable `V` (`TauCeti.Hodge.exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`). It sits on §P.3, the dictionary between Hodge structures and homomorphisms `h : S → GL(V)_ℝ` (l.177). Merged [ShimuraData](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraData/README.md) §1.2 `mumfordTateGroup` (README l.185) specifies the same construction, with connectedness and reductivity in §§1.3–1.4. The Deligne torus, Hopf-ideal joins and polarizable Hodge structures are in Tau Ceti; `h` and `MT(V)` are not. Uses #12 for `MT(A)`.

Proposition 13.3(a) of the source wrongly calls `I_V` a Hopf ideal; see [errata](../sources/errata.md).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Pure rational HS, with tensor, dual, Tate twist and polarizable splitting (`exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`). In Tau Ceti.
2. `O(GL_V)` as a Hopf algebra, joins of Hopf ideals (`HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal`), identity components, unipotent radical. In Tau Ceti.
3. Deligne torus `S = Res_{ℂ/ℝ}𝔾_m` as an algebraic group, with `h: S → GL_{V,ℝ}` and `μ_h`. `S`, its splitting over `ℂ`, `μ` and the characters are in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus`, merged ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.0a.3); the HS-to-`h` dictionary is target §P.3 of merged ShimuraVarieties (not started).
4. `MT(V)` with connectedness, the stable-subspace criterion, scalars and reductivity: target §P.3a of merged ShimuraVarieties (not started); also merged ShimuraData §§1.2–1.4 (not started).
5. `MT(A) := MT(V_A)`. Needs #12 (merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §§A5.1–A5.5); for `A` over a number field, merged ShimuraVarieties §7.0i.1. Definition #23 (Hodge-generic abelian varieties) builds on this, cf. merged ShimuraData §1.5.
6. The Tannakian description is unplanned and not part of the definition.
