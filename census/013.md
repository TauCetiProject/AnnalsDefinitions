## Prerequisite chain
1. Pure rational HS, with tensor, dual and Tate twist. In Tau Ceti.
2. `O(GL_V)` as a Hopf algebra, and joins of Hopf ideals. In Tau Ceti.
3. Deligne torus `S = Res_{ℂ/ℝ}𝔾_m` as an algebraic group, with `h: S → GL_{V,ℝ}` and `μ_h`. Target in merged ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.0a; `S` and its points are in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus.S`), `h` and `μ_h` are not. The HS-to-`h` dictionary is in PR #933 §P.3.
4. `MT(V) = Spec O(GL_V)/I_MT`. Target in PR #1038 §1.2.
5. `MT(A) := MT(V_A)`. Needs #12 (PR #849 A5), then PR #1038 §1.5. Definition #23 (Hodge-generic abelian varieties) builds on this, cf. PR #1038 §1.5.
6. Theorems (connectedness, reductivity, Hodge-tensor description): PR #1038 §§1.3–1.4. The Tannakian description is unplanned and not part of the definition.
