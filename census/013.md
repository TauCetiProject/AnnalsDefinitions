## Status
IN-CODE
Definitions 13.1 and 13.2 are in Tau Ceti. For a rational Hodge structure with a basis `b` of `V_ℚ`, `TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn.deligneHom` is the homomorphism `h_V : S → GL_{V,ℝ}` on coordinate rings, a morphism of commutative Hopf `ℝ`-algebras `ℝ ⊗[ℚ] O(GL_d) ⟶ O(S)` (Galois descent of the split coordinate morphism, `realCoordinateBialgHom`, with `realCoordinateBialgHom_genericMatrix` identifying its complex points with `complexDeligneAction`), and `HodgeStructureOn.mumfordTateHopfIdeal hs hℚ b` is the Hopf ideal of `MT(V)` in `O(GL_V)`, with minimality `le_mumfordTateHopfIdeal_iff` (`Geometry/Hodge/MumfordTate.lean`). For any algebraic `h : S → G_ℝ` the construction `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal` has the factorization through `MT(h)_ℝ` (`mumfordTateLift`, `mumfordTateLift_unique`), transport along isomorphisms, behaviour under inner automorphisms and change of basis (so `MT(V)` is independent of `b` up to conjugation, `mumfordTateHopfIdeal_conjCoordinateIso`), finiteness for noetherian `H`, the rational coordinate kernel `K_h` with `le_mumfordTateHopfIdeal_iff_toIdeal_le_rationalCoordinateKernel`, and `MT(h)` as a closed subgroup scheme, `mumfordTateClosedSubgroup` with `mumfordTateClosedSubgroup_le_iff` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate/{Basic,Conjugation,Kernel,ClosedSubgroup}.lean`). The Hodge cocharacter is `DeligneTorus.mu` through `h`, and Hodge tensors (13.4) are the `(0, 0)` piece of the tensor Hodge structures (`HodgeStructureOn.tensorProduct`, `dual`), with no named declaration. This is the construction of merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) §§P.3–P.3a and [ShimuraData](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraData/README.md) §1.2. Propositions 13.3(c)–(d) and 13.5 are theorems, not part of the definition, and are not in code: connectedness, stable subspaces and scalars are in open Tau Ceti PRs (below), and for reductivity the criterion that a faithful completely reducible representation makes a geometrically connected group reductive is in code (`reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_of_isFaithful_of_isCompletelyReducible`), with its application to `MT(V)` not started. `MT(A) = MT(V_A)` uses #12, which is partly in code.

Proposition 13.3(a) of the source wrongly calls `I_V` a Hopf ideal; see [errata](../sources/errata.md).

1. Pure rational HS, with tensor, dual, Tate twist and polarizable splitting (`exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`). In Tau Ceti.
2. `O(GL_V)` as a Hopf algebra, joins of Hopf ideals (`HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal`), identity components, unipotent radical. In Tau Ceti.
3. Deligne torus `S = Res_{ℂ/ℝ}𝔾_m` as an algebraic group, with `h: S → GL_{V,ℝ}` and `μ_h`. `S`, its splitting over `ℂ`, `μ` and the characters are in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus`, merged ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.0a.3), and so are the actions of `S(ℂ)` and `S(ℝ)` on a Hodge structure (`HodgeStructureOn.complexDeligneAction`, `deligneAction`).
