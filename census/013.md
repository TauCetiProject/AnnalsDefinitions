The construction of Definition 13.2 is in Tau Ceti for any algebraic homomorphism from the Deligne torus: for `h : S → G_ℝ` given on coordinate rings as a morphism of commutative Hopf `ℝ`-algebras `ℝ ⊗[ℚ] H ⟶ O(S)`, `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal h` is the largest Hopf ideal of `H` whose real extension `h` kills, with minimality (`le_mumfordTateHopfIdeal_iff`), the unique factorization of `h` through `MT(h)_ℝ` (`mumfordTateLift`, `mumfordTateLift_unique`), transport along isomorphisms (`comapOfSurjective_mumfordTateHopfIdeal`) and finiteness for noetherian `H` (`exists_finset_sup_eq_mumfordTateHopfIdeal`) (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate.lean`). This is the construction of merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) §P.3a and [ShimuraData](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraData/README.md) §1.2. A Hodge structure gives only the actions of `S(ℂ)` and `S(ℝ)` on points (`HodgeStructureOn.complexDeligneAction`, `deligneAction`, `Geometry/Hodge/DeligneTorus.lean`). Missing: Definition 13.1's algebraic `h : S → GL_{V,ℝ}` of a rational Hodge structure, as a Hopf-algebra map out of `ℝ ⊗ O(GL_V)` (merged ShimuraVarieties §P.3, ShimuraData §1.1), so `MT(V)` is not yet attached to a Hodge structure; and Proposition 13.3(c)–(d) (connectedness, scalars, reductivity; ShimuraVarieties §P.3a, ShimuraData §§1.3–1.4). Uses #12 for `MT(A)`.

Proposition 13.3(a) of the source wrongly calls `I_V` a Hopf ideal; see [errata](../sources/errata.md).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Pure rational HS, with tensor, dual, Tate twist and polarizable splitting (`exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`). In Tau Ceti.
2. `O(GL_V)` as a Hopf algebra, joins of Hopf ideals (`HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal`), identity components, unipotent radical. In Tau Ceti.
3. Deligne torus `S = Res_{ℂ/ℝ}𝔾_m` as an algebraic group, with `h: S → GL_{V,ℝ}` and `μ_h`. `S`, its splitting over `ℂ`, `μ` and the characters are in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus`, merged ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.0a.3), and so are the actions of `S(ℂ)` and `S(ℝ)` on a Hodge structure (`HodgeStructureOn.complexDeligneAction`, `deligneAction`). The algebraic `h` as a Hopf-algebra map out of `ℝ ⊗ O(GL_V)` is target §P.3 of merged ShimuraVarieties and §1.1 of merged ShimuraData (not started).
4. `MT(h)` for an algebraic `h`, with minimality, transport and finiteness: in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal`), the construction of merged ShimuraVarieties §P.3a and ShimuraData §1.2. Connectedness, the stable-subspace criterion, scalars and reductivity: ShimuraVarieties §P.3a and ShimuraData §§1.3–1.4 (not started).
5. `MT(A) := MT(V_A)`. Needs #12 (merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §§A5.1–A5.5); for `A` over a number field, merged ShimuraVarieties §7.0i.1. Definition #23 (Hodge-generic abelian varieties) builds on this, cf. merged ShimuraData §1.5.
6. The Tannakian description is unplanned and not part of the definition.
