## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. RiemannianGeometry R5a.2 (minimality H = 0, first variation), R5b (second variation Q, Jacobi operator, stability, Dirichlet index on relatively compact domains) and R5c (closed manifolds, index on smooth sections) specify it for immersions into Riemannian manifolds, and the MinimalSurfaces sub-roadmap MS6.1–MS6.3 specifies the Morse index and nullity of compact minimal submanifolds (closed or Dirichlet), the index of noncompact ones by exhaustion, and the one-sided hypersurface case; not implemented beyond the pointwise relative volume density of R5a.1 (`relativeVolumeDensity`) and R5a's normal bundle `ν_f` of an immersion (`orthogonalNormalContMDiffVectorBundle`). Merged Varifolds §5.1 gives the varifold reading of minimality. Bumpy metrics (52.4(4)) are not a target. Depends on #49.

## Mathlib
Nothing on minimal submanifolds. Prerequisites: immersions, Riemannian metrics, Levi-Civita, Riemannian measure is not in Mathlib in general.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: H, minimality = H = 0, first variation formula `δVol = ∫div X^⊤ − m⟨H,X^⊥⟩`, stationarity iff H = 0, minimal cones. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.1: orientation-free C² relative-volume functional for compactly supported variations. Partial: the relative density J_t of two metrics with `dvol_(g_t) = J_t dvol_(g_0)`, positivity, smoothness, basis and chart independence and restriction is `relativeVolumeDensity`; the parameter dependence and the functional `V(t) = ∫ (J_t − 1) dvol_(g_0)` with its first two derivatives are missing.
- RiemannianGeometry R5b: second variation Q(V,W), normal Jacobi operator, stability, two-sided scalar form L = Δ⁺ − (|A|² + Ric(ν,ν)), Dirichlet Jacobi operator L_D and "index of Q on V = number of negative eigenvalues of L_D" on relatively compact smooth domains, full-scalar stability inequality, double-cover description of one-sided hypersurfaces. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5c ("Schrödinger operators on compact manifolds"): the closed case, min–max, the index on smooth sections, and for scalar operators simplicity and positivity of the lowest eigenvalue with strict domain monotonicity. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R7.9: two-sided and one-sided closed embedded hypersurfaces, the unit normal double cover. Not implemented.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md):
  - MS6.1: `index(Σ)` of a compact minimal submanifold, closed or with Dirichlet condition, as the supremum of dimensions of subspaces on which Q is negative definite, equal to the number of negative eigenvalues of `−J^⊥`; the nullity `dim ker(−J^⊥)`.
  - MS6.2: the index of a noncompact minimal submanifold by exhaustion, independent of the exhaustion and equal to the supremum over compactly supported normal sections (Definition 52.4(1) in general).
  - MS6.3: hypersurfaces, one-sided through the normal double cover and two-sided through the scalar Jacobi operator.
  - MS6.4–MS6.5 and MS7: instability under `Ric > 0`, the examples in `S³` and `ℝP³`, stability and positive Jacobi functions.
  - None implemented.
- Consumers: [PositiveMass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PositiveMass/README.md) Layer 3 (compact stable minimal hypersurfaces, through R5b), [CalibratedGeometry](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/CalibratedGeometry/README.md) C3 and C6.5 (through R5a.1 and R5b); GeometricCurrents G6/G7 (smooth minimal hypersurfaces as regularity output, 2 ≤ n ≤ 7).
- [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md) §5.1: for a `C²` embedded submanifold `Σ`, closed with locally finite area, `δ[Σ](X) = −k ∫ ⟨H_Σ, X⟩`, so `[Σ]` is stationary iff `Σ` is minimal, and `δ[Σ]` equals R5a.2's first volume variation; this is 53.5(b), the varifold reading of 52.1. Not implemented.
- Gaps vs def #52: bumpy metrics and "geometrically distinct" are not targets.

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR #2491 "MinimalSurfaces: stability across a singular set and strict stability": MS7.8, stability and strict stability (with a weight) of a two-sided hypersurface tested by ambient functions across the singular set of its closure, and the strict-stability criterion `λ₁ + ((n−2)/2)² > 0` for minimal cones; it extends 52.4(2)'s stability to singular hypersurfaces and does not change the definition.

## Prerequisite chain
Riemannian manifold, Levi-Civita [Mathlib] -> normal bundle ν_f [R5a, in Tau Ceti `orthogonalNormalContMDiffVectorBundle`] -> B, S, H (#49) [R5a/R5a.2, not implemented] -> relative volume [R5a.1, partial: the pointwise relative density `relativeVolumeDensity` in Tau Ceti; the C² relative-volume functional missing, open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/43602] -> first variation [R5a.2, not implemented; needs R5a.1 and DifferentialGeometry Layer 12 integration/divergence] -> minimal immersion H = 0 [R5a.2] -> second variation, Jacobi operator, stability [R5b] -> spectral theory on closed manifolds and Dirichlet domains [R5b, R5c] -> index and nullity, compact and by exhaustion, one-sided case [MinimalSurfaces MS6.1–MS6.3, with R7.9's normal double cover] -> bumpy metrics [unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
Essentially covered; the residue is bumpy metrics with White's theorem. A min-max roadmap (Almgren-Pitts, Marques-Neves; Colding-Minicozzi, *A Course in Minimal Surfaces*) would be the natural consumer for the Annals papers (Song, Zhou, Chodosh-Mantoulidis).
