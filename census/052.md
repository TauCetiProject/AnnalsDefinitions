## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. RiemannianGeometry R5a.2 (minimality H = 0, first variation), R5b (second variation Q, Jacobi operator, stability, Dirichlet index on relatively compact domains) and R5c (closed manifolds, index on smooth sections) specify it for immersions into Riemannian manifolds, and the MinimalSurfaces sub-roadmap MS6.1–MS6.3 specifies the Morse index and nullity of compact minimal submanifolds (closed or Dirichlet), the index of noncompact ones by exhaustion, and the one-sided hypersurface case; not implemented. Bumpy metrics (52.4(4)) are not a target. Depends on #49.

## Mathlib
Nothing on minimal submanifolds. Prerequisites: immersions, Riemannian metrics, Levi-Civita, Riemannian measure is not in Mathlib in general.

## Tau Ceti code
No minimal hypersurface, area variation of immersions, Jacobi operator or Morse index. Relevant pieces: the flat-ambient second fundamental form on manifold sources (`TauCeti.manifoldSecondFundamentalForm`, `Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`) and normal projections along immersions into a Riemannian manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Projection.lean`); uniform immersion stability under compactly supported variations (`eventually_forall_injective_mfderiv_of_isCompact`, `Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/Family.lean`); the pointwise first variation `∂_τ ρ = ½ tr_g(h) ρ` of the chart volume density (`TauCeti.hasDerivAt_chartVolumeDensity`, `Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Variation.lean`); coercive-form spectral theory `IsCoercive.formSolutionOperator`, `IsCoercive.exists_hilbertBasis_forall_apply_eq_smul_inner`, `IsCoercive.isLeast_rayleighQuotient` (named in R5b as suppliers); Riemannian volume and the boundaryless divergence theorem from DifferentialGeometry.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: H, minimality = H = 0, first variation formula `δVol = ∫div X^⊤ − m⟨H,X^⊥⟩`, stationarity iff H = 0, minimal cones. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.1: orientation-free C² relative-volume functional for compactly supported variations. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5b: second variation Q(V,W), normal Jacobi operator, stability, two-sided scalar form L = Δ⁺ − (|A|² + Ric(ν,ν)), Dirichlet Jacobi operator L_D and "index of Q on V = number of negative eigenvalues of L_D" on relatively compact smooth domains, full-scalar stability inequality, double-cover description of one-sided hypersurfaces. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5c ("Schrödinger operators on compact manifolds"): the closed case, min–max, the index on smooth sections, and for scalar operators simplicity and positivity of the lowest eigenvalue with strict domain monotonicity. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R7.9: two-sided and one-sided closed embedded hypersurfaces, the unit normal double cover. Not implemented.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md):
  - MS6.1: `index(Σ)` of a compact minimal submanifold, closed or with Dirichlet condition, as the supremum of dimensions of subspaces on which Q is negative definite, equal to the number of negative eigenvalues of `−J^⊥`; the nullity `dim ker(−J^⊥)`.
  - MS6.2: the index of a noncompact minimal submanifold by exhaustion, independent of the exhaustion and equal to the supremum over compactly supported normal sections (Definition 52.4(1) in general).
  - MS6.3: hypersurfaces, one-sided through the normal double cover and two-sided through the scalar Jacobi operator.
  - MS6.4–MS6.5 and MS7: instability under `Ric > 0`, the examples in `S³` and `ℝP³`, stability and positive Jacobi functions.
  - None implemented.
- Consumers: [PositiveMass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PositiveMass/README.md) Layer 3 (compact stable minimal hypersurfaces, through R5b), [CalibratedGeometry](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/CalibratedGeometry/README.md) C3 and C6.5 (through R5a.1 and R5b); GeometricCurrents G6/G7 (smooth minimal hypersurfaces as regularity output, 2 ≤ n ≤ 7).
- Gaps vs def #52: bumpy metrics and "geometrically distinct" are not targets.

## Prerequisite chain
Riemannian manifold, Levi-Civita [Mathlib] -> B, S, H (#49) [R5a/R5a.2, not implemented] -> relative volume [R5a.1, not implemented; needs DifferentialGeometry Layer 12 densities] -> first variation [R5a.2, not implemented; needs R5a.1 and DifferentialGeometry Layer 12 integration/divergence] -> minimal immersion H = 0 [R5a.2] -> second variation, Jacobi operator, stability [R5b] -> spectral theory on closed manifolds and Dirichlet domains [R5b, R5c] -> index and nullity, compact and by exhaustion, one-sided case [MinimalSurfaces MS6.1–MS6.3, with R7.9's normal double cover] -> bumpy metrics [unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
Essentially covered; the residue is bumpy metrics with White's theorem. A min-max roadmap (Almgren-Pitts, Marques-Neves; Colding-Minicozzi, *A Course in Minimal Surfaces*) would be the natural consumer for the Annals papers (Song, Zhou, Chodosh-Mantoulidis).
