Definitions 78.2–78.4 (closure as `Scheme.Hom.image` in the abelian-scheme model over `O_{K,S}`, its flatness and uniqueness, good reduction via `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` / `AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth`, and the equivalent forms) are stated almost verbatim in merged [NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties/README.md) §1.9a "Closures of generic subschemes and good reduction of subvarieties", on its §§1.4, 1.8–1.9 (abelian schemes are Néron models, uniqueness, spreading out). Open PR #2461 §10.1 defines good reduction outside `S` of an ample smooth hypersurface the same way. The scheme-theoretic tools are in Mathlib; abelian schemes over `O_{K,S}` are targets of merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli (§§A0.1, A1.8), not implemented. Tau Ceti has the Néron mapping property and marked Néron models with their uniqueness (§§1.1–1.4), their group law (§1.5), smooth base change (§1.10) and §1.9a's flat-base-change clause for scheme-theoretic images; the definition itself is not in code.

## Merged roadmaps
- [NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties/README.md) §1.9a "Closures of generic subschemes and good reduction of subvarieties" (README l.448): for an abelian scheme `𝒜` over `S` (the ring of integers of a number field or its localisation away from finitely many primes) with generic fibre `A`, the closure of `Z ⊆ A` as `Scheme.Hom.image` of `Z → 𝒜`, its flatness, uniqueness as the `S`-flat closed subscheme with generic fibre `Z`, flat base change; good reduction of `H` at `s` via `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` and over `S` via `AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth`, for hypersurfaces given by `Scheme.IdealSheafData.IsEffectiveCartier`; the equivalent forms (local ring, special fibre, completed local ring), the smooth proper relative effective Cartier divisor of relative dimension `dim A − 1`, and independence of `S`. This is Definitions 78.2–78.4 with their equivalences. *Needs* §1.4 (uniqueness of marked Néron models), §1.8 (abelian schemes are Néron models), §1.9 (spreading out), AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli A0.1–A1, FiniteFlatGroupsAndIntegralPadicHodgeTheory §R07.1.20, AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §5.4. Implemented: the flat-base-change clause (`Scheme.Hom.imagePullbackIso`); the closure, its flatness and uniqueness, and good reduction are not.
- [AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli/README.md): §A0.1 `AbelianScheme S` (README l.144); §A1.8 `weilExtension` (l.506): over a connected Dedekind base every morphism from the generic fibre of a smooth `S`-scheme to an abelian scheme extends uniquely, so an abelian-scheme model over `O_{K,S}` is unique; §A2.10 `AbelianNS` (l.886), the Néron–Severi group of an abelian variety with the geometric Picard number of `NS(A_{K̄})`, and §A6.16 its finiteness (l.2791). §A0.1's `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianScheme` is in code (`AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianScheme/Basic.lean`); the rest is not implemented.
- [AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry/README.md) §5.4 "Closures and normalization" (schematic closure in stacks), the base-change clause used by §1.9a.
- EllipticCurves and ModularCurves put Néron models out of scope. Prerequisites: SchemeAndStackFoundations (descent, Hilbert schemes), JacobianChallenge (abelian varieties, Picard).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Scheme-theoretic image, smooth locus, flatness: in Mathlib.
2. Effective Cartier divisors: in Tau Ceti.
3. Abelian varieties over `K`: in Tau Ceti; abelian schemes over `O_{K,S}`: target in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A0.1 (in code over any base: `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianScheme` with base change, `AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianScheme/`).
4. Good-reduction model = Néron model, uniqueness: merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §1.4 (in code: `NeronModel.unique_iso`) and §1.8 (not started); uniqueness of an abelian-scheme model also follows from merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A1.8.
5. Closure and good reduction of hypersurfaces: target in merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §1.9a (partial: the flat-base-change clause `Scheme.Hom.imagePullbackIso` in code); good reduction outside `S` of ample hypersurfaces also in PR #2461 §10.1.
6. `NS(A_{K̄})` and "represents φ" (used by the paper, not by the definition): merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A2.10 `AbelianNS` (with the Mumford map `AbelianNS.toSymmetricHom`) and §A6.16 finiteness, for abelian varieties of any dimension (not implemented).

## Library-scale gap
