# 89. ℓ-adic Galois representation with values in the Mumford–Tate group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) §7.0i.1 "Mumford–Tate-valued ℓ-adic Galois representations and Frobenius classes" (README l.2029) states Def. 89.2–89.4: `𝐆 = MT(V_B)` for `V_B = H¹(A(ℂ)^an, ℚ)`, the fixer of finitely many untwisted rational Hodge tensors, the field `E^𝐆`, `ρ_ℓ^𝐆 : Γ_E → 𝐆(ℚ_ℓ)`, `Conj_𝐆 = 𝐆//𝐆` with `χ_𝐆`, and `γ_ℓ(v) = χ_𝐆(ρ_ℓ^𝐆(Frob_v))` at good-reduction places with geometric Frobenius, independent of choices, with Checks matching Remarks 2–3. §7.0i (l.2014) is Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem (89.1), and §P.3a (l.189) the Mumford–Tate group (Def. #13). The Mumford–Tate group as a Hopf ideal of `O(GL(V))` with connectedness and reductivity is also merged ShimuraData §§1.2–1.4. The conjugacy quotient `Conj_H = H//H` with `χ_H` (89.3) is merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §1.11, consumed by merged ReductiveGroupsPartIII Layer 0, whose Layer 5 builds the Kisin–Zhou theorem on top of §7.0i.1; the Tate module, Frobenius semisimplicity and complex uniformization of abelian varieties are merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli; the Artin comparison is merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C6.6. Nothing is implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups, continuous representations into `GL_n(ℚ_ℓ)` (as continuous homomorphisms), `ConjClasses`, invariants of group actions on rings.
- Missing: étale cohomology, Betti cohomology of complex varieties with Hodge structure, Mumford–Tate groups, categorical/GIT quotients `Spec O(G)^G`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hodge structures (`Geometry/Hodge/Structure.lean`, from Completed/HodgeStructures), abelian varieties (`AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/`), affine group schemes as Hopf algebras and Hopf ideals (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/`), `GeneralLinear.coordinateHopfAlgebra`.
- Conjugation coaction `TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.conjugationAlgHom` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Hopf/Conjugation.lean`) and finite generation of coinvariants for linearly reductive Hopf algebras (`TauCeti.ComoduleAlgebra.finiteType_coinvariants`, `Algebra/Bialgebra/ComoduleAlgebra/LinearlyReductive.lean`): the inputs of `Conj_G`.
- Missing: Mumford–Tate group (searched `MumfordTate`), `H¹_ét(A_Ē, ℚ_ℓ)` or `V_ℓA` as a Galois representation, the Betti–étale comparison, `Conj_G` itself.
