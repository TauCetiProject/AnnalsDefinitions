# 89. ℓ-adic Galois representation with values in the Mumford–Tate group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) §7.0i.1 "Mumford–Tate-valued ℓ-adic Galois representations and Frobenius classes" (README l.1923) states Def. 89.2–89.4: `𝐆 = MT(V_B)` for `V_B = H¹(A(ℂ)^an, ℚ)`, the fixer of finitely many untwisted rational Hodge tensors, the field `E^𝐆`, `ρ_ℓ^𝐆 : Γ_E → 𝐆(ℚ_ℓ)`, `Conj_𝐆 = 𝐆//𝐆` with `χ_𝐆`, and `γ_ℓ(v) = χ_𝐆(ρ_ℓ^𝐆(Frob_v))` at good-reduction places with geometric Frobenius, independent of choices, with Checks matching Remarks 2–3. §7.0i (l.1908) is Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem (89.1), and §P.3a (l.83) the Mumford–Tate group (Def. #13). The Mumford–Tate group as a Hopf ideal of `O(GL(V))` with connectedness and reductivity is also merged ShimuraData §§1.2–1.4; its construction and minimality (§1.2) are in code as `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal`, with the rational coordinate kernel and the closed subgroup scheme `mumfordTateClosedSubgroup`, and the Mumford–Tate group of a rational Hodge structure through `h_V` (ShimuraVarieties §P.3) as `HodgeStructureOn.mumfordTateHopfIdeal`. The conjugacy quotient `Conj_H = H//H` with `χ_H` (89.3) is merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §1.11, consumed by merged ReductiveGroupsPartIII Layer 0, whose Layer 5 builds the Kisin–Zhou theorem on top of §7.0i.1; the Tate module, Frobenius semisimplicity and complex uniformization of abelian varieties are merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli; the Artin comparison is merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C6.6. Nothing else on the chain beyond Hodge structures and their Mumford–Tate groups, Hopf-algebra group schemes, the conjugation quotient `TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.conjugationQuotient`, invariant-theory inputs, the reductivity criterion for a connected group with a faithful completely reducible representation, the dual form of a Riemann form, and unramified local Galois representations with their Frobenius polynomials is implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups, continuous representations into `GL_n(ℚ_ℓ)` (as continuous homomorphisms), `ConjClasses`, invariants of group actions on rings.
- Missing: étale cohomology, Betti cohomology of complex varieties with Hodge structure, Mumford–Tate groups, categorical/GIT quotients `Spec O(G)^G`.
