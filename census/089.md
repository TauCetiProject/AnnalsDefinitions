# 89. ℓ-adic Galois representation with values in the Mumford–Tate group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) §7.0i.1 "Mumford–Tate-valued ℓ-adic Galois representations and Frobenius classes" (README l.1923) states Def. 89.2–89.4: `𝐆 = MT(V_B)` for `V_B = H¹(A(ℂ)^an, ℚ)`, the fixer of finitely many untwisted rational Hodge tensors, the field `E^𝐆`, `ρ_ℓ^𝐆 : Γ_E → 𝐆(ℚ_ℓ)`, `Conj_𝐆 = 𝐆//𝐆` with `χ_𝐆`, and `γ_ℓ(v) = χ_𝐆(ρ_ℓ^𝐆(Frob_v))` at good-reduction places with geometric Frobenius, independent of choices, with Checks matching Remarks 2–3. §7.0i (l.1908) is Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem (89.1), and §P.3a (l.83) the Mumford–Tate group (Def. #13). The Mumford–Tate group as a Hopf ideal of `O(GL(V))` with connectedness and reductivity is also merged ShimuraData §§1.2–1.4; its construction and minimality (§1.2) are in code as `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal`. The conjugacy quotient `Conj_H = H//H` with `χ_H` (89.3) is merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §1.11, consumed by merged ReductiveGroupsPartIII Layer 0, whose Layer 5 builds the Kisin–Zhou theorem on top of §7.0i.1; the Tate module, Frobenius semisimplicity and complex uniformization of abelian varieties are merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli; the Artin comparison is merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C6.6. Nothing else on the chain beyond Hodge structures, Hopf-algebra group schemes and invariant-theory inputs is implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups, continuous representations into `GL_n(ℚ_ℓ)` (as continuous homomorphisms), `ConjClasses`, invariants of group actions on rings.
- Missing: étale cohomology, Betti cohomology of complex varieties with Hodge structure, Mumford–Tate groups, categorical/GIT quotients `Spec O(G)^G`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hodge structures (`Geometry/Hodge/Structure.lean`, from Completed/HodgeStructures), abelian varieties (`AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/`), affine group schemes as Hopf algebras and Hopf ideals (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/`), `GeneralLinear.coordinateHopfAlgebra`.
- Mumford–Tate group (ShimuraData §1.2, ShimuraVarieties §P.3a, part): `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal h` for `h : S → G_ℝ` with `G` an affine group over `ℚ` (for `G = GL(V)` and rational weight, the Mumford–Tate group of a rational Hodge structure), with minimality `le_mumfordTateHopfIdeal_iff`, the factorisation `mumfordTateLift` and `mumfordTateLift_unique`, transport along isomorphisms `comapOfSurjective_mumfordTateHopfIdeal`, triviality criterion and `exists_finset_sup_eq_mumfordTateHopfIdeal` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate.lean`). Not connectedness, reductivity or the Hodge-tensor description.
- Conjugation coaction `TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.conjugationAlgHom` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Hopf/Conjugation/Basic.lean`) and finite generation of coinvariants for linearly reductive Hopf algebras (`TauCeti.ComoduleAlgebra.finiteType_coinvariants`, `Algebra/Bialgebra/ComoduleAlgebra/LinearlyReductive.lean`): the inputs of `Conj_G`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Galois groups, continuous homomorphisms: Mathlib.
2. Rational Hodge structures, abelian varieties, Hopf-algebra group schemes: Tau Ceti.
3. Mumford–Tate group (Def. #13): target of merged ShimuraVarieties §P.3a, on §P.3, and of merged ShimuraData §§1.2–1.4; the Hopf ideal with minimality is in code (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal`); `h_V` and `MT(V)` of a rational Hodge structure are in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3714, connectedness in open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45254; for reductivity, the faithful completely reducible criterion is in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3764 and its application to `MT(V)` is not started.
4. `H¹_B(A(ℂ),ℚ)` with Hodge structure, `H¹_ét(A_Ē,ℚ_ℓ)` (or `V_ℓA^∨`), comparison isomorphism: targets in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli (Layer A5, A4.1, A6.11) and ComplexComparisonPartII §§C5.1–C5.3, C6.6 (not implemented).
5. Deligne: Hodge cycles on abelian varieties are absolutely Hodge (89.1): target of merged ShimuraVarieties §7.0i (not started).
6. Conjugation quotient `Conj_G`, `χ_G`: target in merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §1.11 (not implemented; conjugation coaction and invariant finite generation in Tau Ceti), with group Chevalley restriction and character separation in merged ReductiveGroupsPartIII Layer 0 (not started).
7. Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, Frobenius at good-reduction places: merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §5.1; merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §§2.1–2.2 and AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli A6.21.
8. `E^𝐆`, `ρ_ℓ^𝐆`, `γ_ℓ(v)`: target of merged ShimuraVarieties §7.0i.1 (not started).

## Library-scale gap
None: every step is a merged target. The chain is very long: Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem needs Betti/de Rham/étale comparisons for abelian varieties and the CM/Weil-class reduction (ShimuraVarieties §7.0i on Layer 5's CM theory).
