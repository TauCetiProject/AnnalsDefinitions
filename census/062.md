## Library-scale gap
None. RealSemisimpleLieGroups Layer 5 is the "Riemannian symmetric spaces" theory: symmetric spaces, `G/K` with its Killing metric, curvature, geodesics and distance, Cartan's fixed-point theorem, flats and rank, quotients by discrete subgroups (Helgason, *Differential Geometry, Lie Groups, and Symmetric Spaces*, Ch. IV-VI; Eberlein, *Geometry of Nonpositively Curved Manifolds*). It consumes LieGroups L7/L9, SmoothBundlesConnections B1.4 and RiemannianGeometry R1.3/R4, which are merged but not implemented for this use. Its §5.1 needs only Tau Ceti's Riemannian isometries, HopfRinow and RiemannianGeometry R1.2. Duality with compact type and the boundary at infinity are outside it and not needed by the definition.
