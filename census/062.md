## Tau Ceti code
- `cartanInvolution` for `sp l R` and `GLSymplectic` only (`Algebra/Lie/Symplectic/Cartan.lean`, `LinearAlgebra/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Symplectic/Cartan.lean`).
- Locally symmetric predicate `CovariantDerivative.HasParallelCurvature` and `Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Isometry/LocallySymmetric.lean`; Cartan-Hadamard ingredients (norm-nondecreasing exponential under K ≤ 0) per RiemannianGeometry supplier list.
- SmoothBundlesConnections B1.4's free proper quotient theorem: for a finite-dimensional real Lie group acting smoothly, freely and properly on a boundaryless manifold, `M ⧸ G` is a manifold of dimension `dim M − dim G` (`ProperSMul.orbitRelQuotientChartedSpace`, `instIsManifoldOrbitRelQuotient`) and the orbit map is a smooth submersion (`isSubmersion_quotientMk`, `ker_mfderiv_quotientMk`) (`Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/Quotient.lean`); with Cartan's closed-subgroup theorem `isEmbeddedLieSubgroup_of_isClosed` (`Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Embedded.lean`) it applies to the right action of a closed subgroup `K` on `G`, but `G/K` is not assembled as a smooth manifold. Inputs: compact stabilizers and closed orbits of proper actions (`ProperSMul.isCompact_stabilizer`, `Topology/Algebra/ProperAction/Orbit.lean`), orbit maps of free points as smooth embeddings (`isSmoothEmbedding_smul_const`, `Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/Orbit.lean`), transverse slice maps as local diffeomorphisms (`isLocalDiffeomorphAt_smul_slice_iff`, `Geometry/Manifold/GroupAction/TransverseSlice.lean`), and Haar-averaged invariant metrics (`exists_contMDiffRiemannianMetric_inner_mfderiv`, `Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Invariant.lean`).
- Riemannian submersions: O'Neill tensors and the integrability criterion (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Submersion/`), and O'Neill's horizontal curvature formula `inner_curvatureTensor_of_mem_orthogonal_ker` with `sectionalCurvature_mfderiv` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Submersion/Curvature.lean`), the formula behind the curvature of a normal homogeneous metric.
- Covering criteria of RiemannianGeometry R4.3: a local Riemannian isometry from a complete manifold is a covering map (`TauCeti.IsLocalRiemannianIsometry.isCoveringMap_of_completeSpace`), and so is a local diffeomorphism from a complete manifold whose differential does not decrease norms (`TauCeti.Manifold.isCoveringMap_of_norm_le_norm_mfderiv`) (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Covering/Criterion.lean`), the covering step of Cartan-Hadamard.
- For the example `SL₂(ℝ)/SO(2)`: `GL(2, ℝ)` acts on the upper half-plane by isometries of the Poincaré metric, `TauCeti.UpperHalfPlane.glIsom : GL (Fin 2) ℝ →* Isom 𝓘(ℝ, ℂ) ℍ` with its descents `pglIsom`, `pslIsom` (`Analysis/Complex/UpperHalfPlane/RiemannianIsometry.lean`, FuchsianOrbifolds L2.1(a)).
- No G/K Riemannian manifold, no Killing-form metric.

## Library-scale gap
None. RealSemisimpleLieGroups Layer 5 is the "Riemannian symmetric spaces" theory: symmetric spaces, `G/K` with its Killing metric, curvature, geodesics and distance, Cartan's fixed-point theorem, flats and rank, quotients by discrete subgroups (Helgason, *Differential Geometry, Lie Groups, and Symmetric Spaces*, Ch. IV-VI; Eberlein, *Geometry of Nonpositively Curved Manifolds*). It consumes LieGroups L7/L9, SmoothBundlesConnections B1.4 and RiemannianGeometry R1.3/R4, which are merged but not implemented for this use. Its §5.1 needs only Tau Ceti's Riemannian isometries, HopfRinow and RiemannianGeometry R1.2. Duality with compact type and the boundary at infinity are outside it and not needed by the definition.
