## Library-scale gap
PR #2229 Layer 5 is the "Riemannian symmetric spaces" theory: symmetric spaces, `G/K` with its Killing metric, curvature, geodesics and distance, Cartan's fixed-point theorem, flats and rank, quotients by discrete subgroups (Helgason, *Differential Geometry, Lie Groups, and Symmetric Spaces*, Ch. IV-VI; Eberlein, *Geometry of Nonpositively Curved Manifolds*). It consumes LieGroups L7/L9, SmoothBundlesConnections B1.4 and RiemannianGeometry R1.3/R4, which are merged but not implemented for this use. Duality with compact type and the boundary at infinity are outside PR #2229 and not needed by the definition.
