# 29. Residual gerbe at a point of an algebraic stack

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes specifies the definition and its theory: monomorphisms and closed substacks of algebraic stacks (Layer 0), gerbes over algebraic stacks (Layer 2), `Stacks.residualGerbe` `𝒵_x` with its characterisation and uniqueness (§3.3, 29.1–29.2), existence for finite type points, quasi-compact inertia and locally Noetherian stacks (§§4.1–4.4, 29.3(a)), the closed-immersion criterion at closed points (§4.5), `𝒵_x ≃ BG_x` at a rational point (§§6.3–6.4, 29.4), and the normal space `Stacks.normalSpace` `N_x` as a `G_x`-representation (§7.2, 29.5). Nothing of it is implemented. Its prerequisites are merged targets: algebraic stacks, `|X|`, `BG` and `QCoh` on stacks in SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.18–1.22, and site-level gerbes in AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §4.1. In code are the site-level ones: §1.18's stacks in groupoids with `ofSheaf`/`ofScheme`, 2-fibre products and the stackification of a prestack as a stack, and §4.1's `Pseudofunctor.IsGerbe` and relative gerbes `StrongTrans.IsRelativeGerbe` with restriction to slice sites, abelian bandings and neutralizations; algebraic stacks, `|X|`, `BG` and gerbes over an algebraic stack are not.

Depends on #28.
