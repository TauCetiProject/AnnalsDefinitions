# 29. Residual gerbe at a point of an algebraic stack

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Residual gerbes are not a target anywhere; their prerequisites (algebraic stacks, `|X|`, stabilizers, `BG`, quasi-coherent sheaves on stacks) are merged targets in SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.14–1.22 (unimplemented). Open PR #1831 records a gap request for zero-dimensional DM residual gerbes against §1.20, which is not a target.

Depends on #28.

## Merged roadmaps
- No merged README mentions residual gerbes.
- Prerequisites in SchemeAndStackFoundations: §1.18 stacks, 2-fibre products (Checks: `pt ×_{BG} pt = G`); §1.19 algebraic/DM stacks; §1.20 `Stacks.points`, `inertia`, `automorphismGroup` (the stabilizer `G_x` of a point; §1.14's `stabilizerAt` is for groupoids in algebraic spaces), locally Noetherian stacks, `IsSeparatedStack`; §1.21 `actionQuotient G X`, `BG` (`T ↦` `G_T`-torsors), `quotientStack_algebraic`; §1.22 `Stacks.QCoh` with Check "`QCoh(BG)` is comodules of `O(G)`" (needed for `N_x` in 29.5). Section 1.x mentions "banded gerbes" in a later section (Galois gerbs §1.25 are a different notion). Monomorphisms of stacks and closed substacks/ideal sheaves on stacks are not named.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Points `|X|`, stabilizers `G_x = automorphismGroup x` (#28): SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 (merged, not implemented).
2. Monomorphisms, reduced/locally Noetherian stacks, strictly full substacks: §§1.18–1.20 partially (monomorphism not named; unplanned but small).
3. `BG = [Spec k/G]`: §1.21 (merged, not implemented).
4. Gerbes over algebraic spaces: unplanned (§1.25 Galois gerbs are a different notion).
5. Residual gerbe `Z_x` (29.1–29.2), existence for quasi-separated / locally Noetherian stacks (29.3(a)): unplanned.
6. `Z_x ≃ BG_x` at a `k`-point (29.4): unplanned (writer's own derivation; route of Remark 3 needs only 1–3).
7. Infinitesimal neighbourhoods and normal space `N_x = (I/I²)^∨` as a `G_x`-representation (29.5): needs ideal sheaves on stacks and §1.22 `QCoh(BG)`; unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A subsection after SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20/§1.21: monomorphisms and closed/open substacks of algebraic stacks, gerbes, residual gerbes with existence theorems (quasi-DM, quasi-compact inertia, locally Noetherian), the identification `Z_x ≃ BG_x` over an algebraically closed field, closed immersion iff closed point, and the normal/tangent space at a point as a stabilizer representation (the input to the Luna slice theorem). Reference: Stacks Project, Properties of Algebraic Stacks §11 (06MT) and Morphisms of Stacks §§28–31 (06QK); Alper, *Stacks and Moduli* notes, §3.5–3.9.
