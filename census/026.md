# 26. Normalized Nash blowup

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged NashBlowups Layer 6 specifies `normalizedNashBlowup` `X̄* = (X*)^ν` with its basic properties (§6.1), universal property (§6.2), locality (§6.3) and isomorphism criteria (§6.4), and Layer 7 the normalised iteration `normalizedNashIterate` with `NormalizedNashIterationResolves` (§7.1) and the never-resolves principle `not_normalizedNashIterationResolves_of_openSubscheme` (§7.3). Nothing of it is implemented. It is #25 followed by normalization, so it rests on the same merged input, AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (`coherentGrassmannian`, not implemented).

Depends on #25.

## Tau Ceti code
- Normalization API extensions: `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/` (e.g. `isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`). Finiteness: `isFinite_fromNormalization_of_isJapanese` (`Normalization/Finite.lean`) gives a finite normalization of an integral scheme in any finite extension of its function field when the sections over nonempty affine opens are Japanese; finite-type algebras over a field are Japanese and Nagata (`TauCeti.Ring.IsUniversallyJapanese.of_field`, `.isJapanese`, `.isNagata_of_finiteType`, `RingTheory/UniversallyJapanese.lean`). Combining these for integral schemes locally of finite type over `k` is a short step not stated in code. Quasi-finite finite-type extension domains of Japanese domains are Japanese (`TauCeti.Ring.IsJapanese.of_quasiFinite`, `RingTheory/Japanese/QuasiFinite.lean`).
- Normality under separable extension of the ground field, ring level (NashBlowups §0.6, partial): normal rings are stable under étale base change and finite separable field extension, `TauCeti.Algebra.Etale.forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed`, `TauCeti.forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed_tensorProduct_of_etale`, `TauCeti.forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed_tensorProduct_of_isSeparable` (`RingTheory/Etale/Normal.lean`), under localization (`IsLocalization.forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed`, `RingTheory/Localization/Normal.lean`) and polynomial rings (`TauCeti.MvPolynomial.forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed`). The infinite separable case and the scheme form `isNormalScheme_pullback_of_isTranscendentalSeparable`, which §6.3's base change uses, are missing.
- Everything listed under #25 (including the comparison map and base change of differentials and `HasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials`, and the NashBlowups §0.2 open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3721 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3841, the closed-immersion clause and the ring forms of the base-change Checks). No Nash or normalized Nash blowup.
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45340 (NashBlowups §0.6: directed colimits of normal rings are normal, *isNormal_directLimit*, and `Algebra.IsGeometricallyNormal`; the transcendental separable base change remains) and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3795 (§0.6, finitely generated case: `TauCeti.forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed_tensorProduct_of_isSeparablyGenerated`, `K ⊗[k] B` normal for a finitely generated separably generated `K/k` and normal `B`, with the localization step `forall_isDomain_and_isIntegrallyClosed_tensorProduct_of_isLocalization`, `RingTheory/Normal/SeparablyGenerated.lean`; the arbitrary `Algebra.IsTranscendentalSeparable` case, through *isNormal_directLimit*, and the scheme form remain).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Nash blowup `(X^*, ν)` (#25): NashBlowups §2.1 (merged, not implemented), on merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (not implemented).
2. Normalization of a reduced scheme: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.normalization` applied to `∐ Spec κ(ξ_i) → X^*` (in Mathlib); merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §5.4 states the same for schemes.
3. Finiteness of normalization for finite-type `k`-schemes: SchemeAndStackFoundations §0.20 (merged, partially implemented); the needed case follows from Tau Ceti's `isFinite_fromNormalization_of_isJapanese` and `IsUniversallyJapanese.of_field`.
4. Smoothness of the normal locus in codim 1 etc. (26.3(a)): mostly Mathlib/Tau Ceti.
5. Normalized Nash blowup, iterates `Y_i` and "resolves singularities": NashBlowups §§6.1–6.4, 7.1–7.3 (merged, not implemented).

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged NashBlowups specifies it with #25. For the paper one also needs normal affine toric varieties as schemes over `k` (`Spec k[σ^∨ ∩ M]`) and their Nash blowups via logarithmic Jacobian ideals, which NashBlowups leaves to a toric roadmap. Toric varieties exist only analytically (Completed/AnalyticToricGeometry); an algebraic toric-varieties roadmap would sit beside SchemeAndStackFoundations. References: Cox–Little–Schenck, *Toric varieties*; González Pérez–Teissier.
