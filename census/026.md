# 26. Normalized Nash blowup

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged NashBlowups Layer 6 specifies `normalizedNashBlowup` `X̄* = (X*)^ν` with its basic properties (§6.1), universal property (§6.2), locality (§6.3) and isomorphism criteria (§6.4), and Layer 7 the normalised iteration `normalizedNashIterate` with `NormalizedNashIterationResolves` (§7.1) and the never-resolves principle `not_normalizedNashIterationResolves_of_openSubscheme` (§7.3). Nothing of it is implemented. It is #25 followed by normalization, so it rests on the same merged input, AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (`coherentGrassmannian`, implemented only as the glued scheme `Scheme.Modules.grassmannian` without its universal property).

Depends on #25.

- Normalization API extensions: `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Normalization/` (e.g. `isRegular_normalization_fromSpecStalk_genericPoint`). Finiteness: `isFinite_fromNormalization_of_isJapanese` (`Normalization/Finite.lean`) gives a finite normalization of an integral scheme in any finite extension of its function field when the sections over nonempty affine opens are Japanese; finite-type algebras over a field are Japanese and Nagata (`TauCeti.Ring.IsUniversallyJapanese.of_field`, `.isJapanese`, `.isNagata_of_finiteType`, `RingTheory/UniversallyJapanese.lean`). ## Prerequisite chain
1. Nash blowup `(X^*, ν)` (#25): NashBlowups §2.1 (merged, not implemented), on merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (not implemented).
2. Normalization of a reduced scheme: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.normalization` applied to `∐ Spec κ(ξ_i) → X^*` (in Mathlib); merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §5.4 states the same for schemes.
3. Finiteness of normalization for finite-type `k`-schemes: SchemeAndStackFoundations §0.20 (merged, partially implemented); the needed case follows from Tau Ceti's `isFinite_fromNormalization_of_isJapanese` and `IsUniversallyJapanese.of_field`.
4. Smoothness of the normal locus in codim 1 etc. (26.3(a)): mostly Mathlib/Tau Ceti.
5. Normalized Nash blowup, iterates `Y_i` and "resolves singularities": NashBlowups §§6.1–6.4, 7.1–7.3 (merged, not implemented).

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged NashBlowups specifies it with #25. For the paper one also needs normal affine toric varieties as schemes over `k` (`Spec k[σ^∨ ∩ M]`) and their Nash blowups via logarithmic Jacobian ideals, which NashBlowups leaves to a toric roadmap. Toric varieties exist only analytically (Completed/AnalyticToricGeometry); an algebraic toric-varieties roadmap would sit beside SchemeAndStackFoundations. References: Cox–Little–Schenck, *Toric varieties*; González Pérez–Teissier.
