# 8. Glauberman correspondence

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII/README.md) (PR #2411) specifies the whole definition: the action of `S` on `Irr(Γ)` and `Irr_S(Γ)` (§1.1), fixed classes under a coprime action and Brauer's permutation lemma (§§1.2–1.3), Gallagher's normalised extension and the canonical extension to `Γ ⋊ S` (§§2.1–2.2), the cyclic case (Layer 3), the `p`-group case with its unique constituent of multiplicity prime to `p` (§§4.2, 4.5, Theorem 8.1), and, for solvable `S`, existence, independence of the series and uniqueness of the compatible family of bijections `π(Γ, S) : Irr_S(Γ) → Irr(C_Γ(S))` defining `θ ↦ θ*` (§§4.3–4.5, Theorem 8.2 and Definition 8.3), with equivariance (§4.6) and Glauberman degree-divisibility as a proposition (§5.2). Of it, Brauer's permutation lemma (§1.3) is in code; the correspondence is not. Its other inputs in code: complex character theory with restriction, inner products and Clifford theory (Tau Ceti), Schur–Zassenhaus (Mathlib).

## Mathlib
- Has: `MulDistribMulAction` (actions by automorphisms), `FixedPoints.subgroup`, `Subgroup.exists_right_complement'_of_coprime` (Schur–Zassenhaus, `GroupTheory/SchurZassenhaus.lean`), `IsSolvable`, `SemidirectProduct`, `FDRep.character` with orthonormality.
- Missing: action of `S` on `Irr(Γ)`, coprime-action lemmas beyond Schur–Zassenhaus, the correspondence.

## Tau Ceti code
- Characters: `TauCeti.ClassFunction`, `irreducibleCharacters` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/{ClassFunction,Table}.lean`), `TauCeti.sum_characterTable_mul_conj`, induction/restriction.
- Automorphisms acting on characters (ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §1.3, complete): the action of `MulAut G` on `TauCeti.irreducibleCharacters k G`, `(σ • χ)(g) = χ(σ⁻¹ g)`, and Brauer's permutation lemma `TauCeti.natCard_fixedBy_irreducibleCharacters_eq_natCard_fixedBy_conjClasses`, with `natCard_orbits_irreducibleCharacters_eq_natCard_orbits_conjClasses` and, for cyclic acting groups, `natCard_invariant_irreducibleCharacters_eq_natCard_invariant_conjClasses_of_isCyclic` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Automorphism.lean`).
- Clifford theory: `TauCeti.inertia`, `FDRep.clifford_restrict_iso`, `FDRep.cliffordCorrespondence`, obstruction and projective extension (`RepresentationTheory/Induction/Clifford/*`, `Induction/Inertia.lean`). These give extension of `S`-invariant characters to `Γ ⋊ S` when gcd conditions kill the obstruction (needed for Remark 2).
- No `Irr_S(Γ)` API, fixed-class lemma under a coprime action, canonical extension or correspondence (grep `invariantIrreducibleCharacters|canonicalExtension|GlaubermanFamily` empty). Open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3633 (§1.1: invariant irreducible characters under an action by automorphisms) and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3275 (§2.1: Gallagher's normalised extension to a semidirect product).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Coprime actions, fixed-point subgroups, Schur–Zassenhaus: Mathlib; conjugacy of coprime complements: InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups IG.3.4 (merged, not implemented), as consumed by ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §1.2; coprime-action lemmas (`G = [G, A] · C_G(A)` and Bender–Glauberman §1): OddOrderTheorem §1.3 (merged, not implemented); invariant Sylow subgroups and Glauberman's lemma also in PR #1634 L3.
2. Complex irreducible characters, restriction, inner products, Clifford theory: Tau Ceti.
3. `S`-action on `Irr(Γ)`, `Irr_S(Γ)`, fixed classes: ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §§1.1–1.2 (merged; §1.1 in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3633); Brauer's permutation lemma: Tau Ceti (§1.3).
4. Canonical extension to `Γ ⋊ S` (Remark 2): ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §§2.1–2.2 (merged; §2.1 in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3275).
5. Theorem 8.1 (`p`-group case): ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §§4.2, 4.5 (`existsUnique_restrict_pairing_not_dvd`).
6. Theorem 8.2 (solvable `S`, independence of the chain, equivariance) and Definition 8.3: ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §§4.3–4.6.
7. Degree divisibility statements: ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII Layer 5.
8. Isaacs correspondence for `|Γ|` odd (Remark 3): unplanned; not needed by the paper.

## Library-scale gap
None: merged ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII (Isaacs, *Character Theory of Finite Groups*, Ch. 13; Navarro, *Character Theory and the McKay Conjecture*, Ch. 2 and 5; Glauberman 1968) is the roadmap, and what remains for the definition is implementation. The paper itself (Geck) also needs Deligne–Lusztig Green functions of finite reductive groups: merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) §4.2 defines them for every connected reductive group over a finite field, with the character formula (§4.3) and their values at regular unipotent elements (§4.10); Green polynomials and character sheaves are outside that roadmap (see #2).
