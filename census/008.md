# 8. Glauberman correspondence

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2411 (ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII) specifies the whole definition: the action of `S` on `Irr(Γ)` and `Irr_S(Γ)` (§1.1), fixed classes under a coprime action and Brauer's permutation lemma (§§1.2–1.3), Gallagher's normalised extension and the canonical extension to `Γ ⋊ S` (§§2.1–2.2), the cyclic case (Layer 3), the `p`-group case with its unique constituent of multiplicity prime to `p` (§4.2, Theorem 8.1), and, for solvable `S`, existence, independence of the series and uniqueness of the compatible family of bijections `π(Γ, S) : Irr_S(Γ) → Irr(C_Γ(S))` defining `θ ↦ θ*` (§§4.3–4.5, Theorem 8.2 and Definition 8.3), with equivariance (§4.6) and Glauberman degree-divisibility as a proposition (§5.2). No merged roadmap specifies it. Its inputs in code: complex character theory with restriction, inner products and Clifford theory (Tau Ceti), Schur–Zassenhaus (Mathlib).

## Mathlib
- Has: `MulDistribMulAction` (actions by automorphisms), `FixedPoints.subgroup`, `Subgroup.exists_right_complement'_of_coprime` (Schur–Zassenhaus, `GroupTheory/SchurZassenhaus.lean`), `IsSolvable`, `SemidirectProduct`, `FDRep.character` with orthonormality.
- Missing: action of `S` on `Irr(Γ)`, coprime-action lemmas beyond Schur–Zassenhaus, the correspondence.

## Tau Ceti code
- Characters: `TauCeti.ClassFunction`, `irreducibleCharacters` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/{ClassFunction,Table}.lean`), `TauCeti.sum_characterTable_mul_conj`, induction/restriction.
- Clifford theory: `TauCeti.inertia`, `FDRep.clifford_restrict_iso`, `FDRep.cliffordCorrespondence`, obstruction and projective extension (`RepresentationTheory/Induction/Clifford/*`, `Induction/Inertia.lean`). These give extension of `S`-invariant characters to `Γ ⋊ S` when gcd conditions kill the obstruction (needed for Remark 2).
- No coprime-action module, no `Irr_S(Γ)`, no correspondence.

Not implemented. This is the conjugacy input PR #2411 consumes.
