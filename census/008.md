# 8. Glauberman correspondence

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. The Glauberman correspondence is not a target anywhere. Its inputs are largely in place: complex character theory with restriction, inner products and Clifford theory are in Tau Ceti code, Schur–Zassenhaus is in Mathlib, and the coprime-action group theory (invariant Sylow subgroups, Glauberman's lemma, fixed points modulo normal subgroups) is a target of open PR #1634 L3. The key missing input is Glauberman's `p`-group theorem (Theorem 8.1).

## Mathlib
- Has: `MulDistribMulAction` (actions by automorphisms), `FixedPoints.subgroup`, `Subgroup.exists_right_complement'_of_coprime` (Schur–Zassenhaus, `GroupTheory/SchurZassenhaus.lean`), `IsSolvable`, `SemidirectProduct`, `FDRep.character` with orthonormality.
- Missing: action of `S` on `Irr(Γ)`, coprime-action lemmas beyond Schur–Zassenhaus, the correspondence.

## Tau Ceti code
- Characters: `TauCeti.ClassFunction`, `irreducibleCharacters` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/{ClassFunction,Table}.lean`), `TauCeti.sum_characterTable_mul_conj`, induction/restriction.
- Clifford theory: `TauCeti.inertia`, `FDRep.clifford_restrict_iso`, `FDRep.cliffordCorrespondence`, obstruction and projective extension (`RepresentationTheory/Induction/Clifford/*`, `Induction/Inertia.lean`). These give extension of `S`-invariant characters to `Γ ⋊ S` when gcd conditions kill the obstruction (needed for Remark 2).
- No coprime-action module, no `Irr_S(Γ)`, no correspondence.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Coprime actions, fixed-point subgroups, Schur–Zassenhaus: Mathlib.
2. Invariant Sylow subgroups, Glauberman's lemma, `C_{Γ/N}(S)` = image of `C_Γ(S)`: PR #1634 L3.
3. Complex irreducible characters, restriction, inner products, Clifford theory: Tau Ceti.
4. `S`-action on `Irr(Γ)`, `Irr_S(Γ)`, and `S/T` acting on `C_Γ(T)`: unplanned (small).
5. Theorem 8.1 (`p`-group case: unique constituent of `χ_{C_Γ(S)}` with multiplicity prime to `p`, bijectivity): unplanned; key nontrivial step (Isaacs Ch. 13, via counting or via the canonical extension and Remark 2).
6. Theorem 8.2 (solvable `S`, independence of the chain, equivariance) and Definition 8.3: unplanned.
7. Isaacs correspondence for `|Γ|` odd (Remark 3): unplanned; not needed by the paper.

## Library-scale gap
A short roadmap "coprime action on characters" (Isaacs, *Character Theory of Finite Groups*, Ch. 13; Navarro, *Character Theory and the McKay Conjecture*, Ch. 2 and 5) would cover: action of automorphism groups on `Irr`, Brauer's permutation lemma (number of `S`-invariant characters = number of `S`-invariant classes), canonical extensions to `Γ ⋊ S` for coprime `S`, the `p`-group correspondence and its solvable composite, and degree divisibility statements. It would sit beside RepresentationTheory/InductionRestriction (Clifford theory) and consume PR #1634 L3. The paper itself (Geck) also needs Deligne–Lusztig Green functions of finite reductive groups, which are unplanned (see #2).
