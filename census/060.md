## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2529 (feat: add the normal Cauchy-Riemann operator to AnalyticStableMaps) specifies the whole definition for a smooth nonconstant J-holomorphic map from a closed connected Riemann surface, with no immersion, simplicity, integrability or symplectic hypothesis: §0.6 the tangential identity `D_u(du·Y) = du∘∂̄Y`, the order of a critical point, the generalized tangent bundle `T_u` as a smooth complex line subbundle and `c₁(T_u) = χ(Σ) + Z(du)` (60.1(b)–(c)); §0.7 the generalized normal bundle `N_u = u*TX/T_u`, complements of `T_u` and independence of the complement (60.2, 60.3), the normal operator `D_u^N` as a real CR operator, its Fredholm realizations and `ind D_u^N = D(u) − 2Z(du)` (60.4(a)); §0.8 normal operators of branched covers `N_{v∘φ} = φ*N_v`; §2.24 super-rigidity (60.4(b)). The finiteness of critical points (60.1(a)) is merged AnalyticStableMaps §0.4 and in Tau Ceti code (`TauCeti.finite_setOf_mfderiv_eq_zero_of_isPseudoholomorphic`). Merged roadmaps specify only the embedded normal operator (TaubesGromov §1.2, HeegaardFloer F2.6e). Tau Ceti has a local-coordinate normal linearization covariant under target and source coordinate changes, with its elliptic real CR symbol, and the complex normal bundle of an embedded almost complex submanifold, but no generalized normal bundle. Depends on #56.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2529 covers the definition, extending merged AnalyticStableMaps Layer 0 (Wendl, *Automatic transversality and orbifolds of punctured holomorphic curves in dimension four* (Comment. Math. Helv. 85 (2010)), §3.3; Wendl 2023, §§2–3; Ivashkovich–Shevchishin). What remains is its review and merging, then implementation on PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis PH6–PH7. Wendl's super-rigidity theorem itself (transversality for multiply covered curves for generic J) is named in PR #2529 as building on §§0.6–0.8 and §2.24, not as a target.
