## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. The normal CR operator of an embedded J-holomorphic curve is a merged target (TaubesGromov TG1, HeegaardFloer F2.6e for spheres), and Tau Ceti has a local-coordinate normal linearization; def #60 needs the generalised normal bundle of a possibly non-immersed curve (critical points allowed), which is not a target. Depends on #56.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.normalCauchyRiemannLinearization` (`Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Normal/Linearization.lean`): local-coordinate descent of the CR linearization to V ⧸ range(du x), shown independent of adding du·a; explicitly "global bundle gluing, Sobolev realizations and Fredholm theory are not asserted". Works pointwise even when u is not immersive, but no generalised normal bundle.

## Merged roadmaps
- TaubesGromov TG1 "Embedded normal operators": quotient normal bundle i*TX/TC of an embedding, normal real CR operator, Fredholm/adjoint realizations from PH6.2-PH6.3, normal index A_C² − κ·A_C. Embedded only. Not implemented.
- HeegaardFloer §4.6e (F2.6e): quotient real CR operator on the normal line of an embedded sphere, automatic surjectivity for degree ≥ −1. Embedded only.
- SymplecticFieldTheory 1.x: linearized operator at trivial cylinders on the normal bundle.
- Not specified: generalised normal bundle N_u ⊃ through critical points (60.1-60.2), independence of complement (60.3), normal operator for multiply covered/non-immersed curves (Wendl).

## Library-scale gap
A modest extension of TaubesGromov TG1 / HF F2.6e: construct T_u and N_u for nonconstant J-holomorphic curves with finitely many critical points and the normal operator with its index c₁(N_u) − Z(du) correction (Wendl, *Lectures on Holomorphic Curves*, §3.4; Ivashkovich-Shevchishin). Wendl's super-rigidity paper further needs the splitting of D_u^N on branched covers into representation-theoretic summands, which DGFloer's multiple-cover regularity item (Taubes-type) partially anticipates.
