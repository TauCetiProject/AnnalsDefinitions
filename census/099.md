## Status
UNPLANNED. Neither lattice φ⁴/Ising measures, rescaled smeared fields, nor "reachable" random fields are targets anywhere. All the measure-theoretic ingredients (finite products, densities, Gaussian/Lebesgue single-site laws, convergence in distribution, Kolmogorov extension) are in Mathlib, so the definition is writable directly; there is no roadmap for classical lattice statistical mechanics.

## Mathlib
- No lattice spin systems, no Schwinger functions, no "random field indexed by test functions".
- Have: `Measure.pi` and `Measure.infinitePi` (`Mathlib/Probability/ProductMeasure.lean`), `withDensity`, Dirac/Bernoulli measures, `MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure` with weak convergence, `TendstoInDistribution` (`MeasureTheory/Function/ConvergenceInDistribution.lean`), `IsProjectiveLimit` (`MeasureTheory/Constructions/Projective.lean`), Gaussian measures (`Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/*`), `CompactlySupportedContinuousMap`/`C_c`, `ZLattice`.
- Nearest missing piece: nothing deep for the definition itself; finite-volume Gibbs measures `e^{-H} ∏ρ / Z` and the double limit are routine.

## Tau Ceti code
- Absent (no Ising/φ⁴/Schwinger). Quantum analogue only: `TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/QuantumSpin/{GibbsState,Interaction}.lean` (finite-volume Gibbs states of quantum spin interactions).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Finite boxes `V_R ⊂ ℤ^d`, nearest-neighbour pairs: Mathlib (elementary).
2. Single-site measures `e^{-λφ⁴+bφ²}dφ` and `½(δ_{-1}+δ_{+1})`, finiteness of Z: Mathlib (`withDensity`, integrability of `exp(-λx⁴+bx²)`), unplanned as API.
3. Finite-volume measure (99.1), smeared field `T_{f,L}` (99.2): unplanned (routine).
4. Random field as linear family indexed by `C_0(ℝ^d)`, Schwinger functions (99.3): unplanned (PR #1772 has Schwinger distributions of free fields only).
5. Weak convergence of finite-dimensional laws, Kolmogorov extension (99.4, 99.5): Mathlib (`TendstoInDistribution`, `IsProjectiveLimit`).
6. Reachable random field (99.4): unplanned.
7. Gaussianity/triviality theory (Griffiths/Lebowitz inequalities, random current representation, Aizenman-Duminil-Copin's improved tree diagram bound): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "ClassicalLatticeSpinSystems" roadmap: finite-volume Gibbs measures for Ising and φ⁴ (and general ferromagnetic single-site measures of Griffiths-Simon class), correlation inequalities (GKS, FKG, Lebowitz, Simon-Lieb), infinite-volume limits and translation-invariant states, susceptibility and critical point, random current representation and switching lemma, reflection positivity, and scaling limits as random fields with Schwinger functions and Newman's Gaussianity criterion. Textbooks: Friedli-Velenik, *Statistical Mechanics of Lattice Systems*; Glimm-Jaffe, *Quantum Physics: A Functional Integral Point of View*; Fernández-Fröhlich-Sokal, *Random Walks, Critical Phenomena, and Triviality in Quantum Field Theory*; Duminil-Copin's lecture notes on random currents. It would sit beside OperatorAlgebraDynamics D9 (quantum analogue) and feed the Euclidean side of PR #1772.
