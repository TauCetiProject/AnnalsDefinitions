## Prerequisite chain
1. Finite boxes `V_R ⊂ ℤ^d`, nearest-neighbour pairs: Mathlib (elementary); PR #2432 Conventions.
2. Single-site measures `e^{-λφ⁴+bφ²}dφ` and `½(δ_{-1}+δ_{+1})`, finiteness of Z: PR #2432 §§0.1–0.2, §1.1.
3. Finite-volume measure (99.1), smeared field `T_{f,L}` (99.2): PR #2432 §1.1, §7.1.
4. Random field as linear family indexed by `C_c(ℝ^d)`, Schwinger functions (99.3): PR #2432 §§6.1–6.2, on StochasticCalculus §1.1 (merged).
5. Weak convergence of finite-dimensional laws, Kolmogorov extension (99.4, 99.5): Mathlib (`ProbabilityMeasure`, `IsProjectiveLimit`), Tau Ceti (countable extension), StochasticCalculus §1.1.
6. Reachable random field (99.4) and its existence (99.5): PR #2432 §§7.2–7.3.
7. Gaussianity/triviality theory (Griffiths/Lebowitz inequalities, random current representation in PR #2432 Layer 3; Aizenman–Duminil-Copin's improved tree diagram bound and the triviality theorems): unplanned (PR #2432's named Part II).

## Library-scale gap
PR #2432 is the roadmap for this definition and for the correlation-inequality and random-current toolkit (Friedli–Velenik, *Statistical Mechanics of Lattice Systems*; Glimm–Jaffe; Fernández–Fröhlich–Sokal; Aizenman–Duminil-Copin): what remains is its review and merging. The paper's marginal triviality theorems need its named sequel ConstructiveFieldTheoryPhi4PartII (random-current intersection probabilities, the improved tree diagram bound, sliding-scale infrared bounds). The DLR formalism of specifications, which #100 needs, is outside PR #2432 and is merged DimerModelsAndLozengeTilings Layer 4.
