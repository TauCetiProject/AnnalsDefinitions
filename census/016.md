Open PR #1996 Layer 2 specifies Vistoli's rational Chow groups of separated DM stacks, including the `1/|G|` degree of `BG`. Merged roadmaps cover only the prerequisites: DM stacks (SSF §1.19), coarse spaces (SSF §1.23), Chow groups of schemes (SSF §5.1–5.2). ## Prerequisite chain
1. Cycles, rational equivalence, Chow groups and degree on schemes. In Mathlib (`AlgebraicCycle`) and Tau Ceti (`Chow`).
2. Proper pushforward on schemes and algebraic spaces. Merged SSF §5.2, partial; algebraic spaces unplanned for Chow.
3. Algebraic spaces and DM stacks, `|M|`, residual gerbes. Target in merged SSF §§1.3–1.20 (implemented: §1.3, the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`, and §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` carrier).
4. Coarse moduli space (Keel–Mori). Target in merged SSF §1.23 (unimplemented).
5. `A_*(M)_ℚ` for DM stacks, and `deg` on proper `M`. Target in PR #1996 Layer 2.
6. Virtual class `[M]^vir`, needed by the paper. PR #1996 Layers 3–7; this is definition #27 (perfect obstruction theories and virtual classes).
