ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged [VirtualFundamentalClass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/VirtualFundamentalClass/README.md) §2.1 builds "Vistoli's dimension-graded rational Chow groups for separated Deligne–Mumford stacks, with cycles on integral closed substacks and the stabilizer and residue-degree factors required for pushforward" (Definitions 16.1–16.3), with the API item "Compute degrees of proper zero-cycles, with the generic stabilizer weight" and the check that `B(ℤ/2)` has degree `1/2`; §2.2 gives proper pushforward with stabilizer weights, and §7.7 the degree `A_0(X)_ℚ → ℚ` of a proper stack as pushforward to `Spec k` (Theorem 16.6). Nothing is implemented. The merged roadmap covers separated stacks only (the paper's stacks are proper, hence separated), and it builds cycles "on integral closed substacks" without defining them; merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3–0.5 supplies closed substacks and the reduced closed substack on a closed subset, not integral closed substacks. Open PR #2446 specifies all of Definition 16: §1.0 integral closed substacks (reduced closed substacks with irreducible support), the bijection `𝒱 ↦ |𝒱|` onto irreducible closed subsets of `|ℳ|` and images under proper morphisms (16.1); §§2.1–2.2 cycles and the Chow groups `A_i(ℳ)`, integral and rational, of DM stacks that need not be separated (16.1–16.3); §2.3 the comparisons with schemes, algebraic spaces and the merged separated groups (16.4(a)); §5.2 the moduli-space comparison (16.4(b)); §5.4 the zero-cycle degree `deg_ℳ[𝒢_x] = [κ(x):k]/|Aut(x̄)|` on `Z_0(ℳ)_ℚ` of every stack, proper or not, its compatibility with proper pushforward, and `∫_ℳ` on proper stacks (16.5, 16.6). Its stacks have diagonal representable by schemes, quasi-compact and separated (Vistoli's setting, its Convention 1). Filed in needs-roadmap-review under PR #2446: no merged roadmap specifies Definition 16.1's integral closed substacks, or Definitions 16.3 and 16.5 for stacks that need not be separated. The separated case and the degree on proper stacks, which is what the paper uses, are merged VirtualFundamentalClass §§2.1, 2.2, 7.7.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Cycles, rational equivalence, Chow groups and degree on schemes. In Mathlib (`AlgebraicCycle`) and Tau Ceti (`Chow`).
2. Integral closed substacks of a DM stack, `𝒱 ↔ |𝒱|`, images under proper morphisms (16.1): PR #2446 §1.0, in no merged roadmap; closed and reduced closed substacks are merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3–0.5 (not implemented).
3. Proper pushforward on schemes and algebraic spaces. Merged SSF §5.2, partial; Chow groups of algebraic spaces are unplanned in merged roadmaps (PR #2446 §2.3 compares with the Stacks Project's Chow groups of spaces).
4. Algebraic spaces and DM stacks, `|M|`, residual gerbes. Target in merged SSF §§1.3–1.20 (implemented: §1.3, the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`, §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel` and `ofAtlas`, and §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` carrier, 2-fibre products and stackification; and §1.19's `IsRepresentableBySpaces`); residual gerbes, `κ(x)` and `G_x` in merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§1.3, 3.3, 5.3 (not implemented; see #29).
5. Dimension of algebraic stacks: merged VirtualFundamentalClass §§0.2–0.3 (not implemented).
6. `A_*(M)_ℚ` for separated DM stacks, proper pushforward, and `deg` on proper `M`: merged VirtualFundamentalClass §§2.1–2.2, 7.7 (not implemented); stacks that need not be separated and the zero-cycle degree `deg[𝒢_x] = [κ(x):k]/|Aut(x̄)|` on every stack in PR #2446 §§2.2, 5.4.
7. Coarse moduli space (Keel–Mori), for 16.4(b): target in merged SSF §1.23 (unimplemented); the comparison itself in PR #2446 §5.2.
8. Virtual class `[M]^vir`, needed by the paper: merged VirtualFundamentalClass Layers 3–7; this is definition #27.
