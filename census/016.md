## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged [VirtualFundamentalClass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/VirtualFundamentalClass/README.md) §2.1 builds "Vistoli's dimension-graded rational Chow groups for separated Deligne–Mumford stacks, with cycles on integral closed substacks and the stabilizer and residue-degree factors required for pushforward" (Definitions 16.1–16.3), with the API item "Compute degrees of proper zero-cycles, with the generic stabilizer weight" and the check that `B(ℤ/2)` has degree `1/2`; §2.2 gives proper pushforward with stabilizer weights, and §7.7 the degree `A_0(X)_ℚ → ℚ` of a proper stack as pushforward to `Spec k` (Theorem 16.6). Nothing is implemented. Flags: the merged roadmap covers separated stacks only (the paper's stacks are proper, hence separated); Definition 16.3 for stacks that need not be separated, and the explicit formula `∫[𝒢_x] = [κ(x):k]/|Aut(x̄)|` of Definition 16.5, are only in open PR #2446 (§2.2, §5.4). The comparison 16.4(b) with a coarse space is not in the merged roadmap; PR #2446 §5.2 states it. Integral closed substacks (Definition 16.1's cycles: reduced closed substacks `𝒱` with `|𝒱|` irreducible, their bijection with the irreducible closed subsets of `|ℳ|`, and images under proper morphisms) are defined in no merged roadmap: VirtualFundamentalClass §2.1 builds cycles "on integral closed substacks" without defining them, and merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3–0.5 supplies closed substacks and the reduced closed substack on a closed subset but leaves integral closed substacks to VirtualFundamentalClass. PR #2446 records this as `gap:closed-substacks-of-stacks` and rests its cycles on it. Filed in needs-roadmap-review under PR #2446: no merged roadmap specifies Definition 16.1's integral closed substacks, or Definitions 16.3 and 16.5 for stacks that need not be separated, and PR #2446 specifies the latter (it computes `∫_ℳ` only on proper stacks, not the cycle-level `deg` on `Z_0(ℳ)_ℚ` of a non-proper stack). The separated case and the degree on proper stacks, which is what the paper uses, are merged VirtualFundamentalClass §§2.1, 2.2, 7.7.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Cycles, rational equivalence, Chow groups and degree on schemes. In Mathlib (`AlgebraicCycle`) and Tau Ceti (`Chow`).
2. Integral closed substacks of a DM stack, `𝒱 ↔ |𝒱|`, images under proper morphisms (16.1): in no merged roadmap (`gap:closed-substacks-of-stacks` of PR #2446); closed and reduced closed substacks are merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3–0.5 (not implemented).
3. Proper pushforward on schemes and algebraic spaces. Merged SSF §5.2, partial; Chow groups of algebraic spaces are unplanned in merged roadmaps (PR #2446 §2.3 compares with the Stacks Project's Chow groups of spaces).
4. Algebraic spaces and DM stacks, `|M|`, residual gerbes. Target in merged SSF §§1.3–1.20 (implemented: §1.3, the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`, §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel`, and §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` carrier); residual gerbes, `κ(x)` and `G_x` in merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§1.3, 3.3, 5.3 (not implemented; see #29).
5. Dimension of algebraic stacks: merged VirtualFundamentalClass §§0.2–0.3 (not implemented).
6. `A_*(M)_ℚ` for separated DM stacks, proper pushforward, and `deg` on proper `M`: merged VirtualFundamentalClass §§2.1–2.2, 7.7 (not implemented); stacks that need not be separated and the residual-gerbe degree formula in PR #2446 §§2.2, 5.4.
7. Coarse moduli space (Keel–Mori), for 16.4(b): target in merged SSF §1.23 (unimplemented); the comparison itself in PR #2446 §5.2.
8. Virtual class `[M]^vir`, needed by the paper: merged VirtualFundamentalClass Layers 3–7; this is definition #27.
