# 25. Nash blowup of a variety

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged NashBlowups specifies the definition: `nashBlowup` as `N(X, Ω_{X/k}, d, X_sm^{(d)})`, the scheme-theoretic image of the classifying map of the smooth locus in the Grassmannian of rank-`d` quotients of `Ω_{X/k}` (§§1.2, 2.1), with `nashBlowupHom`, the Nash cotangent bundle `nashCotangent`, properness and the modification property (§2.2), the universal property (§2.4), locality and field base change (§2.6), iterates `nashIterate` (§2.7), the Gauss-map description (§3.1) and the blowup description (§3.2). No Nash blowup is in code; of Layer 0, the comparison map and base change of differentials (§§0.1–0.2) and the predicate `HasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials` (§0.7) are. Its main input, the Grassmannian `Gr_d(E)` of a quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type, is a target of merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (`coherentGrassmannian`), not implemented (gluing over a non-affine base is open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/44043). The other ingredients are in code (Kähler sheaf, smooth locus, affine Grassmannian scheme, scheme-theoretic image).

Used by #26.

## Mathlib
- `Module.Grassmannian R M k` and `Module.Grassmannian.functor` (quotient convention, `Mathlib/RingTheory/Grassmannian.lean`); its TODO lists "Grassmannians for schemes and quasi-coherent sheaf of modules" and representability.
- Smooth locus: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Smooth.lean`), `Algebra.smoothLocus`.
- Scheme-theoretic image: `Scheme.Hom.image`, `Hom.toImage` (`AlgebraicGeometry/IdealSheaf/Subscheme.lean`).
- Kähler differentials of algebras `Ω[S⁄R]`; no sheaf of differentials on schemes.

## Tau Ceti code
- Sheaf of differentials: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials`, `universalDerivation` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Differentials/Basic.lean`), quasi-coherence, locally free on smooth schemes (`FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.relativeDifferentials`, `Differentials/LocallyFree.lean`). The comparison map `f^*Ω_{Y/R} → Ω_{X/R}` and base change (NashBlowups §§0.1–0.2 under other names): `relativeDifferentialsPullbackHom`, `cotangentMap`, `relativeDifferentialsBaseChangeIso` (`Differentials/Pullback.lean`). Locally free quotients of rank `d` (NashBlowups §0.7): `HasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials k X d` with `.restrict` and `hasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials_of_smoothOfRelativeDimension` (`Differentials/LocallyFreeQuotient.lean`).
- Modifications: `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsModification` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Modification/`), the iso locus and centre of a morphism `Scheme.Hom.isoLocus`, `Scheme.Hom.centre` (`Morphisms/IsoLocus.lean`) with `IsModification.dense_isoLocus`, `IsModification.isNowhereDense_centre` (`Modification/Centre.lean`), strict transform under a blow-up (`Blowup/StrictTransform.lean`).
- Grassmannian scheme of a module over an affine base: `Module.Grassmannian.scheme R M k`, `toSpec`, charts (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/Basic.lean`), points `schemeHomEquiv` (`Points.lean`), valuative criterion/properness for finite `M` (`Proper.lean`), universal quotient (`UniversalQuotient.lean`), base change `G(k, R' ⊗[R] M) ≅ Spec R' ×_{Spec R} G(k, M)` (`Module.Grassmannian.isPullback_baseChangeHom`, `BaseChange.lean`), which identifies the restriction of `G(k, M)` to a basic open with `G(k, M[1/f])`.
- So the affine case `X = Spec A` (Grass of `Ω_{A/k}` over `Spec A`, section over the smooth locus, scheme-theoretic image) is definable from existing code. No `nashBlowup`, `smoothLocusOfDim` or `coherentGrassmannian` declaration exists. Missing for the global definition: Grassmannian of a quasi-coherent sheaf on a non-affine `X` (gluing of the affine construction along the base-change isomorphisms); open Tau Ceti PRs https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/44043 (glue the Grassmannian of a quasi-coherent sheaf over an arbitrary base, ModularCurves §0.7) and https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/43574 (`smoothLocusOfDim`, NashBlowups §0.3).
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45265 (SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.13: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.regularLocus` and `Scheme.Hom.regularLocus_eq_smoothLocus` over a perfect field, the input of NashBlowups §0.5).

## Merged roadmaps
- [NashBlowups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/NashBlowups/README.md), the definition as a target:
  - Layer 0 interfaces on `Scheme.relativeDifferentials`: the comparison map (§0.1), base change and closed immersions (§0.2), the smooth locus of dimension `d` (§0.3), `coherentGrassmannian` along a surjection (§0.4), smoothness at closed points over a perfect field (§0.5), normality under separable field extension (§0.6), `Ω_{X/k}` admitting a locally free quotient of rank `d` (§0.7);
  - the Setting of its Conventions (`X` reduced, separated and of finite type over `k`, pure of dimension `d`, with dense smooth locus) and §1.1's setting lemma; §1.2 the Nash modification `N(X, F, r, U)` of a quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type (scheme-theoretic image of the classifying map of `F|_U` in `Gr_r(F)`), §1.3 its universal property, §1.4 the Plücker form, §1.5 locality and flat base change;
  - §2.1 `nashBlowup` `X* = N(X, Ω_{X/k}, d, X_sm^{(d)})` with `nashBlowupHom` and `nashCotangent` (25.2–25.3); §2.2 `nashBlowupHom_isProper`, `nashBlowupHom_isModification`; §2.3 the Setting is preserved; §2.4 `nashBlowup.lift`; §2.5 triviality criterion; §2.6 `nashBlowup.restrict`, `nashBlowup.baseChange` (25.5(c)); §2.7 `nashIterate`, `NashIterationResolves` (25.4);
  - §3.1 `nashBlowup_eq_closure_graph_gaussMap` (25.5(d)); §3.2 `nashBlowup_iso_blowup` (Remark 2); §3.3 hypersurfaces; Layers 4–5 Nobile's theorem and Duarte–Núñez-Betancourt.
  - Implemented: §§0.1–0.2 under other names and the definition and first bullets of §0.7 (above). Not implemented: the rest, including §0.3 and everything from Layer 1 on. Its "Downstream consumers" names a toric roadmap for the logarithmic-Jacobian computation the paper needs.
- Prerequisites:
  - [AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry/README.md) §1.3 "Coherent quotients, Plücker and partial flags": `coherentGrassmannian` for quasi-coherent finite-type `E` on arbitrary `S`, with universal quotient, unique classifying maps, base change, `Gr_1(E) ≅ P(E)`, agreement with `Module.Grassmannian` on affines and properness over `Spec R` for finite modules; `plucker`. Partial: the affine-base case and its base change are in code; gluing over a non-affine base (open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/44043), the universal property there and the Plücker immersion are missing.
  - SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 `hilbert_quot_schemes` (Quot with `X = S` is `Grass_d(E)`), not started; AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 is the direct route.
  - SSF §4.11 modifications and strict transforms (NashBlowups §2.2 uses `IsModification.Witness`). Partial: `IsModification` with `.birational`, `.comp`, `.isIso_of_isFinite`, the centre (`Scheme.Hom.centre`, `IsModification.isNowhereDense_centre`) and the strict transform under a blow-up are in code; `IsModification.functionField_equiv`, `.isAlteration` and the general strict-transform API are missing. SSF §4.13 `regularLocus_eq_smoothLocus` and §0.11 `field_extension_descent` are also supplier contracts.

## Open roadmap PRs
- No open PR is needed for the definition. The toric computation the paper needs (logarithmic Jacobian ideals, the dimension-four examples) is named in NashBlowups' "Downstream consumers" as a separate toric roadmap, which no PR specifies.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Ω_{X/k}` as coherent sheaf: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.relativeDifferentials`).
2. Smooth locus `X_sm`, density for perfect `k`: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus`; NashBlowups §§0.3, 0.5 (merged, not implemented).
3. Grassmannian of quotients of a module over an affine base: Tau Ceti (`Module.Grassmannian.scheme`).
4. `Gr_d(E)` for quasi-coherent finite-type `E` on a scheme, with base change and Plücker: merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (not implemented), on ModularCurves §0.7 (partial: affine base with base change only; gluing in open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/44043).
5. Section `σ: X_sm → Gr_d(Ω)` from the locally free rank-`d` quotient: follows from 4's universal property (NashBlowups §1.2, merged, not implemented).
6. Scheme-theoretic image and reduced structure: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.image`, Tau Ceti `Scheme.Hom.isReduced_image`.
7. Nash blowup and iterates (25.4), locality (25.5(c)), Gauss-map description (25.5(d)): NashBlowups §§2.1–2.7, 3.1 (merged, not implemented).
