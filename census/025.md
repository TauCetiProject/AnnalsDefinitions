# 25. Nash blowup of a variety

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged NashBlowups specifies the definition: `nashBlowup` as `N(X, Ω_{X/k}, d, X_sm^{(d)})`, the scheme-theoretic image of the classifying map of the smooth locus in the Grassmannian of rank-`d` quotients of `Ω_{X/k}` (§§1.2, 2.1), with `nashBlowupHom`, the Nash cotangent bundle `nashCotangent`, properness and the modification property (§2.2), the universal property (§2.4), locality and field base change (§2.6), iterates `nashIterate` (§2.7), the Gauss-map description (§3.1) and the blowup description (§3.2). No Nash blowup is in code; of Layer 0, the comparison map, base change and the closed-immersion clause of differentials (§§0.1–0.2), the smooth locus of dimension `d` (§0.3, `smoothLocusOfDim`), smoothness at closed points over a perfect field (§0.5, `Scheme.Hom.smooth_iff_isRegularLocalRing_closedPoints`) and the predicate `HasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials` (§0.7) are. Its main input, the Grassmannian `Gr_d(E)` of a quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type, is a target of merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (`coherentGrassmannian`), implemented only in part: the scheme `Scheme.Modules.grassmannian E k` glued over an arbitrary base is in code, its universal quotient and universal property over `X`-schemes are not. The other ingredients are in code (Kähler sheaf, smooth locus and smooth locus of dimension `d`, affine Grassmannian scheme, scheme-theoretic image).

Used by #26.
