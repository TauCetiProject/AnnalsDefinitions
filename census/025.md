# 25. Nash blowup of a variety

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged NashBlowups specifies the definition: `nashBlowup` as `N(X, Ω_{X/k}, d, X_sm^{(d)})`, the scheme-theoretic image of the classifying map of the smooth locus in the Grassmannian of rank-`d` quotients of `Ω_{X/k}` (§§1.2, 2.1), with `nashBlowupHom`, the Nash cotangent bundle `nashCotangent`, properness and the modification property (§2.2), the universal property (§2.4), locality and field base change (§2.6), iterates `nashIterate` (§2.7), the Gauss-map description (§3.1) and the blowup description (§3.2). No Nash blowup is in code; of Layer 0, the comparison map, base change and the closed-immersion clause of differentials (§§0.1–0.2), the smooth locus of dimension `d` (§0.3, `smoothLocusOfDim`) and the predicate `HasLocallyFreeQuotientOfDifferentials` (§0.7) are. Its main input, the Grassmannian `Gr_d(E)` of a quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type, is a target of merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (`coherentGrassmannian`), implemented only in part: the scheme `Scheme.Modules.grassmannian E k` glued over an arbitrary base is in code, its universal quotient and universal property over `X`-schemes are not. The other ingredients are in code (Kähler sheaf, smooth locus and smooth locus of dimension `d`, affine Grassmannian scheme, scheme-theoretic image).

Used by #26.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Ω_{X/k}` as coherent sheaf: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.relativeDifferentials`).
2. Smooth locus `X_sm`, density for perfect `k`: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus`; the smooth locus of dimension `d` (NashBlowups §0.3) in Tau Ceti (`smoothLocusOfDim`); §0.5 merged, not implemented.
3. Grassmannian of quotients of a module over an affine base: Tau Ceti (`Module.Grassmannian.scheme`).
4. `Gr_d(E)` for quasi-coherent finite-type `E` on a scheme, with base change and Plücker: merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (not implemented), on ModularCurves §0.7 (partial: affine base with base change, and the glued scheme `Scheme.Modules.grassmannian E k` over an arbitrary base without its universal property; rank-one charts and the Plücker morphism over an affine base in https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3921 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3924).
5. Section `σ: X_sm → Gr_d(Ω)` from the locally free rank-`d` quotient: follows from 4's universal property (NashBlowups §1.2, merged, not implemented).
6. Scheme-theoretic image and reduced structure: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.image`, Tau Ceti `Scheme.Hom.isReduced_image`.
7. Nash blowup and iterates (25.4), locality (25.5(c)), Gauss-map description (25.5(d)): NashBlowups §§2.1–2.7, 3.1 (merged, not implemented).
