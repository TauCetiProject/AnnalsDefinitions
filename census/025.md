# 25. Nash blowup of a variety

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. The Nash blowup is not a target anywhere, but almost all ingredients already exist in code (Kähler sheaf, smooth locus, affine Grassmannian scheme, scheme-theoretic image), and the global Grassmannian `Grass_d(Ω_{X/k})` of a coherent sheaf is a target in merged SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 (as Quot with `X = S`) and explicitly in open PR #853 §1.3.

Used by #26.

## Mathlib
- `Module.Grassmannian R M k` and `Module.Grassmannian.functor` (quotient convention, `Mathlib/RingTheory/Grassmannian.lean`); its TODO lists "Grassmannians for schemes and quasi-coherent sheaf of modules" and representability.
- Smooth locus: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Smooth.lean`), `Algebra.smoothLocus`.
- Scheme-theoretic image: `Scheme.Hom.image`, `Hom.toImage` (`AlgebraicGeometry/IdealSheaf/Subscheme.lean`).
- Kähler differentials of algebras `Ω[S⁄R]`; no sheaf of differentials on schemes.

## Tau Ceti code
- Sheaf of differentials: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials`, `universalDerivation` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Differentials/Basic.lean`), quasi-coherence, locally free on smooth schemes (`FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.relativeDifferentials`, `Differentials/LocallyFree.lean`).
- Modifications: `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsModification` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Modification/`), strict transform under a blow-up (`Blowup/StrictTransform.lean`).
- Grassmannian scheme of a module over an affine base: `Module.Grassmannian.scheme R M k`, `toSpec`, charts (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/Basic.lean`), points `schemeHomEquiv` (`Points.lean`), valuative criterion/properness for finite `M` (`Proper.lean`), universal quotient (`UniversalQuotient.lean`).
- So the affine case `X = Spec A` (Grass of `Ω_{A/k}` over `Spec A`, section over the smooth locus, scheme-theoretic image) is definable from existing code. No `nashBlowup` declaration exists. Missing for the global definition: Grassmannian of a quasi-coherent sheaf on a non-affine `X` (gluing/base change of the affine construction).

## Merged roadmaps
- Not a target (no merged README mentions Nash blowups; ResolutionOfSingularitiesCharacteristicZero uses Hironaka-style marked ideals, not Nash).
- Prerequisites:
  - SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 `hilbert_quot_schemes` (Quot of a coherent `E` on projective `X/S`; with `X = S`, constant polynomial `d`, this is `Grass_d(E)`). This is the only merged route to the Grassmannian of a coherent sheaf, which `Ω_{X/k}` is on singular `X`; Hilbert/Quot is not started.
  - The relative Grassmannian `Gr(E, d)` with Plücker embedding, which SSF §4.14 states only for `E` locally free, and which ModularCurves §0G owns (finite-locally-free Grassmannian). Partial: the affine-base case is in code; gluing over a non-affine base, base change and the Plücker immersion are missing. ModularCurves §0G assigns the coherent extension to AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3, which is not merged (open PR #853).
  - SSF §4.11 modifications and strict transforms (weak link). Partial: `IsModification` with `.birational`, `.comp`, `.isIso_of_isFinite` and the strict transform under a blow-up are in code; `IsModification.centre` and the general strict-transform API are missing.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #853 "Algebraic moduli and representability for arithmetic geometry" §1.3 `coherentGrassmannian`: for quasi-coherent finite-type `E` on arbitrary `S`, `Gr_d(E)` with universal quotient, base change, Plücker, and agreement with `Module.Grassmannian` on affines. This is exactly 25.2/25.3.
- No PR mentions Nash blowups (the "Nash" hits in #1614, #1761, #1854 etc. are Nash embedding / Nash–Moser).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Ω_{X/k}` as coherent sheaf: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.relativeDifferentials`).
2. Smooth locus `X_sm`, density for perfect `k`: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` (density lemma not checked; small).
3. Grassmannian of quotients of a module over an affine base: Tau Ceti (`Module.Grassmannian.scheme`).
4. `Grass_d(M)` for coherent `M` on a scheme, with base change and Plücker: PR #853 §1.3; in merged roadmaps only implicitly, as Quot with `X = S` in SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 (not started). The locally free case (SSF §4.14 Grassmannian clause, owned by ModularCurves §0G) is partial: affine base only.
5. Section `σ: X_sm → Grass_d(Ω)` from the locally free rank-`d` quotient: follows from 4's universal property.
6. Scheme-theoretic image and reduced structure: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.image`.
7. Nash blowup and iterates (25.4), locality (25.5(c)), Gauss-map description (25.5(d)): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A short section "canonical modifications": Nash blowup via the Grassmannian of `Ω`, its universal property, locality, the embedded Gauss-map description, and (Remark 2) its identification with the blowup of `(∧^d Ω)/torsion`; plus the toric/logarithmic-Jacobian computation for toric varieties needed by the paper. It sits naturally in ResolutionOfSingularitiesCharacteristicZero or SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 4 next to §4.11 modifications. References: Nobile, *Some properties of the Nash blowing-up*; González-Sprinberg; EGA I (1971) §9.7.
