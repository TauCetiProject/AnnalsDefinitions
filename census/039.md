# 39. Hard sphere dynamics on the torus

## Status
UNPLANNED. The hard sphere flow, its collision dynamics and Alexander's theorem 39.3 appear in no merged roadmap or open PR, and open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) names the `N`-particle hard-sphere flow as outside it. PR #2231 does specify the measure-theoretic frame of Definition 39.4 (§3.5: flows defined off a null set, Mackey's point realization of an almost-everywhere group law, disjoint unions for the grand canonical phase space, transport of densities), and the planar polygonal billiard flow (Layer 9); the construction of the flow itself, which is the content of the definition, is unplanned.

## Merged roadmaps
- None. SymplecticGeometry/CotangentLagrangian §C3.1 has convex planar billiards (discrete action and periodic orbits on smooth convex tables), not hard spheres and not a measure-preserving flow on phase space with a null exceptional set.

## Open roadmap PRs
- None specifies hard spheres, Boltzmann or kinetic theory.
- #2231 "Add classical ergodic theory, entropy and flows roadmap" Layer 9 builds the billiard flow of a planar polygonal domain: phase space with reflection identifications, finiteness of collisions, a null invariant singular set, an honest flow on the regular set and invariance of the Liouville measure (§§9.2-9.3). This is the two-dimensional, flat-walled model of item 4's construction, not the `N`-particle flow with spherical scatterers.
- #2255 "MathematicalBilliards: roadmap" Layer 8 (§§8.2–8.3) builds the planar polygonal billiard flow as a measure-preserving action of `ℝ`, the identity on an invariant null singular set (the almost-everywhere flow shape of #2231 §3.5; #2255 lists ClassicalErgodicTheory as consumer of that flow). Its Scope puts dispersing and semi-dispersing billiards and billiards in dimension three and higher outside it, and its Downstream consumers name hard-sphere flows as a consumer of that shape. No hard-sphere dynamics.
- #2231 §3.5 "Flows defined almost everywhere" (standard Borel space, σ-finite measure): a conull flow is a measurable flow on a conull `X₀` preserving `μ`, extended by the identity off `X₀`, with dynamical invariants independent of the carrier (item 1); a jointly measurable family `T t` of measure-preserving maps with `T (s + t) = T s ∘ T t` almost everywhere (exceptional set depending on `s, t`) has a conull flow realization, unique up to null sets (Mackey, item 2; the form of 39.3's group law); countable disjoint unions `Σ N, X_N` with componentwise conull flows (item 3; the grand canonical phase space of 39.4); `(Φ t)_* (μ.withDensity W) = μ.withDensity (W ∘ Φ (-t))`, so trajectories of absolutely continuous random initial data are defined almost surely with density `W ∘ Φ (-t)` (item 4; 39.4's density transport). Its Scope says billiards with curved or spherical scatterers and the `N`-particle hard-sphere flow are outside it and consume §3.5.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Torus, Lebesgue measure, `Flow`, `MeasurePreserving`: Mathlib.
2. Phase space D^ε_N, binary collision, scattering map (involution, conservation laws): unplanned (elementary).
3. Trajectories (Definition 39.2), uniqueness: unplanned (elementary).
4. Alexander's theorem (Theorem 39.3): null set of bad data (triple collisions, grazing, accumulation of collisions), measure preservation of the a.e.-defined flow, group law a.e.: unplanned, this is the real content.
5. From an almost-everywhere flow to Definition 39.4: realization of 39.3's almost-everywhere group law as a conull flow, the grand canonical disjoint union and the transport `W(t) = W⁰ ∘ T(−t)`: PR #2231 §3.5 items 2–4. The weak form of the Liouville equation with specular reflection on `∂D^ε_N`: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "HardSphereDynamics / KineticLimits" roadmap would build billiard flows in phase space with piecewise smooth boundary (collision maps, Liouville measure preservation across specular reflection, measure-zero of singular orbits), Alexander's construction of the N-particle hard sphere flow, and then the BBGKY hierarchy and Lanford's theorem as the natural library goal (Gallagher–Saint-Raymond–Texier, *From Newton to Boltzmann: hard spheres and short-range potentials*; Cercignani–Illner–Pulvirenti, *The Mathematical Theory of Dilute Gases*; Chernov–Markarian, *Chaotic Billiards*, Ch. 2 for the measure-theoretic billiard flow). It would sit beside PR #2231's polygonal billiard flows (Layer 9) and consume its almost-everywhere flows (§3.5), as PR #2231 itself proposes, and the Probability parts of the library; no existing roadmap is a natural parent.
