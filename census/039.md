# 39. Hard sphere dynamics on the torus

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2448 (HardSphereDynamicsAndKineticLimits) specifies the definition in full, on flat tori `ℝᵈ/L` and on `ℝᵈ`: hard-core phase spaces, contacts and the scattering map (§§2.1, 3.1–3.3, 39.1), trajectories with right-continuous velocities and their uniqueness (§§5.1–5.5, 39.2), Alexander's theorem `volume_hardCoreSet_diff_goodSet` (§6.4, 39.3), the flow defined everywhere as the trajectory map on the good set and the identity off it, its measure preservation and the grand canonical phase space (§§7.1–7.5), and transported densities with the weak Liouville equation with specular reflection (§§8.1–8.2, 39.4). It consumes the almost-everywhere flows of ClassicalErgodicTheory §3.5 (open PR #2231). No merged roadmap specifies hard spheres; merged MathematicalBilliards (#2255) puts billiards in dimension three and higher outside it.

## Mathlib
- Has: `UnitAddTorus (Fin d)` (`Topology/Instances/AddCircle/Real.lean`) with its product Haar measure, but with the supremum of the circle norms, not the quotient distance `min |x̃ − ỹ|` over lifts that Definition 39 uses (PR #2448 builds `FlatTorus L` with the Euclidean quotient norm for that reason, and compares only group and measure with `UnitAddTorus`); quotient norms `QuotientAddGroup.instNormedAddCommGroup`, Haar measure on quotients by a lattice, `Flow` (`Dynamics/Flow.lean`), `MeasurePreserving`, ergodic theory basics, `Metric.sphere`.
- Missing: phase space with excluded diagonals, collision maps, piecewise-free-transport trajectories, Alexander's a.e.-existence theorem.

## Tau Ceti code
- No hard-sphere dynamics (grep `HardSphere`, `hardCore`: no hits; `FlatTorus` occurs only in the Wirtinger calculus on complex tori, `Analysis/Complex/Wirtinger/FlatTorus/`).
- Planar billiards on a strictly convex table: `TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.nextCollision` (`TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/NextCollision.lean`), time reversal and the inverse map (`Billiard/Inverse.lean`), the generating function `−‖χ(t) − χ(s)‖` (`Billiard/GeneratingFunction.lean`), periodic billiard polygons `TauCeti.exists_billiard_periodic_reflection` (`Billiard/PeriodicPolygon.lean`), the circular table `TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.circle` (`Billiard/Circle.lean`), area preservation with Poincaré recurrence and exact symplecticity of the billiard map. Discrete-time and two-dimensional; not used by PR #2448.
- Suppliers that PR #2448 cites from Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.Probability.multivariateGaussianPDFReal` (`Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Density.lean`), `TauCeti.Semigroups.StronglyContinuousGroup` (`Analysis/Semigroups/Group/Basic.lean`), `TauCeti.Probability.empiricalFiniteMeasure` (`Probability/Process/EmpiricalMeasure.lean`), `TauCeti.flowOfLipschitz` (`Dynamics/Flow/OfLipschitz.lean`), `ZLattice.successiveMin` (`Algebra/Module/ZLattice/SuccessiveMinima.lean`, giving the injectivity radius of a flat torus).

  - Layer 1: flat position spaces as quotients of `E = ℝᵈ` with an injectivity radius; §1.3 flat tori `E ⧸ L` with quotient norm and Haar measure (the torus `𝕋^d` of 39).
  - Layers 2–3: the specular scattering map `σ_ν` (§2.1), pre/post-collisional variables; hard-core sets `𝒟̄^ε_N`, contacts, contact normals and boundary pieces (§§3.1–3.2); contact scattering and time reversal (§3.3); free transport (§3.4); collision invariants (§3.5) (39.1).
  - Layer 4: the two-body problem, collision map and its Jacobian, measure preservation.
  - Layer 5: trajectories (§5.1), uniqueness and gluing (§5.2), symmetries (§5.3), the left-continuous convention of Deng–Hani–Ma (§5.4), good configurations (§5.5) (39.2).
  - Layer 6: Alexander's theorem for finitely many particles, `HardSphere.volume_hardCoreSet_diff_goodSet` (§6.4), with `G` Borel and the trajectory map Borel on `ℝ × G` (39.3).
  - Layer 7: the flow `T_t` (trajectory map on `G`, identity off `G`; an exact group action, §7.1), measure preservation via ClassicalErgodicTheory §3.5 item 1 (§7.2), symmetries and conservation laws (§7.3), the Koopman group (§7.4), the grand canonical phase space `Σ_N (Fin N → X × E)` with `Λ = Σ_N (1/N!) λ_N` (§7.5).
  - Layer 8: transported densities `W(t) = W⁰ ∘ T_{−t}` (§8.1, via §3.5 item 4), the weak Liouville equation with specular reflection (§8.2), marginals (§8.3) (39.4).

Fluctuations and large deviations (the cited paper's theorems) are named as downstream.
- #2231 "Add classical ergodic theory, entropy and flows roadmap" §3.5 "Flows defined almost everywhere" (standard Borel space, σ-finite measure): conull flows extended by the identity (item 1), Mackey realization of an almost-everywhere group law (item 2), countable disjoint unions (item 3), transport of densities `(Φ t)_* (μ.withDensity W) = μ.withDensity (W ∘ Φ (-t))` (item 4). ## Prerequisite chain
1. Torus, Lebesgue measure, `Flow`, `MeasurePreserving`: Mathlib.
2. Phase space D^ε_N, binary collision, scattering map (involution, conservation laws): PR #2448 Layers 1–3.
3. Trajectories (Definition 39.2), uniqueness: PR #2448 Layer 5.
4. Alexander's theorem (Theorem 39.3): PR #2448 Layers 4 and 6.
5. From an almost-everywhere flow to Definition 39.4: the flow and grand canonical phase space PR #2448 §§7.1–7.5, densities and the weak Liouville equation PR #2448 §§8.1–8.2, on PR #2231 §3.5 items 1, 3, 4.
6. For the paper's theorems (fluctuating Boltzmann equation, large deviations): correlation functions and the Boltzmann–Grad scaling PR #2448 Layers 9–10; Lanford's theorem: stated in PR #2448 §10.4 (`HardSphere.LanfordStatement d`), its proof (assigned by PR #2448 to a BoltzmannGradLimitAndLanford roadmap) not in any open PR; fluctuations and large deviations unplanned (named downstream by PR #2448).

## Library-scale gap
PR #2448 is the roadmap for this definition (Gallagher–Saint-Raymond–Texier, *From Newton to Boltzmann*; Alexander 1976; Bodineau–Gallagher–Saint-Raymond–Simonella 2023): what remains is its review and merging, together with PR #2231 §3.5. Beyond it the paper needs the BBGKY hierarchy and Lanford's proof, which no open PR specifies, and the fluctuation and large-deviation theory of the empirical measure, which PR #2448 lists under Downstream consumers.
