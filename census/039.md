# 39. Hard sphere dynamics on the torus

## Status
UNPLANNED. Neither the hard sphere flow nor billiard/kinetic dynamics in phase space with collisions appears in Mathlib, Tau Ceti, merged roadmaps or open PRs.

## Mathlib
- Has: `UnitAddTorus d` (`Topology/Instances/AddCircle/Real.lean`), Lebesgue/Haar measure on it, `Flow` (`Dynamics/Flow.lean`), `MeasurePreserving`, ergodic theory basics, `Metric.sphere`.
- Missing: phase space with excluded diagonals, collision maps, piecewise-free-transport trajectories, Alexander's a.e.-existence theorem.

## Tau Ceti code
- Nothing (grep for billiard, hard sphere: no hits).

## Merged roadmaps
- None. SymplecticGeometry/CotangentLagrangian §C3.1 has convex planar billiards (discrete action and periodic orbits on smooth convex tables), not hard spheres and not a measure-preserving flow on phase space with a null exceptional set.

## Open roadmap PRs
None (grep for billiard, hard sphere, Boltzmann, kinetic theory across all open-PR READMEs: no hits).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Torus, Lebesgue measure, `Flow`, `MeasurePreserving`: Mathlib.
2. Phase space D^ε_N, binary collision, scattering map (involution, conservation laws): unplanned (elementary).
3. Trajectories (Definition 39.2), uniqueness: unplanned (elementary).
4. Alexander's theorem (Theorem 39.3): null set of bad data (triple collisions, grazing, accumulation of collisions), measure preservation of the a.e.-defined flow, group law a.e.: unplanned, this is the real content.
5. Grand canonical phase space and Liouville transport: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "HardSphereDynamics / KineticLimits" roadmap would build billiard flows in phase space with piecewise smooth boundary (collision maps, Liouville measure preservation across specular reflection, measure-zero of singular orbits), Alexander's construction of the N-particle hard sphere flow, and then the BBGKY hierarchy and Lanford's theorem as the natural library goal (Gallagher–Saint-Raymond–Texier, *From Newton to Boltzmann: hard spheres and short-range potentials*; Cercignani–Illner–Pulvirenti, *The Mathematical Theory of Dilute Gases*; Chernov–Markarian, *Chaotic Billiards*, Ch. 2 for the measure-theoretic billiard flow). It would sit beside the ergodic-theory and Probability parts of the library; no existing roadmap is a natural parent.
