## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact smooth manifold, tangent bundle, `mfderiv`: Mathlib.
2. Riemannian metric on TM: Mathlib (`ContMDiffRiemannianMetric`).
3. C^r flow of a vector field and its generator: Tau Ceti `TauCeti.flowOf` (target of merged DifferentialGeometry, implemented); identification of an abstract C^r `Flow ℝ M` with the flow of its generator: unplanned (small).
4. Continuous vector subbundle of TM: Tau Ceti `IsContMDiffDistribution` at regularity 0 (adequate); fibrewise direct-sum decomposition of TM into three distributions: unplanned (small).
5. Invariance and uniform hyperbolicity estimates (Def 91.2): unplanned.
6. Theory needed by Tsujii-Zhang: stable/unstable foliations, Hölder regularity of E^s/E^u, transitivity/mixing, equilibrium states (#96), exponential mixing: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "UniformlyHyperbolicDynamics" roadmap: hyperbolic sets and Anosov diffeomorphisms/flows, cone fields and adapted metrics, stable manifold theorem (Hadamard-Perron), Hölder continuity of the splitting, shadowing and closing lemmas, structural stability, Markov partitions and symbolic coding, then Bowen-Ruelle thermodynamic formalism (#96) and Smale spectral decomposition. Textbooks: Katok-Hasselblatt, *Introduction to the Modern Theory of Dynamical Systems*; Fisher-Hasselblatt, *Hyperbolic Flows*. It would sit on top of DifferentialGeometry (flows, distributions) and RiemannianGeometry, beside a new ergodic-theory/entropy roadmap (#96, #97).

Related definitions: #96 (equilibrium states of Hölder potentials for Anosov flows; same paper).
