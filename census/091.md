# 91. Anosov flow

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2441 (HyperbolicDynamicsAndThermodynamicFormalism) specifies the definition: §7.2 `IsFlowHyperbolicSet` for a C¹ flow `g` with generator `flowGenerator` (no zero on Λ, an invariant splitting `T_xM = E^s_x ⊕ ℝX(x) ⊕ E^u_x` with uniform exponential contraction and expansion for a continuous Riemannian metric), an Anosov flow being a compact `M` that is a hyperbolic set (Definition 91.2); with independence of the metric (§7.3), uniqueness and continuity of the splitting (§§7.4–7.5), adapted metrics (§7.6), stable and unstable manifolds (Layer 8, including Hölder continuity of the bundles, §8.7) and the examples (Layer 9: suspensions, time changes, geodesic flows in negative curvature). No merged roadmap specifies it. Its prerequisites in code: global flows of vector fields on compact manifolds (`TauCeti.flowOf`), continuous distributions, Riemannian metrics.

## Mathlib
- No `Anosov`/hyperbolic splitting anywhere.
- Have: `Flow` (`Mathlib/Dynamics/Flow.lean`, topological flows as additive actions), manifolds, `mfderiv`, `TangentSpace`, integral curves (`Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/IntegralCurve/*`), Riemannian bundle metrics `ContMDiffRiemannianMetric` / `IsContMDiffRiemannianBundle` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Riemannian.lean`), `IsRiemannianManifold` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Basic.lean`).
- Nearest missing piece: an invariant continuous splitting `TM = E^s ⊕ ℝX ⊕ E^u` with uniform exponential contraction/expansion of `mfderiv (g t)`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Definition absent (only hit: docstring of `TauCeti/Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sol/Basic.lean`, "torus bundles with Anosov monodromy").
- Prerequisites present: `TauCeti.flowOf`, `flowDomain`, `contMDiffOn_flowOf` (`Geometry/Manifold/Flow/Basic.lean`; global flow on compact boundaryless manifolds); `TauCeti.IsContMDiffDistribution` (`Geometry/Manifold/Distribution.lean`, subbundles of TM; regularity 0 gives continuous subbundles); `Dynamics/Flow/Stable.lean` (`stableSet`/`unstableSet`, topological only); `Dynamics/Flow/Variational.lean` (derivative of flow maps on vector spaces).
- Hyperbolicity at a single equilibrium only: exponential dichotomies of hyperbolic linear flows (`Analysis/ODE/ExponentialDichotomy/Hyperbolic.lean`) and Lyapunov–Perron local stable and unstable graphs at a hyperbolic equilibrium of an ODE on a Banach space, with their `C¹` convergence (`Analysis/ODE/LyapunovPerron/*`). No hyperbolic sets, invariant splittings along orbits or stable foliations.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact smooth manifold, tangent bundle, `mfderiv`: Mathlib.
2. Riemannian metric on TM: Mathlib (`ContMDiffRiemannianMetric`; continuous metrics `Bundle.ContinuousRiemannianMetric`).
3. C^r flow of a vector field and its generator: Tau Ceti `TauCeti.flowOf` (target of merged DifferentialGeometry, implemented); the generator `flowGenerator` of an abstract C¹ `Flow ℝ M`: PR #2441 §7.2.
4. Continuous vector subbundle of TM: Tau Ceti `IsContMDiffDistribution` at regularity 0; the pointwise splitting `E^s ⊕ ℝX ⊕ E^u` and its continuity: PR #2441 §§7.2, 7.5.
5. Invariance and uniform hyperbolicity estimates (Def 91.2), metric independence: PR #2441 §§7.2–7.3.
6. Theory needed by Tsujii-Zhang: stable/unstable foliations and Hölder regularity of `E^s`, `E^u`: PR #2441 Layer 8; mixing of flows and exponential mixing for Hölder observables: merged ClassicalErgodicTheory §§3.2, 3.6; equilibrium states and the Bowen–Ruelle theorem (#96): merged ClassicalErgodicTheory §8.1, PR #2441 §10.3. Exponential mixing of Anosov flows (Dolgopyat estimates, the paper's theorem): unplanned, named outside PR #2441.

## Library-scale gap
Covered by PR #2441 for the definition and the uniformly hyperbolic theory (hyperbolic sets and Anosov diffeomorphisms/flows, adapted metrics, Hadamard–Perron, Hölder continuity of the splitting, local product structure, closing, spectral decomposition, Bowen–Ruelle; Katok–Hasselblatt, *Introduction to the Modern Theory of Dynamical Systems*; Fisher–Hasselblatt, *Hyperbolic Flows*), on top of merged ClassicalErgodicTheory. What remains for the definition is review and merging of PR #2441. The paper's theorem (exponential mixing of smooth mixing Anosov flows in dimension three) further needs Dolgopyat-type estimates and transfer operators on anisotropic spaces, which PR #2441 names outside it and no roadmap plans; Markov partitions and structural stability are also outside it.

Related definitions: #96 (equilibrium states of Hölder potentials for Anosov flows; same paper).
