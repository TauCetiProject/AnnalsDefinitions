## Mathlib
- No `Anosov`/hyperbolic splitting anywhere.
- Have: `Flow` (`Mathlib/Dynamics/Flow.lean`, topological flows as additive actions), manifolds, `mfderiv`, `TangentSpace`, integral curves (`Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/IntegralCurve/*`), Riemannian bundle metrics `ContMDiffRiemannianMetric` / `IsContMDiffRiemannianBundle` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Riemannian.lean`), `IsRiemannianManifold` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Basic.lean`).
- Nearest missing piece: an invariant continuous splitting `TM = E^s ⊕ ℝX ⊕ E^u` with uniform exponential contraction/expansion of `mfderiv (g t)`.

## Merged roadmaps
- Not a target. DifferentialGeometry (layers 2-3: global flow of complete vector field, compact-manifold completeness) specifies and has implemented the flow prerequisite. ## Open roadmap PRs
- None specifies Anosov flows. (#1797 "LieHighestWeight, GeometricTopology: one Suggested.lean per folder" only touches the same GeometricTopology Anosov-monodromy example.)
- #2231 "Add classical ergodic theory, entropy and flows roadmap" supplies the ergodic theory the paper uses on top of the definition: mixing and weak mixing of real flows (§§3.1-3.2), flow-invariant probabilities (§3.4), §3.6 correlations and rates of mixing (`flowCorrelation`, `IsExponentiallyMixing μ θ` for `θ`-Hölder observables with `‖f‖_θ = ‖f‖_∞ + nnHolderNorm θ f`, `HasCorrelationBound`, monotonicity in `θ`, invariance under bi-Lipschitz conjugacy), flow pressure and the flow variational principle (§5.5, §7.3), flow equilibrium states (§8.1, #96), and uniqueness of equilibrium states for expansive flows with specification and for suspension flows over subshifts of finite type (§§8.4–8.6). It has no hyperbolic splittings, stable manifolds or Markov sections: its Scope names uniformly hyperbolic smooth dynamics (Anosov and Axiom A diffeomorphisms and flows, stable manifolds, Markov partitions, SRB measures) as covered by no roadmap, and says such a roadmap gets the Bowen–Ruelle theorem from §§8.4–8.5 once it proves that transitive Anosov flows are expansive, have specification on their mixing pieces (or are suspensions of SFTs via Markov sections) and that Hölder potentials have the Bowen property.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact smooth manifold, tangent bundle, `mfderiv`: Mathlib.
2. Riemannian metric on TM: Mathlib (`ContMDiffRiemannianMetric`).
3. C^r flow of a vector field and its generator: Tau Ceti `TauCeti.flowOf` (target of merged DifferentialGeometry, implemented); identification of an abstract C^r `Flow ℝ M` with the flow of its generator: unplanned (small).
4. Continuous vector subbundle of TM: Tau Ceti `IsContMDiffDistribution` at regularity 0 (adequate); fibrewise direct-sum decomposition of TM into three distributions: unplanned (small).
5. Invariance and uniform hyperbolicity estimates (Def 91.2): unplanned.
6. Theory needed by Tsujii-Zhang: stable/unstable foliations, Hölder regularity of E^s/E^u: unplanned; mixing of flows, the definition of exponential mixing for Hölder observables, and equilibrium states (#96): PR #2231 §3.2, §3.6, §8.1; Bowen-Ruelle uniqueness for Anosov flows: §§8.4–8.5 once the hyperbolic inputs above exist.

## Library-scale gap
A "UniformlyHyperbolicDynamics" roadmap: hyperbolic sets and Anosov diffeomorphisms/flows, cone fields and adapted metrics, stable manifold theorem (Hadamard-Perron), Hölder continuity of the splitting, shadowing and closing lemmas, structural stability, Markov partitions and symbolic coding, then the reduction of Bowen-Ruelle thermodynamic formalism (#96) to PR #2231 §§8.4–8.6 (expansiveness, specification, Bowen property of Hölder potentials) and Smale spectral decomposition. Textbooks: Katok-Hasselblatt, *Introduction to the Modern Theory of Dynamical Systems*; Fisher-Hasselblatt, *Hyperbolic Flows*. It would sit on top of DifferentialGeometry (flows, distributions) and RiemannianGeometry, on top of open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory: flows, entropy, pressure, equilibrium states; #96, #97).

Related definitions: #96 (equilibrium states of Hölder potentials for Anosov flows; same paper).
