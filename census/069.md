## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
The λ-adic representation attached to an eigenform away from the level (weight ≥ 2, nebentypus, arithmetic-Frobenius polynomial `X² − λ_p X + ε(p)p^{k−1}`, unramified outside `Nℓ`, uniqueness by good-place semisimple recognition) is the milestone `higherWeight_attachment` of merged [AlgebraicModularFormsAndSerreWeights](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicModularFormsAndSerreWeights/README.md) §6.2 "Classical eigenforms of weight at least two", built on the rank-two cohomological summand of §6.1 and the geometric coefficient systems of merged ModularCurvesPartII §§14.1–14.4a, 14.11. Merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations supplies the continuous-representation carrier, lattices and Čebotarev uniqueness, and merged AutomorphicGaloisRepresentations §§1.5, 1.7 compares the attachment with the DFG motivic realisation and adds density-one rigidity. Tau Ceti has the eigenform/newform side and the `ContinuousRep` carrier, but no Galois representation attached to a form, and none of the étale-cohomological inputs is implemented.
