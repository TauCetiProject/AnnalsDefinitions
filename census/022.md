# 22. Galois action on the étale cohomology of the geometric fibre (rigid-analytic)

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/README.md) (PR #2271) specifies exactly this definition: the completed algebraic closure `C` (§0.1, README l.126) with `G_K` acting continuously (§0.2, l.143, Proposition 22.2), the action `ρ(σ) = (σ_X)^♮` on `H^i(X_C, M_C)` for a rigid space `X/K` with its functoriality (§§1.1–1.3, l.193–259), discreteness and the dictionary with `R^i f_*` on `Spa(K, 𝒪_K)_ét` (§§2.1, 2.3, l.287, l.319, i.e. 22.5(d)), and the `ℓ`-adic groups as continuous representations (Layer 3, l.394). Its suppliers are merged, including PadicDifferentialEquationsAndRigidCohomology §RD.0.33, which §0.1 needs for `C` algebraically closed in characteristic `p`. The scheme analogue is merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations F.4.6 and GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §1.4. Nothing is implemented beyond early adic-space prerequisites.

1. Non-archimedean field `K`, `C = (K̄)^`, continuous `G_K`-action on `C` (Prop. 22.2): Mathlib has completions, the spectral norm and the absolute Galois group; the extension of `σ` to `C` is merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §0.2 (not implemented). `C` algebraically closed: Mathlib `IsAlgClosed.of_denseRange` in characteristic 0; in characteristic `p`, target of merged PadicDifferentialEquationsAndRigidCohomology §RD.0.33 (not implemented).
2. Adic spaces, `Spa(K,O_K)`, rigid spaces locally of finite type: Completed/AdicSpaces (done), AdicSpacesPartII R0 (partial in Tau Ceti).
3. Fibre product `X ×_{Spa K} Spa(C)` and the automorphisms `σ_X`: AdicSpacesPartII R0.7 / AdicEtaleGeometry A0 (merged, partial: affinoid fibre products done, gluing to global fibre products missing).
4. Étale site `X_ét`, pullback `h^{-1}`: AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6, AdicSpacesPartIV R4 (merged, not implemented).
5. Étale cohomology `H^i(X_ét, F)` with pullback maps: Mathlib `Sheaf.H` (generic) / ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology §1.1 (merged, not implemented).
6. `ρ(σ) = (σ_X)^♮` and its left-action, functoriality, continuity (Huber's limit theorem), 22.5(d): merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §§1.1–1.3, 2.1, 2.3 (not implemented; scheme analogue ECF F.4.6 and §1.4).
7. `ℓ`-adic limits `H^i(X_C, ℤ_ℓ(j))`: merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology Layer 3 (not implemented; scheme analogue ECF F.4.x).

## Library-scale gap
Specified in merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology, which sits on merged PadicDifferentialEquationsAndRigidCohomology §RD.0.33 (characteristic `p` only), on the merged adic-space roadmaps (AdicEtaleGeometry A1, AdicSpacesPartII R0.7, AdicSpacesPartIV R4) and on merged ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology for the sheaf theory of analytic étale sites. The merged prerequisites are largely unimplemented: no étale site of an adic space and no glued fibre products exist in code. Textbooks: Huber, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic varieties and adic spaces* (1996), §§2.6, 3.1, 4.x; de Jong–van der Put, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic spaces*, §3.6; Scholze, *p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties*.
