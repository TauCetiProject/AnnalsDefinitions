# 22. Galois action on the étale cohomology of the geometric fibre (rigid-analytic)

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Nowhere is the `G_K`-action on `H^i_ét(X_C, F)` for a rigid space `X/K` a target; the scheme analogue is a merged target (EtaleCohomologyFoundations F.4.6), and adic spaces, their étale sites and (in open PR #875) their étale cohomology are specified, so a modest extension covers it.

## Mathlib
- Generic sheaf cohomology on any site: `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H` (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Basic.lean`); `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup` (`Mathlib/FieldTheory/AbsoluteGaloisGroup.lean`); continuous group cohomology (`RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/`).
- Scheme étale site: `AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale.lean`, `EtalePoint.lean` (`Scheme.pointSmallEtale`).
- No adic spaces, no `Spa`, no rigid spaces, no Galois action on cohomology. Nearest missing piece: adic spaces and their étale site.

## Tau Ceti code
- Adic-space foundations exist: `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/` (`Spa/`, `PreAdicSpace/`, `ValuationSpectrum/`, `Pullback/`, `Morphisms/`), rigid affinoids `AlgebraicGeometry/Rigid/Affinoid/`.
- Completed tensor products and affinoid fibre products (AdicEtaleGeometry A0, AdicSpacesPartII R0.7, partial): `Huber.IsCompletedPushout` and the Huber-pair `completedTensor` (`RingTheory/Huber/TensorProduct/`), with ring-level associativity, symmetry, unit, rational localisation and finite étale base change; `AdicSpace.affinoid_isPullback` and Tate point lifting `exists_ofPair_completedTensor_point` (`AlgebraicGeometry/AdicSpace/Pullback/Affinoid.lean`). Glued (non-affinoid) fibre products are missing.
- No étale site of adic spaces, no geometric fibre `X_C`, no étale cohomology of adic spaces, no Galois action. Definition absent; only early prerequisites present.

- Definition itself: not a target anywhere.
- Scheme analogue: EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.4.6 "Continuous actions and finite-level operation comparison" constructs "the continuous `G_k` action on geometric ordinary/compact cohomology" for `X/k` (unimplemented). This is the algebraic version referenced in Remark (1).
- Prerequisites: Completed/AdicSpaces (Huber rings, `Spa`); AdicSpacesPartII Layer R0 (fibre products R0.7: partial, the affinoid fibre product is in code and global existence by gluing remains; STATUS is stale here); AdicEtaleGeometry A0 (completed tensor/fibre products: partial, ring-level and affinoid content in code, glued fibre products and the adic-space-level comparisons missing; STATUS is stale here), A1.6 (étale and finite étale sites; *test_field*: `smallFiniteEtale (Spa(K,K⁺))` ≃ finite continuous `Gal(K^sep/K)`-sets), A1.7 geometric points, A1.14 change of base field; AdicSpacesPartIV R4 (étale sites on locally noetherian analytic spaces, Huber's comparison with rigid varieties; Checks line ~1712: `Sh(Spa(K,K°)_ét)` ≃ continuous `Gal(K^sep/K)`-sets via `ξ: Spa(C,O_C) → X`, i.e. 22.5(d)'s base case). AdicEtaleGeometry STATUS: A1.3 onward remaining.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Non-archimedean field `K`, `C = (K̄)^`, continuous `G_K`-action on `C` (Prop. 22.2): Mathlib has completions, absolute Galois group; the extension of `σ` to `C` is not packaged (unplanned as a named target; arguably PR #940 PadicPeriodRings §1 for p-adic `K`).
2. Adic spaces, `Spa(K,O_K)`, rigid spaces locally of finite type: Completed/AdicSpaces (done), AdicSpacesPartII R0 (partial in Tau Ceti).
3. Fibre product `X ×_{Spa K} Spa(C)` and the automorphisms `σ_X`: AdicSpacesPartII R0.7 / AdicEtaleGeometry A0 (merged, partial: affinoid fibre products done, gluing to global fibre products missing).
4. Étale site `X_ét`, pullback `h^{-1}`: AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6, AdicSpacesPartIV R4 (merged, not implemented).
5. Étale cohomology `H^i(X_ét, F)` with pullback maps: Mathlib `Sheaf.H` (generic) / PR #875 §1.1.
6. `ρ(σ) = c_σ ∘ σ_X^*` and its left-action, functoriality, continuity (Huber's limit theorem), 22.5(d): unplanned (scheme analogue is ECF F.4.6).
7. `ℓ`-adic limits `H^i(X_C, ℤ_ℓ(j))`: unplanned for rigid spaces (scheme analogue ECF F.4.x).

## Library-scale gap
The missing theory is "étale cohomology of rigid spaces over a non-archimedean field as Galois modules": base change of adic spaces along complete extensions, the geometric fibre `X_C`, the `G_K`-action, Huber's limit theorem `colim_L H^i(X_L) ≅ H^i(X_C)` for qcqs `X`, and the identification with `R^i f_*` under `Sh(Spa K_ét) ≃ G_K`-modules, then twists and ℓ-adic/`ℤ_p` coefficients. This is a natural extra section in PR #875 (ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology), mirroring ECF §F.4.6 for schemes. Textbooks: Huber, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic varieties and adic spaces* (1996), §§2.6, 3.1, 4.x; Scholze, *p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties*.
