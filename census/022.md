# 22. Galois action on the étale cohomology of the geometric fibre (rigid-analytic)

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2271 (GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology) specifies exactly this definition: the completed algebraic closure `C` with `G_K` acting continuously (Layer 0, Proposition 22.2), the action `ρ(σ) = c_σ ∘ σ_X^*` on `H^i(X_C, M_C)` for a rigid space `X/K` with its functoriality (§§1.1–1.3), discreteness and the dictionary with `R^i f_*` on `Spa(K, 𝒪_K)_ét` (§§2.1, 2.3, i.e. 22.5(d)), and the `ℓ`-adic groups as continuous representations (Layer 3). No merged roadmap targets the rigid case; the scheme analogue is merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations F.4.6. Nothing is implemented beyond early adic-space prerequisites.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Non-archimedean field `K`, `C = (K̄)^`, continuous `G_K`-action on `C` (Prop. 22.2): Mathlib has completions, the spectral norm and the absolute Galois group; the extension of `σ` to `C` is PR #2271 Layer 0.
2. Adic spaces, `Spa(K,O_K)`, rigid spaces locally of finite type: Completed/AdicSpaces (done), AdicSpacesPartII R0 (partial in Tau Ceti).
3. Fibre product `X ×_{Spa K} Spa(C)` and the automorphisms `σ_X`: AdicSpacesPartII R0.7 / AdicEtaleGeometry A0 (merged, partial: affinoid fibre products done, gluing to global fibre products missing).
4. Étale site `X_ét`, pullback `h^{-1}`: AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6, AdicSpacesPartIV R4 (merged, not implemented).
5. Étale cohomology `H^i(X_ét, F)` with pullback maps: Mathlib `Sheaf.H` (generic) / PR #875 §1.1.
6. `ρ(σ) = c_σ ∘ σ_X^*` and its left-action, functoriality, continuity (Huber's limit theorem), 22.5(d): PR #2271 §§1.1–1.3, 2.1, 2.3 (scheme analogue is ECF F.4.6).
7. `ℓ`-adic limits `H^i(X_C, ℤ_ℓ(j))`: PR #2271 Layer 3 (scheme analogue ECF F.4.x).

## Library-scale gap
Planned in PR #2271, which sits on the merged adic-space roadmaps (AdicEtaleGeometry A1, AdicSpacesPartII R0.7, AdicSpacesPartIV R4) and on PR #875 for the sheaf theory of analytic étale sites. The merged prerequisites are largely unimplemented: no étale site of an adic space and no glued fibre products exist in code. Textbooks: Huber, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic varieties and adic spaces* (1996), §§2.6, 3.1, 4.x; de Jong–van der Put, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic spaces*, §3.6; Scholze, *p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties*.
