# 22. Galois action on the étale cohomology of the geometric fibre (rigid-analytic)

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/README.md) (PR #2271) specifies exactly this definition: the completed algebraic closure `C` (§0.1, README l.34) with `G_K` acting continuously (§0.2, l.51, Proposition 22.2), the action `ρ(σ) = (σ_X)^♮` on `H^i(X_C, M_C)` for a rigid space `X/K` with its functoriality (§§1.1–1.3, l.101–167), discreteness and the dictionary with `R^i f_*` on `Spa(K, 𝒪_K)_ét` (§§2.1, 2.3, l.195, l.227, i.e. 22.5(d)), and the `ℓ`-adic groups as continuous representations (Layer 3, l.302). Its suppliers are merged. The scheme analogue is merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations F.4.6 and GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §1.4. Not IN-CODE: Tau Ceti has the characteristic-free algebraic closedness of `C` (§RD.0.33), glued fibre products of adic spaces (so `X_C` exists for `X` locally of finite type over `K`) and finite étale morphisms of adic spaces, but no étale site of an adic space, no étale cohomology of `X_C` and no Galois action on it.

6. `ρ(σ) = (σ_X)^♮` and its left-action, functoriality, continuity (Huber's limit theorem), 22.5(d): merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §§1.1–1.3, 2.1, 2.3 (not implemented; scheme analogue ECF F.4.6 and §1.4).

## Library-scale gap
Specified in merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology, which sits on merged PadicDifferentialEquationsAndRigidCohomology §RD.0.33 (in code), on the merged adic-space roadmaps (AdicEtaleGeometry A1, AdicSpacesPartII R0.7, AdicSpacesPartIV R4) and on merged ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology for the sheaf theory of analytic étale sites. The étale-site part of these prerequisites is unimplemented: no étale morphisms or étale site of an adic space exist in code. Textbooks: Huber, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic varieties and adic spaces* (1996), §§2.6, 3.1, 4.x; de Jong–van der Put, *Étale cohomology of rigid analytic spaces*, §3.6; Scholze, *p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties*.
