## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Merged roadmaps specify the linearized operator only implicitly: HeegaardFloer F2.4/F2.6d use the vertical differential of a universal section (genus zero and strips), on top of PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis PH6.2–PH6.3 (real Cauchy–Riemann operators, Fredholm theory) and PH7 (Riemann–Roch). The general operator of Definition 56.3 is not a named target. Tau Ceti has a local-coordinate linearization, the Floer-cylinder covariant D_u, smooth real CR operators, Sobolev-section carriers over surfaces with and without boundary, and the vector similarity principle for `C¹` solutions, but no Sobolev realization or Fredholm theory of real CR operators.

## Mathlib
Nothing on almost complex structures, J-holomorphic curves or CR operators. Has vector bundles, `ContMDiffSection`, Fredholm-adjacent `ContinuousLinearMap` API, `CovariantDerivative`.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.cauchyRiemannOperator`, `TauCeti.cauchyRiemannLinearization` (`Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Linearization.lean`): local-coordinate derivative of du∘j − J(u)∘du in maps and in J (McDuff-Salamon §3.1), no global bundle operator, no Sobolev/Fredholm.
- `Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/CovariantLinearization.lean`: intrinsic D_u ξ for the Floer operator on cylinders with a torsion-free connection (pointwise formula).
- Smooth real Cauchy-Riemann operators (PH6.2, smooth half): `TauCeti.IsRealCauchyRiemannOperator` (Leibniz-rule definition), `difference`/`isZeroOneForm_difference`, `add_zeroOrder`, `sectionComplexLinearPart`/`antilinearPart`, and `IsCovariantDerivativeOn.isRealCauchyRiemannOperator_zeroOnePart` (a complex connection gives `∇^{0,1}`) (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CauchyRiemann/{Basic,Connection}.lean`); local-frame readings, direct sums, transport, straight lines and holomorphic frames on line bundles (`CauchyRiemann/{LocalFrame,Constructions,HolomorphicFrame}.lean`).
- Local analysis: the vector similarity principle for `C¹` solutions of `∂ₛξ + i∂ₜξ + Cξ = 0` with unique continuation and isolated zeros (`TauCeti.exists_similarity_of_contDiffOn`, `Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/Similarity.lean`), reflection across a real boundary condition (`Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/RealBoundaryReflection.lean`), weak real CR equations with Sobolev coefficients (`Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/RoughCoefficients/Weak.lean`).
- Boundaryless Sobolev sections (PH6.1, generic part): `TauCeti.SobolevSection A μ p k` over a `FiniteBundleAtlas`, closedness, completeness, `lowerOrderL` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Sobolev/{Basic,ContMDiffSection}.lean`), and over surfaces modelled on `ℂ` the comparison of chart readings with the local classes `MemWkpLoc` (`SobolevSection.memWkpLoc_coord`, `Sobolev/Local.lean`), Morrey's embedding for `p > 2` (`Sobolev/Morrey.lean`), and the target space of Sobolev `(0,1)`-forms `TauCeti.zeroOneSobolevSections` with its projection (`Sobolev/ZeroOneForm.lean`). Bases with boundary: `TauCeti.BorderedSobolevSection` over a half-plane atlas, complete, with smooth sections included and multiplication by smooth functions (`Sobolev/Bordered/{Basic,SMul}.lean`). No Sobolev realization of a real CR operator, no totally real boundary condition on sections, no Fredholm theory.
- `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure`, `IsPseudoholomorphic` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/{AlmostComplex/Basic,JHolomorphic}.lean`); `IsWeakCauchyRiemannOn` (`Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/Weak.lean`).

## Merged roadmaps
- PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis PH6.2 "Real Cauchy-Riemann operators" (Leibniz-rule definition, D = D₀ + α of class W^{ℓ−1,p}), PH6.3 Fredholm/kernel/cokernel, PH7.2 Riemann-Roch (Prop 56.2). PH6.2 is partial: the smooth real CR operator theory and the Sobolev `(0,1)`-form target space are in code (STATUS lists Layer 6 as untouched); operators of class `W^{ℓ−1,p}` and their realizations with totally real boundary conditions are missing. PH6.1 (Sobolev sections over compact surfaces with boundary) is partial: the boundaryless carrier `SobolevSection` and the bordered carrier `BorderedSobolevSection` exist; atlas independence, Rellich, Morrey up to the boundary and Sobolev multiplication are missing. PH6.3 and PH7.2 are not implemented.
- HeegaardFloer §4.4 (F2.4) universal moduli spaces and split-surjective universal derivative, §4.6d (F2.6d) universal simple-sphere sections and regularity, §4.6b smooth/W^{k,p} topology on J-holomorphic spheres. These implicitly specify D_u as the vertical differential; genus-zero/strip focus, not arbitrary closed Σ. Partial (STATUS: Fredholm/Sard-Smale exist, Floer strip operator not).
- SymplecticFieldTheory (Fredholm linearized operator on weighted spaces at punctured curves), TaubesGromov TG1 (normal operator).
- Not specified: the explicit formula 56.3 with a torsion-free connection and the connection-independence 56.4 for general (Σ, j).

## Prerequisite chain
vector bundles, connections [Mathlib] -> almost complex structures, J-holomorphic maps [Tau Ceti] -> local CR linearization [Tau Ceti `cauchyRiemannLinearization`] -> real CR operators on complex bundles over compact surfaces, Sobolev completions [PH6.1-6.2, partial: smooth real CR operators and a boundaryless Sobolev-section carrier in Tau Ceti; Sobolev-class operators, realizations and the boundary case missing] -> Fredholm property and Riemann-Roch [PH6.3, PH7, not implemented] -> D_u at a J-holomorphic curve, independence of ∇ [HF F2.4/F2.6d implicitly; explicit global formula unplanned] -> regularity of J (D_u onto) [HF F2.4/F2.6d].
