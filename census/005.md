# 5. Infinitesimal character (and Casimir eigenvalue) of a representation of a real reductive group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. The algebraic core is in Tau Ceti for semisimple Lie algebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0: modules with prescribed central character (`TauCeti.centralCharacterModules`), the central character of a finite-dimensional irreducible Lie module (`TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.centralCharacter`), the Harish-Chandra isomorphism (`TauCeti.harishChandraIso`), Verma central characters with `χ_λ = χ_μ ↔ μ ∈ W·λ`, and the Casimir. For a Lie group representation on a normed space, Tau Ceti has the smooth vectors and the derived action of the Lie algebra on them, and for `Z × S` with `Z` abelian the comparison `Z(U(Z × S)) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ Z(U(S))`. The rest is specified by merged roadmaps: the reductive (e.g. `gl_n`) Harish-Chandra isomorphism in LieHighestWeight §9.2, and `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` for representations of real reductive groups, with smooth vectors and `(𝔤,K)`-modules, in [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §§2.1, 2.2, 2.7. Not implemented beyond §2.7's smooth vectors and the §9.3 product comparison.

## Mathlib
- Has: `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra`, `Subalgebra.center`, `killingForm`, `LieAlgebra.IsKilling`, Cartan subalgebras and `rootSystem`, Weyl group of a root pairing.
- Missing: Harish-Chandra isomorphism, central characters, Casimir, smooth vectors of Lie group representations.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.centralCharacterModules (χ : Subalgebra.center R A →ₐ[R] R)` (`Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/CentralCharacter/Basic.lean`): Def 5.2(1) verbatim for `A = U(𝔤)`.
- `TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.centralCharacter` with `representation_eq_centralCharacter_smul`, `centerEigenspace_centralCharacter`, `centralCharacter_congr` (`Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/CentralCharacter.lean`): the central character `Z(U(L)) →ₐ[K] K` of a finite-dimensional irreducible Lie module over an algebraically closed field, for any Lie algebra `L`; built on `TauCeti.centerToEnd : Z(A) →ₐ[k] End_A(M)` for any module over any algebra (`RingTheory/Semisimple/CentralCharacter.lean`), which is the real packaging `Z(U_ℝ(𝔤)) → End_{U(𝔤)}(V)`.
- `TauCeti.harishChandraIso`, `harishChandraLinearIso` (`Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/CentralCharacter/Isomorphism.lean`), `vermaCentralCharacter`, `vermaCentralCharacter_eq_iff` (dot-orbit criterion), `range_hcProjection` (`.../Surjective.lean`), `exists_isDominantIntegral_eq_vermaCentralCharacter`: Theorem 5.1 for Killing (semisimple) `L`, split Cartan, char 0.
- `TauCeti.casimirElement`, `casimirElement_mem_center` (`Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/Casimir.lean`), `vermaCentralCharacter_casimirElement`, `casimirScalar` (`Algebra/Lie/HighestWeight/Casimir.lean`): Def 5.4 for the Killing form.
- `gl_n`: trace-form Casimir `glCasimir` (`Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/Casimir.lean`), `glIrreducible`, reductive structure and `centralWeight` (`Algebra/Lie/Reductive/Basic.lean`, `Algebra/Lie/Weights/Central.lean`). `U(𝔤)` as left-invariant differential operators on smooth functions on a Lie group, `TauCeti.envelopingAction` (`Geometry/Lie/EnvelopingAlgebra/Basic.lean`).
- Product with an abelian factor (LieHighestWeight §9.3): `TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.productEnveloping : U(Z × S) ≃ₐ Sym(Z) ⊗ U(S)` for abelian `Z`, and `productCenterEquiv : Z(U(Z × S)) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ Z(U(S))` (`Algebra/Lie/UniversalEnveloping/ProductCenter.lean`), on `Subalgebra.center_tensorProduct_eq_range_of_free` (`Algebra/Algebra/Subalgebra/Centralizer.lean`). The transport along `L ≃ z(L) × [L, L]` and the reductive Harish-Chandra isomorphism of §9.2 are not in code.
- Smooth vectors (AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.7, first part): `ContRepresentation.IsLieSmoothVector`, `lieSmoothVectors`, `lieDerivative`, and the derived representation `lieSmoothVectorsDerivedAction` of the left-invariant derivations on the smooth vectors, a Lie algebra homomorphism (`lieDerivative_lie`), for a continuous representation of a real Lie group on a normed space (`RepresentationTheory/Continuous/LieSmoothVector.lean`). The induced action of `U(𝔤)` and of its centre on smooth vectors is not packaged.
- Relative Lie algebra cochains `C^•(𝔤, 𝔨; M)` and `(𝔤, K)`-cochain complexes `TauCeti.LieModule.Cohomology.equivariantRelativeCochainComplex` (`Algebra/Lie/Cohomology/Relative.lean`, ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.5); cohomology, not `(𝔤,K)`-modules.
- Not present: reductive `Z(U(𝔤)) ≅ S(𝔥)^W` (gl_n), `(𝔤,K)`-modules and admissibility (grep `HasInfinitesimalCharacter|gKModule|HarishChandraModule` empty).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `U(𝔤)`, centre, Killing form, root systems: Mathlib.
2. Central-character module predicate, HC isomorphism (semisimple, alg. closed), Casimir: Tau Ceti.
3. Descent from `𝔤₀ ⊗ ℂ` with `ℂ` (alg. closed, char 0) is fine; reductive HC iso `Z(U(𝔷 ⊕ 𝔤')) ≅ S(𝔷) ⊗ Z(U(𝔤'))`: merged LieHighestWeight §9.2 (not implemented; its inputs `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.mapEquiv`, `prodEquivTensor`, `symmetricAlgebraEquiv`, `harishChandraIso`, `lieChevalleyRestrictionIso` and the §9.3 product comparison `productCenterEquiv` are in code).
4. Lie algebra of a Lie group: LieGroups Layers 0–5 (merged, partial; Layer 5 has `U(𝔤)` acting on smooth functions only). Smooth vectors of continuous representations and the derived Lie algebra action on them: Tau Ceti (`lieSmoothVectors`, `lieSmoothVectorsDerivedAction`); the rest of AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.7 (smooth Fréchet representations, globalization): merged, not implemented.
5. `(𝔤,K)`-modules, admissibility, `HasInfinitesimalCharacter`: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §§2.1–2.2 (merged).
6. Langlands-parameter compatibility (Remark 2): AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.5 (merged).
