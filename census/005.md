# 5. Infinitesimal character (and Casimir eigenvalue) of a representation of a real reductive group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. The algebraic core is in Tau Ceti for semisimple Lie algebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0: modules with prescribed central character (`TauCeti.centralCharacterModules`), the central character of a finite-dimensional irreducible Lie module (`TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.centralCharacter`), the Harish-Chandra isomorphism (`TauCeti.harishChandraIso`), Verma central characters with `χ_λ = χ_μ ↔ μ ∈ W·λ`, and the Casimir. For a Lie group representation on a normed space, Tau Ceti has the smooth vectors and the derived action of the Lie algebra on them, and for `Z × S` with `Z` abelian the comparison `Z(U(Z × S)) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ Z(U(S))` and the Harish-Chandra isomorphism `Z(U(Z × S)) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ S(H)^{W·}`; over any commutative ring, the action of `Z(U(L))` on a Lie module, generalized `χ`-finiteness, the dual character and `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` for Lie modules. The rest is specified by merged roadmaps: the reductive (e.g. `gl_n`) Harish-Chandra isomorphism in LieHighestWeight §9.2, and `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` for representations of real reductive groups, with smooth vectors and `(𝔤,K)`-modules, in [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §§2.1, 2.2, 2.7. Implemented: §2.7's smooth vectors, the `U(𝔤)`-action on them, their word and Sobolev seminorms and the metrizable locally convex topology they define, smoothness of `G`-finite vectors, Gårding smoothing and density of smooth vectors, §2.1's `K`-finite vectors, Banach isotypic projectors and the decomposition of the `G`-finite vectors as the direct sum of their ranges, the algebraic prefix of §2.2 (generalized infinitesimal characters, the dual character, `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` on Lie modules), and the Harish-Chandra isomorphism for `Z × S` with the PBW-filtration compatibility of `U(Z × S) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ U(S)`; the reductive §9.2 isomorphism, `(𝔤,K)`-modules and `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` for group representations are not.
