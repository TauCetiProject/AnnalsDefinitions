# 5. Infinitesimal character (and Casimir eigenvalue) of a representation of a real reductive group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. The algebraic core is in Tau Ceti for semisimple Lie algebras over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0: modules with prescribed central character (`TauCeti.centralCharacterModules`), the central character of a finite-dimensional irreducible Lie module (`TauCeti.UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra.centralCharacter`), the Harish-Chandra isomorphism (`TauCeti.harishChandraIso`), Verma central characters with `χ_λ = χ_μ ↔ μ ∈ W·λ`, and the Casimir. For a Lie group representation on a normed space, Tau Ceti has the smooth vectors and the derived action of the Lie algebra on them, for `Z × S` with `Z` abelian the comparison `Z(U(Z × S)) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ Z(U(S))` and the Harish-Chandra isomorphism `Z(U(Z × S)) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ S(H)^{W·}`, and for every finite-dimensional reductive `L` the Harish-Chandra isomorphism `Z(U(L)) ≃ Sym(z(L)) ⊗ S(H)^{W·}`; over any commutative ring, the action of `Z(U(L))` on a Lie module, generalized `χ`-finiteness, the dual character and `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` for Lie modules, which apply to the smooth vectors of a continuous representation of a real Lie group as a Lie module over `R ⊗[ℝ] Lie(G)` (`ContRepresentation.hasInfinitesimalCharacter_lieSmoothVectors_iff`). The rest is specified by merged roadmaps: `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` for representations of real reductive groups, with smooth vectors and `(𝔤,K)`-modules, in [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §§2.1, 2.2, 2.7. Implemented: §2.7's smooth vectors, the `U(𝔤)`-action on them, their word and Sobolev seminorms with basis independence and the metrizable locally convex topology they define, independent of the basis, on which each group element acts continuously, smoothness of `G`-finite vectors, Gårding smoothing and density of smooth vectors, §2.1's `K`-finite vectors, Banach isotypic projectors and the decomposition of the `G`-finite vectors as the direct sum of their ranges, §2.1's algebraic `(𝔤,K)`-modules (`TauCeti.GKModule`) with morphisms, contragredients, isotypic components, multiplicities and admissibility, the algebraic prefix of §2.2 (generalized infinitesimal characters, the dual character, `HasInfinitesimalCharacter` on Lie modules, with the Jordan-block Check) and its form for smooth vectors, the Harish-Chandra isomorphism for `Z × S` with the PBW-filtration compatibility of `U(Z × S) ≃ Sym(Z) ⊗ U(S)`, and the reductive splittings `U(L) ≃ Sym(z(L)) ⊗ U([L, L])`, with its PBW-filtration compatibility, and `Z(U(L)) ≃ Sym(z(L)) ⊗ Z(U([L, L]))` of §9.2, and the reductive Harish-Chandra isomorphism `Z(U(L)) ≃ Sym(z(L)) ⊗ S(H)^{W·}` of §9.2 (`TauCeti.reductiveHarishChandraIso`) with central characters of arbitrary central weight; infinitesimal characters of `(𝔤,K)`-modules beyond the Lie-module predicate, existence for irreducible admissible representations (open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/7424, for `(𝔤, K)`-modules) and the identification of every character of `Z(U(𝔤))` with a point of `𝔥*/W` for reductive `𝔤` are not (that two `reductiveCentralCharacter`s agree exactly on one dot orbit is `TauCeti.reductiveCentralCharacter_eq_iff`; that every character is one of them is not stated).
