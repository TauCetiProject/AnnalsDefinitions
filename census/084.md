# 84. Selmer sections and the localisation map

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1739 "GaloisSectionsPadicPeriodMaps: roadmap" (the Betts–Stix paper itself) specifies it. §0.1 gives restriction of sections to decomposition groups (Prop. 84.1), §0.2 injectivity of the `p`-adic section map (Prop. 84.2) and §0.3 Selmer sections `Sec^Sel(X/K)` with `loc_u`, at the generality of geometrically connected separated finite-type `X`. Section classes and the point-section map are §7.1 of open PR #1021. The étale fundamental group itself is a merged target (EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2) and is implemented as far as this definition needs.

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups with Krull topology, continuous homomorphisms, Galois categories (`CategoryTheory.PreGaloisCategory`, `GaloisCategory`, `FiberFunctor`, automorphism group of a fibre functor as a profinite group), finite étale morphisms, completions of number fields.
- Missing: `π₁^ét(X, x̄)` of a scheme as a Galois-category fundamental group, the fundamental exact sequence, section sets.

## Tau Ceti code
- Étale fundamental group (EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2): `PreGaloisCategory (FiniteEtaleScheme S)` (`AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/Quotient.lean`); `FiberFunctor (fiber x)` and `GaloisCategory (FiniteEtaleScheme S)` for connected `S`, with any two geometric points joined by a path (`FiniteEtale/Galois/Category.lean`); `π₁(T,x)` as the profinite group `Aut (FiniteEtaleScheme.fiber x)`, functoriality `mapAutFiber`/`mapFiberIso` and change of basepoint `mapAutFiber_conjAut` (`Galois/Functoriality.lean`); paths on disconnected bases (`Galois/Component.lean`); `galContinuousMulEquivAutFiber : Gal(Ω/K) ≃ₜ* π₁(Spec K,x)` (`Galois/FundamentalGroup.lean`). There is no separately named `π₁` definition.
- Galois-category infrastructure (`CategoryTheory/Galois/`: `FiberFunctorIso`, `Topology`, `ContAction`, `Connected`), finite étale carrier `TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme` (`AlgebraicGeometry/FiniteEtale/`), places and completions of number fields (`NumberTheory/NumberField/Global/Places/Completion`), Abel–Jacobi point separation `abelJacobiMap_app_injective_of_genus_pos`.
- Missing: the homotopy exact sequence, `Sec(X/k)`, the Kummer map for Jacobians over `p`-adic fields, Selmer sections.

## Merged roadmaps
- EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2 "Finite étale Galois categories and the étale fundamental groupoid": `π₁` as the profinite automorphism group of the fibre functor, paths, basepoint conjugation. In code as far as this definition needs: the Galois category, `π₁` as `Aut` of the fibre functor, paths, functoriality and `π₁(Spec K) ≅ Gal`. Only lisse monodromy (`lisseMonodromyEquiv`, needs §F.0.1) and `fieldSheavesEquiv` remain. STATUS (2026-10-23) still lists `π₁` and paths as missing and is stale.
- No merged roadmap has the fundamental exact sequence, Galois sections or the Selmer condition.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #1021 "Anabelian geometry and nonabelian Chabauty", §7.1 "Sections and the section conjecture": `Sect(X/k)` as conjugacy classes of continuous sections of `π₁(X) → Γ_k`, the bijection with `H¹(Γ_k, Δ)`, functoriality, the point-section map `κ`, its injectivity criteria, and the section conjecture as a proposition. §1.1 covers arithmetic paths and endpoint sections.
- #1739 "GaloisSectionsPadicPeriodMaps: roadmap": §0.1 restriction `res_u` (84.1), §0.2 local injectivity over `p`-adic fields (84.2), §0.3 "Selmer sections and the localisation maps" (84.3, with all places including archimedean, and `loc_u` only at finite places, as in Remark 2), §§0.4–0.5 the finite descent locus and Harari–Stix (Remark 2), §9.4 Theorem A.
- #850 "Inverse Galois theory and arithmetic fundamental groups", §IG.1: fundamental exact sequence and (§IG.1.3) base-extension invariance of geometric `π₁` in characteristic zero, consumed by #1739 §0.1.
- #1811 "Roadmap: local anabelian geometry": related, not needed.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Galois groups, Galois categories, completions: Mathlib.
2. Finite étale carrier and Galois-category API: Tau Ceti.
3. `π₁^ét(X, x̄)` profinite, paths: target EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2 (merged; done in Tau Ceti as far as needed here, only lisse monodromy and field sheaves remain).
4. `Sec(X/k)`, `κ_{X/k}`: target PR #1021 §7.1 (and §1.1).
5. Fundamental exact sequence and invariance of geometric `π₁` under extension of algebraically closed fields of char 0 (SGA 1 X): target PR #850 "Inverse Galois theory and arithmetic fundamental groups" §IG.1 / §IG.1.3. Restriction `res_u` (84.1): target PR #1739 §0.1.
6. Kummer map injectivity / local injectivity of `κ` at finite places: target PR #1739 §0.2. It needs Jacobians: JacobianChallenge (merged, partial; the Jacobian scheme is blocked on SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 Hilbert/Quot, not started, through Layer D's Hilbert quotients).
7. `Sec^Sel`, `loc_u`: target PR #1739 §0.3.

## Library-scale gap
None beyond the PRs. The chain is long: it runs through the étale fundamental group, the comparison of `π₁` across algebraically closed base fields (SGA 1 X), Jacobians, and the Kummer theory of abelian varieties over `p`-adic fields (Stix, *Rational points and arithmetic of fundamental groups*, LNM 2054).
