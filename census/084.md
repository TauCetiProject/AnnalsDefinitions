# 84. Selmer sections and the localisation map

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1739 "GaloisSectionsPadicPeriodMaps: roadmap" (the Betts–Stix paper itself) specifies it. §0.1 gives restriction of sections to decomposition groups (Prop. 84.1), §0.2 injectivity of the `p`-adic section map (Prop. 84.2) and §0.3 Selmer sections `Sec^Sel(X/K)` with `loc_u`, at the generality of geometrically connected separated finite-type `X`; the total localisation of Remark 2, with component classes at real places, is not a target. Section classes `Sect(X/k)`, the point-section map `κ_X`, restriction along finite separable extensions and the injectivity of `κ_X` for subvarieties of abelian varieties are merged [AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty/README.md) §7.1 (not implemented); it has no restriction to decomposition groups and no Selmer condition on sections. The étale fundamental group itself is a merged target (EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2, `EtaleFundamentalGroup`) and is implemented as far as this definition needs, as `TauCeti.FiniteEtaleScheme.EtaleFundamentalGroup` with étale paths `EtalePath`; the fundamental exact sequence and the section `s_a` of a rational point are merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.A2 (not implemented), and the invariance of geometric `π₁` under algebraically closed base extension in characteristic zero, which PR #1739 §0.1 consumes, is merged InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups §IG.1.3 (not implemented).

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups with Krull topology, continuous homomorphisms, Galois categories (`CategoryTheory.PreGaloisCategory`, `GaloisCategory`, `FiberFunctor`, automorphism group of a fibre functor as a profinite group), finite étale morphisms, completions of number fields.
- Missing: `π₁^ét(X, x̄)` of a scheme as a Galois-category fundamental group, the fundamental exact sequence, section sets.
