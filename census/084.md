# 84. Selmer sections and the localisation map

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1739 "GaloisSectionsPadicPeriodMaps: roadmap" (the Betts–Stix paper itself) specifies it. §0.1 gives restriction of sections to decomposition groups (Prop. 84.1), §0.2 injectivity of the `p`-adic section map (Prop. 84.2) and §0.3 Selmer sections `Sec^Sel(X/K)` with `loc_u`, at the generality of geometrically connected separated finite-type `X`; the total localisation of Remark 2, with component classes at real places, is not a target. Section classes `Sect(X/k)`, the point-section map `κ_X`, restriction along finite separable extensions and the injectivity of `κ_X` for subvarieties of abelian varieties are merged [AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty/README.md) §7.1 (not implemented); it has no restriction to decomposition groups and no Selmer condition on sections. The étale fundamental group itself is a merged target (EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2, `EtaleFundamentalGroup`) and is implemented as far as this definition needs, without the name; the fundamental exact sequence and the section `s_a` of a rational point are merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.A2 (not implemented), and the invariance of geometric `π₁` under algebraically closed base extension in characteristic zero, which PR #1739 §0.1 consumes, is merged InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups §IG.1.3 (not implemented).

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups with Krull topology, continuous homomorphisms, Galois categories (`CategoryTheory.PreGaloisCategory`, `GaloisCategory`, `FiberFunctor`, automorphism group of a fibre functor as a profinite group), finite étale morphisms, completions of number fields.
- Missing: `π₁^ét(X, x̄)` of a scheme as a Galois-category fundamental group, the fundamental exact sequence, section sets.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Galois groups, Galois categories, completions: Mathlib.
2. Finite étale carrier and Galois-category API: Tau Ceti.
3. `π₁^ét(X, x̄)` profinite, paths: target EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.2 (merged; done in Tau Ceti as far as needed here, without the name `EtaleFundamentalGroup`).
4. `Sec(X/k)`, `κ_{X/k}`: target of merged AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty §7.1 (and §1.1), not implemented.
5. Fundamental exact sequence: target in merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.0.A2 (not implemented). Invariance of geometric `π₁` under extension of algebraically closed fields of char 0 (SGA 1 X): target of merged InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups §IG.1.3 (not implemented). Restriction `res_u` (84.1): target PR #1739 §0.1.
6. Kummer map injectivity / local injectivity of `κ` at finite places: target PR #1739 §0.2, through the field-general injectivity criterion of merged AnabelianGeometryAndNonabelianChabauty §7.1 ("Injectivity of the section map") applied to the Abel–Jacobi immersion of the curve. The immersion is merged JacobianChallenge Layer F: §F.1 `abelJacobi` (*Needs* D.5, D.8, E.4, E.6) and §F.2 `isClosedImmersion_abelJacobi` (*Needs* B.2, B.4, D.6, D.8, F.1), not implemented; both wait on the Jacobian scheme §D.8, which is blocked on SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 Hilbert/Quot (not started) through §D.3's Hilbert quotients. Injectivity on points is in code (`abelJacobiMap_app_injective_of_genus_pos`).
7. `Sec^Sel`, `loc_u`: target PR #1739 §0.3. The total localisation of Remark 2 (with the component class at real places) has no target; its carrier `X(A_K)_•` is PR #1739 §0.4.

None beyond the PRs.
