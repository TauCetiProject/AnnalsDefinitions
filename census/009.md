# 9. Height zero characters of a block

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII/README.md) (PR #2411) specifies the definition: §6.3 defines the height `h(χ) = ν(χ(1)) − (a − d)`, the defect `d(χ)` and `Irr₀(B)`, with independence of the defect group, `Irr₀(B) ≠ ∅` and the change of system, place and level; §6.2 proves Brauer's Theorem 9.1 (`brauer_height`: `p^{a−d} ∣ χ(1)`, minimum attained), on central characters of blocks, Osima's formula and defect classes (§6.1); §6.4 treats the Brauer correspondent and heights in special blocks; §9.1 and §9.2 state Brauer's height zero and the Alperin–McKay properties as propositions. It consumes `Irr(B)`, defect groups and Brauer correspondents from merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (§§4.2, 6.2, 7.1). Not implemented; of the block theory below it the block idempotents with their lifting to `O[G]` (#2) and the relative traces and Brauer homomorphism (#3) are in code. Depends on #2 and #3.

## Tau Ceti code
- None for heights, `Irr(B)` or defect groups. Block idempotents `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent` and the bijection between blocks of `O[G]` and of `k[G]` (`existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue`, `RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/{Basic,Lift}.lean`, see #2); relative traces and the Brauer homomorphism (`TauCeti.relTrace`, `TauCeti.brauerHom`, `GroupAlgebra/{RelativeTrace,BrauerProjection}.lean`, see #3). Character degrees and `irreducibleCharacters` exist (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Table.lean`); degree divides `|G|` is `Representation.finrank_dvd_card`, `FDRep.finrank_dvd_card` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/Degree.lean`), which ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.3 uses.

## Prerequisite chain
1. p-adic valuation of integers, character degrees: Mathlib / Tau Ceti.
2. `Irr(G,b)`: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §4.2 (merged; see #2).
3. Defect group `D` and `|D|` well defined up to conjugacy: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.2 (merged; definition in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3711; see #3).
4. Central characters of blocks, Osima's formula, defect classes: ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.1 (merged).
5. Brauer's Theorem 9.1 (`|G:D|_p ∣ χ(1)`, minimum attained): ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.2 (merged).
6. Height `h(χ)` and `Irr₀(G,b)`: ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.3 (merged).
7. Brauer correspondent for the local side of Alperin–McKay: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §7.1 and ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §6.4 (merged); the Alperin–McKay and height zero statements: ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII §§9.1–9.2 (merged).

## Library-scale gap
None: merged ModularCharactersAndBlocks (the inputs) and ModularCharactersAndBlocksPartII (heights) together (Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*, Ch. 3–4; Feit, *The Representation Theory of Finite Groups*, Ch. IV) are the roadmap, and what remains for the definition is implementation. Part II states the height zero conjecture as a proposition with only its classical instances (defect zero, normal defect group); the papers' proofs (reduction theorems, the classification) are beyond it.
