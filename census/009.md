# 9. Height zero characters of a block

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Heights and `Irr₀(B)` are not a target anywhere, but the inputs are targets of open PR #1775: `Irr(B)` (§4.2), defect groups and their conjugacy (§6.2), and Brauer correspondents (§7.1). Given those, Definition 9.2 is a one-line definition; Brauer's Theorem 9.1 (`|G:D|_p ∣ χ(1)` with equality attained) is not specified in PR #1775 (only the defect-zero case is). Depends on #2 and #3.

- Missing: blocks, defect groups, heights.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #1775 "RepresentationTheory: modular characters and blocks": §4.2 `Irr(B)` and `SameBlock`; §6.2 `IsDefectGroup`, `isDefectGroup_conj`, defect-zero criterion "`|G|_p ∣ χ(1)` ... then `Irr(B) = {χ}`"; §7.1 `brauer_first_main` (correspondent `B` of `N_G(D)` needed for `Irr₀(N_G(D),B)` in the Alperin–McKay statement). No heights, no `Irr₀`, no Theorem 9.1.

## Prerequisite chain
1. p-adic valuation of integers, character degrees: Mathlib / Tau Ceti.
2. `Irr(G,b)`: PR #1775 §4.2 (see #2).
3. Defect group `D` and `|D|` well defined up to conjugacy: PR #1775 §6.2 (see #3).
4. Height `h(χ) = ν_p(χ(1)) − ν_p(|G:D|)` and `Irr₀(G,b)`: unplanned (trivial given 2–3).
5. Brauer's Theorem 9.1 (`|G:D|_p ∣ χ(1)`, minimum attained; hence `h ≥ 0`, `Irr₀ ≠ ∅`): unplanned; standard via central characters and defect classes (Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*, Ch. 3), natural addition to PR #1775 §6.2.
6. Brauer correspondent for the local side of Alperin–McKay: PR #1775 §7.1.

## Library-scale gap
Small extension of PR #1775 Layer 6: heights, `Irr₀(B)`, Brauer's divisibility/min theorem, and the character-theoretic defect `d(χ)`; plus statements of Brauer's height zero and Alperin–McKay conjectures as `Prop`s. Reference: Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*, Ch. 3–4; Feit, *The Representation Theory of Finite Groups*, Ch. IV.
