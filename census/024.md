# 24. Moduli stack of stable maps M̄_{g,n}(X, β)

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1998 (new roadmap `AlgebraicGromovWittenTheory`; the merged `GromovWittenTheory` is the symplectic theory) Layer 3 defines `MbarMap g n X β` and proves it a proper DM stack of finite type, but not the openness of stability or the projective coarse moduli space of Theorem 24.5. Its Layer 2 suppliers are merged, including StableReductionPartII §§3.1–3.3, 3.11 (merged PR #1841). Merged roadmaps specify the object-level stable maps (StableReduction Layers 10–11, partly implemented) and stacks (SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.18–1.23, §4.15) but explicitly stop before the stable-map moduli stack, so no merged roadmap names the definition.

Used by #27 (virtual class); uses #16 only through #27.

## Mathlib
- No stacks in the algebraic-geometry sense, no nodal curves, no stable maps. Category-theoretic descent exists: `CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/IsStack.lean` (`Pseudofunctor.IsStack`), `IsPrestack`, `DescentData`.
- Nearest missing pieces: algebraic stacks; prestable curves.

Algebraic spaces: `Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace` with SSF §1.3 complete, and the category `Spaces.AlgSpace`.
- Stable reduction of curves, the input of the valuative properness criterion (StableReduction Layer 7, partial): `TauCeti.HasNodalReduction` with `baseChange` and `map` (preservation under further finite separable DVR extensions, `Curves/StableReduction/NodalReduction.lean`), and the genus-zero case `HasNodalReduction.exists_finrank_residueField_le_two_and_forall_of_genus_eq_zero` (`Curves/StableReduction/GenusZero.lean`); genus one and genus at least two are not done.
- Missing: `IsStableMap` (no declaration; StableReduction STATUS: "Layer 10 has prestable maps but no `IsStableMap`"), algebraic stacks, Hilbert schemes, and any moduli stack of curves or maps.
- Definition absent.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #1998 "roadmap: develop algebraic Gromov-Witten theory and quantum cohomology", adding `TauCetiRoadmap/AlgebraicGromovWittenTheory/` (distinct from the merged symplectic GromovWittenTheory): Layer 2 `Mpre g n` (smooth Artin stack of prestable curves, dimension `3g−3+n`) and `Mbar g n`; Layer 3 `MbarMap g n X β` (full substack of families with stable geometric fibres of numerical class `β`; boundedness via Hilbert schemes; separated DM of finite type; properness via StableReduction's valuative interface; universal curve, `ev_i`, `st`, gluing). Layer 3 does not state the openness of stability in families or a (projective) coarse moduli space (24.5); openness is merged StableReduction Layer 10, coarse spaces merged SSF §1.23 (Keel–Mori). Curve classes are numerical, `β ∈ NE(X) ⊂ N₁(X)_ℤ ⊂ Hom(N¹(X)_ℤ, ℤ)`, fixed in the Conventions ("Curve classes") and built in §1.1 "Integral curve classes and ample degree" (Layer 0 is the numerical state space `CH_num^*(X)_ℚ`); a variant of 24.2's `Pic(X) → ℤ`, and for the quintic these agree. Its supplier contract (README l.174) and Layer 2 (§2.2, §2.3 *Needs* l.403) consume StableReductionPartII §§3.1–3.3, 3.11, merged (PR #1841).
- #2446 "VirtualFundamentalClassPartII" Layer 9: `RelativeSpaceOfMaps` (§9.1) for a given stack `M` with a morphism to a base `𝔅` and a family of prestable curves, and its perfect relative obstruction theory (§§9.2–9.5). It takes the space of maps as given and does not construct `M̄_{g,n}(X,β)`; its "Downstream consumers" names #1998's Layer 4 as the stable-map case.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Nodal/prestable pointed curves: Tau Ceti (`AtWorstNodalOfRelativeDimensionOne`, pointed prestable families; StableReduction Layers 1, 3 partial).
2. Prestable maps, contracted components, degree, automorphism groups of geometric fibres: Tau Ceti (StableReduction Layers 10–11, partial).
3. `IsStableMap`, openness of stability: StableReduction Layer 10 (merged, not implemented; its inputs, `fiberAut` and `normalizationGenus`, are in code).
4. Stacks in groupoids, algebraic/DM stacks, properness: SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.18–1.20 (merged; `StackInGroupoids`, `ofSheaf`/`ofScheme`, 2-fibre products and DVR lifting after extension are implemented, algebraic stacks are not).
5. Hilbert/Quot/Hom schemes for boundedness: SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14 (merged, not implemented).
6. `M̄_{g,n}(X,β)` as CFG, algebraic DM, proper: PR #1998 Layers 2–3 (Layer 2 on merged StableReductionPartII §§3.1–3.3, 3.11).
7. 24.5: boundedness, finite type, DM and properness in PR #1998 §§3.2–3.3; openness of stability is merged StableReduction Layer 10 (not in PR #1998); the projective coarse moduli space is in neither (coarse spaces in general: merged SSF §1.23).

## Library-scale gap
Covered by PR #1998 once it merges, except the projective coarse moduli space of 24.5. If the definition alone were wanted earlier, the CFG of 24.4 is a small extension of StableReduction Layer 10 plus SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.18 (whose stack-level constructions are largely in code); algebraicity and properness are the real work. References: Behrend–Manin, *Stacks of stable maps and Gromov–Witten invariants*; Fulton–Pandharipande, *Notes on stable maps and quantum cohomology*.
