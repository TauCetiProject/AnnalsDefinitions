# 83. Schwartz (Hecke) algebra of an adelic reductive group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §0.3 specifies `AdelicSchwartzFunction(G)`, the rapidly decreasing group Schwartz algebra of `G(𝔸_F)` with fixed compact finite support and bi-level, its convolution, involution and density of test functions; §0.2 gives `C_c^∞(G(𝔸^∞))` as a restricted tensor product of local Hecke algebras. Its seminorms are height-weighted (`sup H(g)^r |L(u)R(v)f(g)|`) rather than Def. 83.2's bounded algebraic differential operators, and §0.3 flags the completed-tensor identification as a separate comparison: open PR #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII" §1.1 identifies the archimedean factor with Def. 83.2 (with the Sobolev description of Remark (2)), §1.9 gives the adelic LF comparison with Def. 83.4, and §4.3 the multipliers of [2021, 194(2), no. 5]. Borderline: the merged target is the definition up to these comparisons. In code: part of §0.1a (`TauCeti.LieTestFunction` with translations, inversion, involution, `smoothApproximateIdentity`, the fixed-support seminorms and the LF topology) and the adelic point groups `TauCeti.AdelicPoints`, with the change of local normalizations of `restrictedHaar` (AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.0 §0.3) the integrated operators `ContRepresentation.lieIntegratedOperator` of Lie test functions on Banach representations and the word and Sobolev seminorms `ContRepresentation.wordSeminorm`, `sobolevSeminorm` on smooth vectors (§2.7); no Schwartz space on a Lie group or adelic group.
