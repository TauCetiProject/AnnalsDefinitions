# 83. Schwartz (Hecke) algebra of an adelic reductive group

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1037 "Automorphic forms on reductive groups" §0.3 specifies `AdelicSchwartzFunction(G)`, the rapidly decreasing group Schwartz algebra with fixed compact finite support and bi-level. Open PR #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII" §1.1 identifies its archimedean factor with Def. 83.2 (bounded `Df` for all algebraic differential operators, the Nash/Aizenbud–Gourevitch Schwartz space) together with the Sobolev description of Remark (2). §1.9 of #1761 gives the adelic LF comparison and §4.3 the multiplier action of [2021, 194(2), no. 5]. No merged roadmap specifies the definition; its local factor `C_c^∞(G(F_v))` is SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.1.1 and the adelic group AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.1 (merged, not started). Nothing is implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: `SchwartzMap` on real normed spaces (`Analysis/Distribution/SchwartzSpace/Basic.lean`), `TestFunction` (`Analysis/Distribution/TestFunction.lean`), Haar measures, convolution on groups (`MeasureTheory.convolution`), `LieGroup`, `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra`.
- Missing: Schwartz functions on a real algebraic group (seminorms by algebraic differential operators or by a height), adelic groups `G(𝔸_F)` of a reductive group, Hecke algebras `C_c^∞(G(𝔸^∞))`, Casselman–Wallach representations.

## Tau Ceti code
- Adelic and local points: point topology on `G(R)` for affine group schemes (`Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/`), restricted products (Completed/RestrictedProducts), compact-open integral points, `lieExp`, `Ad`, left-invariant vector fields (`Geometry/Lie/`); `U(𝔤)` as left-invariant differential operators on smooth functions, `TauCeti.envelopingAction`, `envelopingAction_injective` (`Geometry/Lie/EnvelopingAlgebra/`).
- Missing: any Schwartz space on a Lie group, Hecke/test-function algebras of `G(𝔸_F)`, multipliers.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #1037 "Automorphic forms on reductive groups": §0.1a `LieTestFunction`, §0.2 `AdelicTestFunction(G) = C_c^∞(G_∞) ⊗ C_c^{lc}(G_f)` with convolution, the restricted tensor product over `S` with `1_{G(O_v)}`, idempotents `e_J`. §0.3 `AdelicSchwartzFunction(G)` has seminorms `sup H(g)^r |L(u)R(v)f(g)|`, a fixed compact finite support and bi-level, an inductive union and a convolution algebra, with `integrable_height_rpow_neg` and Check examples on `ℝ^×` and `GL₁(𝔸_ℚ)`. It is height-based and not visibly the algebraic tensor product of Def. 83.4, and the PR flags that the completed-tensor identification is a separate comparison.
- #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII: roadmap": §1.1 "Real Schwartz and Sobolev descriptions" ("smooth functions for which Df is bounded for every algebraic differential operator D on G(ℝ)", nuclear Fréchet, the `L²` Sobolev equivalence, strict containment in `⋂_p C^p(G)` with equality iff the centre is compact, citing BPLZZ §2.3). Also §1.2 integration on moderate-growth representations (83.3(iii)), §1.9 adelic LF and finite-place compatibility (BPLZZ Def. 3.1), §4.3 "Mixed archimedean and spherical multipliers" (the `T`-multipliers, a special class of Def. 83.6), and Layer 5 isolation theorems.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Schwartz spaces on vector spaces, test functions, Haar measure, convolution: Mathlib.
2. Lie groups, `lieExp`, invariant differential operators from `U(𝔤)`: Tau Ceti (LieGroups Layers 0–2; `U(𝔤)` acting on smooth functions by left-invariant operators, LieGroups Layer 5, `TauCeti.envelopingAction`). The action of `U(𝔤)` on smooth vectors of a representation is not a LieGroups target; #1037 §0.1a covers the test-function side.
3. `G(𝔸_F) = G_∞ × G(𝔸^∞)` with Haar measures and hyperspecial `K_v`: targets in merged AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.0, AA.1, AA.3.1 (not started).
4. `C_c^∞(G(F_v))`: target in merged SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.1.1 (not started); `C_c^∞(G(𝔸^∞))`: target in PR #1037 §0.2.
5. `S(G_∞)` via algebraic differential operators (83.1–83.2): target in PR #1761 §1.1 (as a description of the real factor of #1037 §0.3).
6. Adelic `S(G(𝔸_F))`, level-`K` part, actions `R(f)` and `π(f)` (83.4–83.5): target in PR #1037 §0.3 with PR #1761 §§1.2, 1.9.
7. Multipliers (83.6): the general notion is trivial; the BPLZZ `T`-multipliers are a target in PR #1761 §4.3.

## Library-scale gap
None beyond the PRs, provided #1037 and #1761 merge together. #1761 depends on #1037's §0.3 for its carrier. One reconciliation is needed: Def. 83.4 is the *algebraic* tensor product `S(G_∞) ⊗ C_c^∞(G(𝔸^∞))`, while #1037 §0.3 defines an inductive union of fixed-support, fixed-level stages. #1761 §1.9 identifies each stage with finitely many copies of `S(G_∞)`, which gives the same space.
