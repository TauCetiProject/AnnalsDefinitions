## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Dynamical degrees are not a target anywhere; the main prerequisites (N^1(X)_ℝ, nef/ample classes, Hilbert-polynomial top intersection numbers on projective varieties) are targets only in open PR #545, and rational maps/dominance are in Mathlib. Graphs of rational maps, pull-back of numerical classes and the degree-growth theory are unplanned.

## Mathlib
- No dynamical degree, no N^1, no intersection numbers of divisors.
- Have: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.RationalMap` / `PartialMap` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/RationalMap.lean`), `Birational/{Birational,Composition,Dominant,RationalMap}.lean` (dominant rational maps and composition), `IsDominant`, projective spectrum (`ProjectiveSpectrum/`), `AlgebraicCycle/Basic.lean` (cycles, no intersection product), `Scheme.Pullback`, `IsIntegral`, `IsNormal`.
- Nearest missing pieces: Zariski closure of the graph as an integral subscheme of `X ×_K X`; top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)` of Cartier divisors; `N^1(X)_ℝ`.

## Open roadmap PRs

## Prerequisite chain
1. Normal projective variety over K, dominant rational self-map, composition/iterates: Mathlib (`Scheme.RationalMap`, `Birational/Composition`, `IsDominant`).
2. Graph Γ_f as integral closed subscheme of X×X, birational π_1, generically finite π_2 (95.1): unplanned.
3. Cartier divisors and line bundle classes: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.CartierDivisor`, `LineBundleClass`).
4. Top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)` via Hilbert polynomials (Snapper/Kleiman): target in PR #545 Layer 4D (not merged).
5. `N^1(X)_ℝ`, ample/nef/big classes: targets in PR #545 Layers 4B-4D (big: not specified there).
6. Pull-back of numerical classes along morphisms, projection formula, birational invariance: unplanned (small extension of PR #545).
7. `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (95.2), sub-multiplicativity (95.3, Dinh-Sibony/Truong/Dang), `λ_k(f)` (95.4): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "DegreeGrowthOfRationalMaps" roadmap: graphs and pull-back/push-forward of numerical classes under rational maps, mixed intersection numbers and Khovanskii-Teissier/Hodge index inequalities (log-concavity of λ_k), sub-multiplicativity of degrees, dynamical degrees and their birational invariance, examples (monomial maps, Hénon maps, birational surface maps and the Diller-Favre classification), algebraic stability. Textbooks/sources: Lazarsfeld, *Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I* ch. 1-2 (intersection numbers, N^1, nef/big); Fulton, *Intersection Theory*; Dinh-Sibony and Truong papers; Guedj's survey "Propriétés ergodiques des applications rationnelles". It would build on PR #545 (AbundanceStatement Layer 4) and sit beside the ArithmeticDynamics PRs (#1335, #1686).
