## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. The merged roadmap [DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps/README.md) (merged PR #2431) specifies the definition: the graph `Γ_f ⊆ X ×_K X` of a dominant rational map with its two projections (§1.2, 95.1), Euler-characteristic intersection numbers with real coefficients (Layer 2), the positivity package (Layer 3), the mixed degrees `deg_k(f; L, M)` and `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (`mixedDegree`, §4.1, 95.2) with their independence of the model, sub-multiplicativity (§4.4, 95.3), and `dynamicalDegree K d k f ω = lim deg_{k,ω}(f^n)^{1/n}` with existence, independence of `ω`, log-concavity and birational invariance (Layer 5, 95.4). It takes `N¹(X/K)`, nefness and Kleiman's nonnegativity from the merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII §§1.1, 1.5, 1.7 and degrees of first-Chern intersections from SchemeAndStackFoundations §§5.2–5.3. Of the roadmap, the function-field pullback and topological degree with its finiteness, the determination of a dominant map by its pullback and topological degree one as invertibility (§1.1), the graph `Γ_f` with its projections (§1.2, without birationality of `π_1`), the correspondence of a composite with its projections to the graphs (§1.3) and base change of rational maps with its dominance and graph clauses and the `dim X ≠ dim Y` case of `e(f_{K'}) = e(f)` (§1.4) are implemented; intersection numbers, mixed and dynamical degrees are not (grep `mixedDegree|dynamicalDegree` on Tau Ceti main empty). Of the suppliers, Tau Ceti has numerically trivial classes `Pic^τ(X/k)`, the numerical groups `Num(X/k)`, `Num(X/S)` and the spaces `N¹(X/k)_ℚ`, `N¹(X/k)_ℝ` (§1.1), and nef line-bundle classes (§1.5).

## Mathlib
- No dynamical degree, no N^1, no intersection numbers of divisors.
- Have: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.RationalMap` / `PartialMap` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/RationalMap.lean`), `Birational/{Birational,Composition,Dominant,RationalMap}.lean` (dominant rational maps and composition), `IsDominant`, projective spectrum (`ProjectiveSpectrum/`), `AlgebraicCycle/Basic.lean` (cycles, no intersection product), `Scheme.Pullback`, `IsIntegral`, `IsNormal`.
- Nearest missing pieces: Zariski closure of the graph as an integral subscheme of `X ×_K X`; top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)` of Cartier divisors; `N^1(X)_ℝ`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Normal projective variety over K, dominant rational self-map, composition/iterates: Mathlib (`Scheme.RationalMap`, `Birational/Composition`, `IsDominant`).
2. Graph Γ_f as integral closed subscheme of X×X, birational π_1, generically finite π_2 (95.1): DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §§1.2–1.3 (merged, partial: the graph with its projections and integrality, `Scheme.RationalMap.graph`, and base change of rational maps (§1.4), `Scheme.PartialMap.baseChange`, in code; the topological degree of §1.1 with its finiteness, `Scheme.RationalMap.topologicalDegree`, in code; the correspondence of a composite (§1.3, `Scheme.RationalMap.compositeCorrespondence`) and dominance, graph and, for `dim X ≠ dim Y`, topological degree of a base change (§1.4) in code; birationality of `π_1` and generic finiteness of `π_2` not).
3. Cartier divisors and line bundle classes: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.CartierDivisor`, `LineBundleClass`).
4. Top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)`: degrees of first-Chern intersections, SchemeAndStackFoundations §§5.2–5.3 (merged; §5.2 partial, §5.3 not started); Euler-characteristic form and real coefficients: DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps Layer 2.
5. `N^1(X)_ℝ`, nef, ample and big classes, Kleiman: AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII §§1.1, 1.5–1.7 (merged; `Pic^τ(X/k)`, `Num(X/k)`, `Num(X/S)`, `N¹_ℚ`, `N¹_ℝ` and nef line-bundle classes in code, `RealNumericalClasses`, `LineBundleClass.IsNef`; real nef classes and Kleiman not); real nef classes and approximation: DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §§3.1–3.2.
6. Pull-back along the projections of Γ_f, birational invariance: DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §§4.1–4.3.
7. `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (95.2), sub-multiplicativity (95.3), `λ_k(f)` (95.4): DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps Layers 4–5.

## Library-scale gap
Covered by the merged DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps (Lazarsfeld, *Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I* ch. 1-2; Fulton, *Intersection Theory*; Dinh–Sibony, Truong and Dang for sub-multiplicativity), on top of the merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII Layer 1 and SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 5: what remains for the definition is implementation of those sections. The paper's spectral interpretation (Banach spaces of b-divisor classes, Dang–Favre) is a downstream roadmap that DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps names and no roadmap plans.
