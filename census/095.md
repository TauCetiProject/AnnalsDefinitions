## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. The merged roadmap [DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps/README.md) (merged PR #2431) specifies the definition: the graph `Γ_f ⊆ X ×_K X` of a dominant rational map with its two projections (§1.2, 95.1), Euler-characteristic intersection numbers with real coefficients (Layer 2), the positivity package (Layer 3), the mixed degrees `deg_k(f; L, M)` and `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (`mixedDegree`, §4.1, 95.2) with their independence of the model, sub-multiplicativity (§4.4, 95.3), and `dynamicalDegree K d k f ω = lim deg_{k,ω}(f^n)^{1/n}` with existence, independence of `ω`, log-concavity and birational invariance (Layer 5, 95.4). It takes `N¹(X/K)`, nefness and Kleiman's nonnegativity from the merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII §§1.1, 1.5, 1.7 and degrees of first-Chern intersections from SchemeAndStackFoundations §§5.2–5.3. None of it is implemented (grep `mixedDegree|dynamicalDegree|IsNef` on Tau Ceti main empty); of the suppliers, Tau Ceti has numerically trivial classes `Pic^τ(X/k)` (§1.1 in part).

## Mathlib
- No dynamical degree, no N^1, no intersection numbers of divisors.
- Have: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.RationalMap` / `PartialMap` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/RationalMap.lean`), `Birational/{Birational,Composition,Dominant,RationalMap}.lean` (dominant rational maps and composition), `IsDominant`, projective spectrum (`ProjectiveSpectrum/`), `AlgebraicCycle/Basic.lean` (cycles, no intersection product), `Scheme.Pullback`, `IsIntegral`, `IsNormal`.
- Nearest missing pieces: Zariski closure of the graph as an integral subscheme of `X ×_K X`; top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)` of Cartier divisors; `N^1(X)_ℝ`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Absent.
- Numerical triviality: `LineBundleClass.NumericallyTrivial k x a` (degree zero on every integral closed curve) and, for `X` proper over `k`, the subgroup `LineBundleClass.numericallyTrivialClasses k x` = `Pic^τ(X/k)`, with relative numerical triviality over a base (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/NumericallyTrivial.lean`); on surfaces, `NumericallyEquivalent` and `Num` with its intersection pairing (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/SurfaceIntersection.lean`). No `Num(X/k)` quotient in general dimension, no `N¹(X)_ℝ`, no nef classes. Open Tau Ceti PRs on AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII: https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2759 (`Num(X/k)`, `Num(X/S)`, §1.1), https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2584 (base change for numerical triviality), https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3225 (nef line-bundle classes over a field, §1.5), https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3531 (the curve pairing of Cartier, ℚ-Cartier and ℝ-Cartier divisors).
- Partial prerequisites: `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/Basic.lean` (intersection number `(L·C) = deg ι^*L` of a line bundle with a curve via `LineBundleClass.eulerDegree`; curves only, not top intersections on d-folds), with the projection formula `(f^*a · C') = n (a · C)` along finite flat or finite dominant maps of curves (`LineBundleClass.intersectionNumber_pullback_of_finrank_eq`, `AlgebraicGeometry/IntersectionNumber/ProjectionFormula.lean`); `AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/{Basic,DivisorClass,Pushforward,ClosedImmersion}.lean` (Chow groups, divisor classes, proper pushforward); `Scheme.CartierDivisor.toLineBundleClass`.
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45284 (DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §1.1: the pullback of function fields `Scheme.RationalMap.functionFieldMap` along a dominant rational map and the topological degree `e(f)`; finiteness, birationality and §§1.2–1.3 remain).
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3805 (DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §1.2: the graph `Γ_f` of a rational map as the scheme-theoretic image of `γ_f = (ι_V, g)`, `Scheme.RationalMap.graph`, `graphι`, `graphFst`, `graphSnd`, `toGraph`, closed immersion into `X × Y`, `isDominant_toGraph` and `isIntegral_graph` for integral Noetherian `X`, `AlgebraicGeometry/Birational/Graph.lean`; independence of the representative, properness and birationality of `π_1` and surjectivity of `π_2` remain).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Normal projective variety over K, dominant rational self-map, composition/iterates: Mathlib (`Scheme.RationalMap`, `Birational/Composition`, `IsDominant`).
2. Graph Γ_f as integral closed subscheme of X×X, birational π_1, generically finite π_2 (95.1): DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §§1.2–1.3 (merged, not implemented; the graph with its projections and integrality in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3805).
3. Cartier divisors and line bundle classes: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.CartierDivisor`, `LineBundleClass`).
4. Top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)`: degrees of first-Chern intersections, SchemeAndStackFoundations §§5.2–5.3 (merged; §5.2 partial, §5.3 not started); Euler-characteristic form and real coefficients: DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps Layer 2.
5. `N^1(X)_ℝ`, nef, ample and big classes, Kleiman: AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII §§1.1, 1.5–1.7 (merged; only `Pic^τ(X/k)` in code, open Tau Ceti PRs for `Num(X/S)` and nef classes); real nef classes and approximation: DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §§3.1–3.2.
6. Pull-back along the projections of Γ_f, birational invariance: DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps §§4.1–4.3.
7. `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (95.2), sub-multiplicativity (95.3), `λ_k(f)` (95.4): DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps Layers 4–5.

## Library-scale gap
Covered by the merged DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps (Lazarsfeld, *Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I* ch. 1-2; Fulton, *Intersection Theory*; Dinh–Sibony, Truong and Dang for sub-multiplicativity), on top of the merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII Layer 1 and SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 5: what remains for the definition is implementation of those sections. The paper's spectral interpretation (Banach spaces of b-divisor classes, Dang–Favre) is a downstream roadmap that DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps names and no roadmap plans.
