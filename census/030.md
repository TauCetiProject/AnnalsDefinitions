# 30. Theta divisor of a Jacobian

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. JacobianChallenge §E.5 "Polarizations and theta" constructs `W_{g−1} ⊂ Pic^{g−1}`, theta on `Jac X` by translation, its ampleness and the canonical principal polarization; §C.4 constructs `Hilbᵈ_{X/k} = Symᵈ X` and §D.5 the Abel maps `AJ_d` from it. Merged JacobianChallengePartII adds the theta divisor `θ_α` for an arbitrary degree-one divisor over any field (§3.2), the canonical twice-theta bundle `Θ_X` with its symmetry and relative ampleness (§§3.3, 3.11–3.13), and the relative Jacobian with its canonical principal polarization `λ_X` over a base without a degree-one bundle (§§1.2, 1.4), the forms used in the paper. Not implemented: Tau Ceti has the Picard scheme as a named representing object (`picardScheme`, §D.2) but not its representability, `Hilbᵈ`, the Jacobian or theta (STATUS to Tau Ceti b235000, 2026-11-19: Layer C lacks `Hilbᵈ`, Layer D lacks representability and the Jacobian, Layer E lacks "polarizations and theta").

Related to #80 (Néron–Tate height of `Θ`).

## Mathlib
- No Picard scheme, no Jacobian, no symmetric powers of schemes. `AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Abelian.lean` (proper geometrically integral group schemes are commutative). Divisors via `AlgebraicCycle`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Divisor-class Abel–Jacobi only: `weightedAbelJacobiClass`, `weightedAbelJacobiDivisorClass`, fixed-degree compatibility (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/WeilDivisor/AbelJacobi/`), `LineBundleClass`, `Pic⁰` as divisor classes (`SchemeWeilDivisor.classGroupPicZeroAddEquivPicZero`).
- The Picard scheme as the representing object of the relative Picard fppf sheaf (JacobianChallenge §D.2): `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.HasPicardScheme`, `picardScheme`, `picardSchemeIso`, uniqueness `picardSchemeIsoOfRepresentableBy`, the commutative group law `instGrpObjPicardScheme`, base change `picardSchemeBaseChangeIso` (`AlgebraicGeometry/PicardFunctor/Scheme.lean`). Representability (`HasPicardScheme f` for a curve) is not proved.
- Functor-level inputs of JacobianChallenge §§C.4, D.5: the degree-`d` relative effective Cartier divisor functor `relativeEffectiveCartierDegreeSubfunctor` (`AlgebraicGeometry/EffectiveCartierDivisor/Degree/Basic.lean`, representable for `d ≤ 1` via `graphDivisorDegreeOne`), the fppf Picard sheaves `relativePicardFppfSheaf`, `relativePicardDegreeZeroFppfSheaf` and the Abel map `abelJacobiMapOfDegree` on functors of points (`AlgebraicGeometry/PicardFunctor/`).
- On the way to SSF §4.12 (needed by JacobianChallenge §C.1 and SSF §4.14): the graph-closure construction `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.graphClosure` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Modification/GraphClosure.lean`) and Chow's lemma over an affine base, `exists_chowModification_of_finite_irreducibleComponents`, `exists_projectiveModification` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Chow.lean`). Generic flatness and coherence of `R^q f_*` for proper `f` are not in code.
- Riemann–Roch and the canonical degree on smooth proper curves (`WeilDivisor/Scheme/RiemannRoch/`, `Curves/Differentials.lean`).
- No representable `Pic^d_{C/K}`, no `Jac` as abelian variety, no `C^{(d)}`, no `W_d`, no theta divisor. Definition absent.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Line bundles, degree, Riemann–Roch, Serre duality on curves: Tau Ceti (JacobianChallenge Layers A–B partial; STATUS: A.3 packaging, B.1 finite-dimensional cohomology on proper schemes and B.2 the trace remain).
2. Relative cohomology and base change: JacobianChallenge §§C.1–C.2 (merged, partial: flat base change over an affine base and the algebra of semicontinuity in code; coherence of `R^q f_*` for proper `f`, which needs SSF §4.12, is not; Chow's lemma over an affine base, the first step of §4.12, is in code).
3. `C^{(d)} = Hilbᵈ`: JacobianChallenge §C.4 (merged, not started; needs only A.2 and C.2).
4. `Pic_{C/K}`, `Pic^0`, `J` as abelian variety: JacobianChallenge §§D.2–D.5, D.8 (merged, partial: Picard functors, `Pic⁰_{X/S}`, Abel maps on functors and the named `picardScheme` in code; representability needs §D.3 and SchemeAndStackFoundations §4.14, not started).
5. Abel–Jacobi `u_d`, `W_d` as image: JacobianChallenge §D.5 `AJ_d` (merged, not implemented; `W_d` for general `d` not named, only `W_{g−1}`).
6. `θ_κ`, principal polarization (Riemann's theorem): JacobianChallenge §E.5, after §§E.1, E.3, E.4 (merged, not implemented).
7. `θ_α` for a degree-one `α`, the symmetric `Θ = Δ^*(P^∨)` (the graph bundle of `λ_X`), relative `Θ`: JacobianChallengePartII §§3.2–3.3, 3.10–3.13 (merged, not implemented), on §§1.2, 1.4. Theta characteristics as a `J[2]`-torsor: not named.
