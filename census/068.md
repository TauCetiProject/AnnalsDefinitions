## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Definition 68.3 verbatim (lattice in a real Lie group, connected semisimple `𝐇/ℚ`, `p : 𝐇(ℝ)⁰ → G⁰` with compact kernel, commensurability) is the target "Arithmetic lattices in a real Lie group" of open PR #792 (AdelicAlgebraicGroups, Layer 3 reduction theory); only the `GL₂(ℝ)` special case exists in Mathlib.

## Mathlib
- `Subgroup.IsArithmetic` (`NumberTheory/ModularForms/ArithmeticSubgroups.lean:126`): a subgroup of `GL (Fin 2) ℝ` commensurable with the image of `SL(2,ℤ)`. Special case only (one group, one `ℚ`-form).
- `Subgroup.Commensurable`, `commensurator` (`GroupTheory/Commensurable.lean`); Lie groups (`Geometry/Manifold/Algebra/LieGroup`); Haar measure, `MeasureTheory.IsFundamentalDomain`, `MonoidHom.isOpenMap_of_sigmaCompact`.
- Missing: lattice in a locally compact group (discrete + finite-covolume predicate), linear algebraic groups over `ℚ` and their real points as Lie groups, `𝐇(ℤ)`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Definition absent; no lattice-in-group predicate (grep for `IsLattice`/covolume finds only unrelated notions).
- Prerequisites: Hopf-algebraic affine groups with semisimplicity (`TauCeti.semisimpleCommHopfAlgProperty`, `TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Semisimple`), point topology on `G(R)` for topological rings (`TauCeti/Topology/Algebra/PointTopology/*`), Weil restriction (`TauCeti/RingTheory/WeilRestriction`). No `𝐇(ℝ)` Lie-group structure, no arithmetic subgroups.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #792 "Adelic algebraic groups": "S-arithmetic subgroups and independence of the model" (`Reduction.IsSArithmetic S Γ`), "S-arithmetic subgroups are lattices" (Borel–Harish-Chandra), "Borel–Harish-Chandra criterion" (`Reduction.arithmetic_finite_volume_iff`), and "Arithmetic lattices in a real Lie group", which defines exactly 68.3 (Lie group with finitely many components, lattice via `IsFundamentalDomain` of finite Haar measure, `p : 𝐇(ℝ)⁰ → G⁰` compact kernel, commensurable with `Γ ∩ G⁰`), proves well-definedness, automorphism invariance and that the image is a lattice. Uses AA.1.2 "Archimedean points are a Lie group with finitely many components".
- #2026 "ArithmeticFuchsianGroups": arithmetic lattices in `PSL₂(ℝ)` via quaternion orders (Takeuchi), Margulis commensurator criterion (special case).
- #871 "Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces", #432 "Kleinian groups", #1169 "Teichmüller geometry": consumers / special cases.
- #2229 "feat: add the RealSemisimpleLieGroups roadmap": real rank of Lie algebras and Lie groups, almost-simple and compact factors, higher rank (§§1.3, 2.1), the symmetric space `G/K` with quotients `Λ\X` (Layer 5; §5.6 states its covolume results for arbitrary discrete subgroups), and complex hyperbolic space `PU(1,n)/K` in the ball model with its totally geodesic submanifolds (§5.7), which the papers' theorems use. It names PR #792 (AA.2.2–AA.2.3 and §3.4, "Arithmetic lattices in a real Lie group") as the owner of lattices, arithmetic subgroups and arithmetic lattices with their independence of choices, and leaves outside Selberg's lemma, finite generation, arithmeticity and superrigidity. Not the arithmetic-lattice definition.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Commensurability, Haar measure, fundamental domains, Lie groups: in Mathlib.
2. Affine algebraic groups over `ℚ`, semisimplicity: in Tau Ceti (ReductiveGroups, partial).
3. Point topology on `𝐇(ℝ)`: in Tau Ceti (RG2.0, partial); `𝐇(ℝ)` is a Lie group with finitely many components: target in PR #792 AA.1.2.
4. Integral models, `𝐇(ℤ)`, model independence, arithmetic subgroups: target in PR #792 AA.1.1 / Layer 3.
5. Lattice predicate in a locally compact group, quotient measures: target in PR #792 AA.2.2–AA.2.3.
6. Borel–Harish-Chandra (arithmetic subgroups of semisimple groups are lattices): target in PR #792.
7. Arithmetic lattice in a real Lie group (68.3): target in PR #792.

## Library-scale gap
Covered by #792 for the definition. The papers' theorems (superrigidity, Mostow–Vinberg arithmeticity criteria, totally geodesic submanifolds in `SO₀(n,1)` and `PU(1,n)` quotients) need real rank theory and the geometry of `SO₀(n,1)/SO(n)` and `PU(1,n)/K`, which PR #2229 specifies, and Margulis superrigidity/arithmeticity, which no roadmap plans (PR #2229 names them outside it) (Margulis, *Discrete Subgroups of Semisimple Lie Groups*; Witte Morris, *Introduction to Arithmetic Groups*); the latter would sit beside #792 and #871. Related to #1 (real rank) and #79 (minimal parabolic, real rank one).
