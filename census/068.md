## Library-scale gap
Covered by AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.3.4 for the definition. The papers' theorems (superrigidity, Mostow–Vinberg arithmeticity criteria, totally geodesic submanifolds in `SO₀(n,1)` and `PU(1,n)` quotients) need real rank theory and the geometry of `SO₀(n,1)/SO(n)` and `PU(1,n)/K`, which PR #2229 specifies, and Margulis superrigidity/arithmeticity, which no roadmap plans (PR #2229 names them outside it) (Margulis, *Discrete Subgroups of Semisimple Lie Groups*; Witte Morris, *Introduction to Arithmetic Groups*); the latter would sit beside AdelicAlgebraicGroups and #871. Related to #1 (real rank) and #79 (minimal parabolic, real rank one).
