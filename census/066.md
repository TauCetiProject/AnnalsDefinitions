## Mathlib
Has manifolds with boundary (`ModelWithCorners`, `modelWithCornersEuclideanHalfSpace`, `ModelWithCorners.boundary`/`interior` API), Riemannian metrics, Levi-Civita (defined at boundary points). No boundary second fundamental form or boundary normal coordinates.

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary is the roadmap for this theory: boundary geometry of Riemannian manifolds with boundary, convexity of the boundary and its consequences for geodesics, boundary normal coordinates, the exit time and the scattering relation (Paternain–Salo–Uhlmann, *Geometric Inverse Problems*, Ch. 3; Lee, *Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds*, Ch. 8; Uhlmann–Vasy). What remains is implementation, of which §1.1, the unit normals of §1.2, the boundary defining functions of §1.3, the pointwise `II` of §1.4 with its Hessian, defining-function and connection descriptions, the Weingarten map and curvatures of §1.5, §1.6's laws for `II`, `S` and `H` and the convexity predicates of §2.1 with their principal-curvature characterisation are done. The paper's theorems also need a geodesic X-ray transform and boundary rigidity roadmap (Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy), which it names as a future consumer.
