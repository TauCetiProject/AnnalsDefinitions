## Status

Merged [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary/README.md) specifies the whole definition for manifolds with boundary modelled on Mathlib's half-space: the second fundamental form `II` of `∂M` with def #66's sign (§1.4, 66.1), boundary defining functions and the Hessian formula (§1.3, §1.4, 66.2(a)), boundary normal coordinates `g = dr² + h_r` with `II = −½ ∂_r h_r|₀` (§4.4, 66.2(b)), second-order contact of geodesics with the boundary (§4.2, 66.2(c)), and the predicates strictly convex, convex and strictly concave at a point and everywhere with the Hessian characterisation (§§2.1–2.2, 66.3), openness and uniform bounds (§2.3, Remarks). Of these, Definition 66.1 is in Tau Ceti pointwise, and so are the first layer's carriers: §1.1 (the boundary tangent space `TauCeti.boundaryTangentSpace` with the outward/tangent/inward trichotomy), DifferentialGeometry 12.5's outward unit normal (`TauCeti.outwardUnitNormal`, `exists_halfSpaceOutwardUnitNormal`), §1.2's inward unit normal with the sign test (`TauCeti.inwardUnitNormal`), and §1.3's boundary defining functions with local and global existence, the gradient formula and the comparison `ρ' = uρ` (`TauCeti.IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn`, `exists_isBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn_univ`, `IsBoundaryDefiningFunctionOn.exists_eq_mul`); the second fundamental form `II_q` of `∂M` at a boundary point with Definition 66's sign (§1.4: `TauCeti.boundarySecondFundamentalForm`, symmetric, equal to `−⟨∇_{dι u} W, ν_out⟩` for tangent extensions `W` and to `⟨∇_{dι u} N, dι w⟩` for extensions `N` of `ν_out`, chart independent, with the Hessian formula `−Hess ρ(dι u, dι w)/|dρ_q|` for every boundary defining function `ρ`, 66.2(a)). Not in code: smoothness and naturality of `II`, the convexity predicates (66.3, grep `IsStrictlyConvexAt`: none) and boundary normal coordinates (66.2(b)). §§2.2, 4.4 also need GeometricTopology 1.1's smooth collars (existence not in code).

## Mathlib
Has manifolds with boundary (`ModelWithCorners`, `modelWithCornersEuclideanHalfSpace`, `ModelWithCorners.boundary`/`interior` API), Riemannian metrics, Levi-Civita (defined at boundary points). No boundary second fundamental form or boundary normal coordinates.

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary is the roadmap for this theory: boundary geometry of Riemannian manifolds with boundary, convexity of the boundary and its consequences for geodesics, boundary normal coordinates, the exit time and the scattering relation (Paternain–Salo–Uhlmann, *Geometric Inverse Problems*, Ch. 3; Lee, *Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds*, Ch. 8; Uhlmann–Vasy). What remains is implementation, of which §1.1, the unit normals of §1.2, the boundary defining functions of §1.3 and the pointwise `II` of §1.4 with its Hessian, defining-function and connection descriptions are done. The paper's theorems also need a geodesic X-ray transform and boundary rigidity roadmap (Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy), which it names as a future consumer.
