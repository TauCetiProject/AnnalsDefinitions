## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary/README.md) specifies the whole definition for manifolds with boundary modelled on Mathlib's half-space: the second fundamental form `II` of `∂M` with def #66's sign (§1.4, 66.1), boundary defining functions and the Hessian formula (§1.3, §1.4, 66.2(a)), boundary normal coordinates `g = dr² + h_r` with `II = −½ ∂_r h_r|₀` (§4.4, 66.2(b)), second-order contact of geodesics with the boundary (§4.2, 66.2(c)), and the predicates strictly convex, convex and strictly concave at a point and everywhere with the Hessian characterisation (§§2.1–2.2, 66.3), openness and uniform bounds (§2.3, Remarks). Only the first layer's carriers are in Tau Ceti: §1.1 (the boundary tangent space `TauCeti.boundaryTangentSpace` with the outward/tangent/inward trichotomy) and DifferentialGeometry 12.5's outward unit normal (`TauCeti.outwardUnitNormal`, `exists_halfSpaceOutwardUnitNormal`), which §1.2 consumes. Not IN-CODE: the second fundamental form of the boundary, boundary defining functions, the convexity predicates and boundary normal coordinates are absent (grep `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, `boundaryDefiningFunction`: none). §§2.2, 4.4 also need GeometricTopology 1.1's smooth collars (existence not in code).

## Mathlib
Has manifolds with boundary (`ModelWithCorners`, `modelWithCornersEuclideanHalfSpace`, `ModelWithCorners.boundary`/`interior` API), Riemannian metrics, Levi-Civita (defined at boundary points). No boundary second fundamental form or boundary normal coordinates.

## Tau Ceti code
- Boundary directions (RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §1.1): `TauCeti.boundaryTangentSpace q = range dι_q` of a manifold modelled on `𝓡∂ (n + 1)`, of dimension `n` with `dι_q` injective, the kernel of the differential of the normal coordinate of every chart, the trichotomy outward/tangent/inward, open outward and inward half-spaces with common frontier `T_q∂M`, one-sided velocities of curves, and `Diffeomorph.map_boundaryTangentSpace` (`Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/TangentSpace.lean`); `TauCeti.IsOutwardPointing` with its chart-independent criteria (`Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Orientation.lean`).
- The outward unit normal (DifferentialGeometry 12.5): `TauCeti.outwardUnitNormal q` for a Riemannian metric on a manifold modelled on `𝓡∂ (n + 1)`, with `norm_outwardUnitNormal`, `isOutwardPointing_outwardUnitNormal`, `inner_outwardUnitNormal_mfderiv_subtypeVal`, the characterization `eq_outwardUnitNormal_iff`, the normal line `orthogonalNormalSpace`, the chart formula `outwardUnitNormal_eq_normalize_mgradient`, smoothness `contMDiff_outwardUnitNormal` and the roadmap statement `exists_halfSpaceOutwardUnitNormal`, with no Hausdorff or second-countability hypothesis (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/OutwardUnitNormal.lean`). This is the `ν_out` of 66.1 and of RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §1.2.
- Flat-ambient second fundamental form and shape operator only (`TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm`/`shapeOperator`, and on manifold sources `TauCeti.manifoldSecondFundamentalForm`/`manifoldShapeOperator` in `Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/`), with the Gauss equation for Euclidean immersions (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Gauss.lean`), plus smooth normal projections along immersions into a Riemannian manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Projection.lean`).
- Other suppliers RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary pins: the Riemannian Hessian `TauCeti.Manifold.hessian` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Laplacian.lean`) with its conformal change `Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.hessian_rescale` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hessian/Conformal.lean`), the induced boundary metric `Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.restrictBoundaryTangentSpace` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary.lean`), regular sublevel sets as manifolds with boundary `TauCeti.isManifold_scalarRegularSublevel` (`Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/`), and `TauCeti.EuclideanHalfSpace.collarDiffeomorph` (`Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Basic.lean`).
- Collars: the structure `TauCeti.SmoothCollar` (a `C^k` injective local diffeomorphism `∂M × [0, 1) → M` restricting to the boundary inclusion) with `isOpenEmbedding`, `opensRange`, `diffeomorphOpensRange`, `reparam`, `isCollar` and `ofIsEmpty` (`Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Smooth.lean`); local smooth product collars `TauCeti.IsProductCollarChart.diffeomorphProd` (`Collar/Diffeomorph.lean`); topological collars (`IsCollar`/`IsCollared`, Brown's collaring theorem, `boundaryCollar`, and the collar of the boundary of a topological manifold with corners). There is no existence theorem for a global smooth collar (the module docstring of `Collar/Diffeomorph.lean` says the global theorem "still requires patching these local diffeomorphisms"; GeometricTopology STATUS lists collar existence as not begun) and no uniqueness up to isotopy. Open Tau Ceti PRs toward it: https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3001 (flow the boundary inward along an inward-pointing vector field), https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3145 (uniqueness of integral curves through boundary points), https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/43621 (shrink a smooth collar to depth ε).
- No II_{∂M}, boundary defining function, strict convexity or boundary normal coordinates (grep `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, `boundaryDefiningFunction`: none), and no open Tau Ceti PR naming RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary.

## Merged roadmaps
- [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary/README.md): Layer 1 (§1.1 tangent/inward/outward directions, §1.2 unit normals, §1.3 boundary defining functions with existence and comparison `ρ' = uρ`, §1.4 `secondFundamentalForm` `II_q(u,w) = −Hess ρ(u,w)/|dρ|` with the equivalent descriptions `⟨∇_u N, w⟩` for an extension `N` of `ν_out` and `⟨∇_u W, ν_in⟩`, symmetry, smoothness, naturality, unit ball `II = h`; §1.5 Weingarten map, principal and mean curvature; §1.6 conformal change), Layer 2 (§2.1 `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, convex, strictly concave, `IsStrictlyConvexBoundary`; §2.2 `isStrictlyConvexAt_iff_hessian_neg` for one or every defining function; §2.3 openness, uniform bound `II ≥ c h` on compact boundaries, families of metrics), Layer 3 (regular domains, collar extension and double, realisation as `{ρ̃ ≥ 0}` in a boundaryless manifold, §3.6 comparison with R5a), Layer 4 (§4.2 `(ρ∘γ)''(0) = −|dρ| II(u,u)`, tangent geodesics leave, no interior tangency; §4.3 signed distance; §4.4 boundary normal coordinates `g = dr² + h_r`, `II = −½ ∂_r h_r|₀`; §4.5 convexity functions with strictly concave level sets; §4.6 slight enlargements), Layer 5 (exit time, non-trapping, scattering relation and lens data), Layer 6 (balls, caps, hyperbolic domains, small geodesic balls, conformal metrics). This is 66.1–66.3 and the Remarks, and makes the hypotheses of Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy Theorems 1.1–1.3 expressible (Downstream consumers name future X-ray transform and boundary rigidity roadmaps). Implemented: §1.1 (`boundaryTangentSpace`, complete apart from its concrete Checks) and the existence of `ν_out` for §1.2 (DifferentialGeometry 12.5); §1.2's sign test, chart formula for arbitrary charts and naturality, and §1.3 onward are not. Suppliers: DifferentialGeometry 5.1, 12.2–12.3, 12.5 (12.5's normal in code); RiemannianGeometry R5a, R5c, R7.9; GeometricTopology 1.1, 1.3.

Not implemented. RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §3.6 compares its `II` with R5a.
- RiemannianGeometry R5c (items 1 and 3) presents a compact Riemannian manifold with boundary as the closure of a relatively compact smooth domain of a boundaryless manifold, by a collar and K5a's Seeley extension; R7.9 gives normal collars and defining functions of two-sided hypersurfaces. - GeometricCurrents G7: strict mean convexity "⟨tr B_{∂K}, ν⟩ < 0" of a compact domain as a barrier hypothesis. Mean-convex only, inside a boundaryless ambient.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md) MS10.1 (Reilly's formula) consumes the second fundamental form of the boundary with `A(X,Y) = ⟨D_Xν,Y⟩` for the outward normal (def #66's sign). ## Prerequisite chain
manifold with boundary, Riemannian metric, Hessian, induced boundary metric [Mathlib, Tau Ceti] -> boundary tangent space, inward/outward directions [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §1.1; Tau Ceti `boundaryTangentSpace`] -> outward unit normal [DifferentialGeometry 12.5; Tau Ceti `outwardUnitNormal`] -> smooth collar existence, collar extension and double [GT Layers 1.1 and 1.3, merged; `SmoothCollar` structure in Tau Ceti, existence not] -> boundary defining functions, II_{∂M}, Weingarten map (66.1, 66.2(a)) [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §§1.2–1.5] -> strictly convex / convex / strictly concave boundary, Hessian characterisation (66.3) [§§2.1–2.3] -> realisation in a boundaryless extension [Layer 3, on R5c and R7.9] -> geodesics tangent to ∂M leave (66.2(c)), boundary normal coordinates (66.2(b)) [§4.2, §4.4].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary is the roadmap for this theory: boundary geometry of Riemannian manifolds with boundary, convexity of the boundary and its consequences for geodesics, boundary normal coordinates, the exit time and the scattering relation (Paternain–Salo–Uhlmann, *Geometric Inverse Problems*, Ch. 3; Lee, *Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds*, Ch. 8; Uhlmann–Vasy). What remains is implementation, of which §1.1 and the outward unit normal are done. The paper's theorems also need a geodesic X-ray transform and boundary rigidity roadmap (Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy), which it names as a future consumer.
