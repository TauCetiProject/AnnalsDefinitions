## Status
UNPLANNED: finite-energy `H¹_loc` connections on `ℝ⁴`, admissible gauge transformations and the `π₃(G)` class are nowhere planned; the closed-manifold analogue (instanton charge `c₂` of an SU(2) bundle by Chern–Weil) is a target only in open PR #936.

## Mathlib
Nothing on connections over `ℝ⁴` with Sobolev regularity. Has `HomotopyGroup` (`Mathlib/Topology/Homotopy/HomotopyGroup.lean`) so `π₃(G)` is expressible, compact Lie groups, `unitaryGroup`, exterior algebra / `ContinuousAlternatingMap`, Lebesgue integration. Missing: `π₃` of compact Lie groups (`π₃(SU(2)) ≅ ℤ`), `𝔤`-valued forms with curvature and gauge action, Sobolev `H¹_loc`/`Ḣ¹` of `𝔤`-valued maps as a structured space.

## Tau Ceti code
Absent (no `YangMills`, instanton, finite-energy connection). Prerequisites: Euclidean `TauCeti.W1p`/`HasWeakFDerivOn` (vector-valued), principal bundles and smooth associated bundles (SBC Layers 1–2 partial), differential forms (`Geometry/Manifold/DifferentialForm`). No connections or curvature on principal bundles yet (SBC Layers 3–7 untouched per STATUS.md).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact Lie group, `π₃` as `HomotopyGroup`: in Mathlib; `π₃(G) ≅ ℤ^s` computation: unplanned (AlgebraicTopology has `π₃(S²)` examples only).
2. Euclidean `H¹_loc`, `Ḣ¹(ℝ⁴)`, `W^{1,1}_loc`, `H²_loc`: `W^{k,p}` in Tau Ceti; homogeneous `Ḣ¹` unplanned.
3. Smooth connections, curvature, gauge group: SBC B3–B4 (merged, unimplemented).
4. Finite-energy `H¹_loc` connections, admissible (`H²_loc`, `G`-valued) gauge transformations and their action (48.1): unplanned.
5. Chern–Weil integrand `⟨f∧f⟩`, characteristic number `χ(a)` (48.4(c)): Chern–Weil forms in CharacteristicClasses C4 (merged, untouched); the `ℝ⁴` integral version unplanned. `c₂` on closed 4-manifolds: PR #936.
6. Oh–Tataru good global gauge (48.2), well-definedness (48.3), topological class and triviality (48.4(a),(b)): unplanned. Shortcut per Remark 1: "gauge equivalent to an `Ḣ¹` connection" needs only items 2 and 4.

## Library-scale gap
Same roadmap as #37: low-regularity gauge theory on `ℝ⁴`: Sobolev connections and gauge transformations, Uhlenbeck's gauge fixing and removable singularities, finite-energy classification by `π₃(G)` with the Chern–Weil integral, the BPST family and Bogomol'nyi bound. Natural placement: beside FourManifolds (PR #936) Layer 5 and as the Sobolev continuation of SmoothBundlesConnections (its announced "Part II"), consuming CharacteristicClasses C4 and PDE Layer 1. References: Donaldson–Kronheimer, *The Geometry of Four-Manifolds*; Freed–Uhlenbeck, *Instantons and Four-Manifolds*; Wehrheim, *Uhlenbeck Compactness*; Oh–Tataru (arbitrary topological class paper).
