## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) specifies the definition for maps and for real flows: topological pressure `topologicalPressure T φ` by separated and spanning sets (§5.4) and `flowTopologicalPressure ϕ F` with `P_g(F) = P_{g¹}(F₁)` (§5.5), Abramov's formula `h_ν(ϕ t) = |t| h_ν(ϕ 1)` (§6.4), the variational principles of 96.3 for maps (§7.1) and flows (§7.3), and `IsEquilibriumState T φ ν`, `IsFlowEquilibriumState ϕ F ν` with their characterization as maximizers of 96.2 (§8.1, 96.4), together with existence under upper semicontinuity and invariance under coboundaries (§8.2). Hölder potentials are Mathlib's `HolderWith`. It consumes the Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy of open PRs #851 §4.2 and #1335 §§2.6-2.7. No merged roadmap specifies it. The Anosov-flow side (#91) and the Bowen-Ruelle theorem 96.5 are unplanned.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact metric space, continuous T or flow g, invariant Borel probabilities `M(T)`, `M(g)`: Mathlib (`MeasurePreserving`, `Flow`).
2. KS entropy `h_μ(T)` (#97, 97.1): target in PR #851 §4.2 and consumed by PR #1335 §2.6-2.7 (not merged).
3. Topological entropy: Mathlib `Dynamics.coverEntropy`.
4. Topological pressure of maps and flows, `P_g(F) = P_{g¹}(F₁)`: PR #2231 §§5.4-5.5; pressure as the supremum of 96.2 is the right side of its §7.1 and §7.3.
5. Variational principle with topological pressure (96.3): PR #2231 §7.1 (maps), §7.3 (flows), via Abramov's formula §6.4 and Jacobs' formula §6.2 (PR #1335 has only `h_μ ≤ h_top`).
6. Equilibrium state (96.4): PR #2231 §8.1 (maps and flows), structure in §8.2.
7. Hölder potentials: Mathlib `HolderWith`; Hölder spaces C^θ(M) on manifolds are #32.
8. Anosov flows (#91), Bowen-Ruelle uniqueness (96.5): unplanned. PR #2231 supplies the flow side (flow-invariant probabilities, flow pressure, flow equilibrium states) that 96.5 is stated in.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2231 is the general part of the theory (Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 9; Bowen-Ruelle 1975 for flows): topological pressure via separated/spanning sets, the variational principle for pressure, upper semicontinuity of entropy for expansive maps and existence of equilibrium states, for maps, amenable actions and flows, consuming PR #851's entropy API. What remains for the definition is review and merging of PRs #2231, #851 and #1335. The paper's theorems need the uniformly hyperbolic part: subshifts of finite type with Ruelle-Perron-Frobenius and Gibbs measures for Hölder potentials, uniqueness and Bowen's characterisation, then transfer via Markov partitions to Axiom A diffeomorphisms and flows (needs #91's hyperbolic theory; Bowen, *Equilibrium States and the Ergodic Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms*; Parry-Pollicott, *Zeta Functions and the Periodic Orbit Structure of Hyperbolic Dynamics*). That would sit on PR #2231 and beside PR #1838 (which already builds RPF for countable-branch systems).

Depends on #97 (KS entropy), #91 (Anosov flow), #32 (Hölder spaces).
