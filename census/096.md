## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) specifies the definition for maps and for real flows: Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy (§4.0, owned there with PR #851 §4.2's names), topological pressure `topologicalPressure T φ` by separated and spanning sets (§5.4) and `flowTopologicalPressure ϕ F` with `P_g(F) = P_{g¹}(F₁)` (§5.5), Abramov's formula `h_ν(ϕ t) = |t| h_ν(ϕ 1)` (§6.4), the variational principles of 96.3 for maps (§7.1) and flows (§7.3), and `IsEquilibriumState T φ ν`, `IsFlowEquilibriumState ϕ F ν` with their characterization as maximizers of 96.2 (§8.1, 96.4), together with existence under upper semicontinuity and invariance under coboundaries (§8.2). Its values are extended (`EReal`, `ℝ≥0∞`), and §8.1 proves agreement with Setup 96.1's finite-entropy, real-valued formulation. §§8.4–8.6 prove Bowen's uniqueness theorem for expansive maps and flows with specification and potentials with the Bowen property, the uniqueness for Hölder potentials on transitive subshifts of finite type and their suspension flows, the flow-coboundary invariance of 96.5, and the Ruelle–Perron–Frobenius theorem, so 96.5 is specified except the reduction of transitive Anosov flows to these cases, which PR #2231 names outside it and no roadmap covers. Hölder potentials are Mathlib's `HolderWith`. No merged roadmap specifies it.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact metric space, continuous T or flow g, invariant Borel probabilities `M(T)`, `M(g)`: Mathlib (`MeasurePreserving`, `Flow`).
2. KS entropy `h_μ(T)` (#97, 97.1): PR #2231 §4.0 (also stated in PRs #851 §4.2 and #1335 §§2.6-2.7, not merged).
3. Topological entropy: Mathlib `Dynamics.coverEntropy`.
4. Topological pressure of maps and flows, `P_g(F) = P_{g¹}(F₁)`: PR #2231 §§5.4-5.5; pressure as the supremum of 96.2 is the right side of its §7.1 and §7.3.
5. Variational principle with topological pressure (96.3): PR #2231 §7.1 (maps), §7.3 (flows), via Goodwyn's inequality §6.1, Abramov's formula §6.4 and Jacobs' formula §6.2.
6. Equilibrium state (96.4): PR #2231 §8.1 (maps and flows, with agreement with the finite-entropy formulation of 96.1–96.2), structure in §8.2.
7. Hölder potentials: Mathlib `HolderWith`; Hölder spaces C^θ(M) on manifolds are #32.
8. Bowen-Ruelle uniqueness (96.5): uniqueness for expansive flows with specification and the Bowen property, for suspension flows over SFTs with Hölder roof, and flow-coboundary invariance in PR #2231 §§8.4–8.6; Anosov flows (#91) and the proof that transitive Anosov flows fall under §8.5 (expansiveness, specification on mixing pieces or Markov sections, Bowen property of Hölder potentials): unplanned, outside PR #2231.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2231 is the general part of the theory and the symbolic part of the uniformly hyperbolic theory (Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 4, 8, 9; Bowen, *Equilibrium States and the Ergodic Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms*, ch. 1; Bowen-Ruelle 1975 and Franco 1977 for flows; Parry-Pollicott): Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy, topological pressure via separated/spanning sets, the variational principle, upper semicontinuity of entropy for expansive maps and existence of equilibrium states, for maps, amenable actions and flows; and Bowen's specification theorem, subshifts of finite type, suspension flows and the Ruelle-Perron-Frobenius theorem. What remains for the definition is review and merging of PR #2231. The paper's theorem 96.5 for Anosov flows further needs a uniformly hyperbolic dynamics roadmap (#91's hyperbolic theory: Anosov flows are expansive, have specification on their mixing pieces or Markov sections, and Hölder potentials have the Bowen property; Katok-Hasselblatt; Fisher-Hasselblatt), which PR #2231 names outside it as the consumer of §§8.4–8.6. PR #1838 builds RPF for countable-branch systems separately.

Depends on #97 (KS entropy), #91 (Anosov flow), #32 (Hölder spaces).
