## Library-scale gap
Merged ClassicalErgodicTheory is the general part of the theory and the symbolic part of the uniformly hyperbolic theory (Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 4, 8, 9; Bowen, *Equilibrium States and the Ergodic Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms*, ch. 1; Bowen-Ruelle 1975 and Franco 1977 for flows; Parry-Pollicott): Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy, topological pressure via separated/spanning sets, the variational principle, upper semicontinuity of entropy for expansive maps and existence of equilibrium states, for maps, amenable actions and flows; and Bowen's specification theorem, subshifts of finite type, suspension flows and the Ruelle-Perron-Frobenius theorem. PR #2441 is the smooth hyperbolic part (Katok-Hasselblatt; Fisher-Hasselblatt; Climenhaga–Thompson for weak specification) and states 96.5 for Anosov flows. Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy, the invariant probabilities and topological pressure of maps are in code. What remains for the definition is implementation of the rest of ClassicalErgodicTheory Layers 4–8 (flow-invariant probabilities §3.4, flow pressure §5.5, the variational principles §§7.1, 7.3 and equilibrium states §8.1) (and, for 96.5 for Anosov flows, review and merging of PR #2441). The merged ProbabilisticAndMetricNumberTheoryPartII builds RPF for a countable-branch system separately.

Depends on #97 (KS entropy), #91 (Anosov flow), #32 (Hölder spaces).
