## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) specifies the definition for maps and for real flows: Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy (§4.0, owned there with the names of merged GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2, which specifies `measureEntropy μ T`), topological pressure `topologicalPressure T φ` by separated and spanning sets (§5.4) and `flowTopologicalPressure ϕ F` with `P_g(F) = P_{g¹}(F₁)` (§5.5), Abramov's formula `h_ν(ϕ t) = |t| h_ν(ϕ 1)` (§6.4), the variational principles of 96.3 for maps (§7.1) and flows (§7.3), and `IsEquilibriumState T φ ν`, `IsFlowEquilibriumState ϕ F ν` with their characterization as maximizers of 96.2 (§8.1, 96.4), together with existence under upper semicontinuity and invariance under coboundaries (§8.2). Its values are extended (`EReal`, `ℝ≥0∞`), and §8.1 proves agreement with Setup 96.1's finite-entropy, real-valued formulation. §§8.4–8.6 prove Bowen's uniqueness theorem for expansive maps and flows with specification and potentials with the Bowen property, the uniqueness for Hölder potentials on transitive subshifts of finite type and their suspension flows, the flow-coboundary invariance of 96.5, and the Ruelle–Perron–Frobenius theorem, so 96.5 is specified there except the reduction of transitive Anosov flows to these cases. Open PR #2441 (HyperbolicDynamicsAndThermodynamicFormalism) supplies that reduction and states 96.5: §10.3 `existsUnique_isFlowEquilibriumState_of_isFlowBasicSet` (a Hölder potential on a basic set of a C¹ flow, in particular a transitive Anosov flow, has a unique equilibrium state, ergodic, fully supported, with the lower Gibbs bound, invariant under constants and flow coboundaries), through Bowen's criterion under weak specification (Layer 5) and the abstract hyperbolic theory of Layer 6. Hölder potentials are Mathlib's `HolderWith`. No merged roadmap specifies pressure or equilibrium states.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact metric space, continuous T or flow g, invariant Borel probabilities `M(T)`, `M(g)`: Mathlib (`MeasurePreserving`, `Flow`).
2. KS entropy `h_μ(T)` (#97, 97.1): PR #2231 §4.0; merged GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 (`measureEntropy`, not implemented); also merged ArithmeticDynamics §§2.6–2.7 (not implemented).
3. Topological entropy: Mathlib `Dynamics.coverEntropy`.
4. Topological pressure of maps and flows, `P_g(F) = P_{g¹}(F₁)`: PR #2231 §§5.4-5.5; pressure as the supremum of 96.2 is the right side of its §7.1 and §7.3.
5. Variational principle with topological pressure (96.3): PR #2231 §7.1 (maps), §7.3 (flows), via Goodwyn's inequality §6.1, Abramov's formula §6.4 and Jacobs' formula §6.2.
6. Equilibrium state (96.4): PR #2231 §8.1 (maps and flows, with agreement with the finite-entropy formulation of 96.1–96.2), structure in §8.2.
7. Hölder potentials: Mathlib `HolderWith`; Hölder spaces C^θ(M) on manifolds are #32.
8. Bowen-Ruelle uniqueness (96.5): uniqueness for expansive flows with specification and the Bowen property, for suspension flows over SFTs with Hölder roof, and flow-coboundary invariance in PR #2231 §§8.4–8.6; Anosov flows (#91) and the proof that basic sets of C¹ flows (in particular transitive Anosov flows) have expansivity, weak specification and the Bowen property of Hölder potentials, giving 96.5: PR #2441 Layers 5–8 and §10.3.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2231 is the general part of the theory and the symbolic part of the uniformly hyperbolic theory (Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 4, 8, 9; Bowen, *Equilibrium States and the Ergodic Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms*, ch. 1; Bowen-Ruelle 1975 and Franco 1977 for flows; Parry-Pollicott): Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy, topological pressure via separated/spanning sets, the variational principle, upper semicontinuity of entropy for expansive maps and existence of equilibrium states, for maps, amenable actions and flows; and Bowen's specification theorem, subshifts of finite type, suspension flows and the Ruelle-Perron-Frobenius theorem. PR #2441 is the smooth hyperbolic part (Katok-Hasselblatt; Fisher-Hasselblatt; Climenhaga–Thompson for weak specification) and states 96.5 for Anosov flows. What remains for the definition is review and merging of PR #2231 (and of PR #2441 for 96.5). The merged ProbabilisticAndMetricNumberTheoryPartII builds RPF for a countable-branch system separately.

Depends on #97 (KS entropy), #91 (Anosov flow), #32 (Hölder spaces).
