## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Pressure (as a supremum) and equilibrium states are not targets anywhere, but the Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy they are built from is specified in open PRs #851 and #1335, and topological entropy is in Mathlib; given those, 96.2 and 96.4 are one-line definitions. The Anosov-flow side (#91) and Bowen-Ruelle theory are unplanned.

## Mathlib
- No pressure, no equilibrium state, no KS entropy.
- Have: `Dynamics.coverEntropy`, `coverEntropyInf`, `netEntropy` (topological entropy, `Mathlib/Dynamics/TopologicalEntropy/*`), `MeasurePreserving`, `Ergodic`, `ProbabilityMeasure` with weak topology, `HolderWith` (`Topology/MetricSpace/Holder.lean`), `Flow`, Birkhoff sums/averages (`Dynamics/BirkhoffSum/*`).
- Nearest missing piece: `h_μ(T)` (KS entropy).

## Tau Ceti code
- Absent (no entropy of measure-preserving maps, no pressure). Quantum analogue only: `TauCeti/Analysis/CStarAlgebra/QuantumSpin/GibbsState.lean`, `Action/Gibbs.lean` (Gibbs/KMS states of matrix and quantum-spin dynamics).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Compact metric space, continuous T or flow g, invariant Borel probabilities `M(T)`, `M(g)`: Mathlib (`MeasurePreserving`, `Flow`).
2. KS entropy `h_μ(T)` (#97, 97.1): target in PR #851 §4.2 and consumed by PR #1335 §2.6-2.7 (not merged).
3. Topological entropy: Mathlib `Dynamics.coverEntropy`.
4. Pressure `P_T(φ)` as sup (96.2): unplanned (one line over 1-2).
5. Variational principle with topological pressure (96.3): unplanned (PR #1335 has only `h_μ ≤ h_top`).
6. Equilibrium state (96.4): unplanned (one line over 4).
7. Hölder potentials: Mathlib `HolderWith`; Hölder spaces C^θ(M) on manifolds are #32.
8. Anosov flows (#91), Bowen-Ruelle uniqueness (96.5): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "ThermodynamicFormalism" roadmap built on an "EntropyTheory" roadmap (#97): topological pressure via separated/spanning sets, the variational principle for pressure, upper semicontinuity of entropy for expansive maps and existence of equilibrium states, subshifts of finite type with Ruelle-Perron-Frobenius and Gibbs measures for Hölder potentials, uniqueness and Bowen's characterisation, then transfer via Markov partitions to Axiom A diffeomorphisms and flows (needs #91's hyperbolic theory). Textbooks: Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 9; Bowen, *Equilibrium States and the Ergodic Theory of Anosov Diffeomorphisms*; Parry-Pollicott, *Zeta Functions and the Periodic Orbit Structure of Hyperbolic Dynamics*. It would sit beside PR #1838 (which already builds RPF for countable-branch systems) and consume PR #851's entropy API.

Depends on #97 (KS entropy), #91 (Anosov flow), #32 (Hölder spaces).
