## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged [WeilConjectures](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/WeilConjectures/README.md) §7.12 (README l.1671) defines the Enriques condition in a characteristic-free form over any field: a smooth proper geometrically connected surface with numerically trivial canonical line bundle and `χ(O)=1`, with the API items (l.1680–1686) proving projectivity, equivalence with Schröer's numerical-canonical and `b₂ = 10` convention (Definition 19.2), invariance under field extension, and `b₁=0`, `b₂=ρ=10` including the characteristic-two nonclassical cases. Merged [EnriquesSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/EnriquesSurfaces/README.md) (l.17, l.29) takes this predicate from WeilConjectures and builds the characteristic `≠ 2` theory on it; merged [EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence/README.md) (PR #1488) Layer 0 builds the characteristic-free theory on it. Nothing is implemented. The canonical sheaf `ω_Y ≅ ⋀²Ω` is planned in merged SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.20 (`relativeDualizingModule_smooth`), not implemented.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Smooth proper schemes over `k`. In Mathlib.
2. Coherent cohomology, `χ`, `h⁰`. In Tau Ceti.
3. Canonical sheaf `ω_Y` of a smooth surface. Target of merged SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.20 (`relativeDualizingModule_smooth`, `ω ≅ ⋀^d Ω`), not implemented; Tau Ceti has `Ω¹` and exterior powers of sheaves of modules.
4. Numerical triviality on a surface. In Tau Ceti: `Chow.NumericallyEquivalent` to the trivial class (`Chow/SurfaceIntersection/Basic.lean`, SSF §§5.9–5.10).
5. `b₂` via `ℓ`-adic étale cohomology. Target in merged ECF §F.4.5 (unimplemented); the `χ(O)=1` form of WeilConjectures §7.12 avoids it in the definition, and §7.12 proves the equivalence with `b₂=10` from ECF §§F.5–F.7.
6. Enriques surface. Target in merged WeilConjectures §7.12 (l.1671, unimplemented), used by merged EnriquesSurfaces and merged EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence Layer 0.

## Library-scale gap
The predicate and its invariants are specified in merged WeilConjectures §7.12, the characteristic `≠ 2` theory in merged EnriquesSurfaces, and the characteristic-two types and `Pic^τ` in merged EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence (PR #1488). References: Cossec–Dolgachev, *Enriques Surfaces I*; Bombieri–Mumford II–III. The `b₂ = 10` form needs merged ECF; the `χ(O)=1` form of WeilConjectures §7.12 does not, which makes it the cheaper formal definition.
