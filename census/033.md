# 33. Domain of dependence / development for compressible Euler

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2442 (CompressibleEulerEquations) specifies the isentropic (barotropic) and full Euler systems with a general equation of state (§§1.1–1.3), the acoustical form of a state and its sound-cone causal curves (§§3.1–3.2), `D⁺_D(S)` inside subsets of the half-space `H_{t₀}` (§3.3, as LorentzianGeometry §7.6 of open PR #2232), uniqueness on lens-shaped domains and domains of dependence (§§6.1–6.2), `Hᵏ` local well-posedness of Euler on `ℝⁿ` and the partially periodic carriers (§7.2), developments of open data sets `S ⊆ Σ_{t₀}` (§8.1, Def 33.3 case (i)), their maximal common development, compatibility, the existence of maximal developments by Zorn, a uniqueness criterion (§8.2), existence (§8.3) and nullity of the regular boundary of a maximal development (§8.4, the nullity clause of 33.5). Its §8.2 shows that Proposition 33.4's uniqueness statement fails on a fixed Galilean background (two incompatible developments of constant counter-flowing data on a disconnected `S`), so `D_max` is unique only under its criterion. Open PR #3010 (CompressibleEulerEquationsPartII) specifies the rest: the characteristic boundary of a development and its ruling by null geodesics of the acoustical metric from the edge of the data (§2.2, the ruling clause of 33.5), characteristic data sets on `S₀ ∪ C` with the nullity, constraint and compatibility conditions (§3.2, Def 33.3 case (ii), used by Luo–Yu I), their developments, maximal common developments and maximal developments (§3.3), and local existence near the edge (§3.4).

## Library-scale gap
PR #2442 is the hyperbolic-PDE roadmap that PR #2232 names (Majda, *Compressible Fluid Flow and Systems of Conservation Laws*; Taylor, *PDE III*, Ch. 16; Christodoulou, *The Formation of Shocks in 3-Dimensional Fluids*; Eperon–Reall–Sbierski 2019 for maximal developments), on PRs #3176, #2232 and #2246. PR #3010 is its sequel: characteristic initial data on `S₀ ∪ C` with the constraint and compatibility conditions on `C`, developments of such data, acoustical eikonal functions and null foliations, the null-geodesic ruling of the characteristic boundary and centered rarefaction waves (Christodoulou–Miao, *Compressible Flow and Euler's Equations*; Luo–Yu 2023, Definition 2.13). What remains for the definition is the review and merging of PRs #3176, #2232, #2246, #2442 and #3010; none is implemented.
