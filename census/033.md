# 33. Domain of dependence / development for compressible Euler

## Status
UNPLANNED. No Lorentzian causal theory, quasilinear hyperbolic systems, or compressible Euler appear in Mathlib, Tau Ceti, any merged roadmap or any open PR.

## Mathlib
- Has: ODE/Lipschitz curve basics, `LipschitzWith`, a.e. derivatives of Lipschitz functions (Rademacher), `AddCircle`.
- Missing: Lorentzian metrics, causal/null vectors and curves, domains of dependence, Euler equations, symmetric hyperbolic systems.

## Tau Ceti code
- Nothing for causal structure or compressible fluids (grep for causal curve, domain of dependence, compressible: no hits). `Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/AlongCurve/NondegenerateMetric.lean` handles nondegenerate (pseudo-Riemannian) metrics along curves only.
- Incompressible Euler work is only in draft PR 237 (below).

## Merged roadmaps
- None. LocalVariationalFieldTheory LVF3.CauchyPhase has classical Cauchy data for Lagrangian field theories (Klein–Gordon), not causal domains of dependence. No "symmetric hyperbolic" or "Lorentzian causal" targets.

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR 1772 "Relativistic quantum field theory and perturbative renormalization" §1.2: flat (Minkowski) hyperbolic Cauchy theory and finite propagation speed for Klein–Gordon. Constant-coefficient, flat metric only; not a target for 33.
- PR 237 "Add incompressible flows and Navier–Stokes roadmap" (draft): incompressible only.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lipschitz curves, a.e. differentiability: Mathlib.
2. Lorentzian (time-oriented) metric on ℝ×Σ, causal/null vectors, causal curves: unplanned.
3. Future domain of dependence D⁺_D(S): unplanned.
4. Null hypersurfaces (Definition #51): unplanned (dependency on #51).
5. Compressible Euler system, acoustical metric, developments, Prop 33.4 uniqueness (energy estimates for quasilinear symmetric hyperbolic systems in lens-shaped domains): unplanned.
6. Local well-posedness in H^s, s > n/2 + 1 (for existence of developments): unplanned (PDE roadmap has no hyperbolic layer).

## Library-scale gap
A roadmap "HyperbolicSystems / LorentzianCausality" would need: Lorentzian causal theory on product spacetimes (causal curves, J^±, domains of dependence, Cauchy horizons; Hawking–Ellis Ch. 6, O'Neill *Semi-Riemannian Geometry* Ch. 14), and the energy method for first-order symmetric hyperbolic systems (Friedrichs; local existence, uniqueness and finite speed of propagation in H^s; Majda *Compressible Fluid Flow and Systems of Conservation Laws*, Taylor *PDE III* Ch. 16), with compressible Euler and its acoustical metric as the model instance (Christodoulou, *The Formation of Shocks in 3-Dimensional Fluids*). It would sit beside PDE (consuming Sobolev/Bochner layers) and next to LocalVariationalFieldTheory.
