# 33. Domain of dependence / development for compressible Euler

## Status
UNPLANNED. The compressible Euler equations, their states, developments (33.3), Proposition 33.4 and hence `D_max` (33.5) appear in no merged roadmap or open PR: open PR #2232 (LorentzianGeometry) names them outside it and assigns them to a hyperbolic-PDE roadmap, which does not exist. PR #2232 specifies the Lorentzian side in the generality of the definition: the acoustical metric `−c² dt² + h(dx − v dt, dx − v dt)` on `ℝ × Σ` with its time orientation by `B = ∂_t + v` (§C1.3, 33.1), `t`-parametrized locally Lipschitz causal curves, past-inextendibility and `D^+_D(S)` inside an arbitrary subset `D` of a manifold with boundary or corners for a metric continuous on `D` (§C6.6, 33.1–33.2 and Remark (1)), the self-domain property `D^+_D(S) = D` for `D = D^+_U(S)` used by condition 3 of 33.3, and Cauchy horizons of `C¹` metrics with null-geodesic generators and the characteristic boundary `∂_U D^+_U(S) ∩ {t > t₀} = H^+_U(S) ∩ {t > t₀}` on open `U` (§C6.7, the geometric content of 33.5). Since the definition's object is the development, whose existence and uniqueness are PDE, the status stays UNPLANNED.

  - §C1.3 "Acoustical metrics on product spacetimes": `acousticalForm h c v` on `ℝ × Σ` for a Riemannian `h` on `Σ`, `g = −c² dt² + h(dx − v dt, dx − v dt)`, `B = ∂_t + v`; Lorentzian where `c ≠ 0`, `g(B, B) = −c²`, `g⁻¹ = −c^{−2} B ⊗ B + h⁻¹`, slices spacelike, every causal vector has `dt(X) ≠ 0`, `∂_t + Y` causal iff `|Y − v|_h ≤ c`; a `LorentzianMetric` of class `C^k` on opens where `c > 0` and the data are `C^k`; Checks for `Σ = ℝⁿ` and for `Σ = ℝ × (ℝ/2πℤ)^{n−1}` via `Circle` and the covering by `ℝⁿ` (33.1, both of the definition's `Σ`). The fluid side (equation of state, `c = √(p′(ρ))`, states) is left to the hyperbolic-PDE roadmap.
  - §C2.2: future causal and future timelike locally Lipschitz curves; §C3.2 null hypersurfaces and the acoustical eikonal equation (#51).
  - §C6.6 "Causal curves and domains of dependence in subsets": for any `D ⊆ M` (`M` possibly with boundary or corners) with a Lorentzian form field and timelike field continuous on `D` (`TimeOrientedLorentzianFieldOn D`), future causal curves in `D` on arbitrary intervals (one-point intervals included), ending at a point, past-inextendible in `D`, and `futureDomainOfDependenceIn`, `D^+_D(S)` (33.2). Proved: `S ∩ D ⊆ D^+_D(S)`; for open `D` in a boundaryless `M` it is `D^+(S ∩ D)` of `(D, g|D)` (Remark (1)); for a `C¹` time function `t` (the acoustical `t` with `B`, wherever `c > 0` and `c`, `v` are continuous on `D`) every curve reparametrizes by `t`, and past-inextendibility is equivalent to 33.1's extension form for `t`-parametrized curves, so `D^+_D(S)` is 33.2's set; the slab statement `D^+_D(S) ∩ {t = t₀} = S ∩ D` (33.2's last sentence, with equality); the determination statement `D^+_D(S) = D` for `D = D^+_U(S)`, `U` open, `S` closed in `U` (a development's condition 3, for Proposition 33.4); invariance under coverings (for `Σ = ℝ × (ℝ/2πℤ)^{n−1}`). Check: the constant acoustical state with `S = {t = 0, x¹ ≥ 0}` has `D^+_D(S) = {t ≥ 0, x¹ ≥ (v¹ + c)t}` (Remark (2)).
  - §C6.7 "Achronal boundaries and Cauchy horizons of `C¹` metrics": push-up and `I^+(p) = int J^+(p)` for locally Lipschitz metrics; achronal causal curves are null `C^{1,1}` Filippov geodesics, classical null geodesics for `C¹` metrics (existence, not a flow); relative horizons `H^+_U(S)` on open `U` closed and achronal, ruled by achronal causal curves that are null geodesics for `C¹` `g`, issuing from `edge_U(S)` or past-inextendible; for `S` closed in a slice of a `C¹` temporal function, `edge_U(S) = ∂_{Σ_{t₀}} S` and `∂_U D^+_U(S) ∩ {t > t₀} = H^+_U(S) ∩ {t > t₀}`, null where it is a `C¹` hypersurface, containing its generators' tangents (33.5 for a domain of dependence on an open set).
  - Downstream consumers: a hyperbolic-PDE roadmap consumes §§C6.6–C6.7 and owns equations of state and sound speed, `C¹` states on non-open sets, initial and characteristic data, developments (`D = D^+_D(S)` for the acoustical metric of the solution itself), their uniqueness and maximal developments, local well-posedness, and that a hypersurface is characteristic for `□_g` iff null. Its Scope lists the compressible Euler equations, their states, developments and maximal developments as outside it.
  - Not covered by PR #2232's §C6.7 as stated: 33.5's characteristic boundary of `D_max` itself, where the metric is known only on the (possibly non-open) development; §C6.7 works on open `U`, so the hyperbolic-PDE roadmap has to relate `∂D_max` to `D^+_U(S)` for a metric on an open `U`.

1. Lipschitz curves, a.e. differentiability: Mathlib.
2. Acoustical metric on `ℝ×Σ`, its time orientation, causal/null vectors, `t`-parametrized causal curves (33.1): PR #2232 §C1.3, §§C2.1–C2.2, §C6.6 (#50).
3. Future domain of dependence `D^+_D(S)` in a subset with boundary for a metric continuous on `D` (33.2), the self-domain property: PR #2232 §C6.6.
4. Null hypersurfaces (Definition #51): PR #2232 §C3.2; Cauchy horizons and characteristic boundaries of domains of dependence on open sets for `C¹` metrics, null-geodesic generators: PR #2232 §C6.7.
5. Compressible Euler system, states, developments (33.3), Prop 33.4 uniqueness (energy estimates for quasilinear symmetric hyperbolic systems in lens-shaped domains), `D_max` and its characteristic boundary (33.5): unplanned; PR #2232 assigns them to a hyperbolic-PDE roadmap.
6. Local well-posedness in H^s, s > n/2 + 1 (for existence of developments): unplanned (PDE roadmap has no hyperbolic layer).

## Library-scale gap
Lorentzian causal theory (causal curves, J^±, domains of dependence, Cauchy horizons; Hawking–Ellis Ch. 6, O'Neill *Semi-Riemannian Geometry* Ch. 14) is PR #2232, which also specifies the acoustical metric (§C1.3), domains of dependence inside subsets with boundary for continuous metrics (§C6.6) and Cauchy horizons of `C¹` metrics (§C6.7), and names the hyperbolic-PDE roadmap as their consumer. A "HyperbolicSystems" roadmap would need, on top of it, the energy method for first-order symmetric hyperbolic systems (Friedrichs; local existence, uniqueness and finite speed of propagation in H^s; Majda *Compressible Fluid Flow and Systems of Conservation Laws*, Taylor *PDE III* Ch. 16), with compressible Euler and its acoustical metric as the model instance (Christodoulou, *The Formation of Shocks in 3-Dimensional Fluids*). It would sit beside PDE (consuming Sobolev/Bochner layers) and next to LocalVariationalFieldTheory.
