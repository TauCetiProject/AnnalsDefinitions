# 33. Domain of dependence / development for compressible Euler

## Status
ROADMAP-PR, for data of type (i) only. Open PR #2442 (CompressibleEulerEquations) specifies the isentropic (barotropic) and full Euler systems with a general equation of state (§§1.1–1.3), the acoustical form of a state and its sound-cone causal curves (§§3.1–3.2), `D⁺_D(S)` inside subsets of the half-space `H_{t₀}` (§3.3, as LorentzianGeometry §12.6 of open PR #2232), uniqueness on lens-shaped domains and domains of dependence (§§6.1–6.2), `Hᵏ` local well-posedness of Euler on `ℝⁿ` and the partially periodic carriers (§7.2), developments of open data sets `S ⊆ Σ_{t₀}` (§8.1, Def 33.3 case (i)), their maximal common development, compatibility, the existence of maximal developments by Zorn, a uniqueness criterion (§8.2), existence (§8.3) and nullity of the regular boundary of a maximal development (§8.4, the nullity clause of 33.5). Its §8.2 shows that Proposition 33.4's uniqueness statement fails on a fixed Galilean background (two incompatible developments of constant counter-flowing data on a disconnected `S`), so `D_max` is unique only under its criterion. Characteristic data (Def 33.3 case (ii), used by Luo–Yu I) and the ruling of the characteristic boundary by null geodesics from the edge of the data are left by PR #2442 to a "Compressible Euler equations, Part II", which no PR specifies. Since the open PRs cover only part of the definition, it is filed under roadmaps to write.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2442 is the hyperbolic-PDE roadmap that PR #2232 names (Majda, *Compressible Fluid Flow and Systems of Conservation Laws*; Taylor, *PDE III*, Ch. 16; Christodoulou, *The Formation of Shocks in 3-Dimensional Fluids*; Eperon–Reall–Sbierski 2019 for maximal developments), on PR #2232 and PR #2246. What it leaves out and the papers need is its named sequel, "Compressible Euler equations, Part II": characteristic initial data on `S₀ ∪ C` with the constraint and compatibility conditions on `C`, developments of such data, acoustical eikonal functions and null foliations, and the null-geodesic ruling of the characteristic boundary (Christodoulou–Miao, *Compressible Flow and Euler's Equations*; Luo–Yu 2023, Definition 2.13). It would consume PR #2442 §§3.1, 3.3, 4.1–4.4, 6.2, 7.1–7.2, 8.1–8.4 and PR #2232's geodesics and null hypersurfaces.
