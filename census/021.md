## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Open PR #2531 adds §2.9a "Cohomology on the big fppf site and fppf cohomology with supports" to SchemeAndStackFoundations: abelian sheaves on `Scheme.fppfTopology.over S` valued in `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}`, Tau Ceti's `Sheaf.relativeH'` generalized to that coefficient universe, *Scheme.fppfCohomology*, and *Scheme.fppfCohomologyWithSupport* `F T Z n := F.relativeH' i_Z n` with `H⁰_Z = ker(F(T) → F(U))`, the long exact sequence, the sequence of a triple and Zariski excision: Definition 21.1, Proposition 21.2 and 21.3(a). Open PR #1840 (PurityForFlatCohomology §0.2) specifies `RΓ_Z(X,F)` and `H^i_Z(X,F)` as the fibre of `RΓ_fppf(X,F) → RΓ_fppf(U,F)`, with the long exact sequence, excision (open and étale) and the comparisons of 21.3(b); #2531 assigns those comparisons, and the local case, to it. Open PR #2272 §2.0 defines fppf cohomology on the affine fppf site with the same universe lift. Merged SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.7 targets only the Zariski/module version. Tau Ceti has site-general relative cohomology `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.relativeH'` with the long exact sequence of a pair, which for the open immersion `U → X` is 21.1–21.2 (`H^i_Z = Ext^i(ℤ_{X,U}, F)`). It does not apply to the big fppf site: it takes sheaves valued in `AddCommGrpCat.{v}` for the morphism universe `v` of the site, which for `Scheme.{u}` is `u`, and sheafification of such sheaves on this large site is not available (it is for `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}`); PR #2531 plans the generalization.

## Mathlib
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.fppfTopology` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Fpqc.lean`).
- `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H` (Ext from the constant sheaf, coefficients in `AddCommGrpCat.{w}` with `HasSheafify J AddCommGrpCat.{w}`) and `Sheaf.H'` (cohomology of an object `U`, Ext from the free sheaf on `U`, coefficients fixed in `AddCommGrpCat.{v}` for the morphism universe `v`), in `CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Basic.lean`. On `Scheme.fppfTopology` (`Scheme.{u}`, so `v = u`) the plus-construction sheafification instance needs colimits over covering sieves of size `u+1`; it exists for `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}` and not for `AddCommGrpCat.{u}`. So `Sheaf.H` applies there only to universe-lifted sheaves, and `Sheaf.H'` not at all.
- `localCohomology` (`Algebra/Homology/LocalCohomology.lean`, colimit of Ext).
- No sections with support, and no cohomology with supports on sites.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Big fppf site and abelian sheaves: in Mathlib. Cohomology on it: Mathlib `Sheaf.H` only for sheaves valued in `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}`; `Sheaf.H'` and `Sheaf.relativeH'` need sheafification at the universe `u`, which is not available. PR #2531 §2.9a fixes the coefficients in `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}` and generalizes `relativeH'` to them; PR #2272 §2.0 uses the same universe lift on the affine site.
2. Commutative group scheme as an fppf sheaf. In Tau Ceti (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/`).
3. Zariski cohomology with supports and its comparison with `localCohomology`. Target in merged SSF §2.7; partial in Tau Ceti (topological-space supports, degree-zero comparison). Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3747 (the module-level groups and long exact sequence on schemes).
4. Fppf/étale/Zariski comparisons. Target in merged SSF §2.10 (partial: the étale-to-Nisnevich morphism of topoi only; no fppf comparison).
5. Fppf `RΓ_Z`, `H^i_Z`, and `H^i_𝔪(R,G)`. `H^i_Z` with 21.1–21.2 and Zariski excision: target of PR #2531 §2.9a, as Tau Ceti `Sheaf.relativeH'` generalized to the coefficient universe `u+1`; `RΓ_Z`, étale excision, the comparisons of 21.3(b) and the local case: PR #1840 §0.2. The site-general construction and its long exact sequence are Tau Ceti `Sheaf.relativeH'`, not usable on the big fppf site (item 1).
