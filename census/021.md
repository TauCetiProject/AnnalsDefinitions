## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Open PR #1840 (PurityForFlatCohomology §0.2) specifies `RΓ_Z(X,F)` and `H^i_Z(X,F)` on the big fppf site, with the long exact sequence and excision. Merged SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.7 targets only the Zariski/module version. Tau Ceti has cohomology with supports for sheaves on a topological space and degree-zero local cohomology of modules, but nothing on the fppf site.

## Mathlib
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.fppfTopology` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Fpqc.lean`).
- `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H` (Ext from the constant sheaf).
- `localCohomology` (`Algebra/Homology/LocalCohomology.lean`, colimit of Ext).
- No sections with support, and no cohomology with supports on sites.

## Tau Ceti code
- Fppf sheaves of group schemes and quotients (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/`).
- Punctured spectrum (`AlgebraicGeometry/PuncturedSpectrum/`).
- Zariski coherent cohomology (`AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/`).
- Cohomology with closed supports for sheaves of abelian groups and of modules on a topological space: `H_Sⁿ(W, F)` as relative cohomology `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.supportedH`, sections supported in `S`, the support long exact sequence, the long exact sequence of a pair of supports and excision (`Topology/Sheaves/SupportedCohomology.lean` and `SupportedCohomology/`); local cohomology sheaves `𝓗_Sⁿ(F)` (`supportedHSheaf`), their coefficient long exact sequence, flasque vanishing and the edge comparison (`Topology/Sheaves/LocalCohomology/`).
- Degree-zero local cohomology of modules: `localCohomology.degreeZeroToModule` is injective with image the `J`-power torsion (`localCohomology.degreeZeroToModule_injective`, `range_degreeZeroToModule`; `Algebra/Homology/LocalCohomology/DegreeZero.lean`), SSF §2.7's `localCohomology_degreeZero`.
- Missing: cohomology with supports on a site, in particular on the big fppf site; the SSF §2.7 names `sectionsWithSupport`, `cohomologyWithSupport` for `O_X`-modules.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Big fppf site, abelian sheaves, `Sheaf.H`. In Mathlib.
2. Commutative group scheme as an fppf sheaf. In Tau Ceti (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/`).
3. Zariski cohomology with supports and its comparison with `localCohomology`. Target in merged SSF §2.7; partial in Tau Ceti (topological-space supports, degree-zero comparison).
4. Fppf/étale/Zariski comparisons. Target in merged SSF §2.10 (unimplemented).
5. Fppf `RΓ_Z`, `H^i_Z`, and `H^i_𝔪(R,G)`. Target in PR #1840 §0.2.
