The compactified variant (the curves `X_K`, used in the body of the cited paper) has no target: §7.4 (l.1625) defines completed interior cohomology `im(H̃_c^n → H̃^n)`; its finite-level images are `H¹(X_K)` in degree one, but §7.4 keeps the completed image separate from the inverse limit of those images.
