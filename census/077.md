None for the open-curve definition beyond implementing CompletedCohomologyPartII on ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces, ModularCurvesPartII and the étale-cohomology stack; the compactified variant needs a tower of compactified curves `X_K` and its completed cohomology, which no roadmap specifies.
