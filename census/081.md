# 81. The prismatic site and prismatic cohomology

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [PrismaticCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PrismaticCohomology/README.md) (PR #1057, "Prismatic cohomology, Part I: delta rings, prisms and relative cohomology") specifies the definition: §0.73 maps of prisms and their (faithful) flatness (81.1), §0.79 rigidity (81.2), §0.81 bounded prisms, §1.1 the site of bounded prisms with the flat topology and the sheaves `O`, `Ō` (81.3), §1.2 the relative prismatic site `(X/A)_Δ` for every p-adic formal `X` over `A/I` (81.4(a)–(c)), §1.4 `Δ_{X/A}` and `RΓ_Δ(X/A)` (81.4(d)), §1.5 Hodge–Tate cohomology, §1.6 flat, étale, Nisnevich and Zariski independence (Remark 3), §§1.7–1.8 Čech–Alexander complexes (Remark 2) and §1.9 the Frobenius and its linearisation, with a fixed universe cutoff `κ` in its Conventions (Remark 4). Its topology is generated by finite jointly completely faithfully flat families rather than single covers, which gives the same cohomology. Merged PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2–P4.15.3, which §0.72 imports, specify δ-rings, bounded prisms, rigidity, prismatic envelopes and the affine Čech–Alexander complex. Of all this only δ-ring algebra, prisms and complete flatness (of modules and in `D(A)`) are implemented (`TauCeti.DeltaRing` with δ-maps, the integer and square-zero tests, quotients, completions, monoid algebras and Witt sections, distinguished elements, perfect δ-rings, the ring-level Nygaard filtration of PrismaticCohomologyPartII, the prism structure `TauCeti.Prism` with `Prism.IsBounded`, and `DerivedDeRham.IsCompletelyFlatModule`, `DerivedDeRham.IsCompletelyFlat`); free δ-rings, δ-localization and the direct half of rigidity are also in code; there are no maps of prisms and no prismatic site, so the definition is not in code.

## Mathlib
- Has: `WittVector`, `AdicCompletion`/`IsAdicComplete`, divided powers (`DividedPowers`), `GrothendieckTopology`, sheaves on sites, derived categories of abelian categories, `CommRingCat`, schemes.
- Missing: δ-rings, prisms, derived `I`-completeness and `I`-complete flatness, formal schemes, derived global sections of sheaves of modules on a general site as an `A`-linear object with Frobenius.

Ring-level Nygaard filtration and divided Frobenius `TauCeti.Prismatic.Nygaard.fil`, `dividedFrobenius` (`TauCeti/RingTheory/Prismatic/Nygaard.lean`, PrismaticCohomologyPartII). Distinguished elements (P4.15.2): `TauCeti.DeltaRing.IsDistinguished` with [BS22] Lemmas 2.23–2.25 (`IsDistinguished.unit_mul`, `IsDistinguished.of_dvd`, `isDistinguished_iff_natCast_mem_span_frobenius`, `RingTheory/DeltaRing/Distinguished.lean`). Prisms (P4.15.2): `TauCeti.Prism p B` with an invertible ideal, derived `(p, J)`-completeness and `p ∈ J + φ(J)B`, `Prism.IsBounded`, `Prism.ideal_le_jacobson`, `Prism.natCast_mem_jacobson` (`RingTheory/Prismatic/Prism.lean`), and perfect δ-rings `DeltaRing.IsPerfect` (`DeltaRing/Basic.lean`). Localization (§§0.22–0.28): the δ-structure `DeltaRing.localization` on a localization at a Frobenius-stable submonoid, unique (`existsUnique_isDeltaMap_algebraMap_iff`), with the lifting property `isDeltaMap_lift` (`RingTheory/DeltaRing/Localization.lean`). Rigidity of maps of prisms (P4.15.2, [BS22] Lemma 3.5, the direct half): for a `δ`-map `f` with `f(I) ⊆ J`, `I.map f = J` (`TauCeti.Prism.map_ideal_eq`), the `B`-linear isomorphism `B ⊗[A] I ≃ J` (`Prism.tensorIdealEquiv`), and two prism structures with `I ≤ J` are equal (`Prism.eq_of_ideal_le`) (`RingTheory/Prismatic/Rigidity.lean`). δ-stable ideals and the universal δ-quotient (§§0.47–0.54): `DeltaRing.forall_delta_mem_span_iff`, the δ-stabilization `deltaStabilization` with `deltaStabilization_le_iff`, `IsDeltaMap.delta_mem_ker`, the quotient instance `instDeltaRingQuotientDeltaStabilization` and its universal property `deltaStabilizationLiftEquiv` (`RingTheory/DeltaRing/Quotient.lean`). No maps of prisms as a category, converse half of Lemma 3.5, or prismatic envelopes.
- Missing: no maps of prisms, no prismatic site or cohomology.
