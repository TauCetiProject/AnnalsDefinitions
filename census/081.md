# 81. The prismatic site and prismatic cohomology

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [PrismaticCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PrismaticCohomology/README.md) (PR #1057, "Prismatic cohomology, Part I: delta rings, prisms and relative cohomology") specifies the definition: §0.73 maps of prisms and their (faithful) flatness (81.1), §0.79 rigidity (81.2), §0.81 bounded prisms, §1.1 the site of bounded prisms with the flat topology and the sheaves `O`, `Ō` (81.3), §1.2 the relative prismatic site `(X/A)_Δ` for every p-adic formal `X` over `A/I` (81.4(a)–(c)), §1.4 `Δ_{X/A}` and `RΓ_Δ(X/A)` (81.4(d)), §1.5 Hodge–Tate cohomology, §1.6 flat, étale, Nisnevich and Zariski independence (Remark 3), §§1.7–1.8 Čech–Alexander complexes (Remark 2) and §1.9 the Frobenius and its linearisation, with a fixed universe cutoff `κ` in its Conventions (Remark 4). Its topology is generated by finite jointly completely faithfully flat families rather than single covers, which gives the same cohomology. Merged PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2–P4.15.3, which §0.72 imports, specify δ-rings, bounded prisms, rigidity, prismatic envelopes and the affine Čech–Alexander complex. Of all this only the δ-ring class is implemented (`TauCeti.DeltaRing`); there are no prisms.

## Mathlib
- Has: `WittVector`, `AdicCompletion`/`IsAdicComplete`, divided powers (`DividedPowers`), `GrothendieckTopology`, sheaves on sites, derived categories of abelian categories, `CommRingCat`, schemes.
- Missing: δ-rings, prisms, derived `I`-completeness and `I`-complete flatness, formal schemes, derived global sections of sheaves of modules on a general site as an `A`-linear object with Frobenius.

## Tau Ceti code
- Perfectoid side: `RingTheory/Perfectoid/` (perfectoid rings, Fontaine `θ` in `FontaineTheta.lean`, tilts), `RingTheory/WittVector/` with `WittVector.IsDistinguished` and primitive elements (`WittVector/Primitive/Basic.lean`), formal schemes (`AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/`), Čech machinery for site cohomology (`CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Cech/`).
- Derived `I`-completeness (DerivedModuleAlgebra §1.2): `TauCeti.derivedComplete` in `D(A)`, the ideal `derivedCompleteIdeal`, radical/generator invariance, the module telescope criterion `TauCeti.IsDerivedComplete`, and the cohomology criterion `derivedComplete_iff_isDerivedComplete_homology` (`RingTheory/AdicCompletion/DerivedComplete.lean`, `.../DerivedComplete/Module.lean`). No derived completion reflector and no `I`-complete flatness.
- δ-rings (PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2): `TauCeti.DeltaRing p A` with `delta`, the addition correction `DeltaRing.addCorrection`, the Frobenius lift `DeltaRing.frobenius`, `ofFrobeniusLift` (a δ-structure from a Frobenius lift on a `p`-torsion-free ring) and the unique δ-structure on `ℤ` (`TauCeti/RingTheory/DeltaRing/Basic.lean`). No free δ-algebras, distinguished elements of δ-rings or δ-ring maps.
- Missing: no prism structure, no prismatic site or cohomology (searched `Prism`, `IsPrism`; the "prism" hits are simplicial and cubical topology).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Witt vectors, adic completion, Grothendieck topologies, derived categories: Mathlib.
2. Perfectoid rings, `θ`, distinguished Witt vectors, formal schemes: Tau Ceti.
3. Derived `I`-completion, `I`-completely (faithfully) flat complexes (81.1): target DerivedModuleAlgebra §§1.2–1.3, §1.7 (merged; derived `I`-completeness in code, completion and complete flatness not started), imported by PrismaticCohomology §0.73.
4. δ-rings, prisms, bounded prisms, rigidity (81.2): target PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2 (merged; partial: `TauCeti.DeltaRing` only) and PrismaticCohomology Layer 0, §§0.1–0.7, 0.73, 0.79, 0.81 (merged, not started beyond `TauCeti.DeltaRing`).
5. Bounded p-adic formal schemes and their sites: PrismaticCohomology §1.0 (merged, not started; needs SchemeAndStackFoundations §§4.7–4.8, DerivedDeRhamCohomology §§5.1–5.7, 5.26 and EnhancedDerivedSheaves Layers E2–E3).
6. Flat topology on bounded prisms, pushouts, `O`, `Ō` (81.3): PrismaticCohomology §1.1 (merged, not started; needs §§0.56, 0.60, 0.72–0.81, DerivedModuleAlgebra §§1.1–1.9, DerivedDeRhamCohomology §§1.10–1.23, EnhancedDerivedSheaves Layer E2).
7. `(X/A)_Δ`, `O_Δ`, `Ō_Δ`, `RΓ_Δ(X/A)`, Hodge–Tate cohomology, Frobenius (81.4): PrismaticCohomology §§1.2–1.9 (merged, not started). The affine Čech–Alexander version is also a target in PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.3 (merged).
