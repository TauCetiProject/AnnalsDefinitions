# 81. The prismatic site and prismatic cohomology

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1057 "Prismatic cohomology, Part I: delta rings, prisms and relative cohomology" specifies this exactly. Its §1.1 gives the bounded-prism site, §1.2 the relative site `(X/A)_Δ`, §1.4 `RΓ_Δ(X/A)`, §1.5 Hodge–Tate cohomology, §1.6 topology independence, §§1.7–1.8 Čech–Alexander and §1.9 Frobenius. Merged PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2–P4.15.3 already specify δ-rings, bounded prisms, rigidity, prismatic envelopes and the *affine* Čech–Alexander complex `Δ_{C/B}`, but not the site. Of these only the δ-ring class is implemented (`TauCeti.DeltaRing`); there are no prisms.

## Mathlib
- Has: `WittVector`, `AdicCompletion`/`IsAdicComplete`, divided powers (`DividedPowers`), `GrothendieckTopology`, sheaves on sites, derived categories of abelian categories, `CommRingCat`, schemes.
- Missing: δ-rings, prisms, derived `I`-completeness and `I`-complete flatness, formal schemes, derived global sections of sheaves of modules on a general site as an `A`-linear object with Frobenius.

## Tau Ceti code
- Perfectoid side: `RingTheory/Perfectoid/` (perfectoid rings, Fontaine `θ` in `FontaineTheta.lean`, tilts), `RingTheory/WittVector/` with `WittVector.IsDistinguished` and primitive elements (`WittVector/Primitive/Basic.lean`), formal schemes (`AlgebraicGeometry/FormalScheme/`), Čech machinery for site cohomology (`CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Cech/`).
- Derived `I`-completeness (DerivedModuleAlgebra §1.2): `TauCeti.derivedComplete` in `D(A)`, the ideal `derivedCompleteIdeal`, radical/generator invariance, the module telescope criterion `TauCeti.IsDerivedComplete`, and the cohomology criterion `derivedComplete_iff_isDerivedComplete_homology` (`RingTheory/AdicCompletion/DerivedComplete.lean`, `.../DerivedComplete/Module.lean`). No derived completion reflector and no `I`-complete flatness.
- δ-rings (PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2): `TauCeti.DeltaRing p A` with `delta`, the Frobenius lift `DeltaRing.frobenius`, `ofFrobeniusLift` (a δ-structure from a Frobenius lift on a `p`-torsion-free ring) and the unique δ-structure on `ℤ` (`TauCeti/RingTheory/DeltaRing/Basic.lean`). No free δ-algebras, distinguished elements of δ-rings or δ-ring maps.
- Missing: no prism structure, no prismatic site or cohomology (searched `Prism`, `IsPrism`).

## Merged roadmaps
- PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2 "universal prisms and perfection": δ-rings, free δ-algebras, distinguished elements, prisms `(B,J)`, bounded and perfect prisms, rigidity `JB' = J'`, prismatic envelopes and their complete flatness, perfect prisms vs perfectoid rings. P4.15.3: for a bounded oriented prism and p-completely smooth `C`, the Čech–Alexander `Δ_{C/B}` with Frobenius and the affine Hodge–Tate comparison. It says explicitly that "No global prismatic-cohomology output is required", so the site of Def. 81.4 is not a merged target. PerfectoidSpaces has no STATUS.md; of P4.15.2 only the δ-ring class `TauCeti.DeltaRing` is in code.
- DerivedModuleAlgebra §§1.2–1.3 and §1.7: derived `I`-completeness (cites [Prisms] §1.2), the derived completion reflector, `I`-complete flatness, used for 81.1. §1.2: the completeness predicate in `D(A)` and for modules and the cohomology criterion are in code. §1.3 and §1.7 are not started (§1.3 needs the tensor–Hom adjunction for `derivedHom`, §1.7 the derived tensor bifunctor of §0.2).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Witt vectors, adic completion, Grothendieck topologies, derived categories: Mathlib.
2. Perfectoid rings, `θ`, distinguished Witt vectors, formal schemes: Tau Ceti.
3. Derived `I`-completion, `I`-completely (faithfully) flat complexes: target DerivedModuleAlgebra §§1.2–1.3, §1.7 (merged; derived `I`-completeness in code, completion and complete flatness not started) and PR #1057 §0.71.
4. δ-rings, prisms, bounded prisms, rigidity: target PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.2 (merged; partial: `TauCeti.DeltaRing` only) and PR #1057 Layer 0 (integral extension).
5. Flat topology on bounded prisms, pushouts (81.3): target PR #1057 §1.1.
6. `(X/A)_Δ`, `O_Δ`, `Ō_Δ`, `RΓ_Δ(X/A)`, Frobenius: target PR #1057 §§1.2–1.9. The affine Čech–Alexander version is also a target in PerfectoidSpaces P4.15.3 (merged).

## Library-scale gap
None beyond the roadmapped work (Bhatt–Scholze, *Prisms and prismatic cohomology*, §§2–4). The remaining risk is the dependency web: #1057 imports DerivedDeRhamCohomology, EnhancedDerivedSheaves and adic thickening systems.
