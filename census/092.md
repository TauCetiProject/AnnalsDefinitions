## Library-scale gap
The twist-map theory around the billiard map (Birkhoff's graph theorem, rotation numbers of invariant curves, Birkhoff periodic orbits) is the merged MathematicalBilliards (Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Katok–Hasselblatt ch. 9 and 13; Herman 1983). What remains for the definition is implementation of its Layers 1–5; the roadmap's carrier `ConvexTable` is in code and maps into the Tau Ceti carrier `StrictConvexBilliardTable`, whose next-collision map, inverse, homeomorphism, generating function and invariant measure cover most of Layers 1–3 in the `(s, cos δ)` coordinates and an oriented Euclidean plane rather than `ℂ`, and its invariant graphs cover §§5.3–5.6 in graph form; with the closed-cylinder extension of §2.3 in code, the definition is in code in the graph form of 92.5; rotational invariant curves as curves (§5.1) are in code, and their rotation numbers wait on Birkhoff's graph theorem (§5.3). The paper's theorem further needs caustics, central symmetry and the Birkhoff–Poritsky argument, which MathematicalBilliards names as a downstream roadmap (Bialy–Mironov; Siburg, *The Principle of Least Action in Geometry and Dynamics*).
