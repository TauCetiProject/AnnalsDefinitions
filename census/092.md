## Prerequisite chain
1. Arclength-parametrised strictly convex C^k curve: Mathlib analysis (curve as data; no packaged notion).
2. Billiard map on the open phase cylinder, homeomorphism, area form, twist: target in merged CotangentLagrangian C3.1 (not implemented; C^∞ only).
3. Lift to ℝ×(0,π) and invariant essential circles; rotation number via `CircleDeg1Lift.translationNumber`: Mathlib supplies the rotation number; the invariant-curve notion is unplanned (small extension of C3.1).
4. Central symmetry, caustics, Birkhoff-Poritsky: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
Extending C3.1 to finite regularity C^k (k≥2) and adding the twist-map theory around it: Birkhoff's theorem that rotational invariant curves of monotone twist maps are Lipschitz graphs, rotation numbers of invariant curves, Aubry-Mather sets, caustics and the Lazutkin/KAM picture. Natural home: a "TwistMapsAndBilliards" section after SymplecticGeometry/CotangentLagrangian C3.1. Textbooks: Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Katok-Hasselblatt ch. 9 and 13; Siburg, *The Principle of Least Action in Geometry and Dynamics*.
