Tau Ceti has the lifted billiard ball map of Definition 92.3 for every strictly convex table with a unit-speed derivative, so for the `C²` tables the paper uses: `TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.nextCollision` on the strip `ℝ × (−1, 1)` in the coordinates `(s, cos δ)`, with the exit point, the law of reflection, non-tangency of the next chord and equivariance under `s ↦ s + L` (`TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/NextCollision.lean`); the billiard map `T` itself as a bijection of the open cylinder `AddCircle L × (−1, 1)`, `TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable.billiardMap`, with time reversal `T⁻¹ = I ∘ T ∘ I` (Proposition 92.4(d), `billiardMap_symm_coe`; `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/Inverse.lean`); and the generating function `S(s, t) = −‖χ(t) − χ(s)‖` with `v = ∂_sS`, `w = −∂_tS` at the next collision and the twist `∂_s∂_tS < 0`, so that for fixed `s` the exit parameter is a continuous strictly decreasing bijection of `(−1, 1)` onto `(s, s + L)` with negative derivative (Proposition 92.4(c), `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/GeneratingFunction.lean`). For the circular table (`StrictConvexBilliardTable.circle`, `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/Circle.lean`) the billiard map is computed as the rotation `(s, v) ↦ (s + 2R arccos v, v)`, so every `{v = const}` is a rotational invariant circle, and the primitive identity `dℓ = w dt − v ds` holds. Not in code: the rest of Proposition 92.4 (joint continuity, homeomorphism and smoothness, extension to the closed cylinder, area preservation) and rotational invariant curves with their rotation numbers (Definition 92.5). Merged MathematicalBilliards specifies all of Definition 92 for `C^k` tables, `k ≥ 1`, including 92.5 (Layers 1–5), on its own carrier `ConvexTable` in `ℂ`; merged SymplecticGeometry/CotangentLagrangian C3.1 specifies the map and Proposition 92.4(a)–(c) for `C^∞` tables. ## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable o` (`TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/NextCollision.lean`): an `L`-periodic unit-speed map `ℝ → E` into an oriented Euclidean plane, injective on `[0, L)`, with `0 < ω(χ'(s), χ(t) − χ(s))` whenever `χ(t) ≠ χ(s)` (strict convexity with positive orientation). Only a unit-speed derivative is assumed, so `C²` tables of positive curvature (Setup 92.1) are instances. It is the condition of MathematicalBilliards §1.1's `ConvexTable` (stated there in `ℂ`), which the roadmap does not cite.
- Same file: `outgoingDir` (the unit velocity `v χ'(s) + √(1 − v²) J χ'(s)`), `exists_collision`, `nextParam` with `eq_nextParam_of_areaForm_eq_zero` (the unique exit parameter in `(s, s + L)`, Proposition 92.2's ray statement), `nextTangential_mem_Ioo` (the next chord is not tangent), `outgoingDir_nextCollision` (reflection law), `nextCollision` and `nextCollision_add_period` (equivariance under the deck translation). The tangential coordinate `v = cos δ` is the convention of the first Remark.
- `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/Inverse.lean`: time reversal of the next-collision map, `outgoingDir_nextParam_neg_nextTangential`, `nextParam_nextParam_neg_nextTangential`, `nextTangential_nextParam_neg_nextTangential` (`B(t, −w) = (s + L, −v)`); `nextCollisionEquiv` (the next-collision map as a bijection of the strip, inverse `τ⁻¹ ∘ ι ∘ B ∘ ι`); `billiardMap` (the descended bijection of `AddCircle L × (−1, 1)`), `billiardMap_coe`, `billiardMap_symm_coe` (inverse `ι ∘ β ∘ ι`).
- `TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/GeneratingFunction.lean`: `generatingFunction` (`S(s, t) = −‖χ(t) − χ(s)‖`), `generatingFunction_add_period`, `chordDir` (unit chord); `hasDerivAt_generatingFunction_left`/`_right` (`∂_sS = ⟪χ'(s), u⟫`, `∂_tS = −⟪χ'(t), u⟫`), `deriv_generatingFunction_left_nextParam`, `deriv_generatingFunction_right_nextParam` (`v = ∂_sS`, `w = −∂_tS` at the next collision), `nextParam_eq_iff_deriv_generatingFunction_left_eq` (for `t ∈ (s, s + L)`, `t` is the exit parameter of `(s, v)` exactly when `v = ∂_sS(s, t)`); the mixed partials `hasDerivAt_deriv_generatingFunction_left`/`_right` and the twist `deriv_deriv_generatingFunction_left_neg`/`_right_neg` (`∂_t∂_sS = ω(χ'(s), u) ω(χ'(t), u)/‖χ(t) − χ(s)‖ < 0`); for fixed `s`, `strictAntiOn_nextParam`, `image_nextParam_Ioo`, `continuousOn_nextParam`, `hasDerivAt_nextParam` (`∂t/∂v = −ℓ/(√(1 − v²) √(1 − w²))`), `deriv_nextParam_neg`. Joint continuity and smoothness in `(s, v)` are not treated.
- The chain dictionary of C3.1, `TauCeti.CotangentBundle.exists_equiv_periodic_of_generating`, with the cyclic discrete action `TauCeti.cyclicAction` (`TauCeti/Geometry/Symplectic/Cotangent/GeneratingFunction/Chain.lean`): periodic chains of maps generated by `S_i` correspond to critical points of `Σ S_i(q_i, q_{i+1})`. Not applied to the billiard generating function.
- `TauCeti.exists_billiard_periodic_reflection` (`TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/PeriodicPolygon.lean`): closed billiard polygons with `N ≥ 2` bounces by perimeter maximization, rotation number not controlled; with the perimeter bounds `inscribedPerimeter_le_of_forall_norm_le` and `inscribedPerimeter_eq_iff_of_forall_norm_eq` for polygons inscribed in a ball.

5. Central symmetry, caustics, Birkhoff–Poritsky: unplanned (named as a downstream consumer of MathematicalBilliards Layers 1–5).

## Library-scale gap
The twist-map theory around the billiard map (Birkhoff's graph theorem, rotation numbers of invariant curves, Birkhoff periodic orbits) is the merged MathematicalBilliards (Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Katok–Hasselblatt ch. 9 and 13; Herman 1983). What remains for the definition is implementation of its Layers 1–5; the Tau Ceti carrier `StrictConvexBilliardTable` and its next-collision map, inverse and generating function cover much of Layers 1–3 in the `(s, cos δ)` coordinates and an oriented Euclidean plane rather than `ℂ`. The paper's theorem further needs caustics, central symmetry and the Birkhoff–Poritsky argument, which MathematicalBilliards names as a downstream roadmap (Bialy–Mironov; Siburg, *The Principle of Least Action in Geometry and Dynamics*).
