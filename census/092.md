## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.StrictConvexBilliardTable o` (`TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/NextCollision.lean`): an `L`-periodic unit-speed map `ℝ → E` into an oriented Euclidean plane, injective on `[0, L)`, with `0 < ω(χ'(s), χ(t) − χ(s))` whenever `χ(t) ≠ χ(s)` (strict convexity with positive orientation). Only a unit-speed derivative is assumed, so `C²` tables of positive curvature (Setup 92.1) are instances.
- Same file: `outgoingDir` (the unit velocity `v χ'(s) + √(1 − v²) J χ'(s)`), `exists_collision`, `nextParam` with `eq_nextParam_of_areaForm_eq_zero` (the unique exit parameter in `(s, s + L)`, Proposition 92.2's ray statement), `nextTangential_mem_Ioo` (the next chord is not tangent), `outgoingDir_nextCollision` (reflection law), `nextCollision` and `nextCollision_add_period` (equivariance under the deck translation). The tangential coordinate `v = cos δ` is the convention of the first Remark.
- `TauCeti.exists_billiard_periodic_reflection` (`TauCeti/Dynamics/Billiard/PeriodicPolygon.lean`): closed billiard polygons with `N ≥ 2` bounces by perimeter maximization, rotation number not controlled.
- Missing: the map on `AddCircle L × (−1, 1)`, its inverse, continuity and smoothness, area preservation, twist, the closed-cylinder extension, time reversal, rotational invariant curves and their rotation numbers (grep `billiard` hits only the two files above).

## Merged roadmaps
- **SymplecticGeometry/CotangentLagrangian, §C3.1 "Discrete action, iterates and convex billiards"**: for a smooth `L`-periodic arclength, strictly convex table, construct the next-collision map β̃ on `ℝ×(−1,1)` (coordinates (s, v=cos δ)), existence/uniqueness/smoothness/inverse, τ-equivariance, descent to a homeomorphism β of `AddCircle L × (−1,1)`, generating function `S(s,t)=−‖χ(t)−χ(s)‖`, twist `∂_s∂_tS<0`, `β̃*ω=ω`, `MeasurePreserving`; periodic billiard trajectories via perimeter maximization. The construction of β̃ and the periodic trajectories are in code (above); the rest is not.
- SymplecticGeometry/STATUS.md (5fc1aa8): "C3 is partial: generating functions give canonical transformations".
- Differences from Def 92: the roadmap takes the table `C^∞` (the paper needs `C²`; the code needs only `C¹`), excludes tangent states (no extension to the closed cylinder), and does not define rotational invariant curves or their rotation numbers.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Arclength-parametrised strictly convex curve: Tau Ceti `StrictConvexBilliardTable` (`C¹`); `C^k` carrier and curvature criterion in PR #2255 Layer 1.
2. Next-collision map on the strip: Tau Ceti `nextCollision`.
3. Descent to the cylinder, homeomorphism, area form, twist: merged CotangentLagrangian C3.1 (`C^∞`, not implemented); at regularity `C^k` and with the closed cylinder and time reversal, PR #2255 Layers 2–4.
4. Rotational invariant curves, Birkhoff's graph theorem, rotation numbers: PR #2255 Layer 5, on Mathlib's `CircleDeg1Lift.translationNumber`.
5. Central symmetry, caustics, Birkhoff–Poritsky: unplanned (named downstream of PR #2255).

## Library-scale gap
The twist-map theory around the billiard map (Birkhoff's graph theorem, rotation numbers of invariant curves, Birkhoff periodic orbits) is PR #2255 (Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Katok–Hasselblatt ch. 9 and 13; Herman 1983); what remains for the definition is its review and merging, then implementation on top of the Tau Ceti carrier. The paper's theorem further needs caustics, central symmetry and the Birkhoff–Poritsky argument, which PR #2255 names as a downstream roadmap (Bialy–Mironov; Siburg, *The Principle of Least Action in Geometry and Dynamics*).
