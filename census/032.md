# 32. C^k(M) and C^{k,α}(M) on a compact manifold

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has the Banach space C^k(M) and, for a compact chart atlas of a compact boundaryless manifold, the Banach space C^{k,α}(M, F) of Definition 32.2(b) (`TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace`, complete), independent of the atlas as a set and up to equivalence of norms (Proposition 32.3(i)–(ii): `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.forall_memChartHolder_iff`, `CkHolderSpace.atlasEquiv`, `CkHolderSpace.exists_norm_le_and_norm_le`), so the norm of Definition 32.4 is well defined up to equivalence; plus the C^{0,α} Hölder space for any metric (so for the Riemannian distance). Of 32.3(iii) the exponent inclusion `C^{k,α}(M) → C^{k,β}(M)` for `β ≤ α` and the order-lowering inclusion `C^{k+1,α}(M) → C^{k,β}(M)` for `β ≤ 1` are in code (`CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.inclusionL`, `inclusionSuccL`), and of 32.3(iv) the inclusion of the Riemannian Hölder space into the atlas `C^{0,α}(M)` (`CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.ofHolderSpaceL`). Missing: compactness of the inclusions (32.3(iii); only the Euclidean versions on open sets are in code, and only open roadmap PR #3015 §5.1 specifies it on `M`), the reverse inclusion and norm equivalence of 32.3(iv), the boundary case, and C^{k,α} maps and C^{k,α} diffeomorphisms between manifolds (32.2(c)). No merged roadmap specifies 32.2(c), 32.3(iii) or 32.3(iv); open roadmap PR #3015 (HolderMapsBetweenManifolds) specifies all three and the boundary case, so the definition is filed under roadmap review. The code suffices to state results about C^β test functions (Tsujii–Zhang), but not the C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms of Dolgopyat–Kanigowski–Rodriguez Hertz, so the definition is not yet ready for statements.

## Mathlib
- Has: `ContMDiff`, `ContDiff`, `iteratedFDeriv`, `HolderWith`/`HolderOnWith` (`Topology/MetricSpace/Holder.lean`), `eHolderNorm`/`MemHolder` (`Topology/MetricSpace/HolderNorm.lean`), `IsRiemannianManifold` and `EMetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric`.
- Missing: any normed/Banach structure on C^k(M) or C^{k,α}(M); no C^{k,α} for maps between manifolds.

## Tau Ceti code
- C^k(M): `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasSeminorm`, `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasNormedAddCommGroup`/`finiteAtlasNormedSpace`, completeness `ContMDiffMap.completeSpace_finiteAtlas` (boundaryless, locally compact model), atlas independence `ContMDiffMap.exists_finiteAtlasSeminorm_le`, existence `TauCeti.exists_finite_compact_chart_cover` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/{Banach,FiniteAtlas,Complete}.lean`). This is Definition 32.1/32.2(a) with Prop 32.3(ii),(iii) for C^k, boundaryless only.
- C^{k,α}(M): `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas` (`Geometry/Manifold/ExtChartAt.lean`: finitely many chart centres with compact pieces of the extended chart targets whose relative interiors cover `M`, existing on every compact manifold modelled on a locally compact space, `CompactChartAtlas.instNonempty`); the chart Hölder seminorm `ContMDiffMap.chartHolderNorm` and predicate `ContMDiffMap.MemChartHolder` of the `k`th coordinate derivative on a piece; `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace A k α F`, the `C^k` maps `M → F` (`F` a normed space over `ℝ` or `ℂ`) with `α`-Hölder `k`th coordinate derivatives on every piece, with norm = finite-atlas `C^k` norm + sum of the pieces' Hölder seminorms (`CkHolderSpace.norm_def`), the inclusion `toContMDiffMap` into C^k, evaluation `evalCLM`, and completeness `CkHolderSpace.instCompleteSpace` for boundaryless `M` and complete `F` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder/Basic.lean`). This is Definition 32.2(b) for the atlas `A` (the pieces sit in preferred charts `chartAt`) and Prop 32.3(iii)'s completeness. The definitions also apply to manifolds with corners; completeness and atlas independence are boundaryless.
- Atlas independence (Prop 32.3(i)–(ii)), for a boundaryless `C^{k+1}` manifold and `α ≤ 1` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder/Atlas.lean`): Hölder bounds on a cover give Hölder bounds in every chart on every compact subset of its target, `ContMDiffMap.MemChartHolder.of_cover` and `memChartHolder_of_isCompact` (from the Hölder chain rule `TauCeti.exists_holderOnWith_iteratedFDerivWithin_comp` for the transition map and a Lebesgue-number argument); two compact chart atlases give the same space, `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.forall_memChartHolder_iff`, with the linear equivalence `CkHolderSpace.atlasLinearEquiv`, and for a locally compact model and complete `F` the continuous linear equivalence `CkHolderSpace.atlasEquiv` (closed graph theorem) and equivalent norms `CkHolderSpace.exists_norm_le_and_norm_le`.
- Hölder sections of a vector bundle over a finite bundle atlas, `TauCeti.HolderSection A k α` with `TauCeti.FiniteBundleAtlas.MemHolderSection`, the maximum of the chartwise `CkHolderSpaceOn` norms, complete (`HolderSection.instCompleteSpace`), with smooth sections included (`HolderSection.ofContMDiffSection`) (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holder/{Basic,ContMDiffSection}.lean`). This is the bundle `C^{k,α}` of PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 for a fixed bundle atlas; its comparison with `CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace` is not in code. Its atlas independence (PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4): over a compact boundaryless manifold, for `k + 1 ≤ n` and `α ≤ 1`, having `C^{k,α}` readings does not depend on the finite bundle atlas, charts and frames (`FiniteBundleAtlas.memHolderSection_iff_of_atlas`), and for a complete model fibre the identity on sections is a Banach isomorphism `HolderSection.atlasEquiv` with `exists_norm_atlasEquiv_le` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holder/Atlas.lean`), on compositions of open-set Hölder maps (`Analysis/Holder/OpenSet/Comp.lean`). The bundle Besov spaces of §5.3, whose case `p = q = ∞` is the Zygmund carrier `C^r_*` of §5.4, over a fixed square chart partition: `TauCeti.SquareChartPartition.MemBesov` with `memBesov_iff`, `MemBesov.localization`, `MemBesov.mono`, `MemBesov.mono_exponent`, the complete space `besovSpace` with `isClosed_besovSpace`, the inclusions `besovInclusion`, `besovInclusionOfLT`, `MemBesov.of_bump` and `besovSpaceEquivSobolevSpace` (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Sobolev/Besov.lean`). Explicit constants, connection descriptions, `h^{k,α}(V)`, independence of `C^r_*` from the partition and its identification with `C^{k,α}` are not in code.
- The little Hölder space of sections (PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4): `TauCeti.HolderSection.littleHolder`, the closure of the smooth sections `HolderSection.smoothSections` in `HolderSection A k α`, closed and complete, with `range_ofContMDiffSection` and chartwise vanishing small-scale modulus for `α < 1` (`mem_littleHolder_component_of_mem_littleHolder`) (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holder/Little/Basic.lean`), on the open-set little Hölder space `CkHolderSpaceOn.littleHolder` (`Analysis/Holder/Little/OpenSet.lean`). The Euclidean step of the converse characterization of the little Hölder sections: support-controlled smooth approximation in `h^{k,α}` on a finite-dimensional space, `CkHolderSpace.exists_mem_smoothBoundedDerivatives_tsupport_subset_norm_sub_lt` and `exists_mem_smoothBoundedDerivatives_hasCompactSupport_tsupport_subset_norm_sub_lt` (`Analysis/Holder/Little/Basic.lean`); the partition-of-unity assembly over a bundle atlas is not in code. Atlas independence of the little Hölder space of sections: `HolderSection.atlasLinearEquiv` carries the smooth sections of one finite bundle atlas onto those of another (`smoothSections_map_atlasLinearEquiv`, `smoothSections_comap_atlasLinearEquiv`), and for a complete model fibre `atlasEquiv` carries `littleHolder` onto `littleHolder` (`littleHolder_map_atlasEquiv`, `littleHolder_comap_atlasEquiv`, `atlasEquiv_mem_littleHolder_iff`) (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holder/Little/Atlas.lean`). The first step of the gluing (§5.4), multiplication of `C^{k,α}(Ω)` on an arbitrary open set by a smooth cutoff with explicit operator-norm bound: `TauCeti.CkHolderSpaceOn.cutoffSMulL : C^{k,α}(Ω, F) →L[ℝ] C^{k,α}(E, F)` with `coe_cutoffSMulL`, `norm_cutoffSMulL_le`, and `cutoffSMulOnL` on `C^{k,α}(Ω, F)` with `iteratedFDeriv_cutoffSMulOnL` (`Analysis/Holder/OpenSet/Cutoff.lean`), on `TauCeti.holderWith_iteratedFDeriv_cutoff_smul_of_thickening_subset` (`Analysis/Holder/Localization.lean`).
- The exponent half of 32.3(iii): `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.inclusionL : C^{k,α}_A(M, F) →L C^{k,β}_A(M, F)` for `β ≤ α`, injective, of norm at most `2N + 1` for an atlas with `N` pieces (`norm_inclusionL_le`), with `holderWith_restrict_chartIteratedFDeriv_of_le` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder/Inclusion.lean`). The order-lowering inclusion for `β ≤ 1` and any `α` (PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4): the `k`th coordinate derivative of a `C^{k+1}` map is locally Lipschitz on the extended chart target, `ContMDiffMap.locallyLipschitz_chartIteratedFDeriv`, hence `β`-Hölder on compact pieces, `ContMDiffMap.memChartHolder_of_contMDiff_of_isCompact`, and the injective continuous linear map `CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.inclusionSuccL` (closed graph theorem) compatible with `inclusionL` (`inclusionL_comp_inclusionSuccL`, `inclusionSuccL_comp_inclusionL`) (`Chart/Holder/Inclusion.lean`), for a locally compact model and complete `F`. Compactness of the inclusions is not in code, and no merged roadmap specifies it (open roadmap PR #3015 §5.1 does).
- One half of 32.3(iv): on a boundaryless `C¹` manifold with a continuous Riemannian metric, the injective continuous linear inclusion `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.ofHolderSpaceL : HolderSpace α M F →L[ℝ] A.CkHolderSpace 0 α F` with `opNorm_ofHolderSpaceL_le`, from the Lipschitz bound `TauCeti.IsRiemannianManifold.exists_lipschitzOnWith_extChartAt_symm_of_isCompact` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Distance.lean`) packaged as `CompactChartAtlas.lipschitzOnWith_chartSymmLipschitzConst` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder/Riemannian.lean`). The reverse inclusion and the equivalence of norms are not in code.
- Hölder, Euclidean/metric: `TauCeti.HolderSpace α X Y` on any metric space (`Analysis/Holder/Normed.lean`), `TauCeti.C1HolderSpace`, `TauCeti.C2HolderSpace`, `CkHolderSpace k α E F` on a whole normed space (`Analysis/Holder/Iterated.lean`), little Hölder `littleHolder` (`Analysis/Holder/Little/Basic.lean`); the Banach space `C^{k,α}(Ω, F)` on an open set `TauCeti.CkHolderSpaceOn k α Ω F` with the global Hölder seminorm, completeness, restriction `restrictL` and the inclusion `inclusionSuccL` on convex `Ω` (`Analysis/Holder/OpenSet/Basic.lean`); the exponent inclusions `C^{0,α}(X) → C^{0,β}(X)` and `C^{k,α}(Ω) → C^{k,β}(Ω)` for `β ≤ α`, compact for `β < α` on compact `X` (`Analysis/Holder/Inclusion.lean`); the local spaces `C^{k,α}_loc(Ω)` (`Analysis/Holder/Loc.lean`, `LocalSpace.lean`); the identification of `C^{k,α}` with the Hölder–Zygmund space `B^{k+α}_{∞,∞}` on the whole space for `0 < α < 1`, `TauCeti.BesovSpace.ckHolderSpaceEquiv` (`Analysis/Holder/Zygmund.lean`).
- Pullback along a diffeomorphism `Φ` of the whole space with bounded derivatives is a bounded isomorphism of every Besov space `B^s_{p,q}(ℝⁿ; F)`, `TauCeti.BesovSpace.compCLE` (`Analysis/FunctionalSpaces/BesovSpace/ChangeOfVariables.lean`, PseudodifferentialOperators §5.3).
- Riemannian distance as a metric: `TauCeti.MetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Distance.lean`). Hence C^{0,α}(M) in the form of Prop 32.3(iv) is available as `HolderSpace α M F`; of its comparison with the atlas space (32.3(iv)) only the inclusion `ofHolderSpaceL` is proved.
- Missing: compactness of the embeddings on a manifold (32.3(iii)), the reverse inclusion and norm equivalence of 32.3(iv), completeness and atlas independence with boundary, C^{k,α} maps and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms (32.2(c)).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `HolderWith`, `eHolderNorm`, `ContMDiff`, charts: Mathlib.
2. Banach C^k(M) via finite atlas: Tau Ceti (boundaryless).
3. `HolderSpace` on metric space + Riemannian metric space: Tau Ceti, giving C^{0,α}(M).
4. Euclidean `CkHolderSpace k α` and `CkHolderSpaceOn k α Ω`: Tau Ceti; C^{k,α}(Ω), C^{k,α}_loc(Ω): PDE §1.7 (implemented in large part).
5. Compact-base C^{k,α}(M) for a compact chart atlas, with completeness: Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace`).
6. Atlas independence of the C^{k,α} space and norm (32.3(i)–(ii)): Tau Ceti (`CompactChartAtlas.forall_memChartHolder_iff`, `CkHolderSpace.atlasEquiv`), the PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 atlas comparison. Embeddings on M (32.3(iii)): PR #3015 §5.1; the exponent inclusion in Tau Ceti (`CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.inclusionL`), the order-lowering inclusion in Tau Ceti (`inclusionSuccL`). Comparison 32.3(iv): PR #3015 §5.2; the inclusion `HolderSpace α M F → C^{0,α}_A(M, F)` in Tau Ceti (`CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace.ofHolderSpaceL`).
7. C^{k,α} maps M → M' and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms: PR #3015 §2.1, §3.1, on its Layer 1 Euclidean estimates.
8. Manifolds with boundary: the definitions in 5 apply; completeness and the Psido conventions are boundaryless; PR #3015 §2.2 gives the finite-atlas spaces on compact manifolds with corners.

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: PR #3015 specifies the parts not in code (32.2(c), the rest of 32.3(iii)–(iv), the boundary case); what remains is its review and merging, then implementation, which the Hölder chain rule `exists_holderOnWith_iteratedFDerivWithin_comp` used for atlas independence and the Euclidean inclusions in code (`Analysis/Holder/Inclusion.lean`) largely supply. Reference: Hörmander / Taylor, *PDE I*, Ch. 1 and 13 (Hölder spaces on compact manifolds).
