# 32. C^k(M) and C^{k,α}(M) on a compact manifold

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has the Banach space C^k(M) and, for a fixed compact chart atlas of a compact boundaryless manifold, the Banach space C^{k,α}(M, F) of Definition 32.2(b) (`TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace`, complete), plus the C^{0,α} Hölder space for any metric (so for the Riemannian distance). Missing: independence of the atlas (Proposition 32.3(i)–(ii), so the norm of Definition 32.4 is only defined for a chosen atlas), the embeddings of 32.3(iii), and C^{k,α} maps and C^{k,α} diffeomorphisms between manifolds (32.2(c)). The atlas comparison is the remaining merged PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 target; 32.2(c) is unplanned. The code suffices to state results about C^β test functions (Tsujii–Zhang), but not the C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms of Dolgopyat–Kanigowski–Rodriguez Hertz, so the definition is not yet ready for statements.

## Mathlib
- Has: `ContMDiff`, `ContDiff`, `iteratedFDeriv`, `HolderWith`/`HolderOnWith` (`Topology/MetricSpace/Holder.lean`), `eHolderNorm`/`MemHolder` (`Topology/MetricSpace/HolderNorm.lean`), `IsRiemannianManifold` and `EMetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric`.
- Missing: any normed/Banach structure on C^k(M) or C^{k,α}(M); no C^{k,α} for maps between manifolds.

## Tau Ceti code
- C^k(M): `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasSeminorm`, `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasNormedAddCommGroup`/`finiteAtlasNormedSpace`, completeness `ContMDiffMap.completeSpace_finiteAtlas` (boundaryless, locally compact model), atlas independence `ContMDiffMap.exists_finiteAtlasSeminorm_le`, existence `TauCeti.exists_finite_compact_chart_cover` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/{Banach,FiniteAtlas,Complete}.lean`). This is Definition 32.1/32.2(a) with Prop 32.3(ii),(iii) for C^k, boundaryless only.
- C^{k,α}(M): `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas` (`Geometry/Manifold/ExtChartAt.lean`: finitely many chart centres with compact pieces of the extended chart targets whose relative interiors cover `M`, existing on every compact manifold modelled on a locally compact space, `CompactChartAtlas.instNonempty`); the chart Hölder seminorm `ContMDiffMap.chartHolderNorm` and predicate `ContMDiffMap.MemChartHolder` of the `k`th coordinate derivative on a piece; `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace A k α F`, the `C^k` maps `M → F` (`F` a normed space over `ℝ` or `ℂ`) with `α`-Hölder `k`th coordinate derivatives on every piece, with norm = finite-atlas `C^k` norm + sum of the pieces' Hölder seminorms (`CkHolderSpace.norm_def`), the inclusion `toContMDiffMap` into C^k, evaluation `evalCLM`, and completeness `CkHolderSpace.instCompleteSpace` for boundaryless `M` and complete `F` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder.lean`). This is Definition 32.2(b) for the atlas `A` (the pieces sit in preferred charts `chartAt`) and Prop 32.3(iii)'s completeness. The definitions also apply to manifolds with corners; only completeness is boundaryless. The file states that the comparison of two atlases is not part of it.
- Hölder, Euclidean/metric: `TauCeti.HolderSpace α X Y` on any metric space (`Analysis/Holder/Normed.lean`), `TauCeti.C1HolderSpace`, `TauCeti.C2HolderSpace`, `CkHolderSpace k α E F` on a whole normed space (`Analysis/Holder/Iterated.lean`), little Hölder `littleHolder`.
- Riemannian distance as a metric: `TauCeti.MetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Distance.lean`). Hence C^{0,α}(M) in the form of Prop 32.3(iv) is available as `HolderSpace α M F`; its comparison with the atlas space (32.3(iv)) is not proved.
- Missing: atlas independence of C^{k,α}(M) (32.3(i)–(ii)), the embeddings C^{k,α} ⊆ C^{k',α'} (32.3(iii)), C^{k,α} maps and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms (32.2(c)).

## Merged roadmaps
- PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 "Hölder, Zygmund and little Hölder spaces": "Supply the compact-base bundle C^(k,α) norms by chart/connection comparisons", completeness and atlas/frame independence of finite-chart norms. Scalar C^{k,α}(M) is the trivial-line case. Conventions: boundaryless manifolds. The fixed-atlas space and its completeness are in code (above); the chart comparisons, bundle norms, Zygmund spaces and mapping theorems are not (STATUS Layer 5 remaining "... Zygmund mapping ...").
- PDE §1.7 (A7): C^{k,α}(Ω) and C^{k,α}_loc(Ω) on open Ω ⊆ ℝⁿ (covers the local-coordinate reading used by the naked-singularities paper, Remark (3)). Largely implemented (`CkHolderSpace`; STATUS Layer 1 remaining only Campanato etc.).
- Not specified anywhere: C^{k,α} maps between manifolds and C^{k,α} diffeomorphisms; boundary case.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `HolderWith`, `eHolderNorm`, `ContMDiff`, charts: Mathlib.
2. Banach C^k(M) via finite atlas: Tau Ceti (boundaryless).
3. `HolderSpace` on metric space + Riemannian metric space: Tau Ceti, giving C^{0,α}(M).
4. Euclidean `CkHolderSpace k α`: Tau Ceti; C^{k,α}(Ω), C^{k,α}_loc(Ω): PDE §1.7 (implemented in large part).
5. Compact-base C^{k,α}(M) for a fixed compact chart atlas, with completeness: Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace`).
6. Atlas independence of the C^{k,α} space and norm (32.3(i)–(ii)), embeddings (32.3(iii)): target PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4, not implemented.
7. C^{k,α} maps M → M' and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms: unplanned (small extension of 5–6).
8. Manifolds with boundary: the definitions in 5 apply; completeness and the Psido conventions are boundaryless.

## Library-scale gap
Small: the chart comparison of §5.4 (chain rule and Leibniz estimates for transition maps on compact pieces, a Lebesgue-number argument) gives 32.3(i)–(ii); the map version (32.2(c)) is a chartwise predicate on local representatives plus composition/inverse lemmas, naturally a short addition to PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 or to DifferentialGeometry. Reference: Hörmander / Taylor, *PDE I*, Ch. 1 and 13 (Hölder spaces on compact manifolds).
