# 32. C^k(M) and C^{k,α}(M) on a compact manifold

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Tau Ceti already has the Banach space C^k(M) on a compact boundaryless manifold and the C^{0,α} Hölder space on any metric space (so C^{0,α}(M) for the Riemannian distance); the compact-base C^{k,α} norms for k ≥ 1 are a merged PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 target, not yet implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: `ContMDiff`, `ContDiff`, `iteratedFDeriv`, `HolderWith`/`HolderOnWith` (`Topology/MetricSpace/Holder.lean`), `eHolderNorm`/`MemHolder` (`Topology/MetricSpace/HolderNorm.lean`), `IsRiemannianManifold` and `EMetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric`.
- Missing: any normed/Banach structure on C^k(M) or C^{k,α}(M); no C^{k,α} for maps between manifolds.

## Tau Ceti code
- C^k(M): `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasSeminorm`, `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasNormedAddCommGroup`/`finiteAtlasNormedSpace`, completeness `ContMDiffMap.completeSpace_finiteAtlas` (boundaryless, locally compact model), atlas independence `ContMDiffMap.exists_finiteAtlasSeminorm_le`, existence `TauCeti.exists_finite_compact_chart_cover` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/{Banach,FiniteAtlas,Complete}.lean`). This is Definition 32.1/32.2(a) with Prop 32.3(ii),(iii) for C^k, boundaryless only.
- Hölder, Euclidean/metric: `TauCeti.HolderSpace α X Y` on any metric space (`Analysis/Holder/Normed.lean`), `TauCeti.C1HolderSpace`, `TauCeti.C2HolderSpace`, `CkHolderSpace k α E F` on a whole normed space (`Analysis/Holder/Iterated.lean`), little Hölder `littleHolder`.
- Riemannian distance as a metric: `TauCeti.MetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Distance.lean`). Hence C^{0,α}(M) in the form of Prop 32.3(iv) is available now as `HolderSpace α M F`.
- Missing: C^{k,α}(M) for k ≥ 1 (chartwise Hölder seminorm of order-k derivatives), C^{k,α} maps and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms (32.2(c)), manifolds with boundary.

## Merged roadmaps
- PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 "Hölder, Zygmund and little Hölder spaces": "Supply the compact-base bundle C^(k,α) norms by chart/connection comparisons", completeness and atlas/frame independence of finite-chart norms. Scalar C^{k,α}(M) is the trivial-line case. Conventions: boundaryless manifolds. STATUS: Layer 5 partial, "Holder-Zygmund mapping" listed as remaining; no manifold C^{k,α} code found.
- PDE §1.7 (A7): C^{k,α}(Ω) and C^{k,α}_loc(Ω) on open Ω ⊆ ℝⁿ (covers the local-coordinate reading used by the naked-singularities paper, Remark (3)). Largely implemented (`CkHolderSpace`; STATUS Layer 1 remaining only Campanato etc.).
- Not specified anywhere: C^{k,α} maps between manifolds and C^{k,α} diffeomorphisms; boundary case.

## Open roadmap PRs
None relevant (grep of all open-PR READMEs for Hölder spaces on manifolds found nothing; PR 1614 "PDE Mixed Boundary Conditions" uses Euclidean Hölder only).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `HolderWith`, `eHolderNorm`, `ContMDiff`, charts: Mathlib.
2. Banach C^k(M) via finite atlas: Tau Ceti (boundaryless).
3. `HolderSpace` on metric space + Riemannian metric space: Tau Ceti, giving C^{0,α}(M).
4. Euclidean `CkHolderSpace k α`: Tau Ceti; C^{k,α}(Ω), C^{k,α}_loc(Ω): PDE §1.7 (implemented in large part).
5. Compact-base C^{k,α}(M) (finite-chart norm, completeness, atlas independence): target PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4, not implemented.
6. C^{k,α} maps M → M' and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms: unplanned (small extension of 5).
7. Manifolds with boundary: unplanned (Psido conventions are boundaryless).

## Library-scale gap
Small: once §5.4 lands, the map version (32.2(c)) is a chartwise predicate on local representatives plus composition/inverse lemmas, naturally a short addition to PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 or to DifferentialGeometry. Boundary versions would sit beside the Tau Ceti finite-atlas C^k code. Reference: Hörmander / Taylor, *PDE I*, Ch. 1 and 13 (Hölder spaces on compact manifolds).
