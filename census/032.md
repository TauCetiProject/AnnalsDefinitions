# 32. C^k(M) and C^{k,α}(M) on a compact manifold

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has the Banach space C^k(M) and, for a compact chart atlas of a compact boundaryless manifold, the Banach space C^{k,α}(M, F) of Definition 32.2(b) (`TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace`, complete), independent of the atlas as a set and up to equivalence of norms (Proposition 32.3(i)–(ii): `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.forall_memChartHolder_iff`, `CkHolderSpace.atlasEquiv`, `CkHolderSpace.exists_norm_le_and_norm_le`), so the norm of Definition 32.4 is well defined up to equivalence; plus the C^{0,α} Hölder space for any metric (so for the Riemannian distance). Missing: the embeddings C^{k,α}(M) ⊆ C^{k',α'}(M) of 32.3(iii) (only their Euclidean versions on open sets are in code), the comparison 32.3(iv) with the Riemannian Hölder space, the boundary case, and C^{k,α} maps and C^{k,α} diffeomorphisms between manifolds (32.2(c)), which no roadmap or PR specifies. The code suffices to state results about C^β test functions (Tsujii–Zhang), but not the C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms of Dolgopyat–Kanigowski–Rodriguez Hertz, so the definition is not yet ready for statements.

## Mathlib
- Has: `ContMDiff`, `ContDiff`, `iteratedFDeriv`, `HolderWith`/`HolderOnWith` (`Topology/MetricSpace/Holder.lean`), `eHolderNorm`/`MemHolder` (`Topology/MetricSpace/HolderNorm.lean`), `IsRiemannianManifold` and `EMetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric`.
- Missing: any normed/Banach structure on C^k(M) or C^{k,α}(M); no C^{k,α} for maps between manifolds.

## Tau Ceti code
- C^k(M): `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasSeminorm`, `ContMDiffMap.finiteAtlasNormedAddCommGroup`/`finiteAtlasNormedSpace`, completeness `ContMDiffMap.completeSpace_finiteAtlas` (boundaryless, locally compact model), atlas independence `ContMDiffMap.exists_finiteAtlasSeminorm_le`, existence `TauCeti.exists_finite_compact_chart_cover` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/{Banach,FiniteAtlas,Complete}.lean`). This is Definition 32.1/32.2(a) with Prop 32.3(ii),(iii) for C^k, boundaryless only.
- C^{k,α}(M): `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas` (`Geometry/Manifold/ExtChartAt.lean`: finitely many chart centres with compact pieces of the extended chart targets whose relative interiors cover `M`, existing on every compact manifold modelled on a locally compact space, `CompactChartAtlas.instNonempty`); the chart Hölder seminorm `ContMDiffMap.chartHolderNorm` and predicate `ContMDiffMap.MemChartHolder` of the `k`th coordinate derivative on a piece; `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace A k α F`, the `C^k` maps `M → F` (`F` a normed space over `ℝ` or `ℂ`) with `α`-Hölder `k`th coordinate derivatives on every piece, with norm = finite-atlas `C^k` norm + sum of the pieces' Hölder seminorms (`CkHolderSpace.norm_def`), the inclusion `toContMDiffMap` into C^k, evaluation `evalCLM`, and completeness `CkHolderSpace.instCompleteSpace` for boundaryless `M` and complete `F` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder/Basic.lean`). This is Definition 32.2(b) for the atlas `A` (the pieces sit in preferred charts `chartAt`) and Prop 32.3(iii)'s completeness. The definitions also apply to manifolds with corners; completeness and atlas independence are boundaryless.
- Atlas independence (Prop 32.3(i)–(ii)), for a boundaryless `C^{k+1}` manifold and `α ≤ 1` (`Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiffMap/Chart/Holder/Atlas.lean`): Hölder bounds on a cover give Hölder bounds in every chart on every compact subset of its target, `ContMDiffMap.MemChartHolder.of_cover` and `memChartHolder_of_isCompact` (from the Hölder chain rule `TauCeti.exists_holderOnWith_iteratedFDerivWithin_comp` for the transition map and a Lebesgue-number argument); two compact chart atlases give the same space, `TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.forall_memChartHolder_iff`, with the linear equivalence `CkHolderSpace.atlasLinearEquiv`, and for a locally compact model and complete `F` the continuous linear equivalence `CkHolderSpace.atlasEquiv` (closed graph theorem) and equivalent norms `CkHolderSpace.exists_norm_le_and_norm_le`.
- Hölder sections of a vector bundle over a finite bundle atlas, `TauCeti.HolderSection A k α` with `TauCeti.FiniteBundleAtlas.MemHolderSection`, the maximum of the chartwise `CkHolderSpaceOn` norms, complete (`HolderSection.instCompleteSpace`), with smooth sections included (`HolderSection.ofContMDiffSection`) (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Holder/{Basic,ContMDiffSection}.lean`). This is the bundle `C^{k,α}` of PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 for a fixed bundle atlas; its comparison with `CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace` and its atlas independence are not in code.
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3703 (PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4: the little Hölder space of sections `TauCeti.HolderSection.littleHolder`, the closure of the smooth sections in `HolderSection A k α`, complete, with chartwise vanishing small-scale modulus for `α < 1`).
- Hölder, Euclidean/metric: `TauCeti.HolderSpace α X Y` on any metric space (`Analysis/Holder/Normed.lean`), `TauCeti.C1HolderSpace`, `TauCeti.C2HolderSpace`, `CkHolderSpace k α E F` on a whole normed space (`Analysis/Holder/Iterated.lean`), little Hölder `littleHolder`; the Banach space `C^{k,α}(Ω, F)` on an open set `TauCeti.CkHolderSpaceOn k α Ω F` with the global Hölder seminorm, completeness, restriction `restrictL` and the inclusion `inclusionSuccL` on convex `Ω` (`Analysis/Holder/OpenSet.lean`); the exponent inclusions `C^{0,α}(X) → C^{0,β}(X)` and `C^{k,α}(Ω) → C^{k,β}(Ω)` for `β ≤ α`, compact for `β < α` on compact `X` (`Analysis/Holder/Inclusion.lean`); the local spaces `C^{k,α}_loc(Ω)` (`Analysis/Holder/Loc.lean`, `LocalSpace.lean`); the identification of `C^{k,α}` with the Hölder–Zygmund space `B^{k+α}_{∞,∞}` on the whole space for `0 < α < 1`, `TauCeti.BesovSpace.ckHolderSpaceEquiv` (`Analysis/Holder/Zygmund.lean`).
- Pullback along a diffeomorphism `Φ` of the whole space with bounded derivatives is a bounded isomorphism of every Besov space `B^s_{p,q}(ℝⁿ; F)`, `TauCeti.BesovSpace.compCLE` (`Analysis/FunctionalSpaces/BesovSpace/ChangeOfVariables.lean`, PseudodifferentialOperators §5.3).
- Riemannian distance as a metric: `TauCeti.MetricSpace.ofRiemannianMetric` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Distance.lean`). Hence C^{0,α}(M) in the form of Prop 32.3(iv) is available as `HolderSpace α M F`; its comparison with the atlas space (32.3(iv)) is not proved.
- Missing: the embeddings C^{k,α}(M) ⊆ C^{k',α'}(M) on a manifold (32.3(iii)), the comparison 32.3(iv), completeness and atlas independence with boundary, C^{k,α} maps and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms (32.2(c)).

## Merged roadmaps
- [PseudodifferentialOperators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PseudodifferentialOperators/README.md) §5.4 "Hölder, Zygmund and little Hölder spaces" (README l.1280): "Supply the compact-base bundle C^(k,α) norms by chart/connection comparisons", completeness and atlas/frame independence of finite-chart norms. Scalar C^{k,α}(M) is the trivial-line case. Conventions: boundaryless manifolds. The scalar space, its completeness and its atlas independence are in code (above), as is the fixed-atlas bundle space `HolderSection`; the Zygmund spaces `C^r_*`, the frame comparisons and the mapping theorems are not (STATUS Layer 5 remaining "... Zygmund mapping ..."). §5.4 does not specify the inclusions C^{k,α}(M) ⊆ C^{k',α'}(M).
- PDE §1.7 (A7): C^{k,α}(Ω) and C^{k,α}_loc(Ω) on open Ω ⊆ ℝⁿ (covers the local-coordinate reading used by the naked-singularities paper, Remark (3)). Largely implemented (`CkHolderSpace`, `CkHolderSpaceOn`; STATUS Layer 1 remaining only Campanato etc.).
- [ComplexHessianEquations](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ComplexHessianEquations/README.md) consumes PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4's `C^{k,α}(X)` of the trivial real line bundle on a compact Kähler manifold (Conventions; §5.2 Hölder invertibility of the Kähler Laplacian and Schauder estimates). It does not specify atlas independence or `C^{k,α}` maps.
- Not specified anywhere: C^{k,α} maps between manifolds and C^{k,α} diffeomorphisms; boundary case.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `HolderWith`, `eHolderNorm`, `ContMDiff`, charts: Mathlib.
2. Banach C^k(M) via finite atlas: Tau Ceti (boundaryless).
3. `HolderSpace` on metric space + Riemannian metric space: Tau Ceti, giving C^{0,α}(M).
4. Euclidean `CkHolderSpace k α` and `CkHolderSpaceOn k α Ω`: Tau Ceti; C^{k,α}(Ω), C^{k,α}_loc(Ω): PDE §1.7 (implemented in large part).
5. Compact-base C^{k,α}(M) for a compact chart atlas, with completeness: Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.CompactChartAtlas.CkHolderSpace`).
6. Atlas independence of the C^{k,α} space and norm (32.3(i)–(ii)): Tau Ceti (`CompactChartAtlas.forall_memChartHolder_iff`, `CkHolderSpace.atlasEquiv`), the PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 atlas comparison. Embeddings on M (32.3(iii)): unplanned, a short consequence of the Euclidean inclusions in code (`Analysis/Holder/Inclusion.lean`) applied on the pieces.
7. C^{k,α} maps M → M' and C^{1+α} diffeomorphisms: unplanned (small extension of 5–6).
8. Manifolds with boundary: the definitions in 5 apply; completeness and the Psido conventions are boundaryless.

## Library-scale gap
Small: the embeddings 32.3(iii) on M follow piecewise from the Euclidean inclusions; the map version (32.2(c)) is a chartwise predicate on local representatives plus composition/inverse lemmas (the Hölder chain rule `exists_holderOnWith_iteratedFDerivWithin_comp` used for atlas independence is the main input), naturally a short addition to PseudodifferentialOperators §5.4 or to DifferentialGeometry. Reference: Hörmander / Taylor, *PDE I*, Ch. 1 and 13 (Hölder spaces on compact manifolds).
