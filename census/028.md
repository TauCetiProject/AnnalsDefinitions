# 28. Points of an algebraic stack

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 specifies `Stacks.points X : TopCat` (field-valued objects modulo isomorphism after common extension, quotient topology from a smooth atlas, atlas independence, functoriality) and `automorphismGroup x`, the stabilizer of a point of a stack (§1.14's `stabilizerAt` is the analogue for groupoids in algebraic spaces). Not implemented (Layer 1 has §§1.3–1.4, §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel` with the étale equivalence relation of an étale atlas, and of §1.18 `StackInGroupoids` with its morphisms, the stacks `ofSheaf`/`ofScheme`, 2-fibre products and site-general products and the terminal stack; no algebraic stacks). The parts of 28.2–28.4 that §1.20 does not name (finite type and closed points, `π₀ 𝒳(Spec k) → |𝒳|`, open substacks ↔ open subsets) are targets of merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3, 1.1–1.2, also not implemented. Only smooth points (28.3(c)) are named nowhere.

Used by #29.
