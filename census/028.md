# 28. Points of an algebraic stack

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 specifies `Stacks.points X : TopCat` (field-valued objects modulo isomorphism after common extension, quotient topology from a smooth atlas, atlas independence, functoriality) and `automorphismGroup x`, the stabilizer of a point of a stack (§1.14's `stabilizerAt` is the analogue for groupoids in algebraic spaces). Not implemented (Layer 1 has §1.3, the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`, §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel`, and of §1.18 `StackInGroupoids` with its morphisms, the stacks `ofSheaf`/`ofScheme` and 2-fibre products; no algebraic stacks). The parts of 28.2–28.4 that §1.20 does not name (finite type and closed points, `π₀ 𝒳(Spec k) → |𝒳|`, open substacks ↔ open subsets) are targets of merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3, 1.1–1.2, also not implemented. Only smooth points (28.3(c)) are named nowhere.

Used by #29.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Algebraic spaces and their points: Tau Ceti `Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace`, `Spaces.AlgSpace` (§1.3 done, §1.4 core); §1.7 (merged, not implemented).
2. Stacks in groupoids, 2-fibre products: §1.18 (merged, partial: `StackInGroupoids` with morphisms, `ofSheaf`/`ofScheme`, 2-fibre products and `yonedaEquiv` in Tau Ceti; the stack property of stackification not).
3. Algebraic stacks, smooth atlases: §1.19 (merged, not implemented; `IsRepresentableBySpaces` in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3197).
4. `|X|` with topology, `automorphismGroup`: §1.20 (merged, not implemented).
5. Closed / finite-type points, `X(k)/≅ → |X|` (28.4), open substacks (28.2(c)), `G_x`: StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§0.3, 1.1–1.4 (merged, not implemented). Smooth points (28.3(c)): unplanned (small extension of §1.20).

## Library-scale gap
Covered by SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20, with the finite-type-point density theorem (Stacks, Morphisms of Stacks, points of finite type) and 28.4 in StackPointsAndResidualGerbes Layer 1. Reference: Stacks Project, Properties of Algebraic Stacks §4 (04XE), Morphisms of Stacks §§27–28.
