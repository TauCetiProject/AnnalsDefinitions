# 28. Points of an algebraic stack

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 specifies `Stacks.points X : TopCat` (field-valued objects modulo isomorphism after common extension, quotient topology from a smooth atlas, atlas independence, functoriality) and `automorphismGroup x`, the stabilizer of a point of a stack (§1.14's `stabilizerAt` is the analogue for groupoids in algebraic spaces). Not implemented (Layer 1 has only the §1.3 predicates and the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`).

Used by #29.

## Mathlib
- `CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/IsStack.lean` (`Pseudofunctor.IsStack`), descent data; scheme points/topology. No algebraic stacks, no `|X|`.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicSpace/Basic.lean`): fppf sheaves with representable diagonal and étale atlas; the category `Spaces.AlgSpace` with `toPresheaf`, `ofScheme` (`AlgebraicSpace/Category.lean`). No stacks in groupoids, no points of algebraic spaces or stacks. Definition absent.

## Merged roadmaps
- SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 1:
  - §1.7 points of an algebraic space (`|X|`); §1.14 `stabilizer`, `stabilizerAt` (field-valued point) for groupoids in algebraic spaces, not for stacks,
  - §1.18 `StackInGroupoids`, `yonedaEquiv` (`Hom(h_T, X) ≃ X(T)`, gives `X(k)` as a groupoid, 28.3(a)),
  - §1.19 `IsAlgebraicStack`, `IsDeligneMumford`,
  - §1.20 `inertia`, `automorphismGroup x` (28.3(b)), and **"Underlying topological spaces of algebraic stacks"**: `Stacks.points`, `.ofSpace`, `.ofScheme`, `.atlasIndependent`, `.map`, `.atlasOpenSurjective`, `.pullbackSurjective` (Stacks 04XG, 04XL), plus locally Noetherian and finite-type stacks.
  - In code: the §1.3 predicates and the core of §1.4 (`AlgSpace`, `toPresheaf`, `ofScheme`); missing are the rest of §§1.3–1.4 (`diagonal_locallyQuasiFinite`, `isAlgebraicSpace_of_etale_cover`, `AlgSpace.overEquiv`) and everything from §1.5 on.
- Not named as targets: closed points, finite-type points and their density (28.4(b)), smooth points (28.3(c)), affine stabilizers, open substacks ↔ open subsets (28.2(c)). These are small additions.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Algebraic spaces and their points: Tau Ceti `Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace`, `Spaces.AlgSpace` (§§1.3–1.4 begun); §1.7 (merged, not implemented).
2. Stacks in groupoids, 2-fibre products: §1.18 (merged, not implemented).
3. Algebraic stacks, smooth atlases: §1.19 (merged, not implemented).
4. `|X|` with topology, `automorphismGroup`: §1.20 (merged, not implemented).
5. Closed / finite-type / smooth points, `X(k)/≅ ↪ |X|` over algebraically closed `k` (28.4): unplanned (small extension of §1.20).

## Library-scale gap
Covered by SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 except the finite-type-point density theorem (Stacks, Morphisms of Stacks, points of finite type) and 28.4, which should be appended to §1.20. Reference: Stacks Project, Properties of Algebraic Stacks §4 (04XE), Morphisms of Stacks §§27–28.
