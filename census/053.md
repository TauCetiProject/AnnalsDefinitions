## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. GeometricCurrents G6 specifies the Grassmann bundle, an "auxiliary rectifiable varifold" of an integral current with weight, integer multiplicity, pushforward, weak convergence, and weak first variation/stationarity, but only as a tool for currents; general varifolds (arbitrary Radon measures on G_k), integral varifolds and stationarity as standalone notions are not targets. Depends on #49, #52.

## Mathlib
No varifolds. Has Radon measures and weak convergence of finite measures, `MeasureTheory.Measure.hausdorffMeasure`, `Measure.map`, `Module.Grassmannian` (algebraic). No countably rectifiable sets or approximate tangent planes.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable` (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/Basic.lean`).
- `TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian` with quotient topology (`Topology/Grassmannian/Basic.lean`), oriented/orthonormal variants.
- Euclidean currents: `mass` (`Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Basic.lean`), `FlatNormedCurrent` (`Analysis/Distribution/Flat/Completion.lean`), `formCurrent`.
- Approximate tangent planes: a.e. existence for countably rectifiable sets (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/ApproximateTangent.lean`) and a measurable choice of tangent projections (`Rectifiable/MeasurableTangent.lean`); Lipschitz area formula ℝ^m → ℝ^n with multiplicity (`MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/`).
- Euclidean mass measure, normal currents and their weak compactness (`Analysis/Distribution/Mass/{Measure,Normal,Compactness}.lean`), smooth pushforward of compactly supported currents (`Analysis/Distribution/CutoffPushforward.lean`).
- No varifold, Grassmann bundle over a manifold, or first variation.

## Merged roadmaps
- GeometricCurrents G6 "First variation, normal stability and interior regularity": Grassmann bundle compactness; auxiliary rectifiable varifold of an integral current (restriction, weight, integer multiplicity, weak measure convergence, pushforward); weak first variation under compactly supported flows, tangent-divergence formula, stationarity of minimizers, monotonicity, tangent cones. Not implemented (STATUS: G2 onward not started). Explicitly "not a theorem about every stable varifold".
- GeometricMeasureTheory G2: countably k-rectifiable sets, approximate tangent planes, area formula (Euclidean). Partially implemented: the density theorem, a.e. existence and a measurable choice of approximate tangent planes, and the Lipschitz area formula are in code (STATUS is stale on measurability); a.e. uniqueness of tangent planes, tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula are missing.
- GeometricMeasureTheory README states "Currents, varifolds ... are outside this roadmap".

## Prerequisite chain
Radon measures, Hausdorff measure [Mathlib] -> countably rectifiable sets [Tau Ceti `CountablyRectifiable`; GMT G2] -> approximate tangent planes, area formula [GMT G2, partial: existence and measurable choice of tangent planes and the Lipschitz area formula done; uniqueness and the tangential area formula missing] -> Grassmann bundle G_k(M) [GeometricCurrents G6, not implemented; fibre `finiteGrassmannian` in Tau Ceti] -> varifold = Radon measure on G_k, weight, mass, convergence, pushforward [G6 only for current-induced varifolds; general case unplanned] -> rectifiable/integral varifold v(Γ,θ) [G6 partial (integer multiplicity of currents)] -> first variation, stationarity [G6 for current-induced; general unplanned] -> monotonicity, tangent cones [G6 for currents].

## Library-scale gap
A varifold layer (Allard theory): general varifolds, first variation as a vector-valued Radon measure, generalized mean curvature, monotonicity, Allard compactness for integral varifolds, tangent cones, Allard regularity; then stable varifolds (Schoen-Simon, Wickramasekera). Textbooks: Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory* (Ch. 4 and 8); Allard 1972. Most naturally a new section of GeometricCurrents (generalising its G6 auxiliary varifold) or a sibling "Varifolds" roadmap consuming GeometricMeasureTheory G2.
