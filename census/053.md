## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md) specifies the whole definition on a Riemannian manifold and on open subsets of Euclidean space: the Grassmann bundle (§§1.1–1.2), varifolds with weight, mass, support, restriction and varifold convergence (§§1.3–1.4, 53.1), rectifiable, integral and multiplicity-one varifolds `v(Γ, θ)` and `[Σ]` (§§2.2–2.3, 53.2), push-forward and dilations (§§3.1–3.2, 53.3), the first variation, stationarity and the generalized mean curvature (§§4.1, 4.3–4.4, 53.4), the derivative of mass along flows (§4.1, 53.5(a)), the first variation of a `C²` submanifold and stationarity iff minimality (§5.1, 53.5(b)), monotonicity and densities (§§6.1–6.3) and tangent cones of stationary integral varifolds (Layer 8, 53.6), regular and singular sets (§9.1), and the Simon and Edelen–Spolaor statements as acceptance tests (§9.4). Definition 53.1 is in Tau Ceti: varifolds as measures on the Grassmann bundle `G_k(E)` of a vector bundle with fibre inner products, so on `G_k(M)` for `TM`, with weight, mass, support and restriction `V⌞A = V.restrict (π⁻¹' A)` (`TauCeti.grassmannBundle.weight`, Varifolds §1.3), and varifold convergence (`TauCeti.VarifoldTendsto`, §1.4); Definition 53.4 is in code for varifolds on an open subset of a Euclidean space (`Euclidean.firstVariation`, `Euclidean.IsStationary`, with the total variation measure `Euclidean.firstVariationMeasure`), a special case, and so are the dilations of 53.3 for varifolds on a Euclidean space (`Euclidean.blowUp`, §3.2). The rest (53.2, the push-forward of 53.3 and its Riemannian form, 53.5, 53.6, and 53.4 on a Riemannian manifold) is specified by the merged roadmap and not in code, so the status is PARTIAL-CODE; a borderline call, since 53.1 is the carrier and the defined notion, stationarity, is in code only in Euclidean space. In Tau Ceti the Euclidean Grassmannian `G(E, k)` in projection form (`Euclidean.grassmannian`, Varifolds §1.1) and the convergence clause of 53.1, as vague convergence of measures on any space, `TauCeti.VarifoldTendsto`, with its compactness theorem, its compatibility with restriction to open subsets and its locality (§1.4), the image-plane map of §1.1 (`Euclidean.grassmannian.imagePlane`), the Grassmann bundle of a vector bundle with fibre inner products, so `G_k(M)` for `TM` (`TauCeti.grassmannBundle`, §1.2), the weight and open restriction in the Euclidean carrier (§1.3), approximate tangent spaces of measures (`MeasureTheory.Measure.HasApproxTangentSpace`, §2.1), the first variation of a varifold on an open subset of a Euclidean space (`Euclidean.firstVariation`, §4.1), the Euclidean Riesz representation for vector fields (`Distribution.polarVector` and `TauCeti.RealRMK.exists_apply_eq_integral_dual`, §4.2), Euclidean stationarity, locally bounded first variation and the total first variation with two of the §4.3 Checks (`Euclidean.IsStationary`, `Euclidean.firstVariationMass`, §4.3), the total variation measure `‖δV‖` with the polar field and the extension of `δV` to continuous fields (`Euclidean.firstVariationMeasure`, `firstVariationVector`, `firstVariationExt`, §4.3), and Euclidean dilations with the line Check (`Euclidean.blowUp`, §3.2) are implemented, as is the varifold carrier on the Grassmann bundle with its weight and extensionality (`TauCeti.grassmannBundle.weight`, `grassmannBundle.ext_of_integral_eq`, §1.3) and the four §1.3 Checks in the Euclidean carrier; there is no rectifiable varifold, Riemannian first variation or stationarity, or generalized mean curvature. An open Tau Ceti PR covers the §1.4 Checks (see Tau Ceti code). Depends on #49, #52.

## Prerequisite chain
Radon measures, Hausdorff measure [Mathlib] -> countably rectifiable sets [Tau Ceti `CountablyRectifiable`; GMT G2] -> approximate tangent planes, area formula [GMT G2, partial: existence, measurable choice and uniqueness of tangent planes, the Lipschitz area formula, tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula done] -> Grassmann bundle G_k(M) [Varifolds §§1.1–1.2; `TauCeti.grassmannBundle` and the fibre `Euclidean.grassmannian` ≅ `finiteGrassmannian` in Tau Ceti; the comparison with GeometricCurrents G6 not implemented] -> varifold = Radon measure on G_k, weight, mass, convergence, pushforward [Varifolds §§1.3–1.4, 3.1–3.2; the carrier on `grassmannBundle` with `weight`, convergence and compactness `VarifoldTendsto`, and Euclidean dilations `Euclidean.blowUp`, in code; general pushforward not] -> rectifiable/integral varifold v(Γ,θ) [Varifolds §§2.1–2.3; §2.1's approximate tangent spaces of measures in code] -> first variation, stationarity, generalized mean curvature [Varifolds Layer 4, the Euclidean first variation `Euclidean.firstVariation`, polar vector field `Distribution.polarVector` and stationarity `Euclidean.IsStationary` and the total variation measure `Euclidean.firstVariationMeasure` in code, with #49's second fundamental form and #52's minimality in §5.1] -> monotonicity, tangent cones [Varifolds Layers 6–8].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged Varifolds is the varifold roadmap (Allard theory: general varifolds, first variation as a vector-valued Radon measure, generalized mean curvature, monotonicity, Allard compactness for integral varifolds, tangent cones; Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*, Ch. 4 and 8; Allard 1972), consuming GeometricMeasureTheory G2 and GeometricCurrents G2 and G6. What remains is implementation. Allard regularity (its named Part II), stable varifolds (Schoen–Simon, Wickramasekera) and min-max theory are outside it and are what the Annals theorems use beyond the statements.
