## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md) specifies the whole definition on a Riemannian manifold and on open subsets of Euclidean space: the Grassmann bundle (§§1.1–1.2), varifolds with weight, mass, support, restriction and varifold convergence (§§1.3–1.4, 53.1), rectifiable, integral and multiplicity-one varifolds `v(Γ, θ)` and `[Σ]` (§§2.2–2.3, 53.2), push-forward and dilations (§§3.1–3.2, 53.3), the first variation, stationarity and the generalized mean curvature (§§4.1, 4.3–4.4, 53.4), the derivative of mass along flows (§4.1, 53.5(a)), the first variation of a `C²` submanifold and stationarity iff minimality (§5.1, 53.5(b)), monotonicity and densities (§§6.1–6.3) and tangent cones of stationary integral varifolds (Layer 8, 53.6), regular and singular sets (§9.1), and the Simon and Edelen–Spolaor statements as acceptance tests (§9.4). In Tau Ceti the Euclidean Grassmannian `G(E, k)` in projection form (`Euclidean.grassmannian`, Varifolds §1.1) and the convergence clause of 53.1, as vague convergence of measures on any space, `TauCeti.VarifoldTendsto`, with its compactness theorem, its compatibility with restriction to open subsets and its locality (§1.4), the image-plane map of §1.1 (`Euclidean.grassmannian.imagePlane`), the Grassmann bundle of a vector bundle with fibre inner products, so `G_k(M)` for `TM` (`TauCeti.grassmannBundle`, §1.2), the weight and open restriction in the Euclidean carrier (§1.3), approximate tangent spaces of measures (`MeasureTheory.Measure.HasApproxTangentSpace`, §2.1), the first variation of a varifold on an open subset of a Euclidean space (`Euclidean.firstVariation`, §4.1), the Euclidean Riesz representation for vector fields (`Distribution.polarVector`, §4.2), and Euclidean stationarity, locally bounded first variation and the total first variation with two of the §4.3 Checks (`Euclidean.IsStationary`, `Euclidean.firstVariationMass`, §4.3) are implemented; there is no varifold carrier over a manifold, rectifiable varifold, Riemannian stationarity or generalized mean curvature. Open Tau Ceti PRs cover the varifold carrier on the Grassmann bundle, the §1.2 degenerate-rank Check, the chart-level Riesz representation, the total variation measure of the first variation and the other §4.3 Checks (see Tau Ceti code). Depends on #49, #52.

## Mathlib
No varifolds. Has Radon measures and weak convergence of finite measures, `MeasureTheory.Measure.hausdorffMeasure`, `euclideanHausdorffMeasure`, `Measure.map`, `Module.Grassmannian` (algebraic). No countably rectifiable sets or approximate tangent planes.

## Prerequisite chain
Radon measures, Hausdorff measure [Mathlib] -> countably rectifiable sets [Tau Ceti `CountablyRectifiable`; GMT G2] -> approximate tangent planes, area formula [GMT G2, partial: existence, measurable choice and uniqueness of tangent planes and the Lipschitz area formula done; tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula missing] -> Grassmann bundle G_k(M) [Varifolds §§1.1–1.2; `TauCeti.grassmannBundle` and the fibre `Euclidean.grassmannian` ≅ `finiteGrassmannian` in Tau Ceti; the comparison with GeometricCurrents G6 not implemented] -> varifold = Radon measure on G_k, weight, mass, convergence, pushforward [Varifolds §§1.3–1.4, 3.1; convergence and compactness `VarifoldTendsto` in code] -> rectifiable/integral varifold v(Γ,θ) [Varifolds §§2.1–2.3; §2.1's approximate tangent spaces of measures in code] -> first variation, stationarity, generalized mean curvature [Varifolds Layer 4, the Euclidean first variation `Euclidean.firstVariation`, polar vector field `Distribution.polarVector` and stationarity `Euclidean.IsStationary` in code, the total variation measure `‖δV‖` and the other §4.3 Checks in open Tau Ceti PRs, with #49's second fundamental form and #52's minimality in §5.1] -> monotonicity, tangent cones [Varifolds Layers 6–8].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged Varifolds is the varifold roadmap (Allard theory: general varifolds, first variation as a vector-valued Radon measure, generalized mean curvature, monotonicity, Allard compactness for integral varifolds, tangent cones; Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*, Ch. 4 and 8; Allard 1972), consuming GeometricMeasureTheory G2 and GeometricCurrents G2 and G6. What remains is implementation. Allard regularity (its named Part II), stable varifolds (Schoen–Simon, Wickramasekera) and min-max theory are outside it and are what the Annals theorems use beyond the statements.
