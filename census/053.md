## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md) specifies the whole definition on a Riemannian manifold and on open subsets of Euclidean space: the Grassmann bundle (§§1.1–1.2), varifolds with weight, mass, support, restriction and varifold convergence (§§1.3–1.4, 53.1), rectifiable, integral and multiplicity-one varifolds `v(Γ, θ)` and `[Σ]` (§§2.2–2.3, 53.2), push-forward and dilations (§§3.1–3.2, 53.3), the first variation, stationarity and the generalized mean curvature (§§4.1, 4.3–4.4, 53.4), the derivative of mass along flows (§4.1, 53.5(a)), the first variation of a `C²` submanifold and stationarity iff minimality (§5.1, 53.5(b)), monotonicity and densities (§§6.1–6.3) and tangent cones of stationary integral varifolds (Layer 8, 53.6), regular and singular sets (§9.1), and the Simon and Edelen–Spolaor statements as acceptance tests (§9.4). In Tau Ceti the Euclidean Grassmannian `G(E, k)` in projection form (`Euclidean.grassmannian`, Varifolds §1.1) and the convergence clause of 53.1, as vague convergence of measures on any space, `TauCeti.VarifoldTendsto`, with its compactness theorem (§1.4), are implemented; there is no varifold carrier, Grassmann bundle over a manifold or first variation. Open Tau Ceti PRs cover the image-plane map, the Grassmann bundle, weight and restriction, approximate tangent spaces of measures and the Euclidean first variation (see Tau Ceti code). Depends on #49, #52.

## Mathlib
No varifolds. Has Radon measures and weak convergence of finite measures, `MeasureTheory.Measure.hausdorffMeasure`, `euclideanHausdorffMeasure`, `Measure.map`, `Module.Grassmannian` (algebraic). No countably rectifiable sets or approximate tangent planes.

## Tau Ceti code
- Varifold convergence: `TauCeti.VarifoldTendsto μ ν` (`∫ f dμ n → ∫ f dν` for every `f ∈ C_c(Y, ℝ)`, on any topological space `Y`), uniqueness of limits, locally bounded masses, semicontinuity of masses of compact and open sets, push-forward along proper continuous maps, and the compactness theorem `TauCeti.exists_subseq_varifoldTendsto` on a second countable locally compact Hausdorff space (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/Convergence.lean`). Stated for measures, not for a varifold carrier on `G_k(W)`.
- `TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable` (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/Basic.lean`).
- The Grassmannian of an inner product space in projection form (Varifolds §1.1): `Euclidean.grassmannian 𝕜 E k` over `RCLike 𝕜` (self-adjoint idempotents with range of rank `k`, operator-norm topology), `Euclidean.grassmannian.submoduleEquiv`, the homeomorphism `Euclidean.grassmannian.finiteGrassmannianHomeomorph` with `TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian ℝ k N`, closedness and compactness `Euclidean.isClosed_grassmannian`, `Euclidean.isCompact_grassmannian`, `tr P = k` (`Euclidean.grassmannian.trace_eq`), the tangential trace bound `Euclidean.grassmannian.norm_trace_comp_le`, the complement homeomorphism `Euclidean.grassmannianOrthogonalHomeomorph` (`P ↦ 1 − P`) and the conjugation homeomorphisms `LinearIsometryEquiv.grassmannianCongr` (`P ↦ U P U⁻¹`) (`Topology/Grassmannian/StarProjection.lean`); the unoriented finite Grassmannian as the orbit quotient of the orientation cover of the tautological bundle (`TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian.orientationCoverQuotientHomeomorph`, `Topology/Grassmannian/Unoriented.lean`, the topological input of GeometricCurrents G6's unoriented tangent-plane fibre); `TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian` with quotient topology (`Topology/Grassmannian/Basic.lean`), oriented/orthonormal variants.
- Approximate tangent planes: a.e. existence for countably rectifiable sets (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/Tangent/Approximate.lean`), a measurable choice of tangent projections (`Rectifiable/Tangent/Measurable.lean`) and a.e. uniqueness (`TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable.ae_eq_of_tendsto_measure_outside_cone_div`, `Rectifiable/Tangent/Uniqueness.lean`); Lipschitz area formula ℝ^m → ℝ^n with multiplicity (`MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/`); coarea formula for Lipschitz maps with a.e. rectifiable fibres (`MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Lipschitz.lean`, `Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/Coarea.lean`).
- Euclidean currents: `mass` (`Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Basic.lean`), the mass measure, normal currents and their weak compactness (`Analysis/Distribution/Mass/{Measure,Normal,Compactness}.lean`), `FlatNormedCurrent` (`Analysis/Distribution/Flat/Completion.lean`), `formCurrent`, smooth pushforward of compactly supported currents (`Analysis/Distribution/CutoffPushforward.lean`) with the homotopy formula along non-proper homotopies and flat estimates and flat and weak convergence for pushforwards along nearby smooth maps (`normalFlatMass_comp_cutoffPullbackCLM_sub_le`, `tendsto_normalFlatMass_comp_cutoffPullbackCLM_sub`, `Analysis/Distribution/Flat/Pushforward.lean`; GeometricCurrents G2), the cone operator with mass bounds (`Mass/Cone.lean`), slicing of locally normal currents by a smooth function (`Mass/Slice.lean`).
- Varifolds §1.3 in the Euclidean carrier `W × Euclidean.grassmannian E k`: the weight `Euclidean.weight` with its integration laws and open restriction `Euclidean.restrictOpens` (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Varifold/Euclidean.lean`).
- No varifold carrier over a manifold, Grassmann bundle over a manifold, rectifiable varifold or first variation. ## Prerequisite chain
Radon measures, Hausdorff measure [Mathlib] -> countably rectifiable sets [Tau Ceti `CountablyRectifiable`; GMT G2] -> approximate tangent planes, area formula [GMT G2, partial: existence, measurable choice and uniqueness of tangent planes and the Lipschitz area formula done; tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula missing] -> Grassmann bundle G_k(M) [Varifolds §§1.1–1.2, on GeometricCurrents G6, not implemented; fibre `Euclidean.grassmannian` ≅ `finiteGrassmannian` in Tau Ceti] -> varifold = Radon measure on G_k, weight, mass, convergence, pushforward [Varifolds §§1.3–1.4, 3.1; convergence and compactness `VarifoldTendsto` in code] -> rectifiable/integral varifold v(Γ,θ) [Varifolds §§2.1–2.3] -> first variation, stationarity, generalized mean curvature [Varifolds Layer 4, with #49's second fundamental form and #52's minimality in §5.1] -> monotonicity, tangent cones [Varifolds Layers 6–8].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged Varifolds is the varifold roadmap (Allard theory: general varifolds, first variation as a vector-valued Radon measure, generalized mean curvature, monotonicity, Allard compactness for integral varifolds, tangent cones; Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*, Ch. 4 and 8; Allard 1972), consuming GeometricMeasureTheory G2 and GeometricCurrents G2 and G6. What remains is implementation. Allard regularity (its named Part II), stable varifolds (Schoen–Simon, Wickramasekera) and min-max theory are outside it and are what the Annals theorems use beyond the statements.
