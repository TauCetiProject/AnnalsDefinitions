# 40. The Laplace–Beltrami operator on L² of a complete Riemannian manifold

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2424 (SchrodingerOperatorsAndSpectralTypes) Layer 3 specifies the definition in its generality: on every Riemannian manifold without boundary the initial operator `Δ₀ = −div grad` on `C_c^∞`, its minimal and maximal operators, the energy form and the Friedrichs Laplacian (§§3.2–3.4), and on complete manifolds the Gaffney–Chernoff–Strichartz theorem (`Δ₀` essentially self-adjoint, `closure Δ₀ = Δ_F = Δ_max`, `H¹ = H¹₀`, §3.6) with the kernel, bottom of the spectrum, ordered eigenvalues below `σ_ess` and `λ₁⁺` (§3.7), the closed-manifold comparison (§3.8), and `[1/4, ∞) ⊆ σ_ess(Δ_X)` for a complete surface with a standard cusp end (§3.10). That inclusion is not the direction the paper needs: Hide–Magee compare `σ(Δ_X) ∩ [0, 1/4 − ε)` as multisets of eigenvalues, which needs `inf σ_ess(Δ_X) ≥ 1/4`, and no roadmap or PR specifies it for a general finite-area hyperbolic surface. No merged roadmap covers the non-compact case in general (AutomorphicSpectralTheory §1.16a specifies it for the modular quotients `Γ₀(N)\ℍ`, CongruenceSpectralGap §0.2 for every congruence quotient); the closed-manifold realization is merged (ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.7 (K5a), PseudodifferentialOperators P7/P7a, RiemannianGeometry R5c), as are the Friedrichs extension and essential spectrum (UnboundedOperators UO-D06, UO-C02). The equality `σ_ess = [1/4, ∞)` for a general finite-area hyperbolic surface (Prop 40.4(ii)) is named by PR #2424 as a downstream roadmap's and is specified only for congruence quotients (merged CongruenceSpectralGap §0.6).

## Mathlib
- Has: `IsRiemannianManifold`, Riemannian (e)distance, `LinearPMap` with adjoint, closure, `IsSelfAdjoint` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap.lean`), spectrum.
- Missing: Laplace–Beltrami on manifolds, Riemannian volume L², unbounded spectral theorem, essential spectrum (Tau Ceti has the unbounded spectral PVM; see below).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Riemannian manifolds, distance, completeness, `LinearPMap`: Mathlib (+ Tau Ceti Hopf–Rinow).
2. Smooth Laplace–Beltrami, Green's formula: Tau Ceti.
3. Riemannian volume L² and H¹ on closed manifolds: Tau Ceti (closed only); `H¹(X)`, `H¹₀(X)` and `H²_loc` on any manifold: PR #2424 §§3.3–3.4 (with K5a's interior regularity).
4. Abstract Friedrichs extension / closed forms: target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-D06; the closed-form representation theorem and the Friedrichs extension of a symmetric operator bounded below are in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.existsUnique_selfAdjoint_of_closed_form`, `LinearPMap.friedrichsExtension`); the extremal property in form order (`LinearPMap.friedrichsSpaceMap`) and in resolvent order (`LinearPMap.resolvent_le_resolvent_friedrichsExtension`) is in Tau Ceti, as are form cores (`LinearPMap.IsFormCore`), unitary and orthogonal-sum transport of form operators, and closed semibounded forms on a subspace with their self-adjoint operators (`TauCeti.SemiboundedForm`, `isSelfAdjoint_operator`).
5. Closed-manifold self-adjoint realization, compact resolvent, eigenvalue sequence: target ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.7 (K5a), consumed by PseudodifferentialOperators P7 §7.1 and P7a §8.1 and RiemannianGeometry R5c (merged; the realization is not implemented, the eigenbasis with finite multiplicities of PseudodifferentialOperators §8.1 is, `TauCeti.Manifold.SobolevH1.exists_hilbertBasis_mem_weakEigenspace`); compared with `Δ_X` in PR #2424 §3.8.
6. Essential spectrum σ_ess, discrete spectrum, multiplicity: target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-C02, not implemented (Weyl's criterion requested by PR #2424 as a gap in UO-C02).
7. L² Laplacian on any Riemannian manifold, essential self-adjointness on complete X (Gaffney–Chernoff–Strichartz), kernel, ordered eigenvalues, `λ₁⁺`: PR #2424 §§3.2–3.7.
8. σ_ess ⊇ [1/4, ∞) for a surface with a cusp end: PR #2424 §3.10. The direction the paper needs, `inf σ_ess ≥ 1/4` (finitely many eigenvalues below `1/4`), and equality for finite-area hyperbolic surfaces (Prop 40.4(ii)): only for congruence Γ in merged CongruenceSpectralGap §0.6 (the realization is merged CongruenceSpectralGap §0.2, and AutomorphicSpectralTheory §1.16a for `Γ₀(N)`, neither implemented); general Fuchsian Γ unplanned (PR #2424 names it a downstream roadmap's).

## Library-scale gap
The definition is PR #2424 Layer 3 (Strichartz, J. Funct. Anal. 1983; Grigor'yan, *Heat Kernel and Analysis on Manifolds*, Ch. 11; Braverman–Milatovic–Shubin 2002): what remains for it is review and merging, then implementation on UnboundedOperators UO-D06 (form representation and Friedrichs extension in code) and UO-C02 and K5a's local regularity. What Hide–Magee additionally need is the spectral theory of finite-area hyperbolic surfaces: decomposition into a compact core plus cusps, the decomposition principle, `σ_ess = [1/4, ∞)` via separation of variables in cusps, and finite covers (Borthwick, *Spectral Theory of Infinite-Area Hyperbolic Surfaces*; Iwaniec, *Spectral Methods of Automorphic Forms*, Ch. 4–7; Lax–Phillips 1980), a roadmap beside PR #2424 consuming its Layers 1 and 3, of which merged CongruenceSpectralGap's congruence construction would be an instance.
