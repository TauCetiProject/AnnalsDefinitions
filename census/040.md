# 40. The Laplace–Beltrami operator on L² of a complete Riemannian manifold

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. The smooth Laplace–Beltrami operator is in Tau Ceti, and the closed-manifold self-adjoint realization is a merged target (ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.7 (K5a), consumed by PseudodifferentialOperators P7/P7a; the closed-manifold spectrum of `Δ⁺ + q` in RiemannianGeometry R5c). The abstract Friedrichs extension and essential spectrum are merged targets (UnboundedOperators UO-D06, UO-C02). Open PRs specify the L² Laplacian on congruence hyperbolic surfaces (PR 1504, PR 925). Nobody specifies the general complete, non-compact case (Gaffney/Chernoff essential self-adjointness), which Hide–Magee need for general finite-area hyperbolic surfaces and their covers.

## Mathlib
- Has: `IsRiemannianManifold`, Riemannian (e)distance, `LinearPMap` with adjoint, closure, `IsSelfAdjoint` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap.lean`), spectrum.
- Missing: Laplace–Beltrami on manifolds, Riemannian volume L², unbounded spectral theorem, essential spectrum.

## Tau Ceti code
- Smooth operator: `TauCeti.Manifold.laplaceBeltrami` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Laplacian.lean`, Δ = div ∘ grad, opposite sign to 40).
- Closed manifolds, weak/H¹ level: `TauCeti.Manifold.SobolevH1`, `dirichletForm`, Poincaré–Wirtinger, Poisson solve `existsUnique_dirichletForm_eq`, Schrödinger form solve (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sobolev/`, `Poisson.lean`, `Schrodinger.lean`). No `LinearPMap` realization on manifolds (no LinearPMap in `Geometry/Manifold`).
- Operator theory: Kato–Rellich `IsSelfAdjoint.add_of_isRelativelyBounded`, compact-resolvent/eigenbasis API and the Green operator of a self-adjoint operator with compact resolvent (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/{CompactResolvent,Eigenbasis,Green}.lean`), `TauCeti.ProjValMeasure`. Euclidean model: the Dirichlet realization `TauCeti.PDE.dirichletOperator` on `L²(Ω)`, self-adjoint with compact resolvent on bounded `Ω` (`Analysis/PDE/Spectrum/Operator.lean`).
- Distributions on manifolds: continuous linear maps on smooth sections have compact support (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Section/DualSupport.lean`), the support part of the CKG §5.1 test/distribution layer.
- Hyperbolic geometry files exist (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hyperbolic*`), no spectral theory there.

## Merged roadmaps
- DifferentialGeometry Layer 12: smooth Laplace–Beltrami, Green identities (largely implemented).
- ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.7 (K5a): self-adjoint H² realization with compact resolvent of the Hodge Laplacian (on functions, minus `laplaceBeltrami`) on compact boundaryless Riemannian manifolds, eigensection decomposition. Not started: the smooth `hodgeLaplacian` and the abstract `LinearPMap.HasCompactResolvent` API exist, and integer-order bundle Sobolev spaces `TauCeti.SobolevSection` over a finite bundle atlas exist (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Sobolev/`), but there is no H² realization, real-order distributional Sobolev scale or Rellich on manifolds. It needs PseudodifferentialOperators P1.0 and CKG §5.4 (compact-bundle Sobolev scale) and §5.1 (density/test layer).
- PseudodifferentialOperators P7 and §8.1 (P7a) "Quotients and the scalar realization": closed connected X, Δ = L − 1 with self-adjoint H² domain, pure point spectrum, finite multiplicities, finite-deck transplantation for free isometric actions. These sections consume CKG §5.7's realization rather than construct it. STATUS: Layer 8 untouched. This is Prop 40.4(i) and the finite-cover comparison, closed case only.
- RiemannianGeometry R5c "Schrödinger operators on compact manifolds": for `L = Δ⁺ + q` on a closed manifold (with `Δ⁺ = −laplaceBeltrami`), spectrum, min–max, the lowest eigenvalue and the first positive eigenvalue of `Δ⁺` with its Rayleigh characterization (closed case of Prop 40.4(i)). R7 (scalar/rough Laplacians), Completed/HopfRinow (completeness).
- `TauCetiRoadmap/OperatorTheory/UnboundedOperators/` ([README](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/OperatorTheory/UnboundedOperators/README.md)): UO-D06 (§4.7) closed forms and the Friedrichs extension; UO-C02 (§3.2) `discreteSpectrum`, `essentialSpectrum` of a partial operator with the Fredholm characterization `notMem_essentialSpectrum_iff`; UO-C03 a.c. spectrum (definition #35). Not implemented.
- None specifies the complete non-compact case (Gaffney) or the essential spectrum of a general finite-area hyperbolic surface.

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR 1504 "CongruenceSpectralGap: roadmap" §0.2 "Positive orbifold Laplacian": Friedrichs realization of −y²(∂ₓ²+∂ᵧ²) on L²(Γ\ℍ) for every congruence Γ ≤ SL₂(ℤ), maximal domain, kernel = constants; later sections give σ_ac = [1/4, ∞). Arithmetic Γ only.
- PR 925 "Roadmap: automorphic spectral theory and trace distributions" §1.16/1.16a `modular_laplacian_realization` for Γ₀(N); PR 1717 "feat: roadmap for maass forms" §0.1 (draft).
- None covers an arbitrary complete finite-area hyperbolic surface, its finite covers, or general complete manifolds (Gaffney).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Riemannian manifolds, distance, completeness, `LinearPMap`: Mathlib (+ Tau Ceti Hopf–Rinow).
2. Smooth Laplace–Beltrami, Green's formula: Tau Ceti.
3. Riemannian volume L² and H¹ on closed manifolds: Tau Ceti (closed only).
4. Abstract Friedrichs extension / closed forms: target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-D06, not implemented.
5. Closed-manifold self-adjoint realization, compact resolvent, eigenvalue sequence: target ComplexKahlerGeometry §5.7 (K5a), consumed by PseudodifferentialOperators P7 §7.1 and P7a §8.1 (merged, not implemented).
6. Essential spectrum σ_ess, discrete spectrum, multiplicity: target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-C02, not implemented.
7. L²(X) and H¹(X) on non-compact manifolds, density of C_c^∞ in H¹ for complete X, essential self-adjointness of Δ_0 (Gaffney–Chernoff–Strichartz): unplanned.
8. σ_ess = [1/4, ∞) for finite-area cusped hyperbolic surfaces (Prop 40.4(ii)): only for congruence Γ in PR 1504; general Fuchsian Γ unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
Needed: L² analysis on complete non-compact Riemannian manifolds: Gaffney's cutoff argument, essential self-adjointness and uniqueness of the L² Laplacian, heat/resolvent kernels, Persson's characterization of the bottom of σ_ess, and decomposition of finite-area hyperbolic surfaces into compact core plus cusps with σ_ess = [1/4, ∞) via separation of variables in cusps (Strichartz, J. Funct. Anal. 1983; Grigor'yan, *Heat Kernel and Analysis on Manifolds*, Ch. 11; Borthwick, *Spectral Theory of Infinite-Area Hyperbolic Surfaces*, and Iwaniec, *Spectral Methods of Automorphic Forms*, Ch. 4–7 for cusps). It would sit beside RiemannianGeometry and consume UnboundedOperators (UO-D06, UO-C02); PR 1504's congruence construction would then be an instance.
