# 87. Tate–Shafarevich group of an abelian variety over a number field

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [SelmerIwasawaCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SelmerIwasawaCohomology/README.md) §2.12a "Abelian Kummer and Sha interfaces" (README l.569, merged PR #869) specifies the whole definition: for native `AbelianVariety` over a number field and an isogeny `φ : A → B`, the points `AbelianVariety.Points A L`, torsion, the Kummer map, `Sel_φ(A/K)`, `Ш(A/K)` as Mathlib's `ContinuousCohomology.tateShafarevich` in degree one for the discrete module `A(Kˢ)` over all places, the local `H¹` comparison with `A(K_vˢ)` (the point of Remark 2), the exact sequence 87.4(i), and finiteness of `Sel_φ(A/K)` and of `Ш(A/K)[n]` (87.4(ii)–(iii)). Isogeny invariance of finiteness (87.4(iv)) is not a named target; it follows from §2.12a by the argument of 87.4(iv). Merged HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions §§1.3–1.5 consumes these carriers. Not IN-CODE: Tau Ceti has all of this for elliptic curves (`WeierstrassCurve.sha`, isogeny Selmer groups, `finite_shafarevichTateGroup_iff`) and §2.1 with the module-theoretic and compact/rational parts of §2.2 (`TauCeti.SelmerData.selmerModule`, `TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.TopSelmerStructure`), but no point group, Galois cohomology or Sha of an abelian variety of dimension > 1.

5. `H¹(K,A)`, `res_v`, `Sha(A/K)`, `Sel^φ(A/K)`, 87.4(i)–(iii): target of merged SelmerIwasawaCohomology §2.12a (not implemented), on its §§2.1–2.2 (in code except the arithmetic instantiation and the Kummer Checks: `TauCeti.SelmerData`, `TauCeti.ContinuousCohomology.TopSelmerStructure`), consumed by merged HeightsRationalPointsAndObstructions §§1.3–1.5; finiteness of `Sel_φ` uses ArithmeticGaloisDuality §R02.3.1 (finiteness of `H¹(G_{K,S}, M)`, in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.RestrictedRamification.galoisGroupS`, `finite_H1_galoisGroupS`, `NumberTheory/NumberField/RestrictedRamification/`; the restriction map `G_K → G_{K,S}` in Tau Ceti, `RestrictedRamification.restrictionS`, the localisation maps not; roots of `S`-units unramified outside `S` and `n` in Tau Ceti, `isUnramifiedIn_adjoin_simple_of_pow_eq_of_mem_unit`; the Hermite input `finite_of_finrank_le_of_ramifiedSupport_subset` is in Tau Ceti); `Ш(A/F)` and its identification with the Galois-module version also merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §6.4b.
