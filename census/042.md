The Euclidean quasiregular maps of QuasiregularMaps §2.9, `TauCeti.IsQuasiregularOn` (`TauCeti/Analysis/Quasiregular/Euclidean.lean`), are in code; they are maps of a Euclidean domain to itself, not into a manifold `N`. So is the radial stretch `x ↦ ‖x‖^(a−1) x` as a Euclidean quasiregular map with sharp constant, the §2.9 and §3.2 radial-stretch Checks (`isQuasiregularOn_norm_rpow_smul_self_of_one_le`, `Analysis/Quasiregular/RadialStretch.lean`). For the triangulations of §7.7 (Remark 3), smooth triangulations `TauCeti.IsSmoothTriangulation` are in code (`Geometry/Manifold/SmoothTriangulation/`), with these parts of QuasiregularMaps §6.1: a complex triangulating a finite-dimensional manifold is locally finite (`AbstractSimplicialComplex.finite_setOf_image_range_faceInclusion_inter_nonempty_of_manifold`, `SmoothTriangulation/LocallyFinite.lean`), has no simplices above the dimension (`IsSmoothTriangulation.dimension_le`, `SmoothTriangulation/Dimension.lean`), and its simplices of dimension below `n` have null image, as does the whole lower skeleton for complexes with any number of simplices (`IsSmoothTriangulation.isNullSet_image_range_faceInclusion`, `IsSmoothTriangulation.isNullSet_biUnion_image_range_faceInclusion`, `SmoothTriangulation/Null.lean`), and is pure of dimension `dim E` for a manifold modelled on `E` (`TauCeti.IsSmoothTriangulation.isPure`, `SmoothTriangulation/Pure.lean`).

## Merged roadmaps
- [QuasiregularMaps](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/QuasiregularMaps/README.md) (PR #2134), Layer 7: §7.1 "Quasiregular ellipticity" (l.1293, the definition, 42.1(a)–(b)); §7.2 (l.1316) independence of orientation and §7.3 (l.1332) of the metric for closed `N` (Remark 1); §7.6 (l.1442) flat manifolds are 1-quasiregularly elliptic; §7.7 (l.1483) simplicial branched covers by tori (Remark 3). §7.1 *Needs* §2.1 and §2.6 (non-constant maps have `J_f > 0` on a set of positive measure). Not implemented.
- `TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/` ([README](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/README.md)) supplies `W^{1,n}_loc(ℝⁿ, N)`, `Df`, `‖Df‖` and `J_f` (§§4.2–4.3, §5.1, §§6.1–6.3; see #41 and #47), on source manifolds modelled on real normed spaces as in QuasiregularMaps' conventions; its Layer 8 (pullback of forms by Sobolev maps, weak `d` commutes with pullback) and Layer 11 (degree of `W^{1,n}` maps, `∫ J_f = deg f · vol N`) are the inputs a QuasiregularMaps Part III would consume. Also DifferentialGeometry 2.1–2.2, 12.4; RiemannianGeometry R4 item 7 for flat quotients used in examples.
- [SobolevDifferentialForms](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevDifferentialForms/README.md) (#1609) builds `WᵖΛᵏ(Ω)`, the weak exterior derivative and the L² de Rham complex on Euclidean domains only (§2.1 l.212, §3.1 l.278, §6.2 l.533; Scope l.41 excludes Riemannian manifolds); a Part III input for the theorem, not for the definition. Of it only the Hodge decomposition of an abstract Hilbert complex and the closed-range Poincaré criterion of §6.1 (`LinearPMap.harmonicSpace`, `topologicalClosure_range_sup_harmonicSpace_sup_topologicalClosure_range_adjoint`, `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/HilbertComplex.lean`; `LinearPMap.isClosed_range_iff_exists_norm_le_mul_norm`, `LinearPMap/ClosedRange.lean`) is in code, the rest is not.
