## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2491 "MinimalSurfaces: stability across a singular set and strict stability" adds [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md) §7.8 "Stability across a singular set, strict stability and minimal cones", which specifies the whole definition in a Riemannian `(n+1)`-manifold: `sing Σ = (Σ̄ ∩ U) ∖ Σ` with Simon's convention, locally finite area, ambient test functions `ζ ∈ C^∞_c(U)` that need not vanish near `sing Σ`, stability and strict stability with a continuous weight `w` and constant `κ` read without `∞ − ∞` (65.1, 65.2(a)–(b), the Euclidean case `Ric = 0` being Simon's (1.2)), the weights `|X|` and `|x| + |ξ|` with their comparison (65.2(c)), the variational meaning, `C¹_c` test functions and dilation invariance (Remarks), the reduction 65.3(a) with `κ = ½ min(λ, (1 + c₀/λ)⁻¹)`, and the cone criterion 65.3(b) (`λ₁ + ((n−2)/2)² > 0` iff strictly stable, `≥ 0` iff stable) with the sharp weighted Hardy inequality and the Simons cone as a Check. No merged roadmap has strict stability: the stability form, Jacobi operator and stability of smooth minimal hypersurfaces are RiemannianGeometry R5b and MinimalSurfaces MS6–MS7 targets, minimal cones an R5a.2 target, and merged Varifolds states Simon's theorem without its strict-stability clause. Nothing is implemented. Depends on #49, #52, #53.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #2491 "MinimalSurfaces: stability across a singular set and strict stability": a revision of merged RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces adding §7.8 (README l.1660 of the PR head) for a smooth embedded hypersurface `Σ`, two-sided or not, in an open `U` of a Riemannian manifold, `n ≥ 2`. Definitions: stable in `U` (`∫|A|²ζ² + ∫Ric(ν,ν)ζ² ≤ ∫|∇ζ|²` for every `ζ ∈ C^∞_c(U)`) and strictly stable with weight `w` and constant `κ` (65.2(b) with the Ricci term, `w⁻² = +∞` where `w = 0`). Proofs: variational meaning for minimal two-sided `Σ` and `ζ` vanishing near `sing Σ` (65.1), elementary properties (strict implies stable, `C¹_c` test functions, comparable weights, the `√2` comparison of `|x| + |ξ|` with the distance to the axis, dilation invariance), the reduction (65.3(a)), and the cone theorem for `C` over a closed embedded hypersurface `Γ ⊂ Sⁿ`, `n ≥ 3`, with `|A_C|²(tω) = t⁻²|A_Γ|²(ω)` and `λ₁` of `−Δ_Γ − |A_Γ|²` (65.3(b)), by separation of variables and the weighted Hardy inequality `∫ f′² r^(n−1) ≥ ((n−2)/2)² ∫ f² r^(n−3)`. Checks: the Simons cone (`λ₁ = −6`, `λ₁ + 25/4 = 1/4`), the cones `C_(p,q)`, the plane in `ℝ³` (stable, not strictly stable), a half-plane whose singular line must be crossed, and `ℝP² ⊂ ℝP³`. *Needs:* MinimalSurfaces' stability form and 6.3, R5b, MS1 (a) on the link, R5a.2. Sources: Simon 2023 §1 and 4.12, Simon 2021 §§1, 3, Chodosh Prop. 26.1. It asserts nothing about the singular set itself (regularity, tangent cones), which the paper's theorems use through #53.
- No other PR mentions Simon's inequality (S) or strict stability as a target. (Merged GlobalGaloisDeformations uses "strict stability" in an unrelated, Galois-deformation sense.)

## Prerequisite chain
|A_M|, tangential gradient (#49) [R5a; Euclidean case and the intrinsic `B` of a map in Tau Ceti] -> minimal hypersurface, stability form Q_M (65.1) [R5b] -> stability (65.2a) [R5b, MinimalSurfaces MS6–MS7, smooth case] -> hypersurfaces with singular set, locally finite area, stationary varifold (#53) [Varifolds §§2.3, 4.3, 9.1] -> stability and weighted strict stability (S) with ambient test functions crossing `sing M` (65.2) [PR #2491 §7.8] -> reduction 65.3(a), minimal cones, separation of variables, sharp Hardy inequality, λ₁ + ((N−2)/2)² > 0 criterion, Simons cone (65.3(b)) [PR #2491 §7.8, on R5a.2 and MS1 (a)].

## Library-scale gap
For the definition, PR #2491 §7.8 is the roadmap: it extends MinimalSurfaces Layer 7 (on R5b and R5a.2) by stability across a singular set, strict stability and the cone criterion (Simon, *Stable minimal hypersurfaces in ℝ^(N+1+ℓ) with singular set an arbitrary closed K ⊂ {0} × ℝ^ℓ*, §1 and 4.12; Simon, *A Liouville-type theorem for stable minimal hypersurfaces*, §§1, 3; Chodosh's notes, Prop. 26.1). The paper's theorems need more than the definition: Jacobi fields on cones and the Hardt-Simon foliation, the Caffarelli-Hardt-Simon construction, Allard-Almgren uniqueness of tangent cones and the regularity theory of stationary varifolds near a singular set (Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*; Hardt-Simon 1985), which no roadmap or PR plans beyond Varifolds' tangent cones and GeometricCurrents G6–G7.
