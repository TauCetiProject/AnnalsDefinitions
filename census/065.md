## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Strict stability (Simon's weighted inequality (S)) is not a target; the stability form, Jacobi operator and stability of minimal hypersurfaces are RiemannianGeometry R5b targets and minimal cones over minimal submanifolds of spheres are an R5a.2 target, all unimplemented. Depends on #49, #52, #53.

## Mathlib
Nothing on minimal hypersurfaces or Hardy inequalities on submanifolds. Has Hausdorff measure, `MeasureTheory.Measure.withDensity`, Lp spaces.

## Tau Ceti code
Euclidean `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm` (gives |A_M|² for a parametrized hypersurface in ℝ^{N+1}); coercive-form spectral API `IsCoercive.isLeast_rayleighQuotient` (R5b supplier). No stability form, Hardy inequality, or strict stability.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5b: Q(φν, ψν) = ∫[⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩ − (|A|² + Ric(ν,ν))φψ], stability Q ≥ 0, Dirichlet Jacobi operator and its least eigenvalue on relatively compact domains. Smooth, singular set not allowed; no weight r̃, no strict version. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: minimal-cone equivalence away from the vertex, H_C(rx) = m/((m+1)r) H_M(x). Not implemented.
- GeometricCurrents G6-G7: regularity of minimizing hypersurfaces (singular set codimension ≥ 7), tangent cones; no strict stability.
- No roadmap mentions strict stability, Simon's inequality, Simons cone or Hardy inequality on cones (grep).

## Open roadmap PRs
None (only PR #920, a Galois-deformation PR, contains "strictly stable", unrelated). PR #956 (positive mass) uses stable minimal hypersurfaces via R5b only.

## Prerequisite chain
|A_M|, tangential gradient (#49) [R5a; Euclidean case in Tau Ceti] -> minimal hypersurface, stability form Q_M (65.1) [R5b] -> stability (65.2a) [R5b, smooth case] -> hypersurfaces with singular set, locally finite area, stationary varifold (#53) [unplanned for general varifolds] -> weighted strict stability (S) (65.2b) [unplanned] -> minimal cones, separation of variables, sharp Hardy inequality, λ₁ + ((N−2)/2)² > 0 criterion, Simons cone (65.3) [cones in R5a.2; rest unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
A "singular minimal hypersurfaces and minimal cones" roadmap: stability of cones (Simons; Bombieri-De Giorgi-Giusti), sharp Hardy inequality on cones, Jacobi fields and the Hardt-Simon foliation, strict stability and Simon's/Caffarelli-Hardt-Simon constructions, Allard-Almgren uniqueness of tangent cones (Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*; Simon, *Theorems on Regularity and Singularity of Energy Minimizing Maps*; Hardt-Simon 1985). It would extend RiemannianGeometry R5b and the varifold layer proposed for #53.
