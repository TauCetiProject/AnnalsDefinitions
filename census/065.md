## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Strict stability (Simon's weighted inequality (S)) is not a target; the stability form, Jacobi operator and stability of minimal hypersurfaces are RiemannianGeometry R5b targets, the index and the equivalence of stability with positive Jacobi functions are MinimalSurfaces MS6–MS7 targets, and minimal cones over minimal submanifolds of spheres are an R5a.2 target, all unimplemented and all for smooth hypersurfaces. Depends on #49, #52, #53.

## Mathlib
Nothing on minimal hypersurfaces or Hardy inequalities on submanifolds. Has Hausdorff measure, `MeasureTheory.Measure.withDensity`, Lp spaces.

## Tau Ceti code
Euclidean `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm` (gives |A_M|² for a parametrized hypersurface in ℝ^{N+1}); coercive-form spectral API `IsCoercive.isLeast_rayleighQuotient` (R5b supplier). No stability form, Hardy inequality, or strict stability.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5b: Q(φν, ψν) = ∫[⟨∇φ,∇ψ⟩ − (|A|² + Ric(ν,ν))φψ], stability Q ≥ 0, Dirichlet Jacobi operator and its least eigenvalue on relatively compact domains. Smooth, singular set not allowed; no weight r̃, no strict version. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: minimal-cone equivalence away from the vertex, H_C(rx) = m/((m+1)r) H_M(x). Not implemented.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md) MS6 (Morse index, compact and by exhaustion) and MS7.1–7.2 (stability of a connected two-sided minimal hypersurface is equivalent to positive Jacobi functions on relatively compact domains, `Q(φ) = ∫ h²|∇(φ/h)|²`). Smooth hypersurfaces only, no weight, no singular set, no strict version. Not implemented.
- GeometricCurrents G6-G7: regularity of minimizing hypersurfaces (singular set codimension ≥ 7), tangent cones; no strict stability.
- [PositiveMass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PositiveMass/README.md) Layer 3 uses compact stable minimal hypersurfaces through R5b only.
- GeometricCurrents G7 constructs the minimizing Simons cone in ambient dimension 8 as its sharpness example, and [OperatorTheory/UnboundedOperators](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/OperatorTheory/UnboundedOperators/README.md) has the half-line Hardy inequality `∫|u′|² ≥ ¼∫|u/r|²`. No roadmap mentions strict stability, Simon's inequality (S) or Hardy inequalities on cones (grep).

## Prerequisite chain
|A_M|, tangential gradient (#49) [R5a; Euclidean case in Tau Ceti] -> minimal hypersurface, stability form Q_M (65.1) [R5b] -> stability (65.2a) [R5b, MinimalSurfaces MS6–MS7, smooth case] -> hypersurfaces with singular set, locally finite area, stationary varifold (#53) [unplanned for general varifolds] -> weighted strict stability (S) (65.2b) [unplanned] -> minimal cones, separation of variables, sharp Hardy inequality, λ₁ + ((N−2)/2)² > 0 criterion, Simons cone (65.3) [cones in R5a.2; rest unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
A "singular minimal hypersurfaces and minimal cones" roadmap: stability of cones (Simons; Bombieri-De Giorgi-Giusti), sharp Hardy inequality on cones, Jacobi fields and the Hardt-Simon foliation, strict stability and Simon's/Caffarelli-Hardt-Simon constructions, Allard-Almgren uniqueness of tangent cones (Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*; Simon, *Theorems on Regularity and Singularity of Energy Minimizing Maps*; Hardt-Simon 1985). It would extend RiemannianGeometry R5b and the varifold layer proposed for #53.
