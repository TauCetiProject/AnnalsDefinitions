## Prerequisite chain
1. Simple polygon P ⊂ ℝ², sides, vertices, inward normals: Mathlib affine geometry has the pieces, no packaged polygon: unplanned.
2. Phase space `T_1(P)=(P×S¹)/∼` with reflections: unplanned.
3. Billiard trajectory, collision times, finiteness of collisions in finite time (93.3a), null singular set Σ (93.3b): unplanned.
4. Billiard flow `F^t` on `X_P`, Borel measurability, Liouville measure and its invariance: unplanned.
5. Weak mixing of a measure-preserving ℝ-flow (no measurable eigenfunctions / ergodicity of F×F): unplanned (FactorRigidity has the von Neumann-algebra analogue for group actions).
6. For the rational case (Thm 1.3 of the paper): unfolding to translation surfaces, straight-line flows: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
Two pieces: (a) an "ErgodicTheoryOfFlows" layer with mixing and weak mixing for measure-preserving ℤ- and ℝ-actions, spectral characterisations (Koopman eigenfunctions, ergodicity of the product), suspension flows and cross-sections; (b) a "PolygonalBilliardsAndTranslationSurfaces" roadmap: billiards in polygons, Katok-Zemlyakov unfolding, translation surfaces, straight-line flows and interval exchange transformations, Masur-Veech unique ergodicity basics. Textbooks: Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Masur-Tabachnikov, "Rational billiards and flat structures" (Handbook of Dynamical Systems 1A); Cornfeld-Fomin-Sinai, *Ergodic Theory*; Walters ch. 1-2 for weak mixing. (a) would sit beside FactorRigidity's weak-mixing section; (b) would sit beside the open TeichmüllerGeometry PR #1169.
