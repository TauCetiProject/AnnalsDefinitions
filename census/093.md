The merged roadmap MathematicalBilliards specifies the whole definition: polygonal tables with polygonal holes (§7.1), the reflections, the phase space `(P × Circle)/∼` and the Liouville probability `μ_P` (§7.2, 93.1), trajectories with finitely many reflections in bounded time and the corner bound (§7.3, 93.2, 93.3(a)), the Borel null singular set `Σ` (§8.1, 93.3(b)), the billiard flow as an action of `ℝ` fixing `Σ`, jointly measurable (§8.2, 93.4), its invariance and reversibility (§8.3), and weak mixing of measure-preserving `ℝ`-actions (`IsWeaklyMixing`, §12.1) with the eigenfunction criterion `isWeaklyMixing_iff` and the multiplier property `ergodicVAdd_prod_of_isWeaklyMixing` (§12.5), which give the two forms of weak mixing the paper uses; the rational-polygon remark (unfolding to translation surfaces) is Layers 9–11. In code are the §7.1 carrier `TauCeti.PolygonalTable` with its vertices and sides (not the interior angles), the §7.2 side reflections, the §7.3 trajectory and corner bound on a bare wedge, the strong continuity of the Koopman group (§12.3), Wiener's lemma (§12.4) and the continuous-time mean ergodic projection of unitary groups with the spectral atoms (§12.4.1); the phase space, the trajectories of a polygonal table, the flow and weak mixing are not. Open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) consumes the flow and §§12.1–12.5 from MathematicalBilliards and defines weak mixing again as `WeakMixingVAdd` (§1.0, §3.2).

## Library-scale gap
The merged MathematicalBilliards supplies the polygonal billiard flow, weak mixing of real flows (Cornfeld–Fomin–Sinai, *Ergodic Theory*, Ch. 1, 6; Walters ch. 1-2) and the unfolding of rational polygons (reflection groups, translation surfaces, directional flows and the Katok–Zemlyakov conjugacy; Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Masur–Tabachnikov, "Rational billiards and flat structures", Handbook of Dynamical Systems 1A). What remains for the definition is implementation of the rest of its Layers 7–8 and 12 (the §7.1 carrier and §§12.4–12.4.1 are in code). The paper's further theorems need strata of translation surfaces, the `SL₂(ℝ)` action and Masur–Veech theory, which MathematicalBilliards names outside it and no roadmap plans (the merged TeichmullerGeometry stops at quadratic differentials).
