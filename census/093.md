In code are the §7.1 carrier `TauCeti.PolygonalTable` with its vertices and sides (not the interior angles), the §7.2 side reflections, the Lebesgue-null frontier, the phase space `Z_P` and the Liouville probability `μ_P` with its null frontier states (93.1), the unit-square Checks, the §7.3 trajectory and corner bound on a bare wedge, the reflection group of §9.1, weak mixing of measure-preserving `ℝ`-actions `IsWeaklyMixing` with its `L²` form and weak mixing implying ergodicity (§§12.1–12.2), the strong continuity of the Koopman group (§12.3), Wiener's lemma (§12.4) and the continuous-time mean ergodic projection of unitary groups with the spectral atoms (§12.4.1), and of §12.5's `isWeaklyMixing_iff` the easy direction (eigenfunctions of a weakly mixing action are almost everywhere constant) with both halves of the spectral argument for the converse (correlation averages of unitary groups and measurable eigenfunctions as Koopman eigenvectors), not yet combined into the converse; the trajectories of a polygonal table, the flow and the multiplier property are not. Merged ClassicalErgodicTheory consumes the flow and §§12.1–12.5 from MathematicalBilliards and defines weak mixing again as `WeakMixingVAdd` (§1.0, §3.2).

## Library-scale gap
The merged MathematicalBilliards supplies the polygonal billiard flow, weak mixing of real flows (Cornfeld–Fomin–Sinai, *Ergodic Theory*, Ch. 1, 6; Walters ch. 1-2) and the unfolding of rational polygons (reflection groups, translation surfaces, directional flows and the Katok–Zemlyakov conjugacy; Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Masur–Tabachnikov, "Rational billiards and flat structures", Handbook of Dynamical Systems 1A). What remains for the definition is implementation of the rest of its Layers 7–8 and 12 (the §7.1 carrier, §7.2, §§12.1–12.3, §§12.4–12.4.1 and of §12.5 the easy direction and the two halves of the spectral argument for the converse are in code). The paper's further theorems need strata of translation surfaces, the `SL₂(ℝ)` action and Masur–Veech theory, which MathematicalBilliards names outside it and no roadmap plans (the merged TeichmullerGeometry stops at quadratic differentials).
