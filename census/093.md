## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. The merged roadmap MathematicalBilliards specifies the whole definition: polygonal tables with polygonal holes (§7.1), the reflections, the phase space `(P × Circle)/∼` and the Liouville probability `μ_P` (§7.2, 93.1), trajectories with finitely many reflections in bounded time and the corner bound (§7.3, 93.2, 93.3(a)), the Borel null singular set `Σ` (§8.1, 93.3(b)), the billiard flow as an action of `ℝ` fixing `Σ`, jointly measurable (§8.2, 93.4), its invariance and reversibility (§8.3), and weak mixing of measure-preserving `ℝ`-actions (`IsWeaklyMixing`, §12.1) with the eigenfunction criterion `isWeaklyMixing_iff` and the multiplier property `ergodicVAdd_prod_of_isWeaklyMixing` (§12.5), which give the two forms of weak mixing the paper uses; the rational-polygon remark (unfolding to translation surfaces) is Layers 9–11. None of it is implemented. Open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) consumes the flow and §§12.1–12.5 from MathematicalBilliards and defines weak mixing again as `WeakMixingVAdd` (§1.0, §3.2).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Simple polygon P ⊂ ℝ², sides, vertices: Mathlib `Polygon`, Tau Ceti `IsJordanCurve`, `TauCeti.SimplePolygon`; polygonal table carrier with holes: MathematicalBilliards §7.1 (merged, not implemented).
2. Phase space `T_1(P)=(P×S¹)/∼` with reflections, Liouville measure: MathematicalBilliards §7.2.
3. Billiard trajectory, finiteness of reflections in finite time (93.3a), null singular set Σ (93.3b): MathematicalBilliards §§7.3, 8.1.
4. Billiard flow `F^t` on `X_P`, joint measurability, invariance of the Liouville measure: MathematicalBilliards §§8.2–8.3.
5. Weak mixing of a measure-preserving ℝ-flow (no measurable eigenfunctions, ergodicity of F×F): MathematicalBilliards §§12.1–12.5; PR #2231 §1.0, §3.2.
6. For the rational case (Thm 1.3 of the paper): unfolding to translation surfaces, straight-line flows: MathematicalBilliards Layers 9–11 (merged, not implemented).

## Library-scale gap
The merged MathematicalBilliards supplies the polygonal billiard flow, weak mixing of real flows (Cornfeld–Fomin–Sinai, *Ergodic Theory*, Ch. 1, 6; Walters ch. 1-2) and the unfolding of rational polygons (reflection groups, translation surfaces, directional flows and the Katok–Zemlyakov conjugacy; Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Masur–Tabachnikov, "Rational billiards and flat structures", Handbook of Dynamical Systems 1A). What remains for the definition is implementation of its Layers 7–8 and 12. The paper's further theorems need strata of translation surfaces, the `SL₂(ℝ)` action and Masur–Veech theory, which MathematicalBilliards names outside it and no roadmap plans (the merged TeichmullerGeometry stops at quadratic differentials).
