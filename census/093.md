## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2231 (ClassicalErgodicTheory) Layer 9 specifies the whole definition: simple polygons and polygonal domains with holes (§9.1), the phase space `billiardPhaseSpace D` with reflection identifications and inward representatives (§9.2, 93.1), collision times and trajectories (§9.2, 93.2), finiteness of collisions and the null singular set `billiardSingularSet D` (§9.2, 93.3), and the billiard flow `billiardFlow D` on `billiardRegularSet D` with its invariant Liouville probability `liouvilleMeasure D` and the measurable equivalence with interior states (§9.3, 93.4 and its first remark). Weak mixing of measurable real flows (`WeakMixingVAdd`, ergodicity of the diagonal action) and its eigenfunction characterization are §1.0 and §§3.1–3.2. No merged roadmap specifies any of it.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #2231 "Add classical ergodic theory, entropy and flows roadmap": §9.1 (`PolygonalDomain`: one outer simple polygon with finitely many polygonal holes, sides, vertices, inward normals, reflections `σ_S`), §9.2 (`billiardPhaseSpace D` as the quotient of `P × Circle` by the reflection setoid, standard Borel; inward representatives; recursive collision times `τ_k`; finiteness of collisions by wedge unfolding without rationality; `billiardSingularSet D` of vertex, tangency and vertex-hitting classes, measurable and null; time reversal `J`), §9.3 (`billiardFlow D` as an `AddAction ℝ (billiardRegularSet D)` with `MeasurableVAdd₂`, `liouvilleMeasure D = (2π · area P)⁻¹ • π_*(volume.restrict P ⊗ dθ)` with `VAddInvariantMeasure`, the measurable equivalence with normalized `volume.restrict (interior P) ⊗ dθ`, the collision law, extension by fixing `Σ`; weak mixing as `WeakMixingVAdd ℝ (billiardRegularSet D) (liouvilleMeasure D)`). §1.0 defines `WeakMixingVAdd` as ergodicity of the diagonal action on `X × X` (the paper's `F^t × F^t` form) and §1.3 relates it to mixing for group actions; §3.2 proves for measurable real flows its equivalence with the absence of nonconstant eigenfunctions, Cesàro decay of correlations, atomless spectral measures and weak mixing of time-`t` maps. This covers 93.1–93.4 and the remarks on holes, the quotient-free model and weak mixing. The rational-polygon remark (unfolding to translation surfaces) is not a target.
- #1027 "Add unbounded-operator spectral, extension and form theory roadmap" builds one explicit translation surface as an operator-theory counterexample; #1169 "Teichmüller geometry" has quadratic differentials but no translation flows or billiards.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Simple polygon P ⊂ ℝ², sides, vertices, inward normals: Mathlib `Polygon`, Tau Ceti `IsJordanCurve`; `PolygonalDomain`: PR #2231 §9.1.
2. Phase space `T_1(P)=(P×S¹)/∼` with reflections: PR #2231 §9.2.
3. Billiard trajectory, collision times, finiteness of collisions in finite time (93.3a), null singular set Σ (93.3b): PR #2231 §9.2.
4. Billiard flow `F^t` on `X_P`, Borel measurability, Liouville measure and its invariance: PR #2231 §9.3.
5. Weak mixing of a measure-preserving ℝ-flow (no measurable eigenfunctions / ergodicity of F×F): PR #2231 §1.0, §3.2.
6. For the rational case (Thm 1.3 of the paper): unfolding to translation surfaces, straight-line flows: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2231 supplies piece (a), the ergodic theory of flows (mixing and weak mixing for measure-preserving actions and real flows, spectral characterisations, suspension flows and cross-sections; Cornfeld–Fomin–Sinai, *Ergodic Theory*, Ch. 1, 6, 11; Walters ch. 1-2), and the polygonal billiard flow itself. What remains for the definition is review and merging of PR #2231. The paper's further theorems need (b), a "PolygonalBilliardsAndTranslationSurfaces" roadmap: Katok-Zemlyakov unfolding, translation surfaces, straight-line flows and interval exchange transformations, Masur-Veech unique ergodicity basics (Tabachnikov, *Geometry and Billiards*; Masur-Tabachnikov, "Rational billiards and flat structures", Handbook of Dynamical Systems 1A). (b) would sit beside PR #2231 and the open TeichmüllerGeometry PR #1169.
