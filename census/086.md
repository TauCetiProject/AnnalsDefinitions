# 86. Symmetric power of a Weil–Deligne representation

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1688 "Roadmap: smooth representations of local groups (depends on #785)" has a target *wd-algebraic-composition* (SR.5.2, "WD parameter operations"). It is Def. 86.1–86.3 essentially verbatim: `ρ∘(r,N) := (ρ∘r, dρ(N))` for a morphism of algebraic groups `ρ`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, preservation of Frobenius semisimplicity, `Sym^m∘(r,N)` with its differential, the twist formula and `Sym^{n−1}∘Sp(2) ≅ Sp(n)`. The archimedean case 86.4 has its Weil groups `W_ℝ`, `W_ℂ` and `recGL` in PR #1037 §2.5; the composition with `Sym^m` is not stated there but is trivial. Nothing is merged or implemented beyond the local Weil group.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Symmetric powers of modules, nilpotent endomorphisms: Mathlib.
2. Local Weil group `W_K` with its topology and `|·|`: Tau Ceti (ClassFieldTheory Layer 9).
3. Differential of an algebraic representation, preservation of Jordan decomposition: ReductiveGroups Layers 2 and 4 (merged, done in Tau Ceti). Nilpotence of `dρ(N)` for nilpotent `N` is not stated there: unplanned, small.
4. Weil–Deligne representations and isomorphisms: target PR #1688 *wd-raw-parameters*.
5. `ρ∘(r,N)`, `Sym^m∘(r,N)`: target PR #1688 *wd-algebraic-composition*.
6. Archimedean Weil groups and semisimple representations: target PR #1037 §2.5. `Sym^m` there is unplanned but a one-line composition.
7. `rec_K` (local Langlands for `GL_n`), needed to *use* the definition in [2026, 203(1), no. 4]: not checked in detail; owned by the automorphic PRs (#924 "automorphic L-functions and local factors", #1756), not by #1688.
