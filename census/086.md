# 86. Symmetric power of a Weil–Deligne representation

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups/README.md) SR.5.2 has the target *wd-algebraic-composition* (under "WD parameter operations"). It is Def. 86.1–86.3 essentially verbatim: `ρ∘(r,N) := (ρ∘r, dρ(N))` for a morphism of algebraic groups `ρ`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, preservation of Frobenius semisimplicity, `Sym^m∘(r,N)` with its differential, the twist formula and `Sym^{n−1}∘Sp(2) ≅ Sp(n)`. The archimedean case 86.4 is merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §2.5: `WeilGroupReal`, `WeilGroupComplex`, `recGL`, and the statement that `w ↦ Sym^m(r(w))` of a semisimple continuous representation of `W_ℂ` or `W_ℝ` is semisimple of dimension `binom(n+m−1,m)`. In code: the local Weil group, SR.5.2 *nilpotent-exp-log* (the unipotent logarithm, unipotent roots and the formula `h(x) = exp(x log h(1))` for unipotent homomorphisms from `ℤ_ℓ`), SR.5.2 *wd-raw-parameters* (`TauCeti.WeilDeligneParameter`, `IsFrobeniusSemisimple`, morphisms, isomorphisms and transport), the differential `d Symⁿ` with `Symⁿ(exp N) = exp (d Symⁿ N)` (the linear-algebra part of *wd-algebraic-composition*), the operations ⊕, ⊗, dual, twist, `det`, restriction and scalar extension, the special parameters `Sp(n)` and the inertial type on Weil–Deligne parameters, the archimedean Weil groups `W_ℝ`, `W_ℂ` with the norm, abelianization, the characters of `ℂˣ` and of `W_ℝ`, the two-dimensional inductions, bounded parameters and the bound `dim ≤ 2` for irreducible representations of `W_ℝ`, the relative Maschke theorem used by §2.5's symmetric-power clause, and the clause for `W_ℂ` (symmetric powers of semisimple representations of commutative monoids are semisimple, with `dim Sym^m = binom(n+m−1,m)`); not `Sym^m∘(r,N)`, `recGL` or the semisimplicity of symmetric powers for `W_ℝ`. Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4712 implements `Sym^m∘(r,N)` for raw Weil–Deligne parameters.
