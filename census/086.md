# 86. Symmetric power of a Weil–Deligne representation

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups/README.md) SR.5.2 has the target *wd-algebraic-composition* (under "WD parameter operations"). It is Def. 86.1–86.3 essentially verbatim: `ρ∘(r,N) := (ρ∘r, dρ(N))` for a morphism of algebraic groups `ρ`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, preservation of Frobenius semisimplicity, `Sym^m∘(r,N)` with its differential, the twist formula and `Sym^{n−1}∘Sp(2) ≅ Sp(n)`. The archimedean case 86.4 is merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §2.5: `WeilGroupReal`, `WeilGroupComplex`, `recGL`, and the statement that `w ↦ Sym^m(r(w))` of a semisimple continuous representation of `W_ℂ` or `W_ℝ` is semisimple of dimension `binom(n+m−1,m)`. In code: the local Weil group and the unipotent logarithm (*nilpotent-exp-log*), not Weil–Deligne representations; open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/40845 defines raw Weil–Deligne parameters (*wd-raw-parameters*).

## Mathlib
- Has: symmetric tensor powers `SymmetricPower R ι M` with notation `Sym[R]^n M` (`LinearAlgebra/TensorPower/Symmetric.lean`), `PiTensorProduct`, `Module.End`, `IsNilpotent`, absolute Galois groups, local fields.
- Missing: Weil groups, Weil–Deligne representations, morphisms of algebraic groups `GL(V) → GL(W)` with differentials as used here, `rec_K`.

- Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3780 (the generic input of AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.5's symmetric-power clause: the relative Maschke theorem `Representation.isSemisimpleRepresentation_of_comp_subtype`, semisimplicity ascends from a subgroup of finite index invertible in `k`; diagonalizability of symmetric powers on the finite-index abelian subgroup and the application to `W_ℂ`, `W_ℝ` remain).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Symmetric powers of modules, nilpotent endomorphisms: Mathlib.
2. Local Weil group `W_K` with its topology and `|·|`: Tau Ceti (ClassFieldTheory Layer 9).
3. Differential of an algebraic representation, preservation of Jordan decomposition: ReductiveGroups Layers 2 and 4 (merged, done in Tau Ceti); polynomial one-parameter subgroups: ReductiveGroups Layer 5P (partial).
4. Weil–Deligne representations and isomorphisms: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *wd-raw-parameters* (merged, not started; open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/40845); unipotent logarithm: *nilpotent-exp-log* (merged, in code: `IsNilpotent.log`).
5. `ρ∘(r,N)`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, `Sym^m∘(r,N)`: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *wd-algebraic-composition* (merged, not started).
6. Archimedean Weil groups, semisimple representations and their symmetric powers (86.4): target in merged AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.5 (not started; open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/43493 constructs `W_ℝ`, `W_ℂ`, and open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3780 proves the relative Maschke theorem for the semisimplicity of symmetric powers).
7. `rec_K` (local Langlands for `GL_n`), needed to *use* the definition in [2026, 203(1), no. 4]: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *classical-llc-normalization* (merged, not started), specialized to `GL₂` in merged GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer R16.0.1.
