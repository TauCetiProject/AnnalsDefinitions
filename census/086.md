# 86. Symmetric power of a Weil–Deligne representation

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups/README.md) SR.5.2 has the target *wd-algebraic-composition* (under "WD parameter operations"). It is Def. 86.1–86.3 essentially verbatim: `ρ∘(r,N) := (ρ∘r, dρ(N))` for a morphism of algebraic groups `ρ`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, preservation of Frobenius semisimplicity, `Sym^m∘(r,N)` with its differential, the twist formula and `Sym^{n−1}∘Sp(2) ≅ Sp(n)`. The archimedean case 86.4 has its Weil groups `W_ℝ`, `W_ℂ` and `recGL` in open PR #1037 §2.5; the composition with `Sym^m` is not stated there but is trivial. Nothing is implemented beyond the local Weil group.

- Missing: Weil groups, Weil–Deligne representations, morphisms of algebraic groups `GL(V) → GL(W)` with differentials as used here, `rec_K`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Symmetric powers of modules, nilpotent endomorphisms: Mathlib.
2. Local Weil group `W_K` with its topology and `|·|`: Tau Ceti (ClassFieldTheory Layer 9).
3. Differential of an algebraic representation, preservation of Jordan decomposition: ReductiveGroups Layers 2 and 4 (merged, done in Tau Ceti); polynomial one-parameter subgroups: ReductiveGroups Layer 5P (partial).
4. Weil–Deligne representations and isomorphisms: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *wd-raw-parameters* (merged, not started); unipotent logarithm: *nilpotent-exp-log* (merged, not started).
5. `ρ∘(r,N)`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, `Sym^m∘(r,N)`: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *wd-algebraic-composition* (merged, not started).
6. Archimedean Weil groups and semisimple representations: target PR #1037 §2.5. `Sym^m` there is unplanned but a one-line composition.
7. `rec_K` (local Langlands for `GL_n`), needed to *use* the definition in [2026, 203(1), no. 4]: not checked in detail; owned by the automorphic PRs (#924 "automorphic L-functions and local factors", #1756), not by SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups.
