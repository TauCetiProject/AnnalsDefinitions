# 86. Symmetric power of a Weil–Deligne representation

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups/README.md) SR.5.2 has the target *wd-algebraic-composition* (under "WD parameter operations"). It is Def. 86.1–86.3 essentially verbatim: `ρ∘(r,N) := (ρ∘r, dρ(N))` for a morphism of algebraic groups `ρ`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, preservation of Frobenius semisimplicity, `Sym^m∘(r,N)` with its differential, the twist formula and `Sym^{n−1}∘Sp(2) ≅ Sp(n)`. The archimedean case 86.4 is merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §2.5: `WeilGroupReal`, `WeilGroupComplex`, `recGL`, and the statement that `w ↦ Sym^m(r(w))` of a semisimple continuous representation of `W_ℂ` or `W_ℝ` is semisimple of dimension `binom(n+m−1,m)`. In code: the local Weil group, SR.5.2 *nilpotent-exp-log* (the unipotent logarithm, unipotent roots and the formula `h(x) = exp(x log h(1))` for unipotent homomorphisms from `ℤ_ℓ`), SR.5.2 *wd-raw-parameters* (`TauCeti.WeilDeligneParameter`, `IsFrobeniusSemisimple`, morphisms, isomorphisms and transport), the differential `d Symⁿ` with `Symⁿ(exp N) = exp (d Symⁿ N)` (the linear-algebra part of *wd-algebraic-composition*), the operations ⊕, ⊗, dual and twist on Weil–Deligne parameters, the archimedean Weil groups `W_ℝ`, `W_ℂ` with the norm, abelianization and the characters of `ℂˣ`, and the relative Maschke theorem used by §2.5's symmetric-power clause; not `det`, `Sp(n)`, `Sym^m∘(r,N)`, `recGL` or the semisimplicity of symmetric powers.

1. Symmetric powers of modules, nilpotent endomorphisms: Mathlib.
2. Local Weil group `W_K` with its topology and `|·|`: Tau Ceti (ClassFieldTheory Layer 9).
3. Differential of an algebraic representation, preservation of Jordan decomposition: ReductiveGroups Layers 2 and 4 (merged, done in Tau Ceti); polynomial one-parameter subgroups: ReductiveGroups Layer 5P (partial).
4. Weil–Deligne representations and isomorphisms: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *wd-raw-parameters* (merged, in code: `TauCeti.WeilDeligneParameter`, `LocalWeilDeligneParameter`); unipotent logarithm: *nilpotent-exp-log* (merged, in code: `IsNilpotent.log`, `MonoidHom.padicInt_coe_apply_eq_exp_smul_log`).
5. `ρ∘(r,N)`, nilpotence of `dρ(N)`, `Sym^m∘(r,N)`: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *wd-algebraic-composition* (merged; the differential `d Symⁿ` and `Symⁿ(exp N) = exp (d Symⁿ N)` in code, `SymmetricPower.differential`, `SymmetricPower.map_exp`; the composition with WD parameters not started). The operations ⊕, ⊗, dual and twist of *wd-parameter-operations* are in code (`WeilDeligneParameter.prod`, `tensor`, `dual`, `twist`); `det` and `Sp(n)` are not (`Sp(n)` in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4242).
6. Archimedean Weil groups, semisimple representations and their symmetric powers (86.4): target in merged AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.5 (partial: `WeilGroupReal`, `WeilGroupComplex` with the norm and abelianization and the characters `TauCeti.complexUnitsCharacter` of `ℂˣ` are in code, the induced characters `WeilGroupReal.induceCharacter` are in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4235, and the relative Maschke theorem `Representation.isSemisimpleRepresentation_of_comp_subtype` for the semisimplicity of symmetric powers is in code).
7. `rec_K` (local Langlands for `GL_n`), needed to *use* the definition in [2026, 203(1), no. 4]: target SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.5.2 *classical-llc-normalization* (merged, not started), specialized to `GL₂` in merged GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer R16.0.1.
