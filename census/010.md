# 10. Hochschild–Kostant–Rosenberg antisymmetrisation map

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2447 (HochschildHomologyAndHKR) specifies the definition: the Hochschild cochain complex `C^•(A,M)` of an ordinary algebra with bimodule coefficients on Tau Ceti's `hochschildBimoduleCoface` (§1.2), normalised cochains (§2.2), polyvector fields `T^n_{R/k} = ⋀^n_R Der_k(R)` (§7.4), the determinant cocycle `⟨D₁,…,D_n⟩` (`detCochain`), which is `R`-multilinear, alternating and a normalised cocycle (Lemma 10.3), the unnormalised map and `Φ^n_HKR = (1/n!)⟨…⟩` (§7.5, Definition 10.4), injectivity when `Ω¹` is finite projective, and the cohomological HKR theorem for smooth algebras with the cup product (§7.6, `cohomological_hkr`, Theorem 10.5), with polynomial algebras through the Koszul resolution (Layer 8). Its §7.6 rests, through §6.4(d) (the classical antisymmetrisation `ε^cl_n` is an isomorphism for smooth `R`), on a statement it records as gap:smooth-hkr-antisymmetrisation and assigns to RefinedTraceMethods §1.9, which is open PR #1493 and does not contain it. No merged roadmap specifies the definition. Its inputs in code: derivations and exterior powers (Mathlib), the Hochschild coboundary of an ordinary algebra and Hochschild cochains of `A∞` algebras (Tau Ceti, merged DGAInfinity Layer 8, partial).

## Mathlib
- Has: `Derivation`, `Module.End`-valued derivations as an `R`-module, `KaehlerDifferential` (`Ω[S⁄R]`) and its universal property, `exteriorPower` / `ExteriorAlgebra` with `AlternatingMap` universal property, `MultilinearMap`, `MvPolynomial.pderiv`.
- Missing: Hochschild cochain complex of an associative algebra, `HH^n`, polyvector fields as a named object, HKR.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hochschild cochains of an `A∞` algebra in positive arity: `TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.PositiveHochschild.cochains`, `d`, `complex`, `cycles`, with the differential `TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential`, Gerstenhaber bracket (`Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/{Basic,Complex,Cohomology,Normalized}.lean`); a commutative algebra is the `A∞` algebra with only `m₂`. Also `A∞` deformations (`.../AInfinity/Algebra/Deformation.lean`), relevant to the `A∞` algebra `R_η` of the paper.
- Hochschild cochains of an ordinary algebra `A` with coefficients in an `A`-bimodule `M` (in particular `M = A`, giving `Cⁿ(A,A)` as multilinear maps `Aⁿ → A`, arity zero included): the coboundary `TauCeti.hochschildBimoduleCoboundary`, `δ ∘ δ = 0`, degree-zero cocycles the centre, degree-one cocycles the derivations (`RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Coboundary.lean`). The packaged complex `hochschildCochainComplex` and `hochschildCohomology` (`RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Complex.lean`) have coefficients in `Hom_R(A, M)`, so `HHⁿ(A, A)` itself is not packaged.
- Bounded Hochschild cohomology for Banach algebras (`Analysis/Normed/Algebra/HochschildCohomology/*`) and the Hochschild complex `Cⁿ(H, M) = M ⊗ H^⊗n` of a comodule over a bialgebra (`Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Cohomology/Basic.lean`, the rational cohomology of affine group schemes), not this setting.
- No polyvector fields, multiderivation cocycles or HKR map (grep `polyvector|multiderivation|HKR` empty).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Derivation`, `exteriorPower`, `AlternatingMap`, `KaehlerDifferential`: Mathlib.
2. Hochschild cochain complex `C^•(R,R)` of an ordinary algebra: the cochains and coboundary are Tau Ceti's `hochschildBimoduleCoboundary` with `M = R` (all arities); the complex and `HHⁿ(R,R)` with bimodule coefficients: PR #2447 §1.2 (on Tau Ceti's cofaces); normalised cochains: PR #2447 §2.2. DGAInfinity Layer 8 (merged, partial) has the `A∞` version, compared in PR #2447 §6.1.
3. `⟨D₁,…,Dₙ⟩` alternating `R`-multilinear in the `Dᵢ`, a multiderivation, hence a normalised cocycle (Lemma 10.3): PR #2447 §7.5.
4. `Φⁿ_HKR` with `1/n!` and the unnormalised map (Def 10.4): PR #2447 §7.5.
5. HKR theorem for smooth algebras (Theorem 10.5): PR #2447 §7.6, through §6.4(d), which needs `ε_n` an isomorphism for smooth `A` over any commutative `k`: assigned to PR #1493 §1.9, which does not state it (merged KTheoryFiniteLocalFields §4.0 states the isomorphism without naming the map). Polynomial rings: PR #2447 Layer 8.
6. Embedding `η ↦ m_n = ε Φ(η)` into an `A∞` structure on `R[ε]`: Tau Ceti `A∞` API.
