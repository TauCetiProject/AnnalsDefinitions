# 10. Hochschild–Kostant–Rosenberg antisymmetrisation map

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2447 (HochschildHomologyAndHKR) specifies the definition: the Hochschild cochain complex `C^•(A,M)` of an ordinary algebra with bimodule coefficients on Tau Ceti's `hochschildBimoduleCoface` (§1.2), normalised cochains (§2.2), polyvector fields `T^n_{R/k} = ⋀^n_R Der_k(R)` (§7.4), the determinant cocycle `⟨D₁,…,D_n⟩` (`detCochain`), which is `R`-multilinear, alternating and a normalised cocycle (Lemma 10.3), the unnormalised map `hkrMap` and `Φ^n_HKR = (1/n!)⟨…⟩` (`normalizedHKRMap`, §7.5, Definition 10.4), injectivity when `Ω¹` is finite projective, and the cohomological HKR theorem for smooth `k`-projective algebras with the cup product (§9.2, `cohomological_hkr`, Theorem 10.5), with polynomial algebras through the Koszul resolution (Layer 8). §9.2 rests on the homological HKR theorem of §9.1, which #2447 proves by étale localisation from the polynomial case, consuming the étale base change of Hochschild homology of RefinedTraceMethods §1.8 (open PR #1493, which states it after Weibel–Geller). No merged roadmap specifies the definition. Its inputs in code: derivations and exterior powers (Mathlib), the Hochschild cochain complex and cohomology `HHⁿ(A, M)` of an ordinary algebra with bimodule coefficients, and Hochschild cochains of `A∞` algebras (Tau Ceti, merged DGAInfinity Layer 8, partial).

Theorem 10.5 of the source overstates the unnormalised HKR map; see [errata](../sources/errata.md).

## Mathlib
- Has: `Derivation`, `Module.End`-valued derivations as an `R`-module, `KaehlerDifferential` (`Ω[S⁄R]`) and its universal property, `exteriorPower` / `ExteriorAlgebra` with `AlternatingMap` universal property, `MultilinearMap`, `MvPolynomial.pderiv`; `Algebra.Smooth`, `Algebra.Etale` and the local structure of smooth algebras that #2447 §9.1 uses.
- Missing: Hochschild cochain complex of an associative algebra, `HH^n`, polyvector fields as a named object, HKR.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hochschild cochains of an ordinary algebra `A` with coefficients in an `A`-bimodule `M` (in particular `M = A`, giving `Cⁿ(A,A)` as multilinear maps `Aⁿ → A`, arity zero included): the coboundary `TauCeti.hochschildBimoduleCoboundary`, `δ ∘ δ = 0`, degree-zero cocycles the centre, degree-one cocycles the derivations (`RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Coboundary.lean`); the complex `TauCeti.hochschildBimoduleCochainComplex R A M`, `hochschildBimoduleCocycles`, `hochschildBimoduleCoboundaries` and the cohomology `hochschildBimoduleCohomology R A M n = HHⁿ(A, M)` (`RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Bimodule.lean`), so `HHⁿ(A, A)` is in code. The older packaging `hochschildCochainComplex`, `hochschildCohomology` has coefficients in `Hom_R(A, M)` (`RingTheory/HochschildCohomology/Complex.lean`).
- Hochschild chains: normalised Hochschild chains `TauCeti.normalizedHochschildChains.chainComplex` (`RingTheory/CyclicCohomology/Normalized/Chains.lean`), on which #2447 §9.1 states the homological HKR theorem.
- Hochschild cochains of an `A∞` algebra in positive arity: `TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.PositiveHochschild.cochains`, `d`, `complex`, `cycles`, with the differential `TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.hochschildDifferential`, Gerstenhaber bracket (`Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/{Basic,Complex,Cohomology,Normalized}.lean`); a commutative algebra is the `A∞` algebra with only `m₂`. Also `A∞` deformations (`.../AInfinity/Algebra/Deformation.lean`), relevant to the `A∞` algebra `R_η` of the paper.
- Bounded Hochschild cohomology for Banach algebras (`Analysis/Normed/Algebra/HochschildCohomology/*`) and the Hochschild complex `Cⁿ(H, M) = M ⊗ H^⊗n` of a comodule over a bialgebra (`Algebra/Coalgebra/Comodule/Cohomology/Basic.lean`, the rational cohomology of affine group schemes), not this setting.
- No polyvector fields, multiderivation cocycles or HKR map (grep `polyvector|multiderivation|detCochain|hkrMap` empty).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Derivation`, `exteriorPower`, `AlternatingMap`, `KaehlerDifferential`, smooth and étale algebras: Mathlib.
2. Hochschild cochain complex `C^•(R,R)` of an ordinary algebra and `HHⁿ(R,R)`: Tau Ceti (`hochschildBimoduleCochainComplex`, `hochschildBimoduleCohomology`); its functoriality and long exact sequences: PR #2447 Layer 1; normalised cochains: PR #2447 §2.2. DGAInfinity Layer 8 (merged, partial) has the `A∞` version, compared in PR #2447 §6.1.
3. `⟨D₁,…,Dₙ⟩` alternating `R`-multilinear in the `Dᵢ`, a multiderivation, hence a normalised cocycle (Lemma 10.3): PR #2447 §7.5.
4. `Φⁿ_HKR` with `1/n!` and the unnormalised map (Def 10.4): PR #2447 §7.5.
5. HKR theorem for smooth algebras (Theorem 10.5): PR #2447 §9.2, from §9.1's homological HKR, which needs the étale base change of Hochschild homology of PR #1493 §1.8. Polynomial rings: PR #2447 Layer 8.
6. Embedding `η ↦ m_n = ε Φ(η)` into an `A∞` structure on `R[ε]`: Tau Ceti `A∞` API.
