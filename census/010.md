# 10. Hochschild–Kostant–Rosenberg antisymmetrisation map

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. The cohomological HKR map `⋀ⁿ_R Der_k(R) → Cⁿ(R,R)` (polyvector fields to Hochschild cochains) is not a target anywhere. Its inputs are present or planned: derivations and exterior powers (Mathlib), Hochschild cochains of `A∞`/DG algebras (merged DGAInfinity Layer 8, partly in Tau Ceti code), and the homological HKR antisymmetrisation `Ωⁿ → HH_n` (open PR #1493 §1.9).

## Mathlib
- Has: `Derivation`, `Module.End`-valued derivations as an `R`-module, `KaehlerDifferential` (`Ω[S⁄R]`) and its universal property, `exteriorPower` / `ExteriorAlgebra` with `AlternatingMap` universal property, `MultilinearMap`, `MvPolynomial.pderiv`.
- Missing: Hochschild cochain complex of an associative algebra, `HH^n`, polyvector fields as a named object, HKR.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hochschild cochains of an `A∞` algebra in positive arity: `TauCeti.AInfinityAlgebra.cochains`, `d`, `complex`, `hochschildDifferential`, `cycles`, Gerstenhaber bracket (`Algebra/Homology/AInfinity/Algebra/Hochschild/{Basic,Complex,Cohomology,Normalized}.lean`); a commutative algebra is the `A∞` algebra with only `m₂`. Also `A∞` deformations (`.../AInfinity/Algebra/Deformation.lean`), relevant to the `A∞` algebra `R_η` of the paper.
- Bounded Hochschild cohomology for Banach algebras (`Analysis/Normed/Algebra/HochschildCohomology/*`), not this setting.
- No polyvector fields, multiderivation cocycles or HKR map (grep `polyvector|multiderivation|HKR` empty).

- DGAInfinity Layer 8 "Hochschild cochains, deformations, Massey products, and formality": Hochschild chains/cochains of DG and `A∞` algebras, normalized versions, `[m,-]`, `HH²`/`HH³` for ordinary algebras. STATUS: partial ("normalization and invariance comparisons ..." remaining). No HKR.
- DerivedModuleAlgebra (cotangent complex); NoncommutativeGeometry, ZigzagPreprojective consume Hochschild theory.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Derivation`, `exteriorPower`, `AlternatingMap`, `KaehlerDifferential`: Mathlib.
2. Hochschild cochain complex `C^•(R,R)` of an ordinary algebra: specialization of Tau Ceti's `A∞` Hochschild complex (positive arity only; arity 0 missing) / DGAInfinity Layer 8 (merged, partial).
3. `⟨D₁,…,Dₙ⟩` alternating `R`-multilinear in the `Dᵢ`, a multiderivation, hence a normalized cocycle (Lemma 10.3): unplanned (small).
4. `Φⁿ_HKR` with `1/n!` and the unnormalised map (Def 10.4): unplanned (small).
5. HKR theorem for smooth algebras (Theorem 10.5), at least for polynomial rings in top degree: unplanned for cohomology; homology version in PR #1493 §1.9.
6. Embedding `η ↦ m_n = ε Φ(η)` into an `A∞` structure on `R[ε]`: Tau Ceti `A∞` API.

## Library-scale gap
A small cohomological companion to PR #1493 §1.9: polyvector fields `T^n_{R/k}`, the multiderivation map to Hochschild cocycles, injectivity on cochains when `Ω¹` is finite projective, and HKR for smooth algebras with the cup product (Loday, *Cyclic Homology*, §§1.3, 3.4; Weibel Ch. 9). Natural home: DGAInfinity Layer 8 (Hochschild cochains of ordinary algebras) or PR #1493 next to the homological map.
