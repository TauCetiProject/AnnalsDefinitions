## Merged roadmaps
- GeometricCurrents G3: "A Lipschitz singular complex ... a new subcomplex of native singular chains with the same simplex carrier plus the Lipschitz condition; build its basic API and both comparison maps"; Lipschitz-to-singular inclusion is an integral quasi-isomorphism (Riemannian manifolds and closed retract pairs). Also L-twisted Lipschitz subcomplex. Not implemented.
- GeometricCurrents G1-G2: mass, pushforward of Lipschitz images as integral currents; G4a isoperimetric/small-cycle filling for currents. G1 is partial and Euclidean only: mass and local mass, the mass measure, L¹ extension, normal currents, weak compactness, smooth pushforward of compactly supported currents, cones and slicing are in code. The manifold `TestForm`/`Current` carrier, integer-rectifiable currents, Lipschitz pushforward (G2) and slicing are missing.
- GeometricMeasureTheory G2: area formula for Lipschitz ℝ^m → ℝ^n with multiplicity (gives volume of a Lipschitz simplex in Euclidean target). This part is proved; G2 as a whole is partial (tangent-plane uniqueness and the tangential area formula on rectifiable sets are missing).
- No roadmap targets filling volume, filling functions or Dehn functions (Poincare3D's "filling functional" is unrelated).

## Library-scale gap
A "filling invariants" roadmap: Dehn functions of finitely presented groups, higher filling functions via Lipschitz chains or integral currents, quasi-isometry invariance, Federer-Fleming deformation, isoperimetric inequalities in CAT(0)/symmetric spaces (Gromov's *Filling Riemannian manifolds*; Bridson's survey *The geometry of the word problem*; Ambrosio-Kirchheim for metric currents; Wenger). Would sit beside GeometricCurrents (consuming G3/G4a) and a geometric group theory roadmap (FreeGroupsAndLimitGroups is the closest existing one).
