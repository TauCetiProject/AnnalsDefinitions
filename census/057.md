## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2490 (GeometricCurrents: Lipschitz chains in metric spaces, mass and filling volume) adds GeometricCurrents §G3a, which specifies the whole definition on an arbitrary (extended) metric space with the normalized Hausdorff measure: Lipschitz simplices and the Lipschitz chain complex as a subcomplex of native singular chains, unique expression, support and pushforward (57.1), the volume of a simplex as the Hᵈ-measure of its image with multiplicity, the mass of a chain and `∫_α φ`, `mass_Z` (57.2), the Lipschitz bounds `vol(f∘σ) ≤ Lᵈ vol(σ)`, `mass(f_#α) ≤ Lᵈ mass(α)` and the triangle inequality (57.3), the Riemannian area formula (57.3 for Riemannian manifolds), and the filling volume `FV_d(α)` and filling volume function `FV_d(Y, V)` (57.4). The area formula through Kirchheim's metric differential on a general metric space, and the piecewise Euclidean simplicial complexes of 57.3, are not targets; quasi-isometry invariance and the filling invariants of groups acting geometrically are named outside it. No merged roadmap targets mass of Lipschitz chains or filling volume, and none of it is in code.

## Merged roadmaps
- [GeometricCurrents](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GeometricCurrents/README.md) G3 (l.406): "A Lipschitz singular complex ... a new subcomplex of native singular chains with the same simplex carrier plus the Lipschitz condition; build its basic API and both comparison maps"; Lipschitz-to-singular inclusion is an integral quasi-isomorphism (Riemannian manifolds and closed retract pairs). Also L-twisted Lipschitz subcomplex. No mass of chains, no filling volume. Not implemented.
- GeometricCurrents G1–G2 (l.137, l.240): mass, pushforward of Lipschitz images as integral currents; G4a isoperimetric/small-cycle filling for currents. G1 is partial and Euclidean only: mass and local mass, the mass measure, L¹ extension, normal currents, weak compactness, smooth pushforward of compactly supported currents, cones, slicing and Euclidean integer-rectifiable currents (`Distribution.IsIntegerRectifiable`, `Analysis/Distribution/Rectifiable/IntegerRectifiable.lean`) are in code. G2's pushforward along smooth maps converging uniformly near the support of a compactly supported normal current, with the flat estimate `F(g_# T − f_# T) ≤ A (L^{k+1} M(T) + L^k M(∂T))`, is in code (`Analysis/Distribution/Flat/Pushforward.lean`), as are Euclidean locally integral, integral and compactly supported integral currents (`Distribution.IsIntegralCurrent`, `Analysis/Distribution/Rectifiable/Integral.lean`). The manifold `TestForm`/`Current` carrier, Lipschitz pushforward (G2) and slicing are missing.
- [GeometricMeasureTheory](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GeometricMeasureTheory/README.md) G2: area formula for Lipschitz ℝ^m → ℝ^n with multiplicity (gives volume of a Lipschitz simplex in Euclidean target). This part is proved; G2 as a whole is partial (tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula on rectifiable sets are missing).
- No merged roadmap targets mass of Lipschitz chains, filling volume, filling functions or Dehn functions (Poincare3D's "filling functional" is unrelated).

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR #2490 "GeometricCurrents: Lipschitz chains in metric spaces, mass and filling volume": §G3a. *Lipschitz chains*: for an arbitrary metric space `Y`, Lipschitz `d`-simplices of native `singularChainComplexFunctor`, the group `C^Lip_d(Y)` free on them, the Lipschitz chain complex as a subcomplex with the singular boundary, the inclusion chain map, unique expression with nonzero coefficients and distinct simplices, compact support, pushforward along Lipschitz maps (57.1; Riedweg–Schäppi, Leuzinger–Young §2.4). *Volume and mass*: `vol(σ) = ∫⁻ #σ⁻¹(y) dHᵈ` with the normalized `euclideanHausdorffMeasure`, `mass(α) = Σ |cₖ| vol(σₖ)`, `∫_α φ`, `mass_Z` (57.2); `vol(f∘σ) ≤ Lᵈ vol(σ)` by iterated barycentric subdivision, `vol(σ) ≤ Lip(σ)ᵈ Hᵈ(Δᵈ)`, triangle inequality, `mass(f_#α) ≤ Lᵈ mass(α)`; the Riemannian area formula `vol(σ) = ∫ √det(Dσ*Dσ)` and the comparison `M([α]) ≤ mass(α)` with the integral current of the chain (57.3 on manifolds; the metric-differential form on general metric spaces is not a target). *Filling volume*: `FV_d(α)` and `FV_d(Y, V)` without a connectivity hypothesis, finiteness exactly for boundaries, subadditivity, the Lipschitz bound for `L > 0`, monotonicity in `V`, and finiteness iff the singular class vanishes on manifolds and G3's retracts (57.4). Checks include the normalization against the raw `μH`, multiplicity, mass zero for degenerate simplices and `FV_1` in ℝⁿ. Quasi-isometry invariance and filling invariants of groups are named outside it. On a smooth Riemannian manifold the complex is G3's Lipschitz subcomplex.

## Prerequisite chain
singular chains, Lipschitz maps, normalized Hausdorff measure [Mathlib] -> Lipschitz chain complex on a metric space (57.1) [PR #2490 §G3a; on Riemannian manifolds the merged GeometricCurrents G3 subcomplex, not implemented] -> volume of a Lipschitz simplex with multiplicity, mass of a chain (57.2) [PR #2490 §G3a, via `TauCeti.AffineChain.subdivision` and Mathlib `LipschitzWith.hausdorffMeasure_image_le`] -> area formula (57.3) [PR #2490 §G3a on Riemannian manifolds, on GMT G2's area formula (Euclidean case in code) and GeometricCurrents G2; Kirchheim's metric form unplanned] -> filling volume FV^d and filling function (57.4) [PR #2490 §G3a] -> quasi-isometry invariance up to ≈, filling invariants of groups [unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
PR #2490 covers the definition. What the Annals paper (Leuzinger–Young) needs beyond it is a "filling invariants" theory: quasi-isometry invariance of filling functions, filling invariants of groups acting geometrically on `(d−1)`-connected spaces of bounded geometry, Dehn functions, Federer–Fleming deformation for Lipschitz chains, and isoperimetric inequalities in symmetric spaces and their thick parts (Gromov's *Filling Riemannian manifolds*; Bridson's survey *The geometry of the word problem*; Ambrosio–Kirchheim for metric currents; Wenger). PR #2490 names quasi-isometry invariance and group filling invariants as outside it.
