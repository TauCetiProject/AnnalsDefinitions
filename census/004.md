# 4. Parabolic subgroups of a real semisimple Lie group (minimal and standard parabolics)

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has algebraic parabolic `k`-subgroups (`TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic`) and dynamic cocharacter parabolics `P_G(λ)` with Levi decomposition, i.e. the algebraic-group variant of Remark (1)–(2), and the dynamic parabolic, unipotent and Levi subgroups `Subgroup.dynamicParabolic G X` of a subgroup of `GL m ℝ` and a real matrix; the Lie-group objects the Zimmer-program papers use (minimal `P = MAN`, standard `Q_{Π'} ⊇ P`, Langlands decomposition) are not in code. The rest of the definition is a target of merged roadmaps: minimal parabolics with Langlands decomposition in [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §2.3a (real points of reductive `ℝ`-groups) and [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §3.1 (every connected `G` with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre), extended to every reductive Cartan datum in [LieGroupsPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/LieGroupsPartII/README.md) Layer 3, and standard and general parabolics, Theorem 4.4, Definition 4.5 and Remarks (1)–(2), including the complex-group `P_s` and the `τ`-stable description of the parabolics of a real form `G^τ`, in RealSemisimpleLieGroups §§4.1–4.5 (with §2.5 for real forms). Not implemented. Depends on #1 (𝔞, restricted roots).

## Mathlib
- Has: `LieGroup`, `RootPairing`/`RootSystem` with bases (`LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/*`), `Subgroup.normalizer`.
- Missing: Cartan involutions, restricted roots, Iwasawa data, parabolic subgroups of Lie groups or algebraic groups.

## Tau Ceti code
- Algebraic: `TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic` / `IsParabolicOverAlgClosed` (smooth, contains a Borel after base change), `of_le`, conjugation invariance, `exists_conjugate_le` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.lean`); dynamic parabolics `TauCeti.Cocharacter.parabolic`, `parabolicDefiningHopfIdeal`, `BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso`, `parabolicCoordinateAlgEquiv` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/*`); `GL_n` weight parabolics classification (`GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.lean`). Tits systems and Bruhat cells `TauCeti.TitsSystem.existsUnique_mem_bruhatCell` (`GroupTheory/TitsSystem/Bruhat/Uniqueness.lean`).
- Projectivity of `G/H` forces `H` parabolic over an algebraically closed field (FlagVarieties §4.2): `isParabolicOverAlgClosed_of_isProper_projectiveOrbitToSpec` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/ProjectiveOrbit.lean`).
- Lie group: the dynamic subgroups of a real matrix (LieGroupsPartII §7.2, the self-adjoint linear model): `Subgroup.dynamicParabolic G X`, `dynamicUnipotent`, `dynamicLevi` for `G ≤ GL m ℝ`, with closedness, normality of the unipotent part, `dynamicLevi_inf_dynamicUnipotent` and the diagonal computations `mem_dynamicParabolic_diagonal_iff` (`Analysis/Matrix/GeneralLinearGroup/Dynamic.lean`). This is the dynamical description of Remark (1) for linear groups, without the identification with standard parabolics. Otherwise only the `GL n` Iwasawa decomposition (`TauCeti.iwasawaDiffeomorph`); no `MAN`, `Q_{Π'}` or Langlands decomposition.
- Connected immersed subgroups of the positive triangular group (RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layer 9): for every connected immersed Lie subgroup `C` of `A N`, the intrinsic exponential is a diffeomorphism and `C` is closed with a closed-embedding presentation and contractible (`TauCeti.Lie.ImmersedLieSubgroupData.lieExp_bijective_of_posUpperTriangular`, `posUpperTriangularLieExpDiffeomorph`, `isClosed_of_posUpperTriangular`, `isClosedEmbedding_toHom_of_posUpperTriangular`, `contractibleSpace_of_posUpperTriangular`), and for a Lie subalgebra `S` the subgroup `S.integralLeafSubgroup` is `lieExp '' S` (`LieSubalgebra.coe_integralLeafSubgroup_posUpperTriangular`) (`Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/PosUpperTriangular/Immersed.lean`, from the general step for groups with diffeomorphic `lieExp` in `Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Immersed/Exponential.lean`). The form `lieExp_bijective_of_immersed_positiveTriangular` for an immersion into `GL_n(ℂ)` and `K ∩ C = {1}` for immersed `C` are not in code; open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4365 (LieGroups §9.3, `lieExp_bijective_of_immersed_positiveTriangular`, `inf_range_eq_bot_of_isCompact_of_positiveTriangular`, `Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/PosUpperTriangular/GeneralLinear.lean`) implements both.
- RealSemisimpleLieGroups §0.4 matrix realizations: the isomorphism `𝔰𝔩₂(ℝ) ≅ 𝔰𝔬(2, 1)` intertwining `X ↦ -Xᵀ` (`TauCeti.slTwoLieEquivSoTwoOne`, `slTwoLieEquivSoTwoOne_neg_transpose`, `Algebra/Lie/Orthogonal/SlTwo.lean`) and `𝔰𝔭(p, q)` as a real Lie subalgebra of `𝔰𝔭_{2(p+q)}(ℂ)` closed under conjugate transpose with trivial centre (`TauCeti.quaternionicUnitaryLieAlgebra`, `center_quaternionicUnitaryLieAlgebra_eq_bot`, `Algebra/Lie/Symplectic/QuaternionicUnitary.lean`); no parabolics.

No positive systems or simple restricted roots. Open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4238 (§1.1, the restricted root space decomposition, `LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.iSup_restrictedRootSpace_eq_top`, `isInternal_restrictedRootSpace`, `Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RestrictedRoot/Decomposition.lean`) and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4346 (§1.2, first step: `𝔞 ≃ 𝔞*` from the Killing form, `LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.killingEquivDual`, `Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RestrictedRoot/Vector.lean`).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Root system bases, simple roots: Mathlib.
2. Cartan involution, 𝔞, restricted roots `Σ`, positive system: existence of θ and `IwasawaData` in LieGroups Layers 7, 9 (merged, not implemented), with θ for connected semisimple `G` with finite centre also in NoncompactQuantumSymmetry Layer 3 (M0) item 2 (merged); Lie-algebra theory in RealSemisimpleLieGroups Layers 0–1 (merged; the §0.1 Cartan-involution predicate and decomposition `LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution` and §1.1's maximal abelian subspaces of `𝔭` and restricted root spaces `LieEquiv.IsMaximalAbelianInP`, `Submodule.restrictedRootSpace` are in code, positive systems are not); see #1.
3. `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `A`, `N`, `P = MAN`, Langlands decomposition, conjugacy: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a (merged) for real points of reductive `ℝ`-groups; RealSemisimpleLieGroups §3.1 (merged) for connected `G` with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre; LieGroupsPartII Layer 3 (merged) for every reductive Cartan datum.
4. Tits system `(P, N_G(𝔞))` and Bruhat decomposition: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §3.3, on Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.TitsSystem` and its standard parabolics (`standardParabolic`, `standardParabolicOrderIso`).
5. `𝔮_{Π'}`, `Q_{Π'} = N_G(𝔮_{Π'})`, bijection `Π' ↦ Q_{Π'}` onto subgroups containing `P`, `Q_{Π'} = ⋃ PwP`, Levi `L_{Π'}` and `M_{Π'}A_{Π'}N_{Π'}`, type, maximal parabolics: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §§4.1–4.4.
6. Algebraic ℝ-parabolics and their dynamical description: Tau Ceti code (`IsParabolic`, `Cocharacter.parabolic`); dynamical subgroups of linear groups `Subgroup.dynamicParabolic` (LieGroupsPartII §7.2, in code); comparison `Q = G ∩ 𝐐(ℝ)`: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a for minimal ones, RealSemisimpleLieGroups §4.5 for all.
7. Complex-group `P_s` for `s ∈ i𝔨` (Higgs paper): RealSemisimpleLieGroups §4.4, complex case, with `P_s = dynamicalParabolic (−s)`; dynamic `Cocharacter.parabolic` covers rational `s`.
8. Real forms `G^τ` of complex reductive groups (#54), their Cartan involutions, and their parabolics as `G^τ ∩ Q` for `τ`-stable `Q`: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §2.5 and §4.5, on LieGroups Layer 7's real forms (merged).

## Library-scale gap
None. RealSemisimpleLieGroups Layers 3–4 are the "parabolic subgroups of real reductive groups" theory (Knapp, *Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction*, Ch. VII §7; Borel–Tits §§4–5; Borel–Ji Ch. I.1; Bourbaki *Lie* IV §2 for Tits systems), built on AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a and LieGroups Layer 9. What remains is implementation, after LieGroups Layers 7 and 9.
