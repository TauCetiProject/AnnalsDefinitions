# 4. Parabolic subgroups of a real semisimple Lie group (minimal and standard parabolics)

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has algebraic parabolic `k`-subgroups (`TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic`) and dynamic cocharacter parabolics `P_G(λ)` with Levi decomposition, i.e. the algebraic-group variant of Remark (1)–(2); the Lie-group objects the Zimmer-program papers use (minimal `P = MAN`, standard `Q_{Π'} ⊇ P`, Langlands decomposition) are not in code. The whole definition is a target of open PRs: minimal parabolics with Langlands decomposition in PR #1037 §2.3a (real points of reductive `ℝ`-groups) and PR #2229 §3.1 (every connected `G` with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre), and standard and general parabolics, Theorem 4.4, Definition 4.5 and Remarks (1)–(2), including the complex-group `P_s` and the `τ`-stable description of the parabolics of a real form `G^τ`, in PR #2229 §§4.1–4.5 (with §2.5 for real forms). No merged roadmap specifies the Lie-group parabolics. Depends on #1 (𝔞, restricted roots).

## Mathlib
- Has: `LieGroup`, `RootPairing`/`RootSystem` with bases (`LinearAlgebra/RootSystem/*`), `Subgroup.normalizer`.
- Missing: Cartan involutions, restricted roots, Iwasawa data, parabolic subgroups of Lie groups or algebraic groups.

## Tau Ceti code
- Algebraic: `TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsParabolic` / `IsParabolicOverAlgClosed` (smooth, contains a Borel after base change), `of_le`, conjugation invariance, `exists_conjugate_le` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.lean`); dynamic parabolics `TauCeti.Cocharacter.parabolic`, `parabolicDefiningHopfIdeal`, `BialgHom.parabolicLeviFppfQuotientIso`, `parabolicCoordinateAlgEquiv` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/*`); `GL_n` weight parabolics classification (`GeneralLinear/Weight/Parabolic/Classification.lean`). Tits systems and Bruhat cells `TauCeti.TitsSystem.existsUnique_mem_bruhatCell` (`GroupTheory/TitsSystem/Bruhat/Uniqueness.lean`).
- Lie group: only `GL n` Iwasawa (`TauCeti.iwasawaDiffeomorph`); no `MAN`, `Q_{Π'}` or Langlands decomposition.

- #2229 "feat: add the RealSemisimpleLieGroups roadmap": §1.2 (positive systems, simple restricted roots, Setup 4.1), §2.3 (restricted Weyl group), §3.1 (minimal parabolic datum and `P = MAN` for every connected `G` with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre, linear or not, consuming PR #1037 §2.3a; Definition 4.2), §§3.2–3.3 (`N_G(𝔞)`, Tits system `(P, N_G(𝔞))`, Bruhat decomposition), §4.1 (standard parabolic subalgebras `𝔮_{Π'}` and their classification, Theorem 4.4(1), dynamical description of `𝔮_{Π'}`), §4.2 (`Q_{Π'}` as Tits-system standard parabolics, `Q_{Π'} = N_G(𝔮_{Π'})`, closedness, Theorem 4.4(2)), §4.3 (Levi `L_{Π'}`, `M_{Π'}`, Langlands decomposition, Theorem 4.4(3)), §4.4 (parabolic subgroups and subalgebras, type, maximal parabolics, codimension, complexification criterion, `dynamicalParabolic X` for `X ∈ 𝔭`; Definition 4.5 and Remark (1)), §4.4 also treats a connected complex reductive group `H` viewed as a real group (§2.5): its parabolic subgroups are the complex parabolics `N_H(𝔮)` for complex subalgebras `𝔮` containing a Borel subalgebra, and the Higgs-bundle `P_s = {g | e^{ts} g e^{−ts} bounded as t → ∞}`, `s ∈ i𝔲`, is `dynamicalParabolic (−s)`, every parabolic of `H` being a `P_s` (Remark (1)'s last sentence, with a `SL₂(ℂ)` Check of the sign), §4.5 (comparison with algebraic `ℝ`-parabolics for `𝐆(ℝ)` and `𝐆(ℝ)°`; parabolics as `N_G(𝔮)` with `Lie(N_G(𝔮)) = 𝔮`; for a real form `H^τ` of §2.5, `𝔮 ↦ 𝔮 ⊗ ℂ` onto the `dτ`-stable parabolic subalgebras and parabolics of `H^τ` exactly the `H^τ ∩ Q` for `τ`-stable parabolics `Q` of `H`; all three forms of Remark (2)).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Root system bases, simple roots: Mathlib.
2. Cartan involution, 𝔞, restricted roots `Σ`, positive system: existence of θ and `IwasawaData` in LieGroups Layers 7, 9 (merged, not implemented); Lie-algebra theory in PR #2229 Layers 0–1; see #1.
3. `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `A`, `N`, `P = MAN`, Langlands decomposition, conjugacy: PR #1037 §2.3a for real points of reductive `ℝ`-groups; PR #2229 §3.1 for connected `G` with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre.
4. Tits system `(P, N_G(𝔞))` and Bruhat decomposition: PR #2229 §3.3, on Tau Ceti's `TauCeti.TitsSystem` and its standard parabolics (`standardParabolic`, `standardParabolicOrderIso`).
5. `𝔮_{Π'}`, `Q_{Π'} = N_G(𝔮_{Π'})`, bijection `Π' ↦ Q_{Π'}` onto subgroups containing `P`, `Q_{Π'} = ⋃ PwP`, Levi `L_{Π'}` and `M_{Π'}A_{Π'}N_{Π'}`, type, maximal parabolics: PR #2229 §§4.1–4.4.
6. Algebraic ℝ-parabolics and their dynamical description: Tau Ceti code (`IsParabolic`, `Cocharacter.parabolic`); comparison `Q = G ∩ 𝐐(ℝ)`: PR #1037 §2.3a for minimal ones, PR #2229 §4.5 for all.
7. Complex-group `P_s` for `s ∈ i𝔨` (Higgs paper): PR #2229 §4.4, complex case, with `P_s = dynamicalParabolic (−s)`; dynamic `Cocharacter.parabolic` covers rational `s`.
8. Real forms `G^τ` of complex reductive groups (#54), their Cartan involutions, and their parabolics as `G^τ ∩ Q` for `τ`-stable `Q`: PR #2229 §2.5 and §4.5, on LieGroups Layer 7's real forms (merged).
