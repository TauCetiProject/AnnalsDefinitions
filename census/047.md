# 47. Sobolev class W^{1,p}_loc(M, N) of continuous maps between manifolds

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2230 (SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds) specifies the whole definition as its target: the Euclidean chain rules and locality of Proposition 47.1 (§1.2, §§2.1–2.2), the chartwise class `IsSobolevMap` with chart independence, including sufficiency of one atlas of `M` and one of `N` (§4.2, 47.3(a), Proposition 47.2), the weak differential `HasWeakMFDeriv`/`weakMfderiv` (§4.3, 47.3(b)), `‖Df‖` (§5.1, 47.3(c)), the oriented Jacobian `J_f` with its chart formula (§§6.1–6.2, 47.3(d)), Remark (1) (§5.2, §6.3), Remark (2) (§9.1), Remark (3) (§10.1, §10.4) and Remark (4) (§7.1). It is the supplier that draft PR #2134 (QuasiregularMaps) contracts against, with matching section numbers. No merged roadmap specifies the class; its Euclidean and manifold inputs are in Tau Ceti code.

## Mathlib
- Has: charts/atlases, `ContMDiff`, `mfderiv`, `writtenInExtChartAt`, `Manifold.IsImmersionAt`, `IsRiemannianManifold`, orientations, `LinearMap.det`, `LinearMap.normDet`, `Orientation.volumeForm`, `Measure.map`, Lebesgue measure; Mathlib has no weak derivatives on domains.
- Missing: weak derivatives, W^{1,p}_loc, anything for maps between manifolds.

## Tau Ceti code
- Euclidean Sobolev: `TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn`, `TauCeti.W1p` (`Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/`), with `ChainRule.lean` (Lipschitz C¹ outer function), `ChangeOfVariables.lean` (`HasWeakFDerivOn.comp_contDiffOn_diffeo`), `Gluing.lean`, `Density.lean`; the chain rule `HasWeakFDerivOn.contDiffOn_comp` for continuous inner maps (`WeakDeriv/ChainRule.lean`); local W^{k,p} on planar sets `MemLpLoc`, `MemWkpLoc` (`Analysis/Sobolev/Local/Basic.lean`, ℂ only), with composition of continuous `W^{1,p}_loc` maps with `C¹` maps `MemWkpLoc.contDiffOn_comp` (`Local/ChainRule.lean`) and mixed-exponent products (`Local/Multiplication.lean`), still on planar domains.
- Chartwise null sets `TauCeti.IsNullSet` (`Geometry/Manifold/Measure.lean`) and their agreement with Riemannian-volume null sets `TauCeti.isNullSet_iff_riemannianVolume_eq_zero` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Null.lean`).
- Manifold scalar Sobolev on closed manifolds: `TauCeti.Manifold.SobolevH1` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sobolev/{H1,Chart}.lean`); bundle W^{k,p} `TauCeti.SobolevSection` (Psido Layer 5, finite-dimensional fibres).
- Riemannian volume `TauCeti.riemannianVolume`, `TauCeti.riemannianVolumeForm`, orientations `TauCeti.Manifold.Orientation`: present.
- Missing: the chartwise class of continuous maps M → N, the weak differential `Df`, `‖Df‖`, J_f.
