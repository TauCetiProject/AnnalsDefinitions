# 47. Sobolev class W^{1,p}_loc(M, N) of continuous maps between manifolds

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged `TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/` specifies the whole definition as its target: the Euclidean chain rules and locality of Proposition 47.1 (§1.2, §§2.1–2.2), the chartwise class `IsSobolevMap` with chart independence, including sufficiency of one atlas of `M` and one of `N` (§4.2, 47.3(a), Proposition 47.2), the weak differential `HasWeakMFDeriv` (§4.3, 47.3(b)), `‖Df‖` (§5.1, 47.3(c)), the oriented Jacobian `J_f` with its chart formula (§§6.1–6.2, 47.3(d)), Remark (1) (§5.2, §6.3), Remark (2) (§9.1), Remark (3) (§10.1, §10.4) and Remark (4) (§7.1). Merged QuasiregularMaps (PR #2134) states everything between manifolds on this class, citing these sections under its gaps `sobolev-maps-*`. In code: the oriented Jacobian of a linear map (§6.1, `Orientation.orientedJacobian`), the almost-everywhere filter on a manifold and positive continuous density measures (most of §3.1, `TauCeti.Manifold.ae`, `TauCeti.IsPositiveDensityMeasure`), and the two-dimensional analogue for maps from Riemann surfaces with its weak differential (PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis PH4.1); the local Lebesgue class `TauCeti.MemLpLoc` over every measure space and the first-order local Sobolev class `TauCeti.MemW1pLoc` on open subsets of every real normed space with Banach values (§§1.1–1.2 in large part); not the class `IsSobolevMap`, `HasWeakMFDeriv`, `‖Df‖` or `J_f` for general sources.

## Mathlib
- Has: charts/atlases, `ContMDiff`, `mfderiv`, `writtenInExtChartAt`, `Manifold.IsImmersionAt`, `IsRiemannianManifold`, orientations, `LinearMap.det`, `LinearMap.normDet`, `Orientation.volumeForm`, `Measure.map`, Lebesgue measure; Mathlib has no weak derivatives on domains.
- Missing: weak derivatives, W^{1,p}_loc, anything for maps between manifolds.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Charts, `mfderiv`, orientation, Riemannian metric/volume: Mathlib + Tau Ceti.
2. Euclidean weak derivative, chain rule for continuous inner maps with `C¹` outer map, change of variables under diffeomorphisms: Tau Ceti (`HasWeakFDerivOn.contDiffOn_comp`, `HasWeakFDerivOn.comp_contDiffOn_diffeo`); `W^{1,p}_loc` on opens of every finite-dimensional real normed space with Haar measure, with Banach values: SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§1.1–1.2, in Tau Ceti in large part (`MemLpLoc`, `MemW1pLoc`, with `memWkpLoc_one_iff_memW1pLoc`); Proposition 47.1: SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§1.2, 2.1–2.2.
3. Null sets on manifolds via charts, their agreement with Riemannian-volume null sets, the a.e. filter on `M` and positive continuous density measures: Tau Ceti `TauCeti.IsNullSet`, `TauCeti.isNullSet_iff_riemannianVolume_eq_zero`, `TauCeti.Manifold.ae`, `TauCeti.IsPositiveDensityMeasure` (SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §3.1, apart from the one-atlas criterion, gluing, the Radon property and `Lᵖ` comparison).
4. Class W^{1,p}_loc(M,N), chart independence (Proposition 47.2), weak differential Df: target of merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§4.2–4.3 (the case of two-dimensional sources is in code, `TauCeti.MemWkpLocMap`, `TauCeti.HasWeakMFDerivOn`).
5. ‖Df‖ and J_f (47.3(c),(d)), Hadamard bound: target of merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §5.1, §§6.2–6.3; the linear-algebra Jacobian §6.1 is in Tau Ceti (`Orientation.orientedJacobian`).
6. Consumers: quasiregular maps #41, quasiregularly elliptic manifolds #42 (merged QuasiregularMaps).

## Library-scale gap
Merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds is the roadmap for this theory, and merged QuasiregularMaps contracts against it (Ziemer, *Weakly Differentiable Functions*, Ch. 2; Iwaniec–Martin, *Geometric Function Theory and Non-linear Analysis*, Ch. 4–6; Rickman, *Quasiregular Mappings*, Ch. I; Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen; Convent–Van Schaftingen): Euclidean `W^{1,p}_loc` with locality and chain rules, the manifold a.e. filter, the continuous Sobolev class with its weak differential, norm and Jacobian, pullback of forms (needed by Heikkilä–Pankka for de Rham algebras), the Nash-embedding characterization, extrinsic spaces and degree. It sits between PDE Lane A and QuasiregularMaps. What remains is implementation, continuing from its Layer 1 (`MemLpLoc` and `MemW1pLoc` in every dimension are done in large part) to the Euclidean chain rules of Layer 2 and the class of Layer 4; §6.1, the a.e. filter of §3.1 and the basic theory of positive continuous density measures are done.
