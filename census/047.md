# 47. Sobolev class W^{1,p}_loc(M, N) of continuous maps between manifolds

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged `TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/` specifies the whole definition as its target: the Euclidean chain rules and locality of Proposition 47.1 (§1.2, §§2.1–2.2), the chartwise class `IsSobolevMap` with chart independence, including sufficiency of one atlas of `M` and one of `N` (§4.2, 47.3(a), Proposition 47.2), the weak differential `HasWeakMFDeriv` (§4.3, 47.3(b)), `‖Df‖` (§5.1, 47.3(c)), the oriented Jacobian `J_f` with its chart formula (§§6.1–6.2, 47.3(d)), Remark (1) (§5.2, §6.3), Remark (2) (§9.1), Remark (3) (§10.1, §10.4) and Remark (4) (§7.1). It is the supplier that draft PR #2134 (QuasiregularMaps) contracts against, with matching section numbers. None of it is in code; its Euclidean and manifold inputs are, and the two-dimensional analogue for maps from Riemann surfaces (PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis) is.

## Mathlib
- Has: charts/atlases, `ContMDiff`, `mfderiv`, `writtenInExtChartAt`, `Manifold.IsImmersionAt`, `IsRiemannianManifold`, orientations, `LinearMap.det`, `LinearMap.normDet`, `Orientation.volumeForm`, `Measure.map`, Lebesgue measure; Mathlib has no weak derivatives on domains.
- Missing: weak derivatives, W^{1,p}_loc, anything for maps between manifolds.

## Tau Ceti code
- Euclidean Sobolev: `TauCeti.HasWeakFDerivOn`, `TauCeti.W1p` (`Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/`), with `ChainRule.lean` (Lipschitz C¹ outer function), `ChangeOfVariables.lean` (`HasWeakFDerivOn.comp_contDiffOn_diffeo`), `Gluing.lean`, `Density.lean`; the chain rule `HasWeakFDerivOn.contDiffOn_comp` for continuous inner maps (`WeakDeriv/ChainRule.lean`); local W^{k,p} on planar sets `MemLpLoc`, `MemWkpLoc` (`Analysis/Sobolev/Local/Basic.lean`, ℂ only), with composition of continuous `W^{1,p}_loc` maps with `C¹` maps `MemWkpLoc.contDiffOn_comp` (`Local/ChainRule.lean`) and mixed-exponent products (`Local/Multiplication.lean`), still on planar domains.
- Chartwise null sets `TauCeti.IsNullSet` (`Geometry/Manifold/Measure.lean`), images of null sets under maps differentiable on them between manifolds with `dim M ≤ dim N` `TauCeti.IsNullSet.image_of_mdifferentiableOn` (`Geometry/Manifold/Measure/Differentiable.lean`, the first claim of SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §3.3), and their agreement with Riemannian-volume null sets `TauCeti.isNullSet_iff_riemannianVolume_eq_zero` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Null.lean`).
- Maps from a Riemann surface (possibly with boundary) into a manifold modelled on a real Banach space: `TauCeti.MemWkpLocMap I p k u`, `MemWkpLocMapOn` (continuous, coordinate expressions in the planar `MemWkpLoc`; `Geometry/Manifold/Sobolev/LocalMap.lean`, bordered version `LocalMap/Bordered.lean`), with chart independence for `k ≤ 1` `TauCeti.memWkpLocMapOn_iff_of_subset_maximalAtlas`, composition with `C^ℓ` maps `TauCeti.MemWkpLocMapOn.contMDiffOn_comp` (`LocalMap/Atlas.lean`) and every order for `2 < p < ∞` (`LocalMap/Atlas/Higher.lean`). This is Proposition 47.2 for two-dimensional sources (PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis PH4.1), with no weak differential, `‖Df‖` or `J_f`.
- Manifold scalar Sobolev on closed manifolds: `TauCeti.Manifold.SobolevH1` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Sobolev/{H1,Chart}.lean`); bundle W^{k,p} `TauCeti.SobolevSection` (Psido Layer 5, finite-dimensional fibres).
- Riemannian volume `TauCeti.riemannianVolume`, `TauCeti.riemannianVolumeForm`, orientations `TauCeti.Manifold.Orientation`: present.
- Missing: the chartwise class of continuous maps M → N, the weak differential `Df`, `‖Df‖`, J_f.

## Merged roadmaps
- `TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/` ([README](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/README.md)): §1.1 (generalizes `MemLpLoc`/`MemWkpLoc` in place from ℂ to open subsets of every finite-dimensional real normed space with an additive Haar measure, with Banach values; no inner product is used, the classes are independent of the Haar measure and carried along continuous linear equivalences, and Tau Ceti suppliers on inner product spaces apply through `E ≃L[ℝ] EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)`. Source manifolds are modelled on such normed spaces, as in PR #2134's conventions), §1.2 (`W^{1,p}_loc(Ω; F)`, a.e. uniqueness of the weak derivative, sheaf property; 47.1(c)), §2.1 (chain rule for continuous inner maps and `C¹` outer maps with no derivative bound; 47.1(b)), §2.2 (change of variables under `C¹` diffeomorphisms; 47.1(a)), §3.1 (the a.e. filter from `TauCeti.IsNullSet`, positive continuous density measures; consumes R4.6), §4.1 (`MemW1pLocM`, Sobolev functions on `M`), §4.2 (`IsSobolevMap E E' p f`: continuous `f` with chart representatives in `W^{1,p}_loc`, chart independence: the condition for the preferred charts, for every chart pair of the maximal atlases, and for every chart pair from some atlas of `M` and some atlas of `N` are equivalent; 47.3(a), Proposition 47.2), §4.3 (`HasWeakMFDeriv`, existence `IsSobolevMap.exists_hasWeakMFDeriv`, a.e. uniqueness, chart formula in every chart pair, chart measurability; 47.3(b)), §§4.4–4.6 (`C¹` members with `Df = mfderiv f`, restriction, locality, metric independence, chain rules under `C¹` submersions and postcomposition), §5.1 (`‖Df‖` for compatible continuous tangent norms, Riemannian metrics as a special case; 47.3(c)), §5.2 (`f ∈ W^{1,p}_loc` iff `‖Df‖ ∈ L^p_loc` given `W^{1,1}_loc`; Remark (1)), §6.1 (`orientedJacobian`), §6.2 (`J_f`; the chart formula `J_f(x) = LinearMap.det (dψ ∘ Df(x) ∘ dφ⁻¹) · w_N(ψ(f(x))) / w_M(φ(x))` in positively oriented charts on one model space, with `w_M`, `w_N` the coordinate weights of the Riemannian volumes (§3.1), `√det(g_{ij})` for Lebesgue measure on `ℝⁿ`; 47.3(d)), §6.3 (Hadamard `|J_f| ≤ ‖Df‖ⁿ`, `J_f ∈ L^{p/n}_loc`; Remark (1)), §7.1 (Euclidean agreement, `J_f = det Du`; Remark (4)), §9.1 (split immersions, Nash embedding with the same `‖Df‖`; Remark (2)), §10.1 and §10.4 (the extrinsic space `W^{1,p}_ι(M, N)` for discontinuous maps into compact targets and its comparison with intrinsic weak maps; Remark (3)). Targets are Banach manifolds throughout; only the Jacobian and degree layers need equal finite dimensions. Layer 8 (pullback of forms, weak `d` commutes with pullback) and Layer 11 (degree of `W^{1,n}` maps, `∫ J_f = deg f · vol N`) go beyond the definition. Not implemented (no `IsSobolevMap`, `HasWeakMFDeriv`, `orientedJacobian` in Tau Ceti; `MemLpLoc`/`MemWkpLoc` are still planar).
- Related: Yamabe Y1 (scalar W^{k,p} finite-chart adapter on closed manifolds), PseudodifferentialOperators §5.2 (bundle W^{k,p}), PDE Lane A (Euclidean W^{1,p}, chain rule, change of variables), PseudoholomorphicCurveAnalysis PH1.1 (planar `MemWkpLoc`) and PH4.1 (`W^{k,p}_loc(Σ, M)` from Riemann surfaces, `kp > 2`, in code), RiemannianGeometry R4.6 (null sets, implemented).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Charts, `mfderiv`, orientation, Riemannian metric/volume: Mathlib + Tau Ceti.
2. Euclidean weak derivative, chain rule for continuous inner maps with `C¹` outer map, change of variables under diffeomorphisms: Tau Ceti (`HasWeakFDerivOn.contDiffOn_comp`, `HasWeakFDerivOn.comp_contDiffOn_diffeo`); `W^{1,p}_loc` on opens of every finite-dimensional real normed space with Haar measure, with Banach values: SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§1.1–1.2, refactoring the planar `MemWkpLoc`; Proposition 47.1: SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§1.2, 2.1–2.2.
3. Null sets on manifolds via charts and their agreement with Riemannian-volume null sets: Tau Ceti `TauCeti.IsNullSet`, `TauCeti.isNullSet_iff_riemannianVolume_eq_zero`; the a.e. filter on `M`: SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §3.1.
4. Class W^{1,p}_loc(M,N), chart independence (Proposition 47.2), weak differential Df: target of merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§4.2–4.3 (the case of two-dimensional sources without `Df` is in code, `TauCeti.MemWkpLocMap`).
5. ‖Df‖ and J_f (47.3(c),(d)), Hadamard bound: target of merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §5.1, §§6.1–6.3.
6. Consumers: quasiregular maps #41, quasiregularly elliptic manifolds #42 (PR #2134).

## Library-scale gap
Merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds is the roadmap for this theory, and PR #2134 contracts against it (Ziemer, *Weakly Differentiable Functions*, Ch. 2; Iwaniec–Martin, *Geometric Function Theory and Non-linear Analysis*, Ch. 4–6; Rickman, *Quasiregular Mappings*, Ch. I; Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen; Convent–Van Schaftingen): Euclidean `W^{1,p}_loc` with locality and chain rules, the manifold a.e. filter, the continuous Sobolev class with its weak differential, norm and Jacobian, pullback of forms (needed by Heikkilä–Pankka for de Rham algebras), the Nash-embedding characterization, extrinsic spaces and degree. It sits between PDE Lane A and PR #2134. What remains is implementation, starting with its Layer 1 (`MemLpLoc`/`MemWkpLoc` in every dimension).
