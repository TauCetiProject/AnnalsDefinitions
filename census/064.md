## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Definitions 64.1 and 64.2 are in Tau Ceti: the group Spin^c(n) as a compact Lie group with its maps to SO(n) and U(1) (`CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup`), and Spin^c structures on a real vector bundle with fibre inner products as principal Spin^c(n)-bundles with an equivariant map to the orthonormal frame bundle, with isomorphisms and pullback (`TauCeti.SpinCStructure`, an `OrthonormalFrameLift`), and the structure induced from a lift along a homomorphism to Spin^c(n) (`OrthonormalFrameLift.extendSpinC`, the canonical structure of an almost Hermitian four-manifold through `U(2) → Spinᶜ(4)`), the determinant line `P ×_{z²} ℂ` of 64.2 (`TauCeti.SpinCStructure.determinantLine`) and its first Chern class `c₁(𝔰)` in `H²(X; ℤ)`, natural under pullback (`TauCeti.SpinCStructure.firstChernClass`), with orientation reversal (`SpinCStructure.reverse`, commuting with pullback), twisting by a principal `U(1)`-bundle (`TauCeti.SpinCStructure.twist`), the `Spin^c` structure of a Spin structure and its trivialized determinant line (`TauCeti.SpinStructure.toSpinC`, `determinantLineSection`), the unitary frames of a rank-four bundle with a fibrewise orthogonal complex structure as a lift along `U(2) ⊆ SO(4)` (`OrthonormalFrameBundle.unitaryFrameLift`) with the canonical structure through `extendSpinC` (`OrthonormalFrameBundle.canonicalSpinCStructure`) and its determinant line (`firstChernClass_canonicalSpinCStructure`), and the group part of sums (`CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroupProd`). The relation `c₁(𝔰) ≡ w₂ mod 2`, the change of `c₁` under twisting, Proposition 64.3 (metric independence, existence iff `w₂` lifts, the `H²(M; ℤ)` torsor, restriction to hypersurfaces) and Turaev's model 64.4 are not in code: Tau Ceti has no Stiefel–Whitney classes of vector bundles (its `StiefelWhitney` directories hold the Stiefel–Whitney invariants of quadratic forms in Galois cohomology), so `w₂`, existence and the torsor wait on CharacteristicClasses C3 (C3.1 Leray–Hirsch, C3.2 Stiefel–Whitney classes, C3.5 `w₂` as the spin obstruction); Leray–Hirsch over a base with a finite trivializing cover, the first step of C3.1, is in code (`TopCat.lerayHirsch_bijective_of_finite_cover`), as are `c₁` of Spin-induced and orientation-reversed structures (`SpinStructure.firstChernClass_toSpinC`, `SpinCStructure.firstChernClass_reverse`) and the spinor bundles and determinant line of any `C^k` principal `Spinᶜ(4)`-bundle (`Orientation.spinorBundle`, `CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup.detLineBundle`). The paper uses `c₁`, the torsor and restrictions to 3-manifolds and cobordism ends. Merged [FourManifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/FourManifolds/README.md) §4.1 ("Spinᶜ, Wu and torsion") specifies 64.1–64.3 in general rank (principal lifts, existence iff W₃ = β(w₂) = 0, the H²(−; ℤ) torsor, determinant lines, restriction to boundaries); merged HeegaardFloer §6.2 (F4.B) covers closed oriented 3-manifolds in Turaev's nonvanishing-vector-field model (Prop. 64.4), with c₁ and the H²(Y; ℤ) torsor. The comparison of the two models on 3-manifolds is not a target.

## Mathlib
Has `spinGroup`, `pinGroup` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinGroup.lean`), `Circle`, principal-bundle-adjacent `FiberBundle`/`VectorBundle`. No Spin^c(n), no spin or Spin^c structures, no Stiefel-Whitney classes.

## Tau Ceti code
- The group Spin^c (64.1): `CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup Q hQ` = (Spin(Q) × `Circle`)/`spinCKernel`, with `mk`, `ofSpin`, `ofCircle`, the determinant character `det` (`[s, z] ↦ z²`), `toSpecialOrthogonal`, and `mem_ker_toSpecialOrthogonal_prod_det_iff` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Basic.lean`); its smooth compact Lie-group structure with the quotient map a local diffeomorphism and `contMDiff_realDet` (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/Basic.lean`); half-spin actions descended to Spin^c (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/HalfSpin.lean`).
- Spin^c structures (64.2): `TauCeti.SpinCStructure F E n` (rank `n ≥ 1`), a principal `spinCGroup (realCliffordForm n 0)`-bundle with its quotient topology and a continuous map to the orthonormal frame bundle over the identity, equivariant along `spinCGroup.toSpecialOrthogonal` (`SpinCStructure.toFrame_smul_mk`), the central circle acting trivially on frames (`SpinCStructure.toFrame_smul_ofCircle`) (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Basic.lean`). It is an instance of `TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameLift F E n G ρ` (lifts of the orthonormal frame bundle along `ρ : G →* GL(ℝⁿ)`), which supplies isomorphisms `OrthonormalFrameLift.Equiv` with `refl`, `symm`, `trans`, and `pullback`, `Equiv.pullback` (`Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Lift/Basic.lean`), and extension of structure group `OrthonormalFrameLift.extend` with its Spin^c case `OrthonormalFrameLift.extendSpinC` (`OrthonormalFrame/Extension.lean`). The bundles are topological vector bundles with fibre inner products, the generality of FourManifolds §4.1.
- The determinant line and the rank-four spinor bundles (SeibergWittenTheory §1.2): `TauCeti.SpinCStructure.determinantLine` (`P ×_{z²} ℂ`, a Hermitian line bundle on which the central circle acts with weight two, `finrank_determinantLine`, `mk_ofCircle_smul_determinantLine`), the half-spinor bundles `TauCeti.SpinCStructure.halfSpinorBundle` in rank four with Clifford multiplication `SpinCStructure.cliffordMul` (`cliffordMul_cliffordMul`, `norm_cliffordMul`) and the fibrewise `⋀² W^ε ≃ L`, `exteriorPowerHalfSpinorEquiv` (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Spinor.lean`), all on `TauCeti.AssociatedBundle`.
- Spin structures: `TauCeti.SpinStructure` is the same carrier for `CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinGroupZero n` and `spinVectorAction` (SpinGeometry G4.2), with the induced orientation, the sheet involution and the covering of oriented frames (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Orientation.lean`).
- The canonical `Spinᶜ` lift of the unitary group of an almost Hermitian four-space: `TauCeti.SeibergWitten.unitaryLift : U(V, J) → Spinᶜ(4)` (continuous), `canonicalLineFixingSubgroup`, `canonicalLineFixingSubgroupEquiv`, and `det_unitaryLift` (`Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/UnitaryLift.lean`).
- The structure-group part of restriction to boundaries (FourManifolds §4.1): `Spinᶜ(n)` for the standard form, `CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinCGroupZero`, and the inclusion `CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinCInclusion : Spinᶜ(n) → Spinᶜ(n + 1)` onto the stabilizer of the last coordinate vector, `range_realCliffordSpinCInclusion`, a closed embedding of topological groups (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Inclusion.lean`, `Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Inclusion.lean`).
- Sums (FourManifolds §4.1), the group part: `CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroupProd : Spinᶜ(Q₁) × Spinᶜ(Q₂) →* Spinᶜ(Q₁ ⊕ Q₂)` with `det_spinCGroupProd`, `toSpecialOrthogonal_spinCGroupProd` (block sum of rotations), the antidiagonal-circle kernel `mem_ker_spinCGroupProd_iff`, `ker_spinCGroupProd` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Prod.lean`), and continuity `continuous_spinCGroupProd`, `spinCGroupProdContinuousMonoidHom` (`Topology/Algebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC/Prod.lean`).
