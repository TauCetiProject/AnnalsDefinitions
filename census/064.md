## Status
ROADMAP-PR. The general definition 64.2 (the group Spin^c(n), lifts of the oriented frame bundle, existence iff W₃ = 0, the H² torsor) is a target only of open PR #936 (FourManifolds F4.1), which merged SeibergWittenTheory already cites. Merged HeegaardFloer §6.2 (F4.B) covers only closed oriented 3-manifolds, in Turaev's nonvanishing-vector-field model (Prop. 64.4), with c₁ and the H²(Y; ℤ) torsor. Tau Ceti has the group Spin^c(n) of 64.1 as a Lie group with its maps to SO(n) and U(1); no Spin^c structure, torsor or Turaev model is implemented.

## Mathlib
Has `spinGroup`, `pinGroup` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinGroup.lean`), `Circle`, principal-bundle-adjacent `FiberBundle`/`VectorBundle`. No Spin^c(n), no spin or Spin^c structures, no Stiefel-Whitney classes.

## Tau Ceti code
- The group Spin^c (64.1): `CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup Q hQ` = (Spin(Q) × `Circle`)/`spinCKernel`, with `mk`, `ofSpin`, `ofCircle`, the determinant character `det` (`[s, z] ↦ z²`), `toSpecialOrthogonal`, and `mem_ker_toSpecialOrthogonal_prod_det_iff` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC.lean`); its smooth compact Lie-group structure with the quotient map a local diffeomorphism and `contMDiff_realDet` (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/Basic.lean`); half-spin actions descended to Spin^c (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/HalfSpin.lean`).
- No Spin^c structure (principal lift), no H² torsor, no Turaev model (grep `SpinStructure`, Turaev vector-field model: none).
- Seiberg-Witten algebra on a fixed four-dimensional Spin^c spinor module (`Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/ChargeConjugation.lean`, `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Trace/Free.lean`) without the global structure.
- Spin side: `TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinContinuousMulEquivUnitsRange` (cited by SpinGeometry G4).
