## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Definitions 64.1 and 64.2 are in Tau Ceti: the group Spin^c(n) as a compact Lie group with its maps to SO(n) and U(1) (`CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup`), and Spin^c structures on a real vector bundle with fibre inner products as principal Spin^c(n)-bundles with an equivariant map to the orthonormal frame bundle, with isomorphisms and pullback (`TauCeti.SpinCStructure`, an `OrthonormalFrameLift`), and the structure induced from a lift along a homomorphism to Spin^c(n) (`OrthonormalFrameLift.extendSpinC`, the canonical structure of an almost Hermitian four-manifold through `U(2) → Spinᶜ(4)`), and the determinant line `P ×_{z²} ℂ` of 64.2 (`TauCeti.SpinCStructure.determinantLine`). `c₁(𝔰)` of 64.2 (in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3731, with naturality), Proposition 64.3 (metric independence, existence iff `w₂` lifts, the `H²(M; ℤ)` torsor, restriction to hypersurfaces) and Turaev's model 64.4 are not in code; the paper uses `c₁`, the torsor and restrictions to 3-manifolds and cobordism ends. Merged [FourManifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/FourManifolds/README.md) §4.1 ("Spinᶜ, Wu and torsion") specifies 64.1–64.3 in general rank (principal lifts, existence iff W₃ = β(w₂) = 0, the H²(−; ℤ) torsor, determinant lines, restriction to boundaries); merged HeegaardFloer §6.2 (F4.B) covers closed oriented 3-manifolds in Turaev's nonvanishing-vector-field model (Prop. 64.4), with c₁ and the H²(Y; ℤ) torsor. The comparison of the two models on 3-manifolds is not a target.

## Tau Ceti code
- The group Spin^c (64.1): `CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup Q hQ` = (Spin(Q) × `Circle`)/`spinCKernel`, with `mk`, `ofSpin`, `ofCircle`, the determinant character `det` (`[s, z] ↦ z²`), `toSpecialOrthogonal`, and `mem_ker_toSpecialOrthogonal_prod_det_iff` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC.lean`); its smooth compact Lie-group structure with the quotient map a local diffeomorphism and `contMDiff_realDet` (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/Basic.lean`); half-spin actions descended to Spin^c (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/HalfSpin.lean`).
- Spin^c structures (64.2): `TauCeti.SpinCStructure F E n` (rank `n ≥ 1`), a principal `spinCGroup (realCliffordForm n 0)`-bundle with its quotient topology and a continuous map to the orthonormal frame bundle over the identity, equivariant along `spinCGroup.toSpecialOrthogonal` (`SpinCStructure.toFrame_smul_mk`), the central circle acting trivially on frames (`SpinCStructure.toFrame_smul_ofCircle`) (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Basic.lean`). It is an instance of `TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameLift F E n G ρ` (lifts of the orthonormal frame bundle along `ρ : G →* GL(ℝⁿ)`), which supplies isomorphisms `OrthonormalFrameLift.Equiv` with `refl`, `symm`, `trans`, and `pullback`, `Equiv.pullback` (`Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Lift.lean`), and extension of structure group `OrthonormalFrameLift.extend` with its Spin^c case `OrthonormalFrameLift.extendSpinC` (`OrthonormalFrame/Extension.lean`). The bundles are topological vector bundles with fibre inner products, the generality of FourManifolds §4.1.
- The determinant line and the rank-four spinor bundles (SeibergWittenTheory SW1.2): `TauCeti.SpinCStructure.determinantLine` (`P ×_{z²} ℂ`, a Hermitian line bundle on which the central circle acts with weight two, `finrank_determinantLine`, `mk_ofCircle_smul_determinantLine`), the half-spinor bundles `TauCeti.SpinCStructure.halfSpinorBundle` in rank four with Clifford multiplication `SpinCStructure.cliffordMul` (`cliffordMul_cliffordMul`, `norm_cliffordMul`) and the fibrewise `⋀² W^ε ≃ L`, `exteriorPowerHalfSpinorEquiv` (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Spinor.lean`), all on `TauCeti.AssociatedBundle`.
- Spin structures: `TauCeti.SpinStructure` is the same carrier for `CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinGroupZero n` and `spinVectorAction` (SpinGeometry G4.2), with the induced orientation, the sheet involution and the covering of oriented frames (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure/Orientation.lean`).
- The canonical `Spinᶜ` lift of the unitary group of an almost Hermitian four-space: `TauCeti.SeibergWitten.unitaryLift : U(V, J) → Spinᶜ(4)` (continuous), `canonicalLineFixingSubgroup`, `canonicalLineFixingSubgroupEquiv`, and `det_unitaryLift` (`Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/UnitaryLift.lean`).
- No `c₁` of a Spin^c structure, no H² torsor, no existence criterion, no restriction to hypersurfaces, no Turaev model (grep `SpinCStructure` finds only the files above).
