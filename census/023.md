# 23. Hodge-generic abelian variety

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1038 (ShimuraData) §1.5 defines `hodgeGeneric` for polarized complex abelian varieties (citing this very Masser–Zannier paper), on top of its §1.2 `mumfordTateGroup`; nothing is implemented.

Depends on #12 (Hodge structure on `H^1` of an abelian variety) and #13 (Mumford–Tate group).

## Mathlib
- No Hodge structures, no Mumford–Tate groups, no complex abelian varieties as analytic objects. `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/SymplecticGroup.lean` (`Matrix.symplecticGroup`, as a group of matrices, not a `ℚ`-algebraic group; no `GSp`). `AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Abelian.lean` only proves commutativity of proper geometrically integral group schemes.
- Nearest missing piece: algebraic groups over `ℚ` as group schemes, the Deligne torus, `MT(V)`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hodge structures (Completed/HodgeStructures): `TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/` incl. `WeightOne/Standard.lean`, `WeightOne/RiemannForm.lean`, `WeilOperator.lean`.
- Algebraic-group infrastructure: `TauCeti.GeneralLinear.coordinateHopfAlgebra`, `TauCeti.HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal` (the inputs PR #1038 §1.2 names), and Hopf ideals ↔ closed subgroups `CommHopfAlgCat.hopfIdealOrderIsoClosedSubgroup` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Scheme/Classification.lean`).
- No `mumfordTateGroup`, no `GSp` as algebraic group, no Hodge-genericity. Definition absent.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Rational Hodge structures, polarizations: Completed/HodgeStructures (Tau Ceti, done).
2. `ℚ`-algebraic groups, `GL(V)`, Hopf ideals: Tau Ceti (`coordinateHopfAlgebra`, `HopfIdeal`); ReductiveGroups (merged).
3. Deligne torus `S` and `h: S → GL(V)_ℝ`: ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.0a (merged; no Deligne torus in Tau Ceti code).
4. `MT(V)` (#13): PR #1038 §1.2 / PR #933 §P.3a.
5. `GSp(V,ψ)` as a `ℚ`-group: PR #1038 (uses ReductiveGroups Layer 7 identifications).
6. `H^1(A(ℂ),ℚ)` with its polarized Hodge structure (#12): PR #849 §A5.
7. `hodgeGeneric` (23.3(a)): PR #1038 §1.5. Over `ℚ̄` via a fixed embedding (23.3(b)): trivial once (a) exists. Family version (23.3(c), generic MT of a VHS): unplanned as a target.

## Library-scale gap
Planned in PR #1038 for the pointwise notion. The family notion (generic Mumford–Tate group of an abelian scheme over a smooth complex base, very-general-fibre statement via Cattani–Deligne–Kaplan/André) would belong to the successor "Variations of Hodge structure" roadmap sketched in Completed/HodgeStructures. References: Milne, *Introduction to Shimura varieties* §2; Moonen, *Notes on Mumford–Tate groups*.
