# 23. Hodge-generic abelian variety

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [ShimuraData](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraData/README.md) (PR #1038) §1.5 "Hodge-generic abelian variety" (README l.199) defines `hodgeGeneric` for polarized complex abelian varieties of dimension `g ≥ 1` (`MT(H¹(A,ℚ))` equal to the full symplectic-similitude group of the rational polarization, citing this very Masser–Zannier paper), on its §1.2 `mumfordTateGroup` (l.121). Tau Ceti has the Mumford–Tate group of a homomorphism `h : S → G_ℝ` (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal`), but not `H¹(A,ℚ)` with its Hodge structure, `GSp` as a `ℚ`-group or `hodgeGeneric`. The Mumford–Tate group is also target §P.3a of merged ShimuraVarieties. The family version 23.3(c) is not a target.

Depends on #12 (Hodge structure on `H^1` of an abelian variety, a target of merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli Layer A5) and #13 (Mumford–Tate group, merged ShimuraVarieties §P.3a).

## Mathlib
- No Hodge structures, no Mumford–Tate groups, no complex abelian varieties as analytic objects. `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/SymplecticGroup.lean` (`Matrix.symplecticGroup`, as a group of matrices, not a `ℚ`-algebraic group; no `GSp`). `AlgebraicGeometry/Group/Abelian.lean` only proves commutativity of proper geometrically integral group schemes.
- Nearest missing piece: algebraic groups over `ℚ` as group schemes, the Deligne torus, `MT(V)`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hodge structures (Completed/HodgeStructures): `TauCeti/Geometry/Hodge/` incl. `WeightOne/Standard.lean`, `WeightOne/RiemannForm.lean`, `WeilOperator.lean`.
- The Mumford–Tate group of a homomorphism `h : S → G_ℝ` from the Deligne torus to an affine `ℚ`-group (ShimuraData §1.2, ShimuraVarieties §P.3a, partial): `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.mumfordTateHopfIdeal` (the largest Hopf ideal of `O(G)` whose real extension `h` kills), minimality `le_mumfordTateHopfIdeal_iff`, the factorization `mumfordTateLift` with `mumfordTateLift_unique`, `mumfordTateHopfIdeal_eq_augmentation_iff`, finiteness `exists_finset_sup_eq_mumfordTateHopfIdeal` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/MumfordTate.lean`). - The Deligne torus `TauCeti.DeligneTorus.S` with its points (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/DeligneTorus/Basic.lean`), its splitting over `ℂ`, the cocharacter `μ` and the characters (`complexSplitting`, `mu`, `characterGroup`, `DeligneTorus/Splitting.lean`).
- No `MT(V)` of a rational Hodge structure, no connectedness or reductivity (§§1.3–1.4), no `GSp` as algebraic group, no `H¹(A,ℚ)` of a complex abelian variety, no Hodge-genericity. Definition absent.

## Library-scale gap
Specified in merged ShimuraData §1.5 for the pointwise notion, on merged ShimuraVarieties §P.3a for the Mumford–Tate group. The family notion (generic Mumford–Tate group of an abelian scheme over a smooth complex base, very-general-fibre statement via Cattani–Deligne–Kaplan/André) would belong to the successor "Variations of Hodge structure" roadmap sketched in Completed/HodgeStructures. References: Milne, *Introduction to Shimura varieties* §2; Moonen, *Notes on Mumford–Tate groups*.
