# 79. Minimal parabolic subgroups and the Langlands decomposition (real rank one)

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) has a section §2.3a whose title and content are exactly this definition: `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `P = MAN`, Prop. 79.2(a)–(f), the real-rank-one clause and the algebraic comparison with the minimal ℝ-parabolic of a cocharacter, with the `SO₀(n,1)` example as a Check. Merged [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §3.1 extends `P = MAN` to connected groups with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre, and merged [LieGroupsPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/LieGroupsPartII/README.md) Layer 3 to every reductive Cartan datum (finite central covers, disconnected groups). Not implemented; the upstream Cartan/Iwasawa/restricted-root inputs (RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layer 9) are merged but implemented only for `GL_n`.

## Mathlib
- Has: `LieGroup`, `LeftInvariantDerivation` (Lie algebra of a Lie group), Lie algebra theory (Killing form, Cartan subalgebras, root spaces for split Cartan subalgebras), `Matrix.specialOrthogonalGroup`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup`.
- Missing: Lie exponential for abstract Lie groups, closed-subgroup theorem, Cartan involutions, maximal abelian subspaces of `𝔭`, restricted roots, Iwasawa decomposition, parabolic subgroups of real groups. (Mathlib's `GroupTheory/GroupAction/Iwasawa.lean` is Iwasawa's simplicity criterion and is unrelated.)

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lie groups, left-invariant derivations: Mathlib.
2. `lieExp`, `Ad`, closed subgroups as Lie subgroups: Tau Ceti (LieGroups Layers 0–2 done).
3. Cartan involution and Cartan decomposition `K × 𝔭 ≅ G`: target LieGroups Layer 9 (not implemented beyond matrix groups); algebraic Cartan involutions in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.IsCartanInvolution`); their conjugacy: target in ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.0.1 (merged).
4. Maximal abelian `𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭`, restricted roots `Σ(𝔤,𝔞)`, `N₊`, Iwasawa `G = KAN`: target LieGroups Layer 9 (only `GL_n` Iwasawa implemented).
5. `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `P = MAN`, Langlands decomposition, real-rank-one clause: target in AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a (merged); RealSemisimpleLieGroups §3.1 for non-linear groups with finite centre; LieGroupsPartII §§3.1–3.5 for every reductive Cartan datum (merged).
6. Comparison with the minimal ℝ-parabolic of a cocharacter: dynamic parabolics in Tau Ceti; the comparison, with `𝐒` the Zariski closure of `A`, in AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a; conjugacy of maximal ℝ-split tori in RealSemisimpleLieGroups §2.4.
7. Worked example `SO₀(n,1)`: a Check of AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a and of RealSemisimpleLieGroups §3.1 (`M = SO(n − 1)`, `N ≅ ℝ^{n−1}`, `G/P ≅ S^{n−1}`), with its Bruhat cells in RealSemisimpleLieGroups §3.2.
