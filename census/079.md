# 79. Minimal parabolic subgroups and the Langlands decomposition (real rank one)

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1037 "Automorphic forms on reductive groups" has a section §2.3a whose title and content are exactly this definition: `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `P = MAN`, Prop. 79.2(a)–(f), the real-rank-one clause and the algebraic comparison with the minimal ℝ-parabolic of a cocharacter. Its upstream Cartan/Iwasawa/restricted-root inputs are merged (RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layer 9) but are only implemented for `GL_n`.

## Mathlib
- Has: `LieGroup`, `LeftInvariantDerivation` (Lie algebra of a Lie group), Lie algebra theory (Killing form, Cartan subalgebras, root spaces for split Cartan subalgebras), `Matrix.specialOrthogonalGroup`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup`.
- Missing: Lie exponential for abstract Lie groups, closed-subgroup theorem, Cartan involutions, maximal abelian subspaces of `𝔭`, restricted roots, Iwasawa decomposition, parabolic subgroups of real groups. (Mathlib's `GroupTheory/GroupAction/Iwasawa.lean` is Iwasawa's simplicity criterion and is unrelated.)

## Tau Ceti code
- Lie group basics: `TauCeti.lieExp` (`Geometry/Lie/Exponential/Basic.lean`), `Ad` (`Geometry/Lie/Adjoint/Derivation.lean`), maximal tori and Weyl group (`TauCeti.Lie.IsMaximalTorus`, `Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Weyl/`).
- Matrix Iwasawa decomposition for `GL_n(ℝ/ℂ)` only: `TauCeti.iwasawaDiffeomorph` (`Geometry/Lie/Iwasawa.lean`), with the positive upper-triangular Lie group (`Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/PosUpperTriangular.lean`). Matrix Cartan decompositions of `Sp(2n, ℝ)` and the realified `GL(n, ℂ)`: `cartanHomeomorph`, `realifyCartanHomeomorph` (`Analysis/Matrix/Cartan/Realify.lean`).
- Algebraic side: dynamic parabolics for a cocharacter, `TauCeti.Cocharacter.parabolicDefiningHopfIdeal` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Dynamic/Representability.lean`), dynamic Levi and unipotent subgroups (`Dynamic/Levi/`, `Dynamic/Unipotent/`), `IsParabolic` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Parabolic/Basic.lean`).
- Missing: `CartanInvolution`, `IwasawaData`, restricted roots, `M`, `A`, `N`, `P = MAN` for a general real reductive group; no `SO₀(n,1)` model.

## Merged roadmaps
- RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layer 9 (Cartan, Iwasawa and KAK decompositions): `CartanInvolution`, `IsRealReductive`, `IwasawaData`, `IsIwasawaTriple`, `iwasawaDecomposition`, the restricted root system with `N_K(𝔞)/Z_K(𝔞)` and the canonical `N₊` from positive restricted roots. These are the prerequisites, not the definition: there is no `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, no `P = MAN` and no Langlands decomposition. RepresentationTheory/STATUS.md: LieGroups Layer 9 partial (only `GL n` Iwasawa).
- ReductiveGroups Layer 7: algebraic parabolics, unipotent radicals, the dynamic `P(λ)`, `U(λ)` and `Z(λ)` (dynamic constructions implemented; Levi complements, `P_G(λ)` parabolic for general reductive `G`, the root datum and the relative root system missing). The relative root system is stated for a given maximal split torus (README l.2929); existence and conjugacy of maximal k-split tori and k-rank are not targets, and no merged roadmap targets them.
- FlagVarieties Layer 2: standard parabolics `P_J`. Not started for general `G` (only the `GL_n` case, in Layer 1); STATUS: blocked on ReductiveGroups 7/7b.
- These are inputs to the algebraic comparison 79.2(f), which needs maximal ℝ-split tori in addition.
- [AdelicAlgebraicGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md) AA.3.2 "Horospherical decomposition for a fixed maximal compact": for connected semisimple `𝐆/ℚ`, a maximal compact `K` with Cartan involution `θ` and a `ℚ`-parabolic `𝐏`, the unique `θ`-stable real Levi lift and `𝐏(ℝ) = N_P A_P M_P` with `N_P × A_P × (M_P^+ K) ≅ G`. This is the Langlands decomposition of the real points of a *rational* parabolic, not of a minimal ℝ-parabolic, and has no real-rank-one clause. Not implemented.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #1037 "Automorphic forms on reductive groups", §2.3a "Minimal parabolic subgroups and the Langlands decomposition": the full definition and Prop. 79.2, the real-rank-one statement (`Σ ⊆ {±λ, ±2λ}`, exactly two minimal parabolics `P`, `θ(P)` containing `MA`) and the comparison `P = G ∩ 𝐏(ℝ)` with `N = R_u(𝐏)(ℝ)`, `A = 𝐒(ℝ)°`. Needs LieGroups Layer 9 and #871.
- #871 "Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces and their cohomology", ALS.0.1–ALS.0.3: Cartan involutions of real reductive algebraic groups, their conjugacy (`IsCartanInvolution.conj`), and `K_∞ = G(F_∞)^θ` maximal compact. ALS.2.1 also has the geodesic action and `A_P` for rational parabolics (Borel–Serre).
- #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII: roadmap" (Layer 1, generic decomposition of induced modules) uses a minimal parabolic `P=MAN` as input.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lie groups, left-invariant derivations: Mathlib.
2. `lieExp`, `Ad`, closed subgroups as Lie subgroups: Tau Ceti (LieGroups Layers 0–2 done).
3. Cartan involution and Cartan decomposition `K × 𝔭 ≅ G`: target LieGroups Layer 9 (not implemented beyond matrix groups); conjugacy of Cartan involutions: target in PR #871 ALS.0.1.
4. Maximal abelian `𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭`, restricted roots `Σ(𝔤,𝔞)`, `N₊`, Iwasawa `G = KAN`: target LieGroups Layer 9 (only `GL_n` Iwasawa implemented).
5. `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `P = MAN`, Langlands decomposition, real-rank-one clause: target in PR #1037 §2.3a.
6. Comparison with the minimal ℝ-parabolic of a cocharacter: dynamic parabolics in Tau Ceti; maximal ℝ-split tori unplanned (not a target of ReductiveGroups Layer 7 or any other merged roadmap); bridge in PR #1037 §2.3a.
7. Worked example `SO₀(n,1)`: a Check in open roadmap PR [#2229](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/pull/2229) §3.1 (`M = SO(n − 1)`, `N ≅ ℝ^{n−1}`, `G/P ≅ S^{n−1}`), with its Bruhat cells in §3.2.

## Library-scale gap
None beyond merging #1037 and implementing LieGroups Layer 9 for general real reductive groups (Knapp, *Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction*, Ch. VI–VII). The `SO₀(n,1)` case, which [2021, 193(3), no. 4] uses, is a Check in open roadmap PR [#2229](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/pull/2229) §3.1, which also extends `P = MAN` to connected semisimple groups with finite centre that are not linear.
