# 79. Minimal parabolic subgroups and the Langlands decomposition (real rank one)

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) has a section §2.3a whose title and content are exactly this definition: `M = Z_K(𝔞)`, `P = MAN`, Prop. 79.2(a)–(f), the real-rank-one clause and the algebraic comparison with the minimal ℝ-parabolic of a cocharacter, with the `SO₀(n,1)` example as a Check. Merged [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §3.1 extends `P = MAN` to connected groups with semisimple Lie algebra and finite centre, and merged [LieGroupsPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/LieGroupsPartII/README.md) Layer 3 to every reductive Cartan datum (finite central covers, disconnected groups). Not implemented; the upstream Cartan/Iwasawa/restricted-root inputs (RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layer 9) are merged but implemented on the group level only for `GL_n`; on the Lie algebra level, Cartan involutions, maximal abelian `𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭`, the restricted root space decomposition, restricted `𝔰𝔩₂` triples, reflections, the restricted root system `restrictedRootPairing` and its centre clauses are in code (RealSemisimpleLieGroups §§1.1–1.2), as is the Cartan involution of a real form from a commuting compact real form (LieGroups §7.1).

- Has: `LieGroup`, `LeftInvariantDerivation` (Lie algebra of a Lie group), Lie algebra theory (Killing form, Cartan subalgebras, root spaces for split Cartan subalgebras), `Matrix.specialOrthogonalGroup`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup`.
- Missing: Lie exponential for abstract Lie groups, closed-subgroup theorem, Cartan involutions, maximal abelian subspaces of `𝔭`, restricted roots, Iwasawa decomposition, parabolic subgroups of real groups. (Mathlib's `GroupTheory/GroupAction/Iwasawa.lean` is Iwasawa's simplicity criterion and is unrelated.)
