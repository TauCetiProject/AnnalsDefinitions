# 85. Stable Faltings height of an abelian variety

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights specifies the number-field definition: §1.1 hermitian bundles over `O_K`, §1.3 the Arakelov degree with §1.4 the product formula and §1.6 finite extension (85.1–85.2), §2.1 the Faltings metric in exactly the normalisation of 85.5, §2.4 the metrized Néron Hodge line, §3.1 `h(A/K)`, §3.3 finite extension and the bad-place correction, and §3.4 the stable height `h_st(A)`, with the global form of 85.4(i) over number fields (`L = K(A[12])` is everywhere semistable, from NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §3.12). Merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeightsPartII §13.1 specifies the function-field case (85.3, 85.6) for curves of genus > 1, `h_Fal(C)` through the Néron model of the Jacobian over a semistable extension, with the base-change scaling of the [errata](../sources/errata.md); its §5.4 identifies `h_Fal(X_s)` of a curve over a number field with the stable Faltings height of its Jacobian. Borderline: the function-field `h_Fal(A)` of a general abelian variety (Def. 85.6 as stated) is a target nowhere; the merged targets cover the generality [2026, 203(1), no. 2] needs (curves of genus > 1 over number fields and one-variable function fields). Néron models, the invariant-differential lattice with its Hodge line, and local semistable reduction over a discrete valuation ring are targets of merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties (Layer 1, §1.11, §§3.3, 3.10, 3.12). In code: hermitian vector bundles over `O_K` with sums, tensor products and duals, the degree expression of a section and its independence of the section in rank one (`TauCeti.HermitianVectorBundle`, `sectionDegree`, `sectionDegree_eq_sectionDegree`), the section degree on the `HermitianMetric` carrier and the lattice index term (`HermitianMetric.sectionDegree`, `sectionDegree_map_eq_add_log_cardQuot_range`) and the Néron mapping property with marked Néron models (`TauCeti.NeronModel`); no Hodge line, Faltings metric or height.

Definition 85.6 of the source wrongly claims base-change invariance over function fields; see [errata](../sources/errata.md).

## Mathlib
- Has: rings of integers, infinite places and embeddings `K →+* ℂ`, `Module.Finite`/projective modules, `Ideal.absNorm`, schemes, Kähler differentials `Ω[S⁄R]`, complex manifolds basics.
- Missing: metrised line bundles on `Spec O_K` and arithmetic degree, abelian varieties, Néron models, semistable reduction, integration of top forms on complex tori, regular projective model curves of function fields.

## Library-scale gap
Small. The number-field definition is planned in merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights and the curve case over function fields in merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeightsPartII §13.1; the long poles are the Néron models of NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties and the complex uniformization of AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.1. The one generality gap is Def. 85.6 over a one-variable function field for an arbitrary abelian variety, not only Jacobians: the definition is identical (Néron model over `B′`, Hodge line degree), and the merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties' Néron models and semistable reduction already work over Dedekind bases, so it is a short addition to §13.1. References: Faltings, *Endlichkeitssätze*; Cornell–Silverman, *Arithmetic Geometry*, Ch. II and X.
