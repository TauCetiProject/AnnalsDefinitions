# 85. Stable Faltings height of an abelian variety

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED, over number fields. Merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights specifies the number-field definition: §1.1 hermitian bundles over `O_K`, §1.3 the Arakelov degree with §1.4 the product formula and §1.6 finite extension (85.1–85.2), §2.1 the Faltings metric in exactly the normalisation of 85.5, §2.4 the metrized Néron Hodge line, §3.1 `h(A/K)`, §3.3 finite extension and the bad-place correction, and §3.4 the stable height `h_st(A)`, with the global form of 85.4(i) over number fields (`L = K(A[12])` is everywhere semistable, from NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §3.12). The function-field case is specified only in open PR #1833 (ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeightsPartII §13.1), for Jacobians of curves of genus > 1, which is the case [2026, 203(1), no. 2] needs. `h_Fal(C) := h_Fal(Jac C)` over number fields is specified nowhere. Néron models, the invariant-differential lattice with its Hodge line, and local semistable reduction over a discrete valuation ring are targets of merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties (Layer 1, §1.11, §§3.3, 3.10, 3.12). Nothing is implemented.

Definition 85.6 of the source wrongly claims base-change invariance over function fields; see [errata](../sources/errata.md).

## Mathlib
- Has: rings of integers, infinite places and embeddings `K →+* ℂ`, `Module.Finite`/projective modules, `Ideal.absNorm`, schemes, Kähler differentials `Ω[S⁄R]`, complex manifolds basics.
- Missing: metrised line bundles on `Spec O_K` and arithmetic degree, abelian varieties, Néron models, semistable reduction, integration of top forms on complex tori, regular projective model curves of function fields.

Arakelov-style heights of points and subspaces: `TauCeti.arakelovMulHeight`, `relativeSubspaceArakelovHeight` (`NumberTheory/Height/Arakelov/`), which are Euclidean heights and not degrees of hermitian line bundles.
- Missing: hermitian line bundles and `deĝ`, Néron models (no `NeronModel` anywhere), the Hodge bundle `e^*Ω^g`, the Faltings metric, `h_Fal`.

5. Global 85.4(i): target of merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights §3.4 (number fields) and PR #1833 §13.1 (function fields, Jacobians only).
6. Faltings metric (integration on `A_σ(ℂ)`): target of merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights §2.1, on AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.1 (on ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1) and ComplexComparisonPartII §§C0.4, C5.1 (merged, not started).
7. `h_Fal(A)` over number fields: target of merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights §§3.1, 3.4. `h_Fal(C) := h_Fal(Jac C)` over number fields: unplanned.
8. Function-field `h_Fal(C)` via regular projective model curve and `deg_{B'/k}`: target PR #1833 §13.1 (curves only).

## Library-scale gap
Small. The number-field definition is planned in merged ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights; its long poles are the Néron models of NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties and the complex uniformization of AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.1. `h_Fal(C)` for a curve over a number field is specified nowhere (a one-line definition from ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights §3.4 and the JacobianChallenge §D.8 Jacobian). The function-field stable height is only in open PR #1833 §13.1, which should be stated for an arbitrary abelian variety over a one-variable function field, not only Jacobians of curves: the definition is identical (Néron model over `B'`, Hodge line degree), and the merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties' Néron models and semistable reduction already work over Dedekind bases. References: Faltings, *Endlichkeitssätze*; Cornell–Silverman, *Arithmetic Geometry*, Ch. II and X.
