## Library-scale gap
Merged ClassicalErgodicTheory is the entropy theory of classical ergodic theory (Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 4, 7, 8; Downarowicz, *Entropy in Dynamical Systems*): KS entropy (with merged GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2's names), SMB and the ergodic decomposition of entropy, the Kolmogorov-Sinai generator theorem, Abramov and Abramov-Rokhlin formulas, the full variational principle, upper semicontinuity for expansive maps and existence of MMEs, uniqueness under specification, entropy of shifts and toral automorphisms; it consumes the generic parts of the merged ArithmeticDynamics §§2.6–2.7 and GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 and feeds #96 (pressure). The definitions of GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 (`measureEntropy`) and the invariant probabilities of ClassicalErgodicTheory §5.1 are in code, and so is the finite-partition formula of ArithmeticDynamics §2.6 (`measureEntropy_eq_iSup_fin`). What remains for the definition is implementation of ClassicalErgodicTheory Layers 4–8 (`IsMeasureOfMaximalEntropy` in §8.1). The paper's theorems further need smooth ergodic theory (Ruelle inequality, Pesin theory, Yomdin/Newhouse; Barreira-Pesin, *Nonuniform Hyperbolicity*), which no roadmap plans; ClassicalErgodicTheory names smooth ergodic theory outside it.

Depends on #96 only through the remark that MME = equilibrium states of potential 0 (the definition is independent).
