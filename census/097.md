## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged ClassicalErgodicTheory (PR #2231) specifies `IsMeasureOfMaximalEntropy T ν` (§8.1, 97.3) on top of Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy and Mathlib's `Dynamics.coverEntropy`. KS entropy (97.1) is a target of merged GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 (`measureEntropy μ T`, over countable labels of finite entropy) with the finite-partition formula `h_μ(T) = sup_ξ h_μ(T, ξ)` of merged ArithmeticDynamics §2.6, both consumed by ClassicalErgodicTheory Layer 4 (§4.0), which extends them to countable labels. ClassicalErgodicTheory specifies Goodwyn's inequality (§6.1), the variational principle (§7.2, 97.2(a)), affinity, the ergodic decomposition with entropy integration and Jacobs' formula (§4.1 item 4, §4.10, §6.2, 97.2(b)), convexity, ergodic extreme points and uniqueness, and existence and compactness under upper semicontinuity (§8.2 items 1-2, 97.4(a)-(b)), a homeomorphism with finite entropy and no m.m.e. (§8.2 Check, 97.4(c)), and `MME(f) = MME(f⁻¹)` (§4.1 item 2, §8.2 item 4). §8.1 proves that under `h_top < ∞` its extended-valued predicate is 97.3's real equality. Borderline only in 97.4: Misiurewicz's `C^r` diffeomorphisms without an m.m.e. and Newhouse's theorem (97.4(b)'s `C^∞` example) are outside ClassicalErgodicTheory (its Scope) and outside open PR #2441 (which proves uniqueness of the m.m.e. for uniformly hyperbolic basic sets, §10.4), and are in no roadmap; 97.4(c) is specified by a homeomorphism. Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy (97.1) is in Tau Ceti code in the roadmap's form, `TauCeti.measureEntropy μ T : ℝ≥0∞`, the supremum of the entropy rates `partitionEntropyRate` over measurable labels into `ℕ` of finite entropy (GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2), with the chain rule and conditional entropy given a σ-algebra (ArithmeticDynamics §2.7); so are the invariant probabilities `TauCeti.invariantProbabilities T` (`M(f)`, ClassicalErgodicTheory §5.1), the Bowen–Dinaburg forms of `Dynamics.coverEntropy` and topological mixing (§5.2), so 97.3 can be written over code. Not in code: the predicate `IsMeasureOfMaximalEntropy` (§8.1), the finite-partition formula and Fekete limit of 97.1 (the limit in an open Tau Ceti PR), and 97.2 and 97.4.

## Library-scale gap
Merged ClassicalErgodicTheory is the entropy theory of classical ergodic theory (Walters, *An Introduction to Ergodic Theory* ch. 4, 7, 8; Downarowicz, *Entropy in Dynamical Systems*): KS entropy (with merged GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2's names), SMB and the ergodic decomposition of entropy, the Kolmogorov-Sinai generator theorem, Abramov and Abramov-Rokhlin formulas, the full variational principle, upper semicontinuity for expansive maps and existence of MMEs, uniqueness under specification, entropy of shifts and toral automorphisms; it consumes the generic parts of the merged ArithmeticDynamics §§2.6–2.7 and GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 and feeds #96 (pressure). The definitions of GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 (`measureEntropy`) and the invariant probabilities of ClassicalErgodicTheory §5.1 are in code. What remains for the definition is the rest of GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic §4.2 and ArithmeticDynamics §§2.6–2.7 (the Fekete limit, the finite-partition formula) and implementation of ClassicalErgodicTheory Layers 4–8 (`IsMeasureOfMaximalEntropy` in §8.1). The paper's theorems further need smooth ergodic theory (Ruelle inequality, Pesin theory, Yomdin/Newhouse; Barreira-Pesin, *Nonuniform Hyperbolicity*), which no roadmap plans; ClassicalErgodicTheory names smooth ergodic theory outside it.

Depends on #96 only through the remark that MME = equilibrium states of potential 0 (the definition is independent).
