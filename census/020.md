## Prerequisite chain
1. Schemes, flat, proper and f.p. morphisms, fppf topology. In Mathlib.
2. Algebraic spaces, their morphism properties and fppf descent. Target in merged SSF Layer 1 (§§1.2–1.9; §1.3, the core of §1.4 and §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel` implemented). Stacks in groupoids: SSF §1.18 (`StackInGroupoids`, stacks of sheaves, 2-fibre products and stackification implemented). The remaining *Needs* of EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence §2.1 in merged SSF: §4.1 and §2.6 (partial), §2.3 (partial: pullback of torsor classes missing), §2.2 (Leray edge maps only), §2.4 (in code), §§2.11, 2.15, 2.20, 2.22 (not started).
3. Geometric fibres of an algebraic space over `S` being schemes (so that the Enriques predicate applies to them): stated nowhere; neither merged SSF Layer 1 nor EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence contains it.
4. Enriques surface over an algebraically closed field (#19). Target in merged WeilConjectures §7.12 (unimplemented).
5. Family of Enriques surfaces and the fibred category `M_Enr`: target in merged EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence §2.1 (unimplemented). `M_Enr` as an fppf stack (20.3(a)): asserted in §2.1 but not an API item.
