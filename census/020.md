## Prerequisite chain
1. Schemes, flat, proper and f.p. morphisms, fppf topology. In Mathlib.
2. Algebraic spaces, their morphism properties and fppf descent. Target in merged SSF Layer 1 (§1.2 and §§1.3–1.9; implemented only for parts of §§1.3–1.4).
3. Geometric fibres of algebraic spaces being schemes. Merged SSF Layer 1; Artin's theorem for surfaces is cited only as automatic.
4. Enriques surface over an algebraically closed field (#19). PR #1208 §7.12.
5. Family of Enriques surfaces and `M_Enr`. Target in PR #1488 §2.1.
