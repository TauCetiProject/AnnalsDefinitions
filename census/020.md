Flags: Proposition 20.3(a), that `M_Enr` is an fppf stack, is asserted in §2.1's prose and its method ("Check descent of the geometric Enriques condition and the isomorphism groupoid") but is not an API item; and no roadmap says how a geometric fibre of an algebraic-space family is a scheme, which WeilConjectures §7.12's surface predicate requires (Definition 20.1 includes it as a condition, automatic by Artin's theorem). ## Prerequisite chain
1. Schemes, flat, proper and f.p. morphisms, fppf topology. In Mathlib.
2. Algebraic spaces, their morphism properties and fppf descent. Target in merged SSF Layer 1 (§§1.2–1.9; §§1.3–1.4 and §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel`, `ofAtlas`, quotient sheaf, `represented_of` and `restrict` implemented). Stacks in groupoids: SSF §1.18 (`StackInGroupoids`, stacks of sheaves, 2-fibre products, site-general products and the terminal stack, and stackification with its unit implemented). The remaining *Needs* of EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence §2.1 in merged SSF: §4.1 and §2.6 (partial), §2.3 (in code except the sheaf half of the Galois-cocycle Check beyond the group and torsor stalks `GrpStalk`, `TorsorStalk`, and the constant nonabelian Check apart from its `S₃` part), §2.2 (Leray edge maps and the Čech-to-cohomology spectral sequence only), §2.4 (in code), §2.11 (the étale Kummer sequence only), §§2.15, 2.20 (not started), §2.22 (the ring-level fibre criterion for Azumaya algebras and its Checks, and base change of Azumaya algebras, in code; the sheaf algebras `SheafAlgebra` in code).
3. Geometric fibres of an algebraic space over `S` being schemes (so that the Enriques predicate applies to them): stated nowhere; neither merged SSF Layer 1 nor EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence contains it.
4. Enriques surface over an algebraically closed field (#19). Target in merged WeilConjectures §7.12 (unimplemented).
5. Family of Enriques surfaces and the fibred category `M_Enr`: target in merged EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence §2.1 (unimplemented). `M_Enr` as an fppf stack (20.3(a)): asserted in §2.1 but not an API item.
