Merged ComplexKahlerGeometry targets Hermitian and Kähler metrics on complex manifolds and `dvol_g = ω^n/n!` (K5b.0 / §5.9, §6.1). Tau Ceti already has the Riemannian volume measure `riemannianVolume`, so `μ_ω := n!·riemannianVolume(g_ω)` follows once the Hermitian-metric carrier exists.

## Mathlib
- Present: complex manifolds (`IsManifold` over `ℂ`), Riemannian metrics (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Basic.lean`, `VectorBundle/Riemannian.lean`), Lebesgue measure and change of variables.
- Missing: `(p,q)`-forms, Hermitian or Kähler metrics on complex manifolds, and the Riemannian volume measure.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Smooth manifolds, Riemannian metric, `riemannianVolume`. In Mathlib and Tau Ceti.
2. Almost complex structure, `(p,q)`-type calculus, Dolbeault complex. In Tau Ceti (CKG K1 and K2, partial).
3. Hermitian metric on a complex manifold, with fundamental form `ω` and `g_ω(u,v) = ω(u,Jv)`. Target in merged CKG (Conventions, §§5.9 and 6.1; unimplemented).
4. `ω^n` and `dvol_g = ω^n/n!`; measure `μ_ω = n!·vol_{g_ω}`. Target in merged CKG §5.9 (unimplemented).
5. Kähler predicate (`dω = 0`). Target in merged CKG §6.1 (unimplemented).
6. Ratio `η/ω^n` and the relative volume `det h_φ`. Not stated explicitly; a small corollary of item 4.

## Library-scale gap
Essentially planned. The Monge–Ampère consumers (Guo–Phong–Tong) would need a further roadmap on fully nonlinear elliptic PDE on compact Hermitian manifolds (Kołodziej, Błocki), next to ComplexKahlerGeometry and PDE. That is beyond this definition.
