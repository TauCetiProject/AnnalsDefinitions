## Status
IN-CODE. Tau Ceti has the measure of Definition 14.3 on any manifold: for a smooth two-form `ω` on a manifold modelled on a space of real dimension `2n`, `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.volumeForm ω n` is `ωⁿ/n!` and `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.liouvilleMeasure ω n` is the Borel measure `|ωⁿ/n!|`, locally finite (so finite on a compact `X`), so `μ_ω = n! • liouvilleMeasure ω n`. A Hermitian form on a complex manifold is a two-form `Compatible` with `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.ofComplexManifold`, and `SmoothTwoForm.IsKahler` (ComplexKahlerGeometry §6.1's real-carrier predicate) adds `dω = 0`. Not in code: the Wirtinger comparison `ωⁿ/n! = dvol_{g_ω}` of Proposition 14.2 (CKG §5.9), the relative-volume formula `ω'ⁿ = det(h) ωⁿ`, a named ratio `η/ωⁿ`, and the cohomological invariance of Proposition 14.4. These are theorems about the definition; the cited papers' statements can be written against these declarations.

## Mathlib
- Present: complex manifolds (`IsManifold` over `ℂ`), Riemannian metrics (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Basic.lean`, `VectorBundle/Riemannian.lean`), Lebesgue measure and change of variables.
- Missing: `(p,q)`-forms, Hermitian or Kähler metrics on complex manifolds, and the Riemannian volume measure.

## Tau Ceti code
- The measure `ωⁿ`:
  - `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.volumeForm ω n`, the form `ωⁿ/n!` built from `SmoothForm.wedgePow`, equal to `1` on Darboux bases (`IsNondegenerate.volumeForm_apply_reindex`), nowhere zero exactly when `ω` is nondegenerate (`isNondegenerate_iff_volumeForm_apply_ne_zero`), with its orientation `IsNondegenerate.volumeOrientation` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Volume.lean`);
  - `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.liouvilleMeasure ω n = RoughForm.measure (volumeForm ω n)`, the chart-wise Lebesgue measure weighted by the absolute coefficient (`liouvilleMeasure_apply_of_subset`), on any Lindelöf manifold, with `isLocallyFiniteMeasure_liouvilleMeasure`, `IsNondegenerate.isOpenPosMeasure_liouvilleMeasure`, `liouvilleMeasure_smul` (`|r|ⁿ` scaling), naturality under maps pulling back the form and restriction to open sets (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/LiouvilleMeasure.lean`).
- Hermitian and Kähler forms:
  - `SmoothTwoForm.Compatible` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/TwoForm.lean`): invariance under `J` and positivity on complex lines, that is a positive real `(1,1)`-form for `J = SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.ofComplexManifold E M`;
  - `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsKahler` (closed, compatible, `J.nijenhuis = 0`), with `isKahler_iff`, `IsKahler.isSymplectic`, `isKahler_ofComplexManifold_iff` (on a complex manifold: closed and compatible) and the flat example `isKahler_constSmooth_stdSymplecticForm_product` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Kahler.lean`).
- Volumes and integration:
  - `TauCeti.riemannianVolume` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Volume.lean`), the glued chart measure, locally finite;
  - `riemannianVolumeForm` (`Riemannian/VolumeForm.lean`) with `measure_riemannianVolumeForm` (`|dV| = riemannianVolume`) and the agreement of `integralTopForm` of `f dV` with the integral against `riemannianVolume` (`Riemannian/VolumeForm/Measure.lean`);
  - `integralTopForm` (`DifferentialForm/Integration/TopForm.lean`).
- Forms and complex structure: type projections `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.typeProjection` and the Dolbeault complex (`DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/`), `dC` with `ddᶜ = 2i∂∂̄` on smooth forms (`Dolbeault/DC.lean`), wedge products, pointwise Hermitian algebra (`Geometry/Symplectic/Complex/HermitianForm.lean`).
- Missing: the comparison of `liouvilleMeasure ω n` with `riemannianVolume` of `g_ω(u,v) = ω(u,Jv)` (Wirtinger), the ratio `η/ωⁿ` and `det h` as named declarations, cohomological invariance of `∫ωⁿ`, and `i∂∂̄φ` for `C²` (not smooth) `φ`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Smooth manifolds, Riemannian metric, `riemannianVolume`. In Mathlib and Tau Ceti.
2. Almost complex structure, `(p,q)`-type calculus, Dolbeault complex, `ddᶜ`. In Tau Ceti (CKG K1 and K2, partial).
3. Hermitian form as a compatible two-form, with `g_ω(u,v) = ω(u,Jv)`. In Tau Ceti (`SmoothTwoForm.Compatible`); the structure of CKG Conventions and §5.9 is not separately implemented.
4. `ωⁿ/n!` and the measure `μ_ω = n!·|ωⁿ/n!|`. In Tau Ceti (`volumeForm`, `liouvilleMeasure`). The comparison `dvol_g = ωⁿ/n!` is a CKG §5.9 target (unimplemented).
5. Kähler predicate (`dω = 0`). In Tau Ceti (`SmoothTwoForm.IsKahler`, CKG §6.1).
6. Ratio `η/ωⁿ` and the relative volume `det h_φ`. Not stated explicitly; a small corollary of item 4.

## Library-scale gap
Essentially planned. The Monge–Ampère consumers (Guo–Phong–Tong) would need a further roadmap on fully nonlinear elliptic PDE on compact Hermitian manifolds (Kołodziej, Błocki), next to ComplexKahlerGeometry and PDE. That is beyond this definition.
