## Status
IN-CODE. Tau Ceti has the measure of Definition 14.3 on any manifold: for a smooth two-form `ω` on a manifold modelled on a space of real dimension `2n`, `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.volumeForm ω n` is `ωⁿ/n!` and `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.liouvilleMeasure ω n` is the Borel measure `|ωⁿ/n!|`, locally finite (so finite on a compact `X`), so `μ_ω = n! • liouvilleMeasure ω n`. A Hermitian form on a complex manifold is a two-form `Compatible` with `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.ofComplexManifold`, and `SmoothTwoForm.IsKahler` (ComplexKahlerGeometry §6.1's real-carrier predicate) adds `dω = 0`. Not in code: the Wirtinger comparison `ωⁿ/n! = dvol_{g_ω}` of Proposition 14.2 (CKG §5.9, ComplexHessianEquations §3.2), the relative-volume formula `ω'ⁿ = det(h) ωⁿ`, a named ratio `η/ωⁿ`, and the cohomological invariance of Proposition 14.4 (for `i∂∂̄`-exact changes of a Kähler form, ComplexHessianEquations §3.2). These are theorems about the definition; the cited papers' statements can be written against these declarations.

## Mathlib
- Present: complex manifolds (`IsManifold` over `ℂ`), Riemannian metrics (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Basic.lean`, `VectorBundle/Riemannian.lean`), Lebesgue measure and change of variables.
- Missing: `(p,q)`-forms, Hermitian or Kähler metrics on complex manifolds, and the Riemannian volume measure.

## Tau Ceti code
- The measure `ωⁿ`:
  - `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.volumeForm ω n`, the form `ωⁿ/n!` built from `SmoothForm.wedgePow`, equal to `1` on Darboux bases (`IsNondegenerate.volumeForm_apply_reindex`), nowhere zero exactly when `ω` is nondegenerate (`isNondegenerate_iff_volumeForm_apply_ne_zero`), with its orientation `IsNondegenerate.volumeOrientation` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Volume.lean`);
  - `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.liouvilleMeasure ω n = RoughForm.measure (volumeForm ω n)`, the chart-wise Lebesgue measure weighted by the absolute coefficient (`liouvilleMeasure_apply_of_subset`), on any Lindelöf manifold, with `isLocallyFiniteMeasure_liouvilleMeasure`, `IsNondegenerate.isOpenPosMeasure_liouvilleMeasure`, `liouvilleMeasure_smul` (`|r|ⁿ` scaling), naturality under maps pulling back the form and restriction to open sets (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/LiouvilleMeasure.lean`).
- Hermitian and Kähler forms:
  - `SmoothTwoForm.Compatible` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/TwoForm.lean`): invariance under `J` and positivity on complex lines, that is a positive real `(1,1)`-form for `J = SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.ofComplexManifold E M`;
  - `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.IsKahler` (closed, compatible, `J.nijenhuis = 0`), with `isKahler_iff`, `IsKahler.isSymplectic`, `isKahler_ofComplexManifold_iff` (on a complex manifold: closed and compatible) and the flat example `isKahler_constSmooth_stdSymplecticForm_product` (`Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Kahler.lean`).
- Volumes and integration:
  - `TauCeti.riemannianVolume` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/VolumeDensity/Volume.lean`), the glued chart measure, locally finite;
  - `riemannianVolumeForm` (`Riemannian/VolumeForm.lean`) with `measure_riemannianVolumeForm` (`|dV| = riemannianVolume`) and the agreement of `integralTopForm` of `f dV` with the integral against `riemannianVolume` (`Riemannian/VolumeForm/Measure.lean`);
  - `integralTopForm` (`DifferentialForm/Integration/TopForm.lean`).
- Forms and complex structure: type projections `SmoothAlmostComplexStructure.typeProjection` and the Dolbeault complex (`DifferentialForm/Dolbeault/`), `dC` with `ddᶜ = 2i∂∂̄` on smooth forms (`Dolbeault/DC.lean`), wedge products, pointwise Hermitian algebra (`Geometry/Symplectic/Complex/HermitianForm.lean`).
- `i∂∂̄φ` for `C²` functions: `TauCeti.manifoldLeviForm` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/LeviForm.lean`), the Levi form of `φ` at a point as a sesquilinear form on the model space, chart-independent for `φ` real `C²` by `leviForm_comp_symm_apply_of_mem_maximalAtlas`.
- Pointwise relative eigenvalues (ComplexHessianEquations §1.1): `LinearMap.IsSymm.relEigenvalues`, the decreasing eigenvalue vector `λ[a]` of a Hermitian form relative to an inner product, with an orthonormal diagonalizing basis (`relEigenvectorBasis`), `Σ λ_i = tr`, `∏ λ_i = det`, `det(Id + tA) = Σ σ_k(λ) t^k` (`det_id_add_smul`), the Gram-matrix characterization (`det_toMatrix₂_sub_smul_gram`) and monotonicity (`TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RelativeEigenvalues/Basic.lean`).
- Eigenvalue vectors of `α + i∂∂̄φ` relative to a Hermitian metric (ComplexHessianEquations §3.1): the Hermitian form `H_α(u, v) = α(u, iv) + i·α(u, v)` of a real two-form, `ContinuousAlternatingMap.hermitianForm` (`Topology/Algebra/Module/Alternating/HermitianForm.lean`), and on a complex manifold `TauCeti.twoFormHermitianForm E α x` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/TwoFormHermitianForm.lean`) and `TauCeti.ddbarHermitianForm E α φ x = H_α(x) + 2·L_φ(x)` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/DdbarHermitianForm.lean`); the predicate `TauCeti.IsRelEigenvalueVector h a μ` (a basis `b` with `h(b_i, b_j) = δ_ij`, `a(b_i, b_j) = μ_i δ_ij`), with existence of a sorted one `exists_isRelEigenvalueVector`, its uniqueness `IsRelEigenvalueVector.eq_of_antitone` and `exists_perm_eq` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RelativeEigenvalues/Vector.lean`), and for a Hermitian metric `κ` and `C²` `φ` the sorted eigenvalue vector of `α + i∂∂̄φ` relative to `κ`, `SmoothTwoForm.Compatible.exists_isRelEigenvalueVector_ddbarHermitianForm`, with `Compatible.isRelEigenvalueVector_self_iff` (`λ_κ[κ] = e`); the trace identity `Σ_j λ_j[α] = Σ_k α(u_k, i u_k)` for a real (1,1)-form `α` on a finite-dimensional complex inner product space and an orthonormal basis `u`, `ContinuousAlternatingMap.sum_relEigenvalues_hermitianSesqForm` and `sum_relEigenvalues_hermitianSesqForm_of_orthonormal` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RelativeEigenvalues/HermitianForm.lean`). The form side of the trace identity in an orthonormal frame, `α ∧ ω^m/m! = (∑ᵢ α(uᵢ, J uᵢ))·ω^{m+1}/(m+1)!` for an orthogonal `J` (the case `k = 1` of the §3.2 identity `α^k ∧ ω^{n−k} = c σ_k(λ[α]) ω^n` at the level of linear algebra), `TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.wedge_kahlerPowerForm` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/HodgeStar/Kahler/Trace.lean`), on `ContinuousAlternatingMap.pairedWedge_wedgePow_apply_finProdFinEquiv` (`Topology/Algebra/Module/Alternating/Wedge/Pow/Trace.lean`); the two halves combined, on a complex inner product space with `ω` the Kähler form of multiplication by `i`, `α ∧ ω^m/m! = (Σ_j λ_j[α])·ω^{m+1}/(m+1)!`, that is `n·α ∧ ω^{n−1} = (Σ_j λ_j[α])·ω^n` (`TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.wedge_kahlerPowerForm_ofComplexModule`, `nsmul_wedge_wedgePow_ofComplexModule`, `Kahler/Trace.lean`). The pointwise statement on a Hermitian manifold through `relEigenvalueFunction` is not in code.
- The eigenvalue function (ComplexHessianEquations §3.1): `λ_κ[α + i∂∂̄φ](x)` relative to a Hermitian metric `κ` as a function on the manifold, `TauCeti.relEigenvalueFunction`, an eigenvalue vector of `H_{α + i∂∂̄φ}(x)` (`SmoothTwoForm.Compatible.isRelEigenvalueVector_relEigenvalueFunction`), and the complex Laplacian `TauCeti.complexLaplacian` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/EigenvalueFunction.lean`), on the sorted relative eigenvalue vector `TauCeti.relEigenvalueVector` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/RelativeEigenvalues/Sorted.lean`). On a complex inner product space with the standard Hermitian metric `TauCeti.standardHermitianMetric` (`ω(u, v) = Im ⟪u, v⟫`), `Δ_ω` is half the Euclidean Laplacian, `complexLaplacian_standardHermitianMetric` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/EigenvalueFunction/Standard.lean`), with the §3.1 Checks on `|z|²`, `|z₁|²`, `x₁²` and `Re z₁²` (`Examples/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/EigenvalueFunction/Standard.lean`).
- Missing: the comparison of `liouvilleMeasure ω n` with `riemannianVolume` of `g_ω(u,v) = ω(u,Jv)` (Wirtinger), the ratio `η/ωⁿ` and `det h` as named declarations, and cohomological invariance of `∫ωⁿ`.
- The integral against `ωⁿ` (ComplexHessianEquations §3.2): `∫_X g·ωⁿ` against the top wedge power for the canonical orientation of a complex manifold, `TauCeti.formPowIntegral` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/FormPowIntegral.lean`), with `ωⁿ = n!·(ωⁿ/n!)` against `SmoothTwoForm.volumeForm` (`TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.wedgePow_eq_factorial_smul_volumeForm`, `Geometry/Symplectic/Manifold/Volume.lean`), positivity of `ωⁿ` on positively oriented bases for a form taming the complex structure, in particular a Hermitian metric (`SmoothTwoForm.Tames.wedgePow_apply_pos`, `SmoothTwoForm.Compatible.wedgePow_apply_pos`), and linearity, monotonicity and positivity of the integral on compact `X` (`formPowIntegral_add`, `Tames.formPowIntegral_mono`, `Tames.formPowIntegral_pos`), with `V = ∫_X ωⁿ > 0` (`Tames.formPowIntegral_one_pos`); the Check `ω²(∂x₁, ∂y₁, ∂x₂, ∂y₂) = 2` for the standard Kähler form of `ℂ²` is in `Examples/Geometry/Manifold/Complex/FormPowIntegral.lean`. The comparison `ωⁿ = n!·dvol_g` with `riemannianVolume` and Kähler invariance are not in code.
- ComplexHessianEquations §3.1: the real (1,1)-form `i∂∂̄u` of a real function as a rough two-form, `TauCeti.ddbarForm E u`, built from `manifoldLeviForm`, alternating and `J`-invariant, continuous for `C²` functions, with `mk_ddbarForm_apply_eq_two_mul_manifoldLeviForm` (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/DdbarForm/Basic.lean`).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Smooth manifolds, Riemannian metric, `riemannianVolume`. In Mathlib and Tau Ceti.
2. Almost complex structure, `(p,q)`-type calculus, Dolbeault complex, `ddᶜ`. In Tau Ceti (CKG K1 and K2, partial).
3. Hermitian form as a compatible two-form, with `g_ω(u,v) = ω(u,Jv)`. In Tau Ceti (`SmoothTwoForm.Compatible`); the structure of CKG Conventions and §5.9 is not separately implemented.
4. `ωⁿ/n!` and the measure `μ_ω = n!·|ωⁿ/n!|`. In Tau Ceti (`volumeForm`, `liouvilleMeasure`). The integral `∫_X g·ωⁿ` against the top power is in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.formPowIntegral`, ComplexHessianEquations §3.2). The comparison `dvol_g = ωⁿ/n!` is a target of CKG §5.9 and ComplexHessianEquations §3.2 (unimplemented).
5. Kähler predicate (`dω = 0`). In Tau Ceti (`SmoothTwoForm.IsKahler`, CKG §6.1).
6. Ratio `η/ωⁿ` and the relative volume `det h_φ`. Not stated explicitly; a small corollary of item 4. The pointwise eigenvalue vector is in Tau Ceti (`LinearMap.IsSymm.relEigenvalues`, ComplexHessianEquations §1.1), and so is the eigenvalue vector of `α + i∂∂̄φ` relative to a Hermitian metric at each point of a manifold (`IsRelEigenvalueVector`, `Compatible.exists_isRelEigenvalueVector_ddbarHermitianForm`, §3.1); the eigenvalue function `λ_ω[α]` as a function on the manifold and `α^k ∧ ω^{n−k} = c σ_k(λ[α]) ω^n`: ComplexHessianEquations §§3.1–3.2 (merged; the form `i∂∂̄u`, the Hermitian form `ddbarHermitianForm` and the eigenvalue function `relEigenvalueFunction` of §3.1 are in code, as is the linear-algebra case `k = 1` of the §3.2 identity in an orthonormal frame (`AlmostComplexStructure.wedge_kahlerPowerForm`) and with the eigenvalues, `n·α ∧ ω^{n−1} = (Σ_j λ_j[α])·ω^n` (`AlmostComplexStructure.wedge_kahlerPowerForm_ofComplexModule`); the rest is not implemented).
