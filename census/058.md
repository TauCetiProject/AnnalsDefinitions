## Mathlib
Has `FundamentalGroup`, `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`, `MulAction.orbit`, `Set.Finite`, `MulAut.conj`. No mapping class groups, no Out.

## Tau Ceti code
No mapping class groups, no surface-group representation spaces. Surface groups exist as presented groups, `TauCeti.SurfaceGroup.Orientable g m` with its peripheral elements (`GroupTheory/SurfaceGroup/Basic.lean`), not identified with the topological `π₁`. `TauCeti.ContinuousOut` (`Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Basic.lean`) gives Out(G) for discrete G.

## Merged roadmaps
GeometricTopology 1.7.2 defines Mod(F) for compact surfaces with boundary (diffeomorphisms fixing ∂F) and Dehn twists, not punctured surfaces, and no action on representations. Nothing else.
