## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. "MCG-finite" (finite Mod_{g,n}-orbit of [ρ] for ρ : π₁(Σ_{g,n}) → GL_r(ℂ)) is not a target; its prerequisites, the mapping class group of a punctured surface (#59) and its outer action on conjugacy classes of representations (#61), are targets only of open PRs (PR #1231 §5.9, PR #1169, PR #1728). Depends on #59 and #61.

## Mathlib
Has `FundamentalGroup`, `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`, `MulAction.orbit`, `Set.Finite`, `MulAut.conj`. No mapping class groups, no Out.

## Tau Ceti code
No mapping class groups, no surface-group representation spaces. `TauCeti.ContinuousOut` (`Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Basic.lean`) gives Out(G) for discrete G.

## Merged roadmaps
GeometricTopology 1.7.2 defines Mod(F) for compact surfaces with boundary (diffeomorphisms fixing ∂F) and Dehn twists, not punctured surfaces, and no action on representations. Nothing else.

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR #1231 (Mordell finiteness by the Lawrence-Venkatesh method) Layer LV.5 §5.9: Mod(S) for surfaces with punctures (homeomorphisms may permute punctures) and its action "on conjugacy classes of homomorphisms π₁(S) → G" via Out(π₁ S); §5.19-5.21 point-pushing, Birman sequence.
- PR #1169 (Teichmüller geometry): `OuterAut`, Dehn-Nielsen-Baer for closed surfaces.
- PR #1728 (group actions on Riemann surfaces): consumes PR #1231 §5.9 for the outer action on surface-kernel epimorphisms.
- No PR defines finite-orbit (MCG-finite) representations.

## Prerequisite chain
π₁, GL_r(ℂ) [Mathlib] -> Mod_{g,n} of punctured surface (#59) [PR #1231 §5.9] -> outer action Mod_{g,n} → Out(π₁) and on conjugacy classes X_r (#61) [PR #1231 §5.9] -> MCG-finite: orbit of [ρ] finite (58.1) [unplanned, one line] -> basepoint/model independence (58.2) [unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
Small once #59/#61 land; the Annals paper (Landesman-Litt) then needs monodromy of isomonodromic deformations, the character variety (#54, with GL_r(ℂ)), Hodge theory of variations and the Corlette-Simpson correspondence. Those belong to a nonabelian Hodge / character variety roadmap (Simpson, *Higgs bundles and local systems*; Goldman's surveys), next to PR #1169.
