Open PR #2266 (MappingClassGroupsAndCanonicalRepresentations) specifies it: §2.1 defines `HasFiniteConjugacyOrbitUnder H ρ` for a subgroup `H` of `MulAut G` and calls a representation of `π₁(Σ_{g,n}, x)` MCG-finite when it holds for the based action of `PMod(g, n + 1)` (§0.2). Its target API gives the finite-index stabilizer form, invariance under conjugation of `ρ` (58.2(a) for conjugacy only; basepoint and model independence are not targets), passage to and from finite-index subgroups of `H`, and the finite-image Check (58.2(c)). The identification with the orbit of `[ρ]` under the outer action of `PMod(g, n)` and under `Mod(g, n)` (58.1, 58.2(b)) is prose in §2.1, not a target; the `Mod_{g,n}` action on conjugacy classes and the equivalence of finite orbits under `Mod_{g,n}` and under a finite-index subgroup such as `PMod_{g,n}` are targets of open PR #2430 §3.5. #2266 takes `Mod(Σ_{g,b,p})` with punctures from merged [MordellLawrenceVenkatesh](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/MordellLawrenceVenkatesh/README.md) §5.9. Its Lean prototype states the headline with the algebraic group `pureMappingClassAut g n` (automorphisms of the presented surface group fixing each peripheral loop up to conjugacy), which equals the image of `PMod(g, n + 1)` only by Dehn–Nielsen–Baer for punctured surfaces, used in #2266 §0.2 only for the containment. No merged roadmap specifies MCG-finiteness. Depends on #59 and #61.

## Mathlib
Has `FundamentalGroup`, `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`, `MulAction.orbit`, `Set.Finite`, `MulAut.conj`. No mapping class groups, no Out.

## Tau Ceti code
No mapping class group of a punctured surface (only the smooth `TauCeti.MappingClassGroup I M` = π₀ Diff(M, ∂M) of a compact manifold, `Geometry/Diffeomorphism/MappingClassGroup.lean`, with no action on `π₁`), no surface-group representation spaces. Surface groups exist as presented groups, `TauCeti.SurfaceGroup.Orientable g m` with its peripheral elements (`GroupTheory/SurfaceGroup/Basic.lean`), not identified with the topological `π₁`. `TauCeti.OuterAut G` for every group (`GroupTheory/Automorphism/Outer.lean`) and `TauCeti.ContinuousOut` (`Topology/Algebra/Group/ContinuousAut/Basic.lean`, isomorphic to `OuterAut` for discrete `G`).
