# 34. Neumann eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the Laplacian on a bounded domain

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1614 "PDE Mixed Boundary Conditions" specifies the definition in full as §4.6 "Mixed and Neumann spectra (D21)" of the PDE roadmap's Layer 4, with §4.5 (mixed and Neumann problems) and §1.8 (boundary parts, `H¹_Γ(Ω)`): for `Γ = ∅` on a bounded Lipschitz domain, weak Neumann eigenvalues against all of `H¹(Ω)` (34.1), the eigenbasis and nondecreasing enumeration with finite multiplicities, the min–max formula, the Neumann kernel as the locally constant functions with multiplicity of `0` the number of components (Theorem 34.2), the second eigenvalue as the mean-zero Rayleigh minimum with its minimisers (34.4(c)), and the self-adjoint realization with compact resolvent (Remark (1)). Merged PDE has only the Dirichlet spectrum, which is implemented. Tau Ceti's `IsCoercive.formOperator` applied to `W^{1,2}(Ω)` with the Rellich value map gives the Neumann operator; this application is not in code. Proposition 34.3 (regularity, the classical problem), which both papers use, is left by PR #1614 to a later PDE part and is not specified anywhere.

## Mathlib
- Has: Laplacian `InnerProductSpace.instLaplacian`, `LinearPMap.IsSelfAdjoint`, compact operators and the spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators, whole-space Bessel potential spaces `BesselPotentialSpace` (`H^{s,p}(E,F)`).
- Missing: H¹(Ω) on a domain, Neumann form, Neumann eigenvalues.

- The abstract eigenbasis of a coercive form along a compact map, `IsCoercive.exists_hilbertBasis_forall_apply_eq_smul_inner` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/Spectrum.lean`), which PR #1614 §4.6 consumes.
- The abstract operator of a symmetric form coercive after a shift along `J : V → H`: `IsCoercive.formOperator` with its graph and domain (`mem_graph_formOperator_iff`, `mem_domain_formOperator_iff`), shift independence `formOperator_eq`, `isSelfAdjoint_formOperator`, the lower bound, `hasCompactResolvent_formOperator` for compact `J` and the eigenbasis `exists_hilbertBasis_formOperator` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/FormOperator.lean`). With `V = W^{1,2}(Ω)`, `J` the Rellich value map and `Q(u, v) = ∫∇u·∇v`, this is the Neumann realization with its eigenbasis. `TauCeti.W1p mu Ω 2` is an `InnerProductSpace ℝ` by instance search: `W1p` is an `abbrev` for the closed submodule `w1pSubmodule` of the `L²` jet space `Sobolev1JetLp` (`Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Basic.lean`), and `#synth InnerProductSpace ℝ (W1p mu Omega 2)` returns `Submodule.innerProductSpace`; with `CompleteSpace` it is a Hilbert space. The specialization of `formOperator` to it is not in code.
- Missing: Neumann solution operator / eigenvalues (the only "Neumann" in `Analysis/PDE` and `Analysis/Sobolev` is the Neumann series bound in `Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/`).

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR #1614 "PDE Mixed Boundary Conditions", written in merged PDE's Layer structure (PR README):
  - §1.8 "Boundary parts, functions vanishing on them, and Poincaré–Friedrichs (A8)" (l.1097): `W^{1,p}_Γ(Ω)`; for `Γ = ∅` it is `H¹(Ω)`.
  - §4.5 "Mixed and Neumann problems (D20)" (l.1955): weak mixed problem on `H¹_Γ(Ω)`, the coercive case by Lax–Milgram, the Fredholm alternative, and the pure Neumann problem (`neumann_solvable_iff`), with the disconnected check `(0, 1) ∪ (2, 3)`.
  - §4.6 "Mixed and Neumann spectra (D21)" (l.2015): `IsMixedEigenvalue` for the energy form, the Neumann case `Γ = ∅` with test space `H¹(Ω)` (34.1); the eigenbasis from Tau Ceti's `IsCoercive.exists_hilbertBasis_forall_apply_eq_smul_inner` with `J` the Rellich value map (`exists_hilbertBasis_mixedEigenvalue`, `mixedEigenvalue_spectrum`); min–max for every `k` (`mixedEigenvalue`, `isLeast_mixedEigenvalue`); the Neumann kernel as the locally constant functions with `finite_connectedComponents_of_isLipschitzDomain`, so the multiplicity of `0` is the number of components (`neumann_kernel_iff_isLocallyConstant`); the second Neumann eigenvalue on connected `Ω` as the mean-zero Rayleigh minimum with its minimisers (`isLeast_rayleigh_meanZero_mixedEigenvalue_one`, 34.4(c)); the realization `−Δ_Γ` as a nonnegative self-adjoint `LinearPMap` with compact resolvent and spectrum `{λₖ}` (`exists_isSelfAdjoint_mixedLaplacian`), built as the operator of the form along `J`. Indexing from `0`. Theorem 34.2 and Definition 34.4 are covered.
  - Its scope (l.48) leaves regularity up to the boundary for the mixed and Neumann problems (global `H²` and `C^∞` on smooth domains, corner behaviour on polygons, Grisvard) to "a later PDE part"; interior regularity of eigenfunctions follows from §5.1.
- Not specified anywhere: Prop 34.3 regularity (real analyticity inside, C^∞ up to smooth boundary, polygon corner behaviour, classical ⇔ weak equivalence), which the two papers use to pass between classical and weak formulations.

## Prerequisite chain
1. H¹(Ω), Lipschitz domains, Rellich, Poincaré–Wirtinger: Tau Ceti (PDE §1.1, §1.5, §1.6 targets, implemented).
2. Compact self-adjoint spectral theorem / Courant–Fischer: Mathlib + Tau Ceti.
3. Neumann form, resolvent, eigenvalue sequence μ_k, eigenspace of 0 = locally constant functions, min–max and the second eigenvalue: target of open PR #1614 §4.6 (on §§1.8, 4.5), not implemented; the abstract eigenbasis and operator are Tau Ceti `IsCoercive.exists_hilbertBasis_forall_apply_eq_smul_inner` and `IsCoercive.formOperator` on the Hilbert space `W1p mu Ω 2`.
4. Min–max on the form domain: also target of merged VariationalMethods §4.4 in abstract form, not implemented.
5. Interior regularity (Δu = μu classically, analytic): PDE §5.1 (E20) interior H^k estimates and C^∞ bootstrap (merged; the interior part is implemented, only the global boundary versions of Layer 5 remain); analyticity unplanned.
6. Boundary regularity on C^∞ domains and on polygons, and the classical–weak equivalence (Prop 34.3(ii),(iii)): unplanned (PDE Layer 5 lists "global boundary estimates" as remaining but for Dirichlet; Neumann boundary regularity and corner analysis not specified).

## Library-scale gap
The definition itself is covered by PR #1614 §4.6. What the papers additionally need is Neumann boundary regularity: global H² / C^∞ regularity up to a smooth boundary for the Neumann problem and corner regularity on polygons (Grisvard, *Elliptic Problems in Nonsmooth Domains*, Ch. 2, 4–5; Evans §6.3 for the smooth case). PR #1614 assigns this to a later PDE part, after Layer 5's global (Dirichlet) estimates.
