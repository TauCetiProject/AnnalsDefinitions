# 34. Neumann eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the Laplacian on a bounded domain

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged [PDE](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PDE/README.md) specifies the definition in full as §4.6 "Mixed and Neumann spectra (D21)" of its Layer 4 (merged PR #1614), with §4.5 (mixed and Neumann problems) and §1.8 (boundary parts, `H¹_Γ(Ω)`): for `Γ = ∅` on a bounded Lipschitz domain, weak Neumann eigenvalues against all of `H¹(Ω)` (34.1), the eigenbasis and nondecreasing enumeration with finite multiplicities, the min–max formula, the Neumann kernel as the locally constant functions with multiplicity of `0` the number of components (Theorem 34.2), the second eigenvalue as the mean-zero Rayleigh minimum with its minimisers (34.4(c)), and the self-adjoint realization with compact resolvent (Remark (1)). The Dirichlet spectrum of §4.4 is implemented; §1.8 is partly in code (admissible boundary parts, `W^{1,p}_Γ(Ω)` with its empty case `H¹(Ω)`, Poincaré–Friedrichs, the mollified Lipschitz shears and the transport of `W^{1,p}_Γ(Ω)` along them, the trace characterization for an endpoint of an interval, for a half-hyperplane in a half-space and on a bounded Lipschitz domain away from the relative boundary of `Γ`); of §4.5 the pure Neumann problem with its compatibility condition, the coercive mixed problem, the Fredholm alternative on `H¹_Γ(Ω)` and the interval Checks are in code, and of §4.6 the Neumann kernel (locally constant functions, of dimension the number of components on a bounded Lipschitz domain), the realization `−Δ_Γ` (`TauCeti.PDE.mixedOperator`, for `Γ = ∅` the Neumann Laplacian of Remark (1)), self-adjoint and nonnegative, with compact resolvent and spectrum the set of weak eigenvalues of 34.1 on a domain with the Rellich property, the positive-`Γ` bullet, the non-invertibility of `−Δ_∅`, the second Neumann eigenvalue as the least positive eigenvalue, the mean-zero Rayleigh minimum with the minimisers as its eigenfunctions (34.4(c), `TauCeti.PDE.exists_isLeast_neumannEigenvalue_pos_rayleigh_meanZero`), and the interval Neumann and mixed spectra Checks are in code. Not in code: the eigenbasis, the nondecreasing enumeration `μ_k` with multiplicities and the min–max formula (Theorem 34.2's enumeration and the indexed eigenvalues of Definition 34.4). Proposition 34.3 (regularity, the classical problem), which both papers use, is left by the PDE roadmap's scope (README l.48) to a later PDE part and is not specified anywhere.

## Prerequisite chain
1. H¹(Ω), Lipschitz domains, Rellich, Poincaré–Wirtinger: Tau Ceti (PDE §1.1, §1.5, §1.6 targets, implemented).
2. Compact self-adjoint spectral theorem / Courant–Fischer: Mathlib + Tau Ceti.
3. Neumann form, resolvent, eigenvalue sequence μ_k, eigenspace of 0 = locally constant functions, min–max and the second eigenvalue: target of merged PDE §4.6 (on §§1.8, 4.5), partly in code; §1.8's `H¹_Γ(Ω)` with its empty case and Poincaré–Friedrichs, §4.6's finiteness of components and its Neumann kernel (the locally constant functions, of dimension the number of components) are in code (`w1pVanishingOnSubmodule`, `Set.IsLipschitzDomain.finite_connectedComponents`, `PDE.forall_energyFormH1_one_zero_zero_eq_zero_iff_exists_isLocallyConstant`, `W1p.finrank_ker_gradientL_of_isLipschitzDomain`), as are the Neumann realization with compact resolvent and its spectrum (`PDE.mixedOperator` at `Γ = ∅`, `PDE.spectrum_mixedOperator_laplacian`) and the second Neumann eigenvalue as the mean-zero Rayleigh minimum (`PDE.exists_isLeast_neumannEigenvalue_pos_rayleigh_meanZero`); §4.5's pure Neumann problem, coercive mixed problem and Fredholm alternative are in code (`PDE.exists_forall_energyFormH1_eq_setIntegral_iff`, `PDE.mixedSolutionL`, `PDE.exists_sub_mem_w1pVanishingOnSubmodule_forall_energyFormH1_eq_iff_of_garding`); the abstract eigenbasis and operator are Tau Ceti `IsCoercive.exists_hilbertBasis_forall_apply_eq_smul_inner` and `IsCoercive.formOperator` on the Hilbert space `W1p mu Ω 2`.
4. Min–max on the form domain: also target of merged VariationalMethods §4.4 in abstract form, not implemented; its §§3.1–3.2 compact form pairs and constrained minima are partly in code (`TauCeti.CompactFormPair`, `exists_isMinOn_energy_unitConstraint`), and its §3.4 form kernel and kernel-orthogonal Poincaré connector are in code (`CompactFormPair.formKernel`, `kernelOrthogonal`).

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged PDE §4.6 specifies it, so what remains is implementation of the rest of §1.8 and of §4.6 (the eigenbasis, enumeration and min–max) along the Dirichlet route in code. What the papers additionally need is Neumann boundary regularity: global H² / C^∞ regularity up to a smooth boundary for the Neumann problem and corner regularity on polygons (Grisvard, *Elliptic Problems in Nonsmooth Domains*, Ch. 2, 4–5; Evans §6.3 for the smooth case). The PDE roadmap assigns this to a later PDE part, after Layer 5's global (Dirichlet) estimates.
