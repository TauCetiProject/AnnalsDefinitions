## Merged roadmaps

Its *Needs* for §2.1 are SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §4.2, §4.3, §5.1, §§6.1–6.3 (gaps `sobolev-maps-class`, `-weak-differential`, `-norm`, `-jacobian`). Not implemented.
- `TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/` ([README](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds/README.md)) is QuasiregularMaps' supplier, section for section: §4.2, §4.4, §4.5 (class `IsSobolevMap`), §4.3 and §3.1 (weak differential `HasWeakMFDeriv`, null sets), §§5.1–5.3 (norm), §§6.1–6.3 (Jacobian `orientedJacobian`), §4.6(a)–(b) and §3.3 (chain rules), §1.2 and §7.1 (Euclidean agreement). Its source manifolds are modelled on finite-dimensional real normed spaces with an additive Haar measure (Conventions, §1.1), as QuasiregularMaps' are (`𝓘(ℝ, E)` on a real normed space `E`); §6.2 gives QuasiregularMaps the chart formula for `J_f` in positively oriented charts. It leaves to QuasiregularMaps the membership of locally Lipschitz maps (§2.8), `ACL^p` (§§5.1–5.2) and all distortion theory. Its Layers 8 and 11 (pullback of forms, degree) are what QuasiregularMaps Parts II–III would consume. Not implemented.
- [SobolevDifferentialForms](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SobolevDifferentialForms/README.md) (#1609), named by QuasiregularMaps Part III: the weak exterior derivative and `WᵖΛᵏ(Ω)` on open subsets of `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)` (§2.1 l.212, §3.1 l.278), pullback under `C¹` and bi-Lipschitz maps (§4.1 l.384), the L² de Rham complex (§6.2 l.533). ## Prerequisite chain
1. Singular values, `normDet`, `Orientation.volumeForm`: in Mathlib.
2. Euclidean weak derivative / `W^{1,p}`: in Tau Ceti (`HasWeakFDerivOn`, `W1p`); linear oriented Jacobian `TauCeti.orientedJacobian`: in Tau Ceti.
3. Manifold orientations, Riemannian volume, chartwise null sets: in Tau Ceti (DifferentialGeometry 2.1–2.2, 12.4).
4. `W^{1,p}_loc(M,N)`, weak differential `Df`, measurable `‖Df‖`, oriented Jacobian `J_f` (definition #47): targets of merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§4.2–4.3, §5.1, §§6.1–6.3, not implemented.
5. Minimal stretch and linear distortion quotients: targets of merged QuasiregularMaps Layer 1 (§§1.1–1.3), not implemented (open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2950 for §1.1).
6. Quasiregular map, outer distortion: targets of merged QuasiregularMaps §§2.1–2.3.

## Library-scale gap
The definition needs implementation of merged QuasiregularMaps Layers 1–2 on merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds (#47: chartwise `W^{1,p}_loc` maps between manifolds, the weak differential as a measurable section of `Hom(TM, f^*TN)`, its norm and Jacobian, chain rules with `C¹` factors; Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen, Hélein–Wood), which sits beside PDE Layer 1 and DifferentialGeometry. The Annals theorem itself also needs QuasiregularMaps Parts II–III (Reshetnyak theory, Rickman *Quasiregular Mappings*; cohomological bounds via Sobolev forms), which no roadmap or PR specifies.
