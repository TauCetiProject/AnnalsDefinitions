In code: QuasiregularMaps Layer 1 linear algebra (the minimal stretch of §1.1, the outer and inner distortion of a linear map of §1.2, §§1.3–1.4 (the comparison of the distortions with its equality cases, the Hilbert–Schmidt distortion with its sharp constant), §1.5 (the planar maps `z ↦ az + b z̄` with their distortions) and the Layer 1 Examples) and the Euclidean case of 41.1(a)–(b) (§2.9): `K`-quasiregular maps of open subsets of a finite-dimensional real inner product space to itself, `TauCeti.IsQuasiregularOn`, with its inner and outer inequalities (the Euclidean form of §3.2). PARTIAL-CODE rests on the Euclidean case, a borderline call: the paper's maps go from `ℝⁿ` or a ball into a closed manifold.

## Library-scale gap
The definition needs implementation of merged QuasiregularMaps Layers 1–2 on merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds (#47: chartwise `W^{1,p}_loc` maps between manifolds, the weak differential as a measurable section of `Hom(TM, f^*TN)`, its norm and Jacobian, chain rules with `C¹` factors; Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen, Hélein–Wood), which sits beside PDE Layer 1 and DifferentialGeometry. The Annals theorem itself also needs QuasiregularMaps Parts II–III (Reshetnyak theory, Rickman *Quasiregular Mappings*; cohomological bounds via Sobolev forms), which no roadmap or PR specifies.
