## Library-scale gap
Once #2134 merges, the remaining gap is the SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds roadmap (#47): chartwise `W^{1,p}_loc` maps between manifolds, the weak differential as a measurable section of `Hom(TM, f^*TN)`, its norm and Jacobian, chain rules with `C¹` factors (Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen, Hélein–Wood). It sits beside PDE Layer 1 and DifferentialGeometry. The Annals theorem itself also needs #2134's Parts II–III (Reshetnyak theory, Rickman *Quasiregular Mappings*; cohomological bounds via Sobolev forms).
