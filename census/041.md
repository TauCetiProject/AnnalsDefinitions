1. Singular values, `normDet`, `Orientation.volumeForm`: in Mathlib.
2. Euclidean weak derivative / `W^{1,p}`: in Tau Ceti (`HasWeakFDerivOn`, `W1p`); linear oriented Jacobian `TauCeti.orientedJacobian`: in Tau Ceti.
3. Manifold orientations, Riemannian volume, chartwise null sets: in Tau Ceti (DifferentialGeometry 2.1–2.2, 12.4).
4. `W^{1,p}_loc(M,N)`, weak differential `Df`, measurable `‖Df‖`, oriented Jacobian `J_f` (definition #47): targets of merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds §§4.2–4.3, §5.1, §§6.1–6.3, not implemented.
5. Minimal stretch and linear distortion quotients: targets of merged QuasiregularMaps Layer 1 (§§1.1–1.3); the minimal stretch (§1.1, `LinearMap.minimalStretch`) and the outer and inner distortion with their basic API (§1.2, `Orientation.outerDistortion`, `Orientation.innerDistortion`) in Tau Ceti; the rest of §1.2 and §§1.3–1.4 in open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4850, https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4858, https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4885 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4894.

## Library-scale gap
The definition needs implementation of merged QuasiregularMaps Layers 1–2 on merged SobolevMapsBetweenManifolds (#47: chartwise `W^{1,p}_loc` maps between manifolds, the weak differential as a measurable section of `Hom(TM, f^*TN)`, its norm and Jacobian, chain rules with `C¹` factors; Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen, Hélein–Wood), which sits beside PDE Layer 1 and DifferentialGeometry. The Annals theorem itself also needs QuasiregularMaps Parts II–III (Reshetnyak theory, Rickman *Quasiregular Mappings*; cohomological bounds via Sobolev forms), which no roadmap or PR specifies.
