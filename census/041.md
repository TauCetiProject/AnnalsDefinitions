## Library-scale gap
The definition needs review and merging of PR #2134 and of its supplier PR #2230 (#47: chartwise `W^{1,p}_loc` maps between manifolds, the weak differential as a measurable section of `Hom(TM, f^*TN)`, its norm and Jacobian, chain rules with `C¹` factors; Hajłasz–Iwaniec–Malý–Onninen, Hélein–Wood), which sits beside PDE Layer 1 and DifferentialGeometry.
