# 6. Convergence of a spectral sequence

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has the spectral sequence of a filtered cochain complex in any abelian category, in the classical decreasing `E₁` indexing, with its `E∞` objects, the abutment `H^n(K)` with its image filtration, and strong convergence for finite filtrations (`CochainComplex.filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfFinite`). That is Proposition 6.3, which is what [2020, 191(3), no. 4] uses. For complexes of modules it also has convergence for exhaustive filtrations, classical convergence for exhaustive filtrations bounded below (Weibel 5.5.1(2)), convergence for complete exhaustive filtrations with degreewise bounded pages (Weibel 5.5.10, 5.5.11), and weak convergence of the regular spectral sequence of a complete exhaustive filtration (the weak half of Weibel 5.5.10). The general notions of Definition 6.2 (weak, exhaustive/separated and strong convergence of an arbitrary spectral sequence to a filtered target, and convergence in a range of degrees, 6.2(5)) are not in code; the merged roadmap SpectralSequencesAndConvergence specifies them exactly (§1.4 the limit page of an arbitrary spectral sequence, §3.2 convergence structures in a set `I` of total degrees, §3.4 finite filtrations, §4.4 convergence in a range of degrees), and of these only the convergence bounds of §4.4 are implemented (`CategoryTheory.Abelian.SpectralObject.HasConvergenceBounds`, with the first- and third-quadrant bounds of §4.5), on the implemented §2.1 bidegree assignments and §4.1 towers; the filtered targets of §3.1, decreasing filtrations of an object with graded pieces and the exhaustive, separated, complete and finite predicates of Definition 6.2, are in code (`CategoryTheory.FilteredObject.gr`, `IsExhaustive`, `IsSeparated`, `IsComplete`, `IsFinite`).

## Mathlib
- Has: `CategoryTheory.SpectralSequence C c r₀` (pages and homology isos, `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Basic.lean`), spectral objects and their spectral sequences (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/*`, `HasSpectralSequence.lean`, first-quadrant `E₂` recipes).
- Missing: `E∞`, abutment filtrations, any convergence notion.

## Merged roadmaps
- [SpectralSequencesAndConvergence](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SpectralSequencesAndConvergence/README.md) (README l.130 "## Layer 1: cycle and boundary towers and the limit page"):
  - Layer 1: cycle and boundary towers `Z_r`, `B_r` of an arbitrary spectral sequence in Mathlib's carrier (§§1.1–1.2), stabilisation (§1.3), the limit page `E_∞ = Z_∞/B_∞` (§1.4, l.239);
  - Layer 2: bidegree assignments (§2.1), boundedness (§2.2);
  - Layer 3: decreasing filtrations (§3.1); §3.2 (l.464) weak convergence, convergence and strong convergence in a set `I ⊆ ℤ` of total degrees to a filtered abutment `H : ℤ → FilteredObject C ℤᵒᵖ`, the three notions of Definition 6.2(1)–(3); §3.4 (l.531) finite filtrations, which gives 6.2(4); §3.5 the comparison theorem;
  - Layer 4: the towers and limit page of the spectral sequence of a spectral object compared with Tau Ceti's `EInfty` (§§4.1–4.3), §4.4 (l.840) `HasConvergenceBounds` and strong convergence in degrees `I`, Definition 6.2(5); §4.5 Mathlib's quadrant spectral sequences converge;
  - Layer 5: filtered complexes (§5.3 convergence under cohomological boundedness, §5.4 exhaustive filtrations bounded below); Layers 6–7: exact couples, lim¹, conditional convergence and Boardman's theorems.
  - It consumes the Tau Ceti abutment and filtered-complex API above. §§1.1–1.2 are implemented: the cycle and boundary towers `CategoryTheory.SpectralSequence.cyclesSubobject`, `boundariesSubobject` of an arbitrary spectral sequence (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Tower.lean`). §4.4's convergence bounds `HasConvergenceBounds` of a spectral object are implemented (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/ConvergenceBounds.lean`); so are the first- and third-quadrant bounds (`hasConvergenceBounds_of_isFirstQuadrant`, `hasConvergenceBounds_of_isThirdQuadrant`); the stabilisation of pages is not. Tau Ceti has no limit page of an arbitrary spectral sequence and no convergence structure (grep `-w limitPage|HasLimitPage|ConvergenceStructure` empty). §2.1's bidegree assignments `TauCeti.BidegreeAssignment` with the §2.2 vanishing of differentials, and §4.1's towers of the spectral sequence of a spectral object (`cyclesSubobject_spectralSequence`, `boundariesSubobject_spectralSequence`) with the finitely-many-rows first-page computation, are implemented, and so is §3.1 (graded pieces and exhaustive, separated, complete and finite filtrations, `CategoryTheory/Filtration/{Basic,Decreasing}.lean`). The roadmap has no STATUS.md.
- [SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII/README.md): the two spectral sequences of a double complex and their convergence (§§1.2–1.3), Cartan–Eilenberg resolutions and the hypercohomology spectral sequences (Layer 2), composite-functor (Layer 3), universal-coefficient and Künneth (Layer 4) spectral sequences, products (Layer 5), and the Bockstein spectral sequence with its limit page (Layer 6). It states convergence in the language of SpectralSequencesAndConvergence and does not redefine it. Tau Ceti has a Cartan–Eilenberg resolution of chain complexes, `cartanEilenbergResolution` (`Algebra/Homology/CartanEilenbergResolution.lean`); the spectral sequences of this roadmap are not in code.
- Consumers of the filtered-complex sequence: DerivedModuleAlgebra (§S.1 first-quadrant double complex, "convergent"), TopologicalSheafCohomology §1.4, SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.2 (Leray, Čech), ComplexKahlerGeometry K8 (Frölicher), AlgebraicTopology (Serre).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Spectral sequences, spectral objects: Mathlib.
2. `E∞`, abutment, image filtration, finite-filtration strong convergence, decreasing `E₁` indexing, and for module complexes exhaustive, bounded-below, complete-filtration and regular weak convergence: Tau Ceti.
3. Limit page of an arbitrary spectral sequence and the convergence structures of Definition 6.2(1)–(4): SpectralSequencesAndConvergence §§1.4, 3.2, 3.4 (merged, not implemented; §1.2's cycle and boundary towers are in code, `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Tower.lean`; §2.1's bidegree assignments are in code, `TauCeti.BidegreeAssignment`; §3.1's decreasing filtrations are in code, `CategoryTheory.FilteredObject.gr`, `IsExhaustive`, `IsSeparated`, `IsComplete`, `IsFinite`).
4. Convergence in a range of total degrees (6.2(5)): SpectralSequencesAndConvergence §4.4 (merged; its convergence bounds `HasConvergenceBounds` in Tau Ceti, strong convergence in degrees `I` not implemented), on §§4.1–4.3's comparison of towers (§4.1's towers in Tau Ceti, `cyclesSubobject_spectralSequence`, `boundariesSubobject_spectralSequence`, `Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/Tower/Basic.lean`, and their `δ` forms through the first page, `Tower/FirstPage.lean`; §§4.2–4.3 not implemented).
5. The paper's coefficient category (continuous ℓ-adic Galois representations, abelian): see the étale-cohomology roadmaps; outside this definition.

## Library-scale gap
Small, and specified by the merged SpectralSequencesAndConvergence: convergence structures on a spectral sequence with a filtered abutment (Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, §5.2; Boardman, *Conditionally convergent spectral sequences*; Stacks, Tag 012K), with the filtered-complex sequence from Tau Ceti as the first instance. What remains is implementation of its Layers 1–4 on top of Tau Ceti's `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Filtered/` and `SpectralObject/` code.
