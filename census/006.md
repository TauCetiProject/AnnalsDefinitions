# 6. Convergence of a spectral sequence

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has the spectral sequence of a filtered cochain complex in any abelian category, in the classical decreasing `E₁` indexing, with its `E∞` objects, the abutment `H^n(K)` with its image filtration, and strong convergence for finite filtrations (`CochainComplex.filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfFinite`). That is Proposition 6.3, which is what [2020, 191(3), no. 4] uses. Missing: the general convergence notions of Definition 6.2 (weak, exhaustive/separated and strong convergence of an arbitrary spectral sequence to a filtered target) and convergence in a range of degrees (6.2(5)).

## Mathlib
- Has: `CategoryTheory.SpectralSequence C c r₀` (pages and homology isos, `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Basic.lean`), spectral objects and their spectral sequences (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/*`, `HasSpectralSequence.lean`, first-quadrant `E₂` recipes).
- Missing: `E∞`, abutment filtrations, any convergence notion.

## Tau Ceti code
- `Abelian.SpectralObject.abutment`, `abutmentFiltration`, `abutmentFiltrationι`, `EInftyIsoCokernel` (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/Abutment.lean`); stabilization `spectralSequencePageXIsoEInfty` (`SpectralObject/Stabilization.lean`).
- Filtered complexes: `CochainComplex.spectralSequence`, `filtrationAbutmentIso` (abutment = `H^n(K)`), `toFiltrationAbutmentFiltration` (image filtration), `isZero_filtrationAbutmentFiltration`, `isIso_filtrationAbutmentFiltrationι`, `filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfFinite` (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Filtered/Convergence.lean`); `filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoEInftyOfFinite` (`.../Filtered/Stabilization.lean`).
- Decreasing filtrations, `E₁^{p,q} ≅ H^{p+q}(F^p/F^{p+1})`: `CochainComplex.decreasingFiltrationSpectralSequence`, `decreasingFiltrationSpectralSequenceFirstPageXIso` (`.../Filtered/Indexing.lean`).
- Degeneration: `SpectralSequence.page_d_eq_zero_of_le`, `pageXIsoOfLEOfIsZero` (`.../SpectralSequence/Degeneration.lean`); bicomplex and Čech spectral sequences built on these.

## Open roadmap PRs
- None specify an abstract convergence notion (grep for `ConvergesTo|StronglyConverges|weakly converg` empty); many PRs consume spectral sequences.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Spectral sequences, spectral objects: Mathlib.
2. `E∞`, abutment, image filtration, finite-filtration strong convergence, decreasing `E₁` indexing: Tau Ceti.
3. General `ConvergesTo` structure on `(E_r, (H^n, F))` with weak/strong variants, and the agreement theorem for bounded sequences (6.2(4)): unplanned (small).
4. Convergence in a range of total degrees (6.2(5)): unplanned (trivial restriction of 3).
5. The paper's coefficient category (continuous ℓ-adic Galois representations, abelian): see the étale-cohomology roadmaps; outside this definition.

## Library-scale gap
Small: a `SpectralSequence.ConvergesTo` structure (Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, §5.2; Boardman, *Conditionally convergent spectral sequences*), with the filtered-complex instance from Tau Ceti as the first example. It would naturally sit with Tau Ceti's `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Filtered/` code or as an item in DerivedModuleAlgebra.
