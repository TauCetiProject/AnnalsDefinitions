## Prerequisite chain
1. `LinearPMap`, self-adjointness, Lebesgue decomposition of measures: in Mathlib.
2. `TauCeti.ProjValMeasure`, diagonal scalar measures, bounded Borel calculus: in Tau Ceti (SAST Part B, mostly implemented).
3. Resolvents of self-adjoint `LinearPMap`, Cayley transform: in Tau Ceti (SAST Part D done).
4. Spectral PVM `P_A` of an unbounded self-adjoint operator, resolvent formula, projections commute with resolvent/preserve domain: target in merged SelfAdjointSpectralTheory SA-E01, E03–E07 (not implemented).
5. Spectral measure `μ_f` of a vector: SA-B21 applied to `P_A` (merged; trivial once 4 exists).
6. Unbounded spectral integral (UO-A01) and approximate eigenvectors/spectral projections (UO-C01): targets of merged UnboundedOperators §1.1, §3.1, not implemented.
7. Spectral subspaces H_ac/H_sc/H_pp, reducing property, restricted operators H_ac, and σ_ac(H) = σ(H_ac): target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-C03 (§3.3), not implemented.
8. Application in the paper: H = −Δ+V on L²(ℝ^d) with bounded V, self-adjoint on H² (Kato–Rellich in Tau Ceti; see #45).

## Library-scale gap
Planned: the gap is implementation, SA-E01 (unbounded spectral PVM), then UO-A01, UO-C01 and UO-C03. Scattering-theoretic tools that the paper's theorem needs to be *proved* (Kato–Rosenblum, wave operators, Mourre theory) are not planned anywhere; a later OperatorTheory/ScatteringTheory sub-roadmap beside UnboundedOperators would cover them (Reed–Simon III, Yafaev).
