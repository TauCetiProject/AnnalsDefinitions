- Prerequisites present: unbounded operators and adjoints `LinearPMap`, `IsSelfAdjoint` for `LinearPMap` (`Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap.lean`, `LinearPMap.isSelfAdjoint_def`); Lebesgue decomposition of measures `MeasureTheory.Measure.singularPart`, `rnDeriv`, `HaveLebesgueDecomposition`, `MutuallySingular`, `AbsolutelyContinuous` (`Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Decomposition/Lebesgue.lean`); bounded continuous functional calculus; `spectrum`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Definition absent (no `acSpectrum`/`H_ac`/spectral-type declarations for operators; the only "singular continuous" hits are the scalar-measure split below).
- Prerequisites present: `TauCeti.ProjValMeasure` (`TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ProjValMeasure/Basic.lean`, with integral, null sets, diagonal measures in `BorelCalculus/DiagonalMeasure.lean`); the bounded-operator spectral PVM `spectralPVM` (`TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/BorelCalculus/Basic.lean`, on `spectrum ℂ a`); the spectral PVM of an unbounded self-adjoint operator `LinearPMap.spectralPVM A hA : ProjValMeasure ℝ H` (pushforward of the Cayley transform's PVM), with the resolvent formula `IsSelfAdjoint.inner_resolvent_apply_eq_integral`, `integral_spectralPVM_eq_resolvent`, its uniqueness `IsSelfAdjoint.eq_spectralPVM_iff`, commutation with resolvents `commute_spectralPVM_proj_resolvent`, reducing spectral subspaces `isReducing_range_spectralPVM_proj` and self-adjoint restrictions `isSelfAdjoint_restrict_range_spectralPVM_proj` (`TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/LinearPMap/ProjValMeasure/Basic.lean`, SA-E01, SA-E05–E07); the unbounded spectral integral `ProjValMeasure.spectralIntegral` with `existsUnique_spectralIntegral` (`ProjValMeasure/SpectralIntegral.lean`, UO-A01); Kato–Rellich `IsSelfAdjoint.add_of_isRelativelyBounded`; resolvents of `LinearPMap` (`TauCeti.Analysis.Normed.Operator.Resolvent.Unbounded`); Yosida approximants; unbounded Stone theorem.
- The scalar-measure split that UO-C03 starts from: `MeasureTheory.Measure.singularContinuousPart μ ν` with `atomicPart_add_withDensity_rnDeriv_add_singularContinuousPart` (`μ = μ_pp + μ_ac + μ_sc`), its uniqueness `eq_atomicPart_and_eq_withDensity_rnDeriv_and_eq_singularContinuousPart_of_eq_add`, and `not_isAtom_singularContinuousPart` (`MeasureTheory/Measure/Decomposition/SingularContinuous.lean`), on `atomicPart`/`diffusePart` (`.../Decomposition/Atomic.lean`). Applied to the diagonal measures of a PVM on `ℝ` with Lebesgue `ν`, these are the three spectral types of a vector.
- Missing: the subspaces `H_pp`, `H_ac`, `H_sc` of a PVM and the operator-level spectral types (UO-C03), and the operator-level spectral characterisations of UO-C01 (approximate eigenvectors exist only for bounded operators, `TauCeti/Analysis/Normed/Operator/ApproximatePointSpectrum.lean`). `OperatorTheory/STATUS.md` (at `b235000`) lags the code: it lists the unbounded spectral PVM and resolvent formula as remaining.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `LinearPMap`, self-adjointness, Lebesgue decomposition of measures: in Mathlib; the pp/ac/sc split of a σ-finite measure on `ℝ`: in Tau Ceti (`singularContinuousPart`).
2. `TauCeti.ProjValMeasure`, diagonal scalar measures, bounded Borel calculus: in Tau Ceti (SAST Part B, mostly implemented).
3. Resolvents of self-adjoint `LinearPMap`, Cayley transform: in Tau Ceti (SAST Part D done).
4. Spectral PVM `P_A` of an unbounded self-adjoint operator, resolvent formula, projections commute with resolvent and reduce `A`: SelfAdjointSpectralTheory SA-E01, E05–E07, in Tau Ceti (`LinearPMap.spectralPVM`, `IsSelfAdjoint.isReducing_range_spectralPVM_proj`).
5. Spectral measure `μ_f` of a vector: SA-B21 applied to `P_A` (merged; trivial once 4 exists).
6. Unbounded spectral integral (UO-A01): in Tau Ceti (`ProjValMeasure.spectralIntegral`). Approximate eigenvectors/spectral projections (UO-C01): target of merged UnboundedOperators §3.1, not implemented (needs §1.5 spectral families and §1.6 support and spectrum).
7. Spectral subspaces H_ac/H_sc/H_pp, reducing property, restricted operators H_ac, and σ_ac(H) = σ(H_ac): target of merged UnboundedOperators UO-C03 (§3.3); its scalar-measure step is in code, the rest is not. The Lebesgue-decomposition description of the type projections and `σ_ac` with its characterisations: PR #2424 §§1.2–1.5.
8. Application in the paper: H = −Δ+V on L²(ℝ^d) with bounded V, self-adjoint on H² (bounded Kato–Rellich in Tau Ceti; PR #2424 §4.4; see #45).
