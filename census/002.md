# 2. p-block (Brauer block) of a finite group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775) specifies the definition as a target: block idempotents `IsBlockIdempotent` of `R[G]` as primitive idempotents of the centre with the block decomposition (§0.2), the splitting `p`-modular system (§1.1), the bijection of blocks of `O[G]` and `k[G]` (`existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue`, §1.3), the partitions `Irr(G) = ⊔ Irr(B)` and `IBr(G) = ⊔ IBr(B)`, Brauer's central-character criterion (`exists_blockIdempotent_iff_centralCharacter_congr`) and `SameBlock` over the algebraic integers (§4.2), and the principal block (§4.3). Not implemented. The unipotent `ℓ`-blocks of finite reductive groups that the 2020 paper uses need Deligne–Lusztig theory, which no roadmap specifies.

## Mathlib
- Has: `MonoidAlgebra`, `Subalgebra.center`, `CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents`/`OrthogonalIdempotents` (`RingTheory/Idempotents.lean`), idempotent lifting along nilpotent/complete ideals, complete DVRs, `FDRep.character` and orthonormality of characters over alg. closed char-0 fields.
- Missing: p-modular systems, blocks, Brauer characters, decomposition matrices.

- No block idempotents, `Irr(B)`, `IBr`, or modular systems (grep `blockIdempotent|IsBlockIdempotent|brauerCharacter` empty).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Group algebras, centres, idempotents, complete DVRs: Mathlib.
2. Primitive idempotents, central characters, `Irr(G)`, complete-adic idempotent lifting: Tau Ceti.
3. Block idempotents over `k`, `O`; lifting bijection: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §§0.2, 1.3 (merged, not implemented).
4. Splitting p-modular system: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.1 (merged).
5. `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)`, `SameBlock` (Brauer's criterion), principal block: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §§4.2–4.3 (merged).
6. Decomposition matrix of a block (Remark 3): ModularCharactersAndBlocks §2.4 and Layer 4 (merged).
7. Unipotent ℓ-blocks of finite reductive groups (paper 2020, 192(2), no. 7): needs Deligne–Lusztig unipotent characters, unplanned (CharacterTheory README lists Deligne–Lusztig theory as out of scope).

## Library-scale gap
None for the 2024 and 2025 papers: merged ModularCharactersAndBlocks (Webb, *A Course in Finite Group Representation Theory*, Ch. 12; Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*) awaits implementation. The 2020 Brunat–Dudas–Taylor paper additionally needs Deligne–Lusztig theory and unipotent characters of `G^F` (Digne–Michel, *Representations of Finite Groups of Lie Type*; Geck–Malle, *The Character Theory of Finite Groups of Lie Type*), a separate roadmap beside ReductiveGroups Layer 7a (finite fields, Frobenius) and RepresentationTheory/CharacterTheory.
