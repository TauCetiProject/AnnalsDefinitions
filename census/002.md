# 2. p-block (Brauer block) of a finite group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Definition 2.3(1) over any commutative ring is in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent b` (a primitive idempotent of `Z(R[G])`), with existence of a complete family of block idempotents over an Artinian ring, uniqueness of complete families, transport along group isomorphisms, the block algebras and the decomposition `R[G] ≃ₐ[R] ∏_B e_B R[G]`, scalar extension of blocks, and the splitting `M = ⨁ e_B M` of modules with each indecomposable module in exactly one block (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/{Basic,Algebra,ScalarExtension}.lean`, `RingTheory/Idempotents/Module.lean`). This is ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2. The bijection between the blocks of `O[G]` and of `k[G]` for a Henselian local ring `O`, such as a complete discrete valuation ring, with residue field `k` (Proposition 2.2(2), `existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue`, §1.3) is in code too (`GroupAlgebra/Block/Lift.lean`), as is the splitting `p`-modular system at every place of `ℚ(ζ_e)` over `p` (§1.1, `NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/Completion.lean`, `RepresentationTheory/Induction/Cyclotomic/Completion.lean`), as are `G`-stable full lattices over a discrete valuation ring and the independence of the class of their reduction in `G₀(k[G])` (§1.2, `GrothendieckGroup/GroupAlgebra/StableLattice.lean`). What the papers use beyond it is not in code: `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)` (no Brauer characters; open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/7282 defines them), Brauer's central-character criterion and the principal block `B_0(G)` with its characters. The central character `λ_B` of a block over an algebraically closed field is in code (`TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent.centralCharacter`, `GroupAlgebra/Block/CentralCharacter.lean`). The block congruence `TauCeti.SameBlock M` of complex characters (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/SameBlock.lean`) and the principal block idempotent over a field (`TauCeti.principalBlockIdempotent`, `GroupAlgebra/Block/Principal.lean`) are in code. These are targets of merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775): the splitting `p`-modular system (§1.1), `existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue` (§1.3), the partitions `Irr(G) = ⊔ Irr(B)` and `IBr(G) = ⊔ IBr(B)`, `exists_blockIdempotent_iff_centralCharacter_congr` and `SameBlock` over the algebraic integers (§4.2), and the principal block (§4.3). The unipotent `ℓ`-blocks of finite reductive groups that the 2020 paper uses need Deligne–Lusztig theory, which merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) (merged PR #2954) specifies for every connected reductive group over a finite field (Layer 4), and the classification of unipotent characters beyond type A and the `ℓ`-modular theory of finite reductive groups, which no roadmap specifies.

## Mathlib
- Has: `MonoidAlgebra`, `Subalgebra.center`, `CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents`/`OrthogonalIdempotents` (`RingTheory/Idempotents.lean`), idempotent lifting along nilpotent/complete ideals, complete DVRs, `FDRep.character` and orthonormality of characters over alg. closed char-0 fields.
- Missing: p-modular systems, blocks, Brauer characters, decomposition matrices.

The central character `λ_B : Z(k[G]) →ₐ[k] k` of a block over an algebraically closed field (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §6.2), unique over every field, with the augmentation as the central character of the principal block: `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent.centralCharacter`, `centralCharacter_self`, `centralCharacter_eq_zero_of_mul_eq_zero`, `eq_centralCharacter_iff`, `TauCeti.eq_centerAugmentation`, `IsBlockIdempotent.centralCharacter_principalBlockIdempotent` (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/CentralCharacter.lean`, on `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent.algHom_ext`, `RingTheory/Idempotents/Primitive/AlgHom.lean`). Every `k`-algebra homomorphism `Z(k[G]) → k` extends uniquely to `Z(k'[G]) → k'` along a ring homomorphism `k → k'` (§6.2 scalar extension: `TauCeti.existsUnique_algHom_centerMapRingHom`, `RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/ClassSum/CenterMap.lean`, from `TauCeti.existsUnique_algHom_comp_eq_of_basis`, `LinearAlgebra/Basis/AlgHom.lean`). 1. Group algebras, centres, idempotents, complete DVRs: Mathlib.
2. Primitive idempotents, central characters, `Irr(G)`, complete-adic idempotent lifting: Tau Ceti.
3. Block idempotents of `R[G]`, complete families over Artinian `R`, uniqueness, block decomposition, scalar extension, splitting of modules: Tau Ceti (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2).
4. Splitting p-modular system: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.1 (merged; in Tau Ceti, `charP_residueField_adicCompletionIntegers`, `closure_residue_zetaInteger_pow_ordProj_eq_top`, and splitting every subgroup, `exists_simple_nonempty_iso_baseChange_adicCompletion_subgroup`; the `ℚ₇` part of the `(3, 7)` Check, `adicCompletionEquivPadic`).
5. Lifts of non-central and primitive idempotents and the correspondence of projective indecomposable modules: Tau Ceti (§1.3, `exists_projective_reduction_residue_of_projective`, `isIndecomposableModule_iff_of_reduction_residue`).

Unipotent ℓ-blocks of finite reductive groups (paper 2020, 192(2), no. 7): Deligne–Lusztig induction, Green functions and Lusztig series for every connected reductive group over a finite field are merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) Layer 4 (§§4.1–4.11), with Gelfand–Graev characters in §§2.1–2.2 (not started). ## Library-scale gap
None for the 2024 and 2025 papers: merged ModularCharactersAndBlocks (Webb, *A Course in Finite Group Representation Theory*, Ch. 12; Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*) is the roadmap, with §0.2, the splitting `p`-modular system of §1.1 and the block lifting of §1.3 in code and the rest of Layers 1–4 awaiting implementation. The 2020 Brunat–Dudas–Taylor paper additionally needs Deligne–Lusztig theory and unipotent characters of `G^F`. Deligne–Lusztig induction, Green functions and Lusztig series are merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) Layer 4, on ReductiveGroups Layers 7 and 9 (Digne–Michel, *Representations of Finite Groups of Lie Type*). The classification of unipotent characters beyond type A, Lusztig's Jordan decomposition and the ℓ-modular theory, which that roadmap places outside its scope, are unplanned (Geck–Malle, *The Character Theory of Finite Groups of Lie Type*).
