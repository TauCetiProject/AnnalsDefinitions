# 2. p-block (Brauer block) of a finite group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Definition 2.3(1) over any commutative ring is in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent b` (a primitive idempotent of `Z(R[G])`), with existence of a complete family of block idempotents over an Artinian ring, uniqueness of complete families, transport along group isomorphisms, the block algebras and the decomposition `R[G] ≃ₐ[R] ∏_B e_B R[G]`, scalar extension of blocks, and the splitting `M = ⨁ e_B M` of modules with each indecomposable module in exactly one block (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/{Basic,Algebra,ScalarExtension}.lean`, `RingTheory/Idempotents/Module.lean`). This is ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2. The bijection between the blocks of `O[G]` and of `k[G]` for a Henselian local ring `O`, such as a complete discrete valuation ring, with residue field `k` (Proposition 2.2(2), `existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue`, §1.3) is in code too (`GroupAlgebra/Block/Lift.lean`). What the papers use beyond it is not in code: splitting `p`-modular systems (the residue field and roots of unity at a cyclotomic place over `p` are in code), `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)` (no Brauer characters), Brauer's central-character criterion and the principal block `B_0(G)` with its characters. The block congruence `TauCeti.SameBlock M` of complex characters (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/SameBlock.lean`) and the principal block idempotent over a field (`TauCeti.principalBlockIdempotent`, `GroupAlgebra/Block/Principal.lean`) are in code. These are targets of merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775): the splitting `p`-modular system (§1.1), `existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue` (§1.3), the partitions `Irr(G) = ⊔ Irr(B)` and `IBr(G) = ⊔ IBr(B)`, `exists_blockIdempotent_iff_centralCharacter_congr` and `SameBlock` over the algebraic integers (§4.2), and the principal block (§4.3). The unipotent `ℓ`-blocks of finite reductive groups that the 2020 paper uses need Deligne–Lusztig theory, which merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) (merged PR #2954) specifies for every connected reductive group over a finite field (Layer 4), and the classification of unipotent characters beyond type A and the `ℓ`-modular theory of finite reductive groups, which no roadmap specifies.

## Mathlib
- Has: `MonoidAlgebra`, `Subalgebra.center`, `CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents`/`OrthogonalIdempotents` (`RingTheory/Idempotents.lean`), idempotent lifting along nilpotent/complete ideals, complete DVRs, `FDRep.character` and orthonormality of characters over alg. closed char-0 fields.
- Missing: p-modular systems, blocks, Brauer characters, decomposition matrices.

## Tau Ceti code
- Block idempotents (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2): `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent` (the native `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent` in `Subalgebra.center R (MonoidAlgebra R G)`, any commutative semiring `R`), `exists_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_isBlockIdempotent` (commutative Artinian `R`, finite `G`), `complete_blockIdempotents_unique` (any commutative ring), `IsBlockIdempotent.domCongr` (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/Basic.lean`); the block algebra `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent.BlockAlgebra` (the corner `e_B R[G]` with identity `e_B`) and `TauCeti.algEquivPiBlockAlgebra : R[G] ≃ₐ[R] ∏_B e_B R[G]` (`Block/Algebra.lean`, on `CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents.algEquivOfIsMulCentral`, `RingTheory/Idempotents/Corner/Basic.lean`); scalar extension along `f : R →+* S`: the centre map `MonoidAlgebra.centerMapRingHom` (`Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/Center.lean`) sends a block of `R[G]` to a sum of blocks of `S[G]` (`exists_centerMapRingHom_eq_sum`, `exists_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_centerMapRingHom_eq_sum`, `Block/ScalarExtension.lean`); the module splitting `TauCeti.isInternal_smul_top` and `IsIndecomposableModule.existsUnique_forall_smul_eq_self` for a complete family of central idempotents (`RingTheory/Idempotents/Module.lean`).
- Blocks of `O[G]` (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3): for `O` Henselian along the kernel of a surjection `π : O → k` and `G` finite, reduction `Z(O[G]) → Z(k[G])` is surjective with kernel `I Z(O[G])` (`centerMapRingHom_surjective`, `ker_centerMapRingHom`, `RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/ClassSum/CenterMap.lean`) and is a bijection on central idempotents and on block idempotents (`bijOn_centerMapRingHom_isIdempotentElem`, `isBlockIdempotent_centerMapRingHom_iff`, `bijOn_centerMapRingHom_isBlockIdempotent`), with `existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue` for a Henselian local `O` and its residue field (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/Lift.lean`).
- Prerequisites: `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent` (`RingTheory/Idempotents/Primitive/Basic.lean`) and `TauCeti.exists_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_isPrimitiveIdempotent` (`Primitive/Decomposition.lean`); complete-adic idempotent lifting `IsAdicComplete.exists_isIdempotentElem_eq` (`RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Idempotent.lean`); central characters of a group algebra `centralCharacter` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/CentralCharacter.lean`); `TauCeti.ClassFunction`, `irreducibleCharacters` (`CharacterTable/{ClassFunction,Table}.lean`); `TauCeti.pPart`/`pFreePart` (`GroupTheory/OrderOfElement/PPart.lean`); Grothendieck-group `cartanMatrix` (`RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/CartanMatrix.lean`).
- No splitting modular systems, `Irr(B)`, Brauer characters or `IBr(B)` (grep `brauerCharacter|blockCharacters` empty). Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3502 (§1.2: the reduction class of a stable lattice is independent of the lattice).
- Block congruence (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §4.2, the part with no modular system): the block congruence `TauCeti.SameBlock M χ ψ` of functions `G → ℂ` modulo an ideal `M` of `integralClosure ℤ ℂ`, with `sameBlock_iff`, `SameBlock.mono`, the equivalence relation `SameBlock.setoid`, and `TauCeti.sameBlock_character_iff`, the congruence of central characters on class sums for irreducible complex representations (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/SameBlock.lean`, Checks in `Examples/RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/SameBlock.lean`).
- The principal block idempotent (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §4.3): the augmentation on the centre `TauCeti.centerAugmentation`, `TauCeti.principalBlockIdempotent` as the unique block idempotent with augmentation `1` over a field and finite `G` (`existsUnique_isBlockIdempotent_centerAugmentation_eq_one`, `IsBlockIdempotent.eq_principalBlockIdempotent_iff`), every other block idempotent with augmentation `0` (`IsBlockIdempotent.centerAugmentation_eq_zero`), and the trivial module in the principal block (`asAlgebraHom_trivial_principalBlockIdempotent`, `IsBlockIdempotent.asAlgebraHom_trivial_eq_zero`) (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/Principal.lean`). The trivial character in the principal block needs §4.2's partitions.
- Checks of §0.2 and §1.3 and the Examples of Layer 0: block idempotents of `𝔽₂[C₂]`, `𝔽₃[C₂]`, `𝔽₂[C₃] → K[C₃]`, `𝔽₂[S₃]`, `𝔽₃[S₃]` and the `ℤ₂` lifts (`Examples/RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block.lean`), through the block criteria `TauCeti.isBlockIdempotent_of_coeff`, `eq_of_isBlockIdempotent_of_coeff` and `isBlockIdempotent_of_mul_of_eq_smul` (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block/Criterion.lean`) and `TauCeti.not_isPrimitiveIdempotent_add` (`RingTheory/Idempotents/Primitive/Basic.lean`).
- Higman's criterion at `H = 1` (§6.2, for the defect-zero bullet): a representation projective over `k` is projective iff its identity is a norm `∑ g, g α g⁻¹`, `Rep.id_mem_range_norm_linHom_of_projective`, `Rep.projective_iff_id_mem_range_norm_linHom`, `Rep.moduleProjective_iff_id_mem_range_norm_linHom` (`RepresentationTheory/NormSplit/Projective.lean`). The rest of the defect-zero bullet waits on defect groups.
- Lifting idempotents (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3): idempotents and primitive idempotents lift from `k[G]` to `O[G]`, and primitivity is detected on the reduction (`RingTheory/AdicCompletion/{Idempotent,PrimitiveIdempotent}.lean`, `Algebra/MonoidAlgebra/AdicComplete.lean`).
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45377 (§1.3: central idempotents lift uniquely for any ring complete along an ideal, and finitely generated projective `O[G]`-modules correspond to projective `k[G]`-modules under reduction, with indecomposability detected on the reduction, `TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Modular/{CentralLift,ProjectiveLift}.lean`).
- The `p`-modular system at a cyclotomic place (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.1, the part not using CharacterTheory §4.5): the cyclotomic field `F = ℚ(complexRoot e)` as `TauCeti.Cyclotomic.complexField e`, a number field with generator `complexField.zetaInteger e` in `𝓞 F` (`RingTheory/Cyclotomic/ComplexField.lean`); at a place `v ∣ p` the system `(F_v, O, k)` with `O = v.adicCompletionIntegers F` has residue characteristic `p` (`IsDedekindDomain.HeightOneSpectrum.charP_residueField_adicCompletionIntegers`, `RingTheory/DedekindDomain/AdicValuation/Completion.lean`), `F_v` and `O` contain the primitive `e`-th root `complexRoot e` (`TauCeti.Cyclotomic.isPrimitiveRoot_embedding_zeta`, `isPrimitiveRoot_algebraMap_zetaInteger`), `k` is generated by the reduction of `complexRoot e'` for `e'` the prime-to-`p` part (`closure_residue_zetaInteger_pow_ordProj_eq_top`) and has `p` elements when `e` is a power of `p` (`natCard_residueField_of_ordCompl_eq_one`) (`NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/Completion.lean`); the reduction of prime-to-`p` roots of unity is Tau Ceti's `rootsOfUnityEquivResidueField`, and the Checks `e = 1`, `(3, 3)` and `k ≠ 𝔽₂` for `(3, 2)` are in `Examples/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/Completion.lean`. Splitting every subgroup, the exact residue field `𝔽₄` and the `(3, 7)` Check are not in code.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Group algebras, centres, idempotents, complete DVRs: Mathlib.
2. Primitive idempotents, central characters, `Irr(G)`, complete-adic idempotent lifting: Tau Ceti.
3. Block idempotents of `R[G]`, complete families over Artinian `R`, uniqueness, block decomposition, scalar extension, splitting of modules: Tau Ceti (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2).
4. Splitting p-modular system: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.1 (merged; the part not using CharacterTheory §4.5 is in Tau Ceti, `charP_residueField_adicCompletionIntegers`, `closure_residue_zetaInteger_pow_ordProj_eq_top`; splitting every subgroup is not implemented).
5. Blocks of `O[G]` and the lifting bijection with blocks of `k[G]` (Proposition 2.2): Tau Ceti (`existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue`, `bijOn_centerMapRingHom_isBlockIdempotent`; ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3). Lifts of non-central and primitive idempotents: Tau Ceti (§1.3). The rest of §1.3 (projective indecomposable modules): open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45377.
6. `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)`, Brauer's criterion, principal block: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §§4.2–4.3 (merged), after Brauer characters (Layer 2). `SameBlock M` on complex characters: Tau Ceti (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/SameBlock.lean`). The principal block idempotent: Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.principalBlockIdempotent`, `GroupAlgebra/Block/Principal.lean`).
7. Decomposition matrix of a block (Remark 3): ModularCharactersAndBlocks §2.4 and Layer 4 (merged).
8. Unipotent ℓ-blocks of finite reductive groups (paper 2020, 192(2), no. 7): Deligne–Lusztig induction, Green functions and Lusztig series for every connected reductive group over a finite field are merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) Layer 4 (§§4.1–4.11), with Gelfand–Graev characters in §§2.1–2.2 (not started). The classification of unipotent characters beyond type A, Lusztig's Jordan decomposition and the ℓ-modular representation theory of finite reductive groups are outside that roadmap and unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
None for the 2024 and 2025 papers: merged ModularCharactersAndBlocks (Webb, *A Course in Finite Group Representation Theory*, Ch. 12; Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*) is the roadmap, with §0.2 and the block lifting of §1.3 in code and the rest of Layers 1–4 awaiting implementation. The 2020 Brunat–Dudas–Taylor paper additionally needs Deligne–Lusztig theory and unipotent characters of `G^F`. Deligne–Lusztig induction, Green functions and Lusztig series are merged [FiniteReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/FiniteReductiveGroups/README.md) Layer 4, on ReductiveGroups Layers 7 and 9 (Digne–Michel, *Representations of Finite Groups of Lie Type*). The classification of unipotent characters beyond type A, Lusztig's Jordan decomposition and the ℓ-modular theory, which that roadmap places outside its scope, are unplanned (Geck–Malle, *The Character Theory of Finite Groups of Lie Type*).
