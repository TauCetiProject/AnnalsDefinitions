# 2. p-block (Brauer block) of a finite group

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Block idempotents of `R[G]`, lifting from `k[G]` to `O[G]`, the partitions `Irr(G) = ⊔ Irr(B)` and `IBr(G) = ⊔ IBr(B)`, Brauer's central-character criterion (`SameBlock`) and the principal block are all targets of open PR #1775 (RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks); nothing merged or in code.

## Mathlib
- Has: `MonoidAlgebra`, `Subalgebra.center`, `CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents`/`OrthogonalIdempotents` (`RingTheory/Idempotents.lean`), idempotent lifting along nilpotent/complete ideals, complete DVRs, `FDRep.character` and orthonormality of characters over alg. closed char-0 fields.
- Missing: p-modular systems, blocks, Brauer characters, decomposition matrices.

## Tau Ceti code
- Prerequisites only: `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent` (`RingTheory/Idempotents/Primitive/Basic.lean`); central characters of a group algebra `centralCharacter` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/CentralCharacter.lean`); `TauCeti.ClassFunction`, `irreducibleCharacters` (`CharacterTable/{ClassFunction,Table}.lean`); `TauCeti.pPart`/`pFreePart` (`GroupTheory/OrderOfElement/PPart.lean`); Grothendieck-group `cartanMatrix` (`RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/CartanMatrix.lean`).
- No block idempotents, `Irr(B)`, `IBr`, or modular systems (grep `blockIdempotent|IsBlockIdempotent|brauerCharacter` empty).

## Merged roadmaps
- RepresentationTheory README "No modular representation theory proper": blocks, Brauer characters, decomposition matrices explicitly out of scope of the merged RepresentationTheory sub-roadmaps.
- Prerequisites: CharacterTheory §4.3 central characters (done), §§3.x orthogonality; ModularInduction (complete; `G₀(k[G])`, no Brauer characters); SemisimpleAlgebras (complete).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Group algebras, centres, idempotents, complete DVRs: Mathlib.
2. Primitive idempotents, central characters, `Irr(G)`: Tau Ceti.
3. Block idempotents over `k`, `O`; lifting bijection: target PR #1775 Layers 0.2, 1.3.
4. Splitting p-modular system: PR #1775 Layer 1.1.
5. `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)`, `SameBlock` (Brauer's criterion), principal block: PR #1775 Layer 4.
6. Decomposition matrix of a block (Remark 3): PR #1775 Layers 2.4, 4.
7. Unipotent ℓ-blocks of finite reductive groups (paper 2020, 192(2), no. 7): needs Deligne–Lusztig unipotent characters, unplanned (CharacterTheory README lists Deligne–Lusztig theory as out of scope).

## Library-scale gap
Covered by PR #1775 for the 2024 and 2025 papers. The 2020 Brunat–Dudas–Taylor paper additionally needs Deligne–Lusztig theory and unipotent characters of `G^F` (Digne–Michel, *Representations of Finite Groups of Lie Type*; Geck–Malle, *The Character Theory of Finite Groups of Lie Type*), a separate roadmap beside ReductiveGroups Layer 7a (finite fields, Frobenius) and RepresentationTheory/CharacterTheory.
