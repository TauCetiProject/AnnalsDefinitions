# 2. p-block (Brauer block) of a finite group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Definition 2.3(1) over any commutative ring is in Tau Ceti: `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent b` (a primitive idempotent of `Z(R[G])`), with existence of a complete family of block idempotents over an Artinian ring, uniqueness of complete families, transport along group isomorphisms, and the splitting `M = ⨁ e_B M` of modules with each indecomposable module in exactly one block (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block.lean`, `RingTheory/Idempotents/Module.lean`). This is ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2. What the papers use beyond it is not in code: the existence of blocks of `O[G]` for a complete discrete valuation ring and their bijection with the blocks of `k[G]` (Proposition 2.2), `p`-modular systems, `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)` (no Brauer characters), Brauer's central-character criterion and the principal block. These are targets of merged [RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RepresentationTheory/ModularCharactersAndBlocks/README.md) (PR #1775): the splitting `p`-modular system (§1.1), `existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue` (§1.3), the partitions `Irr(G) = ⊔ Irr(B)` and `IBr(G) = ⊔ IBr(B)`, `exists_blockIdempotent_iff_centralCharacter_congr` and `SameBlock` over the algebraic integers (§4.2), and the principal block (§4.3). The unipotent `ℓ`-blocks of finite reductive groups that the 2020 paper uses need Deligne–Lusztig theory, which no roadmap specifies.

## Mathlib
- Has: `MonoidAlgebra`, `Subalgebra.center`, `CompleteOrthogonalIdempotents`/`OrthogonalIdempotents` (`RingTheory/Idempotents.lean`), idempotent lifting along nilpotent/complete ideals, complete DVRs, `FDRep.character` and orthonormality of characters over alg. closed char-0 fields.
- Missing: p-modular systems, blocks, Brauer characters, decomposition matrices.

## Tau Ceti code
- Block idempotents (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2): `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent` (the native `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent` in `Subalgebra.center R (MonoidAlgebra R G)`, any commutative semiring `R`), `exists_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_isBlockIdempotent` (commutative Artinian `R`, finite `G`), `complete_blockIdempotents_unique` (any commutative ring), `IsBlockIdempotent.domCongr` (`RepresentationTheory/GroupAlgebra/Block.lean`); the module splitting `TauCeti.isInternal_smul_top` and `IsIndecomposableModule.existsUnique_forall_smul_eq_self` for a complete family of central idempotents (`RingTheory/Idempotents/Module.lean`). Over a complete discrete valuation ring `O` (not Artinian) the existence of a complete family of block idempotents of `O[G]` is not in code.
- Prerequisites: `TauCeti.IsPrimitiveIdempotent` (`RingTheory/Idempotents/Primitive/Basic.lean`) and `TauCeti.exists_completeOrthogonalIdempotents_isPrimitiveIdempotent` (`Primitive/Decomposition.lean`); complete-adic idempotent lifting `IsAdicComplete.exists_isIdempotentElem_eq` (`RingTheory/AdicCompletion/Idempotent.lean`); central characters of a group algebra `centralCharacter` (`RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/CentralCharacter.lean`); `TauCeti.ClassFunction`, `irreducibleCharacters` (`CharacterTable/{ClassFunction,Table}.lean`); `TauCeti.pPart`/`pFreePart` (`GroupTheory/OrderOfElement/PPart.lean`); Grothendieck-group `cartanMatrix` (`RepresentationTheory/GrothendieckGroup/CartanMatrix.lean`).
- No modular systems, lifting of blocks to `O[G]`, `Irr(B)`, Brauer characters or `IBr(B)` (grep `brauerCharacter|SameBlock|existsUnique_blockIdempotent_map_residue` empty). Open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2899 (§1.3: lifting block idempotents from `k[G]` to `O[G]` along a Henselian reduction) and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3502 (§1.2: the reduction class of a stable lattice is independent of the lattice).
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45249 (§0.2 the block algebra `TauCeti.IsBlockIdempotent.BlockAlgebra` and `TauCeti.algEquivPiBlockAlgebra : R[G] ≃ₐ[R] ∏_B e_B R[G]`).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Group algebras, centres, idempotents, complete DVRs: Mathlib.
2. Primitive idempotents, central characters, `Irr(G)`, complete-adic idempotent lifting: Tau Ceti.
3. Block idempotents of `R[G]`, complete families over Artinian `R`, uniqueness, splitting of modules: Tau Ceti (ModularCharactersAndBlocks §0.2).
4. Splitting p-modular system: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.1 (merged, not implemented).
5. Blocks of `O[G]` and the lifting bijection with blocks of `k[G]` (Proposition 2.2): ModularCharactersAndBlocks §1.3 (merged; open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2899).
6. `Irr(B)`, `IBr(B)`, `SameBlock` (Brauer's criterion), principal block: ModularCharactersAndBlocks §§4.2–4.3 (merged, not implemented), after Brauer characters (Layer 2).
7. Decomposition matrix of a block (Remark 3): ModularCharactersAndBlocks §2.4 and Layer 4 (merged).
8. Unipotent ℓ-blocks of finite reductive groups (paper 2020, 192(2), no. 7): needs Deligne–Lusztig unipotent characters, unplanned (CharacterTheory README lists Deligne–Lusztig theory as out of scope).

## Library-scale gap
None for the 2024 and 2025 papers: merged ModularCharactersAndBlocks (Webb, *A Course in Finite Group Representation Theory*, Ch. 12; Navarro, *Characters and Blocks of Finite Groups*) is the roadmap, with §0.2 in code and Layers 1–4 awaiting implementation. The 2020 Brunat–Dudas–Taylor paper additionally needs Deligne–Lusztig theory and unipotent characters of `G^F` (Digne–Michel, *Representations of Finite Groups of Lie Type*; Geck–Malle, *The Character Theory of Finite Groups of Lie Type*), a separate roadmap beside ReductiveGroups Layer 7a (finite fields, Frobenius) and RepresentationTheory/CharacterTheory.
