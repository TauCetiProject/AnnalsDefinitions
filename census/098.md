## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. For countable relational languages, `TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration L ℕ` is Mod_L(ω) with the product topology, Polish and standard Borel instances, the relabelling action of `Equiv.Perm ℕ` (Polish S∞), `isoSetoid = orbitRel`, `IsomorphismInvariant` and Borel `modelsOf φ`. Missing: languages with function symbols (the cited paper, Paolini–Shelah, works in the language of groups; the graph coding of function symbols exists only syntactically), joint continuity of the action, and López-Escobar. These are targets of open PR #1316.
