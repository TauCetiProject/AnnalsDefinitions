## Status
PARTIAL-CODE
Tau Ceti has the abstract effective weight-one Hodge structure (with its polarizations) on a lattice that carries a complex structure, and it has complex tori `E/L` with `H_1 = L`. What is missing is the abelian-variety side: analytification, `Λ = ker exp`, and the identification with `H^1(A(ℂ),ℚ)`. Merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli Layer A5 (§§A5.1, A5.2, A5.5, A5.7) specifies all of it, on the analytification of merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1.

## Mathlib
- No Hodge structures of any kind. `IsZLattice` exists (`Mathlib/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Basic.lean`), as do singular homology prerequisites and complex manifolds.
- There is no analytification of schemes, no Lie exponential for a general complex Lie group, and no abelian varieties.

## Tau Ceti code
- The abstract Hodge theory is complete (Completed/HodgeStructures):
  - `TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn` and `HodgeStructure` (`Geometry/Hodge/Structure.lean`);
  - `HodgeStructureOn.dual` (`Geometry/Hodge/Dual.lean`);
  - `IsPolarization` and `Polarization` (`Geometry/Hodge/Polarization.lean`).
- The weight-one model of Definition 12.2 exists abstractly:
  - `TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.latticeHodgeStructure` is the effective weight-one HS on a lattice `V` with complex structure `J` on `ℝ⊗V`, with `latticeHodgeStructure_piece_one/zero`, `latticeHodgeStructure_weilOperator` and the equivalence `latticeHodgeStructureEquiv` between complex structures and effective weight-one Hodge structures (`Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne/Lattice.lean`).
  - `IsRiemannForm` and `isRiemannForm_iff_isPolarization` (`Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne/RiemannForm.lean`).
  - `isPolarization_of_weilOperator_invariant_on_realPoints_of_pos` (`WeightOne/Polarization.lean`).
  - The standard principally polarized structure on `ℤ^{2g}` (`WeightOne/Standard.lean`).
- Complex tori:
  - `Submodule.instLieAddGroupOrbitRelQuotient` gives the compact complex Lie group `E/L`.
  - `Submodule.orbitRelQuotientHomologyEquiv` identifies `H_*(E/L,ℤ) = ⋀L`, with `π_1 = H_1 = L` (`AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/ZLattice.lean`).
  - `lieExp` (`Geometry/Lie/`).
- Abelian varieties: `AbelianVariety K` (`AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/Basic.lean`), with a tangent space (`AbelianVariety/TangentSpace.lean`).
- Hodge structures as Deligne-torus representations: `HodgeStructureOn.complexDeligneAction` and `deligneAction` (the actions of `S(ℂ)` and `S(ℝ)` on a pure Hodge structure, with `mem_piece_iff_complexDeligneAction_mu` and compatibility with Tate twists, duals and tensor products, `Geometry/Hodge/DeligneTorus.lean`).
- Dimension one only: the points of a complex elliptic curve form a compact Riemann surface embedded in `ℂℙ²` (`WeierstrassCurve.Projective.Point.instIsManifold`, `AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Projective/ComplexManifold.lean`) and a complex Lie group (`WeierstrassCurve.Projective.Point.instLieAddGroup`, `Projective/LieGroup.lean`), built from the Weierstrass equation, not from a general analytification.

  - the actual object `V_A = H^1(A(ℂ),ℚ)` with its Hodge structure;
  - `Q_L` built from `c_1(L)`.
- Nothing applies `latticeHodgeStructure` to the dual lattice of a complex torus.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Abstract polarized HS, weight one, from `(Λ,J)` and its dual. In Tau Ceti.
2. Complex torus `E/L`, with `H_1 = L`. In Tau Ceti (ComplexTori M1, done).
3. Abelian variety over `ℂ`. In Tau Ceti (`AbelianVariety`).
4. Scheme analytification `A^an` as a complex analytic space. Target in merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1 (not implemented).
5. `Λ = ker exp`, with `V/Λ ≅ A^an`. Target in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.1 (not implemented).
6. `V_A = Hom(Λ,ℚ)` with `F^1 = Hom_ℂ(T_0,ℂ)`, and a polarization from a Riemann form. Target in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §§A5.2, A5.3, A5.5 (open Tau Ceti PRs https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45228 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3788 for §A5.2). This step is a short assembly of items 1 and 5.
7. (Optional) Comparison with singular `H^1` and de Rham. Merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.7, using ComplexComparisonPartII §C5.1.
