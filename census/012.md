## Status
PARTIAL-CODE
Tau Ceti has the abstract effective weight-one Hodge structure (with its polarizations) on a lattice that carries a complex structure, and it has complex tori `E/L` with `H_1 = L`. What is missing is the abelian-variety side: analytification, `Λ = ker exp` for `A^an` (in code for a compact connected commutative complex Lie group), and the identification with `H^1(A(ℂ),ℚ)`. Merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli Layer A5 (§§A5.1, A5.2, A5.5, A5.7) specifies all of it, on the analytification of merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1.

## Mathlib
- No Hodge structures of any kind. `IsZLattice` exists (`Mathlib/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Basic.lean`), as do singular homology prerequisites and complex manifolds.
- There is no analytification of schemes, no Lie exponential for a general complex Lie group, and no abelian varieties.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Abstract polarized HS, weight one, from `(Λ,J)` and its dual. In Tau Ceti.
2. Complex torus `E/L`, with `H_1 = L`. In Tau Ceti (ComplexTori M1, done).
3. Abelian variety over `ℂ`. In Tau Ceti (`AbelianVariety`).
4. Scheme analytification `A^an` as a complex analytic space. Target in merged ComplexComparisonPartII §C-1.1 (not implemented).
5. `Λ = ker exp`, with `V/Λ ≅ A^an`. Target in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.1 (not implemented for `A^an`; its Lie-group part, `E ⧸ Λ ≃ G` for a compact connected commutative complex Lie group, in Tau Ceti, `TauCeti.mulInvariantExpQuotientKerDiffeomorph`; homomorphisms as lattice maps in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4352, `H₁ ≅ Λ` for a connected commutative Lie group in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4442, and `G[n] ≅ Λ ⧸ nΛ` in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4673).
6. `V_A = Hom(Λ,ℚ)` with `F^1 = Hom_ℂ(T_0,ℂ)`, and a polarization from a Riemann form. Target in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §§A5.2, A5.3, A5.5 (of §A5.2 the Riemann form API, the homological structure with its polarization `IsRiemannForm.homologicalPolarization` and the scaled dual form polarization `IsPolarization.scaledDualForm` in Tau Ceti; its Checks on the standard lattice in Tau Ceti; open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4537 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4264 for §A5.2). This step is a short assembly of items 1 and 5.
7. (Optional) Comparison with singular `H^1` and de Rham. Merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A5.7, using ComplexComparisonPartII §C5.1.
