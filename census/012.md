## Status
PARTIAL-CODE
Tau Ceti has the abstract effective weight-one Hodge structure (with its polarizations) on a lattice that carries a complex structure, and it has complex tori `E/L` with `H_1 = L`. What is missing is the abelian-variety side: analytification, `Λ = ker exp`, and the identification with `H^1(A(ℂ),ℚ)`. Open PR #849 §A5 specifies all of it.

## Mathlib
- No Hodge structures of any kind. `IsZLattice` exists (`Mathlib/Algebra/Module/ZLattice/Basic.lean`), as do singular homology prerequisites and complex manifolds.
- There is no analytification of schemes, no Lie exponential for a general complex Lie group, and no abelian varieties.

## Tau Ceti code
- The abstract Hodge theory is complete (Completed/HodgeStructures):
  - `TauCeti.Hodge.HodgeStructureOn` and `HodgeStructure` (`Geometry/Hodge/Structure.lean`);
  - `HodgeStructureOn.dual` (`Geometry/Hodge/Dual.lean`);
  - `IsPolarization` and `Polarization` (`Geometry/Hodge/Polarization.lean`).
- The weight-one model of Definition 12.2 exists abstractly:
  - `TauCeti.AlmostComplexStructure.latticeHodgeStructure` is the effective weight-one HS on a lattice `V` with complex structure `J` on `ℝ⊗V`, with `latticeHodgeStructure_piece_one/zero` and `latticeHodgeStructure_weilOperator` (`Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne/Lattice.lean`).
  - `IsRiemannForm` and `isRiemannForm_iff_isPolarization` (`Geometry/Hodge/WeightOne/RiemannForm.lean`).
  - `isPolarization_of_weilOperator_invariant_on_realPoints_of_pos` (`WeightOne/Polarization.lean`).
  - The standard principally polarized structure on `ℤ^{2g}` (`WeightOne/Standard.lean`).
- Complex tori:
  - `Submodule.instLieAddGroupOrbitRelQuotient` gives the compact complex Lie group `E/L`.
  - `Submodule.orbitRelQuotientHomologyEquiv` identifies `H_*(E/L,ℤ) = ⋀L`, with `π_1 = H_1 = L` (`AlgebraicTopology/Singular/Torus/ZLattice.lean`).
  - `lieExp` (`Geometry/Lie/`).
- Abelian varieties: `AbelianVariety K` (`AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/Basic.lean`), with a tangent space (`AbelianVariety/TangentSpace.lean`).
- Missing:
  - analytification `A^an`;
  - `Λ = ker(exp: Lie A → A^an)`;
  - the actual object `V_A = H^1(A(ℂ),ℚ)` with its Hodge structure;
  - `Q_L` built from `c_1(L)`.
- Nothing applies `latticeHodgeStructure` to the dual lattice of a complex torus.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Abstract polarized HS, weight one, from `(Λ,J)` and its dual. In Tau Ceti.
2. Complex torus `E/L`, with `H_1 = L`. In Tau Ceti (ComplexTori M1, done).
3. Abelian variety over `ℂ`. In Tau Ceti (`AbelianVariety`).
4. Scheme analytification `A^an` as a complex manifold. Target in PR #848 C-1.1 (unplanned in merged roadmaps).
5. `Λ = ker exp`, with `V/Λ ≅ A^an`. Target in PR #849 A5.1.
6. `V_A = Hom(Λ,ℚ)` with `F^1 = Hom_ℂ(T_0,ℂ)`, and a polarization from a Riemann form. Target in PR #849 A5.2 and A5.5. This step is a short assembly of items 1 and 5.
7. (Optional) Comparison with singular `H^1` and de Rham. PR #849 A5.7, using #848 C5.1.

## Library-scale gap
The gap is fully planned in PRs #848 and #849. It is the complex uniformization theory of abelian varieties (Mumford, *Abelian Varieties* Ch. I; Birkenhake–Lange Ch. 1–4), sitting on GAGA. The Tau Ceti linear-algebra side is already done.
