## Library-scale gap
A roadmap "Geometric wave and heat flows on ℝ⁴" (or "Energy-critical gauge theory") building: `𝔤`-valued Sobolev connections and gauge transformations on `ℝ^d`, homogeneous Sobolev and Besov spaces with homogeneous Littlewood–Paley theory, local and global well-posedness of the Yang–Mills heat flow, Uhlenbeck-type gauge fixing, the caloric gauge and its manifold structure, then hyperbolic Yang–Mills energy and the Threshold Theorem framework. It would extend PDE Layers 6–8 and sit beside FourManifolds (PR #936) Layer 5. References: Oh–Tataru (arXiv:1709.08599 and the Annals papers), Tao, *Nonlinear Dispersive Equations*, Donaldson–Kronheimer, *The Geometry of Four-Manifolds*, Ch. 2 & 4; Struwe's heat-flow papers.
