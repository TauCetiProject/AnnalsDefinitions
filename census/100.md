## Status
UNPLANNED. Lozenge tilings, height functions, DLR/Gibbs specifications and translation-invariant ergodic Gibbs measures of given slope are not targets anywhere. Mathlib supplies everything the definition needs (Cantor-type product space, conditional expectation, ergodicity of ℤ²-actions, perfect matchings), so it is writable directly, but no roadmap covers dimers or classical Gibbs measures.

## Mathlib
- No tilings/dimers, no DLR Gibbs measures, no height functions.
- Have: `SimpleGraph`, `SimpleGraph.Subgraph.IsPerfectMatching` (`Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Matching.lean`), product topology/σ-algebra on `E → Bool`, `ProbabilityMeasure`, conditional expectation `MeasureTheory.condExp` and `ProbabilityTheory.condDistrib`, `ErgodicSMul`/`ErgodicVAdd` (`Dynamics/Ergodic/Action/Basic.lean`), `Measure.map`, extreme points of invariant measures (`Dynamics/Ergodic/Extreme.lean`, for a single map).
- Nearest missing piece: the specification `μ(B | F_A) = |B∩Ω_A(M)|/|Ω_A(M)|` (uniform Gibbs property) as a predicate, and the slope via height functions.

## Tau Ceti code
- Absent (no lozenge/dimer/Gibbs measure in the classical sense). `TauCeti/Combinatorics/ChordDiagram.lean` and quantum Gibbs states are unrelated.

## Merged roadmaps
- Not a target. TensorProductCombinatorics (README ~l.1590) has finite puzzles as tilings by triangles/rhombi (Knutson-Tao puzzles), combinatorial only. OperatorAlgebraDynamics D9 has quantum translation-invariant states and the variational principle, not DLR measures on tilings. Completed/Exchangeability and Completed/DenseGraphLimits treat invariant random structures under permutations, not ℤ²-translations. The determinantal-process material in RandomMatrices and DysonBrownianMotion is for eigenvalue processes, not the dimer kernel.

## Open roadmap PRs
- None. #867 "Temperley-Lieb positivity, C*-towers and annular modules" has finite Potts-Fortuin-Kasteleyn partition functions (not the Kasteleyn dimer method).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Triangular lattice graph on ℤ², faces, lozenge tilings as perfect matchings of the hexagonal dual (100.1): Mathlib graph theory, unplanned as API.
2. Tiling space as closed subset of `{0,1}^{E}`, compact metrizable, shift action of ℤ²: Mathlib (product topology), unplanned as API.
3. Height function `H_M` (100.2): unplanned.
4. Gibbs (uniform DLR) property via conditional expectation (100.3.1): Mathlib `condExp`; unplanned.
5. Translation invariance and ergodicity under ℤ² (100.3.2): Mathlib `ErgodicVAdd`/`ErgodicSMul` (extreme-point equivalence for group actions: unplanned).
6. Slope and Sheffield's existence/uniqueness (100.3.3, 100.4); Kenyon-Okounkov-Sheffield local statistics and the determinantal kernel: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "DimersAndLozengeTilings" roadmap: Kasteleyn's theorem (Pfaffian/determinant counts of perfect matchings of planar graphs), height functions and Thurston's tileability criterion, MacMahon's boxed plane partition formula, the inverse Kasteleyn matrix and the translation-invariant ergodic Gibbs measures μ_{s,t} as determinantal measures with the incomplete-beta kernel, Sheffield's classification, Cohn-Kenyon-Propp variational principle and the arctic circle. Classical Gibbs/DLR formalism for discrete spin systems (specifications, extremal decomposition) would be a shared base with #99's lattice roadmap. Textbooks/sources: Kenyon, "Lectures on dimers" (IAS/Park City); Gorin, *Lectures on Random Lozenge Tilings*; Georgii, *Gibbs Measures and Phase Transitions*; Sheffield, *Random Surfaces*. It would sit beside RandomMatrices (determinantal kernels) and TensorProductCombinatorics (puzzle tilings).
