Merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.1 (`VanishingCycles.finiteCoefficientCohomology`: "Build cup products ...") targets cup products in étale cohomology of schemes; it is unimplemented. Open PR #1891 sharpens this to an explicit `ShiftedHom` definition. The rigid-analytic case that the paper needs is defined only in open PR #2271 (GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §1.1 "Cup products", for analytic étale and pro-étale sites and for schemes, through EnhancedDerivedSheaves' `ClassicalDerived.tensorHom`); PRs #875 and #1549 only use it. Flag: the merged target covers schemes only.

- `ShiftedHom`, derived categories, monoidal sheaves (`CategoryTheory/Sites/Monoidal.lean`).
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.smallEtaleTopology` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale.lean`); pro-étale `EllAdicCohomology`.
- No cup product on sheaf cohomology.

## Tau Ceti code
- Cup products of cochains along a diagonal (`TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Monoidal/Cup.lean`), used for the singular cup product.
- Galois-cohomology cup products (e.g. `AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/`).
- Étale module sheaves (ECF §F.0.1, partial): stalks at geometric points `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.etaleStalk`, their exactness and joint conservativity (`shortExact_map_etaleStalk`, `isIso_of_forall_isIso_etaleStalk`, `exact_iff_forall_exact_map_etaleStalk`), and finite lisse sheaves `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.IsFiniteLisse` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/ModuleSheaf.lean`).
- Missing: a cup product on `Sheaf.H` or on étale cohomology of schemes or adic spaces; constructible sheaves and derived tensor products of étale sheaves.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #1891 "EtaleCohomologyFoundations: definitions aligned with the Weil-conjectures design", "Classes as shifted morphisms, cup products with supports and purity": `Hⁿ(X,K) = ShiftedHom Λ_X K n`; "Define the cup product `Hᵃ(X,K) × Hᵇ(X,L) → Hᵃ⁺ᵇ(X,K⊗L)`" with a Koszul-sign check.

- #1040 "Étale duality and perverse sheaves" has `cupProductTracePairing`.
- #2271 "GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology: roadmap" §1.1 defines `x ∪ y` for `x ∈ H^i(Y, p^{-1}M)`, `y ∈ H^j(Y, p^{-1}N)` as a composite of shifted morphisms through `p^{-1}M ⊗^L_Λ p^{-1}N`, for commutative `Λ`, on analytic étale sites (AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6), analytic pro-étale sites and schemes, and proves its Galois equivariance (§1.3(d), §1.4); on schemes with finite coefficients it is identified with ECF §F.1's cup product.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Sheaf cohomology on sites as Ext, `ShiftedHom`, derived categories. In Mathlib.
2. Derived tensor products of `Λ`-module sheaves on a site. Target in merged EnhancedDerivedSheaves §§0.3–0.4 (`tensorHom`).
3. Étale site of schemes (Mathlib) and of adic spaces (merged AdicEtaleGeometry A1, partial).
4. Cup product on `RΓ` for schemes. Target in merged ECF §F.1 (unimplemented); explicit form in PR #1891.
5. Cup product for étale sheaves on rigid-analytic spaces. Target in open PR #2271 §1.1, on AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6 and EnhancedDerivedSheaves §§0.1–0.4; no merged roadmap. Used in PR #1549 §5.4.
6. Galois equivariance and the perfect pairing. PR #1549 §5.4.

## Library-scale gap
Small. Open PR #2271 §1.1 states the cup product uniformly for analytic étale, pro-étale and scheme étale sites; once it merges, the rigid case is a merged target. A single statement for ringed sites (Stacks Tag 0B6C, "Cup product" in Cohomology on Sites) in EnhancedDerivedSheaves would serve both. References: SGA 4 XVII; Stacks Project, Cohomology on Sites.
