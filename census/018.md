## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.1 (`VanishingCycles.finiteCoefficientCohomology`: "Build cup products ...") targets cup products in étale cohomology of schemes; it is unimplemented. Open PR #1891 sharpens this to an explicit `ShiftedHom` definition. The rigid-analytic case that the paper needs is only used, not defined, in open PRs #875 and #1549. The construction is site-general, but no roadmap states it for adic spaces. Flag: the merged target covers schemes only.

- Missing: a cup product on `Sheaf.H` or on étale cohomology of schemes or adic spaces. Étale module sheaves are not started (ECF STATUS, 2026-10-23).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Sheaf cohomology on sites as Ext, `ShiftedHom`, derived categories. In Mathlib.
2. Derived tensor products of `Λ`-module sheaves on a site. Target in merged EnhancedDerivedSheaves §§0.3–0.4 (`tensorHom`).
3. Étale site of schemes (Mathlib) and of adic spaces (merged AdicEtaleGeometry A1, partial).
4. Cup product on `RΓ` for schemes. Target in merged ECF §F.1 (unimplemented); explicit form in PR #1891.
5. Cup product for étale sheaves on rigid-analytic spaces. Not an explicit target; it would come from the site-general construction applied to PR #875 Layer 1 cohomology. Used in PR #1549 §5.4.
6. Galois equivariance and the perfect pairing. PR #1549 §5.4.

## Library-scale gap
Small. The right fix is to state the cup product once for ringed sites (Stacks Tag 0B6C, "Cup product" in Cohomology on Sites), in EnhancedDerivedSheaves or ECF §F.1, and specialize it to adic spaces in PR #875's Layer 1. References: SGA 4 XVII; Stacks Project, Cohomology on Sites.
