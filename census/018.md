## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations targets cup products in étale cohomology of schemes: §F.1 (`VanishingCycles.finiteCoefficientCohomology`: "Build cup products ...") and §0.9 "Classes as shifted morphisms, cup products with supports and purity", which defines `Hⁿ(X,K) = ShiftedHom Λ_X K n` and the cup product `Hᵃ(X,K) × Hᵇ(X,L) → Hᵃ⁺ᵇ(X,K⊗L)` with a Koszul-sign check. The rigid-analytic case that the paper needs is defined in merged [GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/README.md) §1.1, bullet "Cup products" (README l.204): `x ∪ y` as a composite of shifted morphisms through `p^{-1}M ⊗^L_Λ p^{-1}N`, for commutative `Λ`, uniformly on analytic étale sites (AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6), analytic pro-étale sites and schemes, through EnhancedDerivedSheaves' `ClassicalDerived.tensorHom`, with its Galois equivariance (§1.3(d), §1.4). None of these is implemented; Tau Ceti has only the cochain-level Čech cup product `cechCup`. Merged ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology and open PR #1549 only use the cup product, and merged EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheaves §0.5 uses the scheme cup product in `cupProductTracePairing`.

## Mathlib
- `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H` (Ext-based sheaf cohomology, `CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Basic.lean`), with long exact sequences and Mayer–Vietoris.
- `ShiftedHom`, derived categories, monoidal sheaves (`CategoryTheory/Sites/Monoidal.lean`).
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.smallEtaleTopology` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale.lean`); pro-étale `EllAdicCohomology`.
- No cup product on sheaf cohomology.

## Tau Ceti code
- Cup products of cochains along a diagonal (`TauCeti/Algebra/Homology/Monoidal/Cup.lean`), used for the singular cup product.
- Galois-cohomology cup products (e.g. `AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/WeilPairing/`).
- The cup product of Čech cochains of a presheaf of `R`-algebras on a category with finite products, `TauCeti.CategoryTheory.cechCup` on `cechAlgebraComplex U F`, with the Leibniz rule `d_cechCup`, associativity `cechCup_assoc`, the unit `cechOne`, and naturality for refinements and for morphisms of presheaves (`cechComplexMap_cechCup`, `cechComplexFunctor_map_cechCup`) (`CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Cech/Cup.lean`). It is cochain-level: no product on cohomology, no graded commutativity, and no comparison with `Sheaf.H`.
- Étale module sheaves (ECF §F.0.1, partial): stalks at geometric points `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.etaleStalk`, their exactness and joint conservativity (`shortExact_map_etaleStalk`, `isIso_of_forall_isIso_etaleStalk`, `exact_iff_forall_exact_map_etaleStalk`), and finite lisse sheaves `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.IsFiniteLisse` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/ModuleSheaf.lean`).
- The (underived) tensor product of étale sheaves of `Λ`-modules: the symmetric monoidal structure `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.monoidalCategorySheafModuleCat`, `symmetricCategorySheafModuleCat`, compatibility of sheafification with tensor products `isMonoidal_W_smallEtaleTopology`, and monoidal stalks `monoidalEtaleStalk` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Etale/Monoidal.lean`); localization of the small étale site at an étale `U` (`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Etale.overEquivalence`, `sheafRestrict`, `Sites/Etale/Localization.lean`).
- Missing: a cup product on `Sheaf.H` (or on Čech cohomology) or on étale cohomology of schemes or adic spaces; constructible sheaves and derived tensor products of étale sheaves.
