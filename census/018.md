## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged EtaleCohomologyFoundations targets cup products in étale cohomology of schemes: §F.1 (`VanishingCycles.finiteCoefficientCohomology`: "Build cup products ...") and §0.9 "Classes as shifted morphisms, cup products with supports and purity", which defines `Hⁿ(X,K) = ShiftedHom Λ_X K n` and the cup product `Hᵃ(X,K) × Hᵇ(X,L) → Hᵃ⁺ᵇ(X,K⊗L)` with a Koszul-sign check. The rigid-analytic case that the paper needs is defined in merged [GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology/README.md) §1.1, bullet "Cup products" (README l.112): `x ∪ y` as a composite of shifted morphisms through `p^{-1}M ⊗^L_Λ p^{-1}N`, for commutative `Λ`, uniformly on analytic étale sites (AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6), analytic pro-étale sites and schemes, through EnhancedDerivedSheaves' `ClassicalDerived.tensorHom`, with its Galois equivariance (§1.3(d), §1.4). None of these is implemented; Tau Ceti has only the Čech cup product of a fixed family, on cochains (`cechCup`) and on its Čech cohomology (`cechCupHomology`), and its colimit over a directed system of families (`cechCohomologyCup`). Merged ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology and [ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomologyPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomologyPartII/README.md) only use the cup product, and merged EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheaves §0.5 uses the scheme cup product in `cupProductTracePairing`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Sheaf cohomology on sites as Ext, `ShiftedHom`, derived categories. In Mathlib.
2. Derived tensor products of `Λ`-module sheaves on a site. Target in merged EnhancedDerivedSheaves §§0.3–0.4 (`tensorHom`); chain-level currying into the internal Hom complex and the derived tensor–internal Hom adjunction of a closed braided monoidal abelian category (instantiated on `D(𝒪_X)`, not on étale sheaves) in Tau Ceti, `curryInternalHom`, `derivedTensorInternalHomAdj`.
3. Étale site of schemes (Mathlib) and of adic spaces (merged AdicEtaleGeometry A1, partial).
4. Cup product on `RΓ` for schemes. Target in merged ECF §F.1 and, as a `ShiftedHom` construction, §0.9 (unimplemented).
5. Cup product for étale sheaves on rigid-analytic spaces. Target in merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §1.1 (l.112), on AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6 and EnhancedDerivedSheaves §§0.1–0.4 (unimplemented). Used in merged ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomologyPartII §5.4.
6. Galois equivariance and the perfect pairing. Merged ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomologyPartII §5.4 (unimplemented).

## Library-scale gap
Small. Merged GaloisActionOnEtaleCohomology §1.1 states the cup product uniformly for analytic étale, pro-étale and scheme étale sites, so the work is implementation of its suppliers (EnhancedDerivedSheaves §§0.1–0.4, AdicEtaleGeometry A1.6). A single statement for ringed sites (Stacks Tag 0B6C, "Cup product" in Cohomology on Sites) in EnhancedDerivedSheaves would serve both. References: SGA 4 XVII; Stacks Project, Cohomology on Sites.
