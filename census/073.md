## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
`centralCharacter` of an automorphic representation of a general connected reductive group, together with `HasCentralCharacter(Z,χ,f)` and `centralTranslationFinite`, is a target of merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §3.2. The local central character of an irreducible smooth representation (73.3(c)) is a target of merged SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.3a.1, and `Z_G(𝔸_F)` and the split centre `A_G` of merged AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.1.3 and AA.2.1. The central character is not in code; of its inputs, `Z_G(𝔸_F)` is (`TauCeti.AdelicPoints.centerPoints`, AA.1.3), `A_G` and the local Schur lemma are not.
