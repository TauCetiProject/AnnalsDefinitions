## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 specify B, S, normal connection, principal curvatures and mean curvature for immersions into a Riemannian manifold; only the flat-ambient (Euclidean, parametrized) second fundamental form and shape operator exist in Tau Ceti code.

## Mathlib
No second fundamental form, shape operator or mean curvature. Prerequisites present: `IsImmersion`/`IsImmersionAt` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion.lean`), `Bundle.RiemannianMetric`, `IsRiemannianManifold`, `CovariantDerivative` and Levi-Civita (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/{Basic,LeviCivita}.lean`), pullback bundles (`VectorBundle/Pullback.lean`). Missing: pullback connection along a map, normal bundle of an immersion.

## Tau Ceti code
Flat-ambient special case only: `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`, normal part of D²i for a map into an inner product space) and `TauCeti.shapeOperator`, `inner_fderiv_shapeOperator_apply` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.lean`); Gauss formula `CovariantDerivative.IsLeviCivitaConnection.inner_apply_eq_inner_mvfderiv`. No Riemannian-ambient B/S/H, no principal curvatures, no convexity notions.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5a ("Immersion geometry and first variation"): pullback ambient connection, tangent/normal splitting, B, S, normal connection, Gauss/Codazzi/Ricci. Target, not implemented (STATUS: R5a has flat-ambient groundwork only).
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: principal curvatures as eigenvalues of S_ν for a chosen unit normal, H = tr B / m, minimality H = 0; identification of R5a's intrinsic B,S with the Euclidean `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm`. Not implemented.
- Consumers: GeometricCurrents G6/G7 (mean-convex barriers), PositiveScalarCurvature, AffineMorseLefschetz 2.1.
- Convention clash to note: R5a.2 defines H = (tr B)/m (averaged, vector-valued); def #49 uses H = tr S (unnormalised scalar). Convexity/k-convexity of 49.4(6) is not a target anywhere.

## Open roadmap PRs
PR #946 (Calibrated geometry) and PR #956 (Riemannian positive mass) consume R5a/R5a.2; no new target.

## Prerequisite chain
Riemannian metric, Levi-Civita [Mathlib] -> immersion [Mathlib `IsImmersion`] -> pullback bundle [Mathlib] -> pullback connection, normal bundle [RiemannianGeometry R5a, not implemented; R5a builds the pullback ambient Levi-Civita connection itself and uses SmoothBundlesConnections B2.1 only for pullback/Hom bundles, since B2 has no connection content] -> B, S, ∇^⊥ [R5a, not implemented; Euclidean case in Tau Ceti] -> principal curvatures, H [R5a.2, not implemented] -> k-convexity / two-convexity [unplanned, trivial once eigenvalues exist].

## Library-scale gap
Covered by RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 once implemented; the only unplanned pieces are the convexity notions (k-convex, uniformly two-convex) used in mean curvature flow, a few lines on top of principal curvatures. A mean curvature flow roadmap (Mantegazza, *Lecture Notes on Mean Curvature Flow*; Ecker) would naturally sit beside RiemannianGeometry R5.
