## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 specify B, S, normal connection, principal curvatures and mean curvature for immersions into a Riemannian manifold, and R7.9 two-sidedness of closed embedded hypersurfaces; merged RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §§1.4–1.5, 2.1 specifies the boundary case with strict convexity. Tau Ceti code has the flat-ambient case (second fundamental form and shape operator of a map from a manifold into an inner product space, with the Gauss equation), the smooth tangent and normal projections and the orthogonal normal bundle of an immersion into a Riemannian manifold, covariant differentiation along maps with its metric compatibility and the pullback connection `CovariantDerivative.pullback`, the vector-valued `B`, `S` and `∇⊥` of a map into a manifold with a metric and a covariant derivative, with tensoriality, symmetry, the Weingarten equation, self-adjointness of `S` and metric compatibility of `∇⊥` (RiemannianGeometry R5a), and for the boundary `Σ = ∂M` the special case of 49.4(1)–(3) and (5): the boundary tangent space, the outward and inward unit normals, boundary defining functions, the boundary second fundamental form `II` with its smoothness, naturality and conformal change law, the Weingarten map, principal curvatures and mean curvature `TauCeti.boundaryWeingarten`, `boundaryPrincipalCurvatures`, `boundaryMeanCurvature` with their conformal change laws (RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §§1.1–1.6), and strict convexity, convexity and strict concavity of the boundary `TauCeti.IsStrictlyConvexAt`, `IsConvexAt`, `IsStrictlyConcaveAt`, `IsStrictlyConvexBoundary`, with their characterisation by the signs of the principal curvatures (§2.1), under conformal change (§2.1) and by the Hessian of any boundary defining function (§2.2). For an immersion into a Riemannian manifold, the pointwise shape operator `S_ν`, the ordered principal curvatures and the mean curvature vector `H = (tr B)/m` are in code (`TauCeti.immersionShapeOperatorAt`, `immersionPrincipalCurvatures`, `immersionMeanCurvatureVector`, RiemannianGeometry R5a.2), so 49.4(1), (3) and (5) are in code pointwise for hypersurfaces, up to the sign of `ν` and the normalization `H = tr S` (`inner_immersionMeanCurvatureVector`, `trace_immersionShapeOperatorAt`); `H` is a smooth normal field along a `C^{k+2}` immersion (`TauCeti.contMDiff_immersionMeanCurvatureVector`); the k-convexity and two-convexity of 49.4(6) are not in code, so the status is PARTIAL-CODE.

## Library-scale gap
Covered by RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2, whose pointwise principal curvatures and mean curvature vector are in code; the only unplanned pieces are the convexity notions (k-convex, uniformly two-convex) used in mean curvature flow, a few lines on top of principal curvatures. A mean curvature flow roadmap (Mantegazza, *Lecture Notes on Mean Curvature Flow*; Ecker) would naturally sit beside RiemannianGeometry R5.
