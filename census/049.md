## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 specify B, S, normal connection, principal curvatures and mean curvature for immersions into a Riemannian manifold, and R7.9 two-sidedness of closed embedded hypersurfaces; merged RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §§1.4–1.5, 2.1 specifies the boundary case with strict convexity. Tau Ceti code has the flat-ambient case (second fundamental form and shape operator of a map from a manifold into an inner product space), the smooth tangent and normal projections and the orthogonal normal bundle of an immersion into a Riemannian manifold, and covariant differentiation along maps, not B, S or H for a Riemannian ambient.

## Mathlib
No second fundamental form, shape operator or mean curvature. Prerequisites present: `IsImmersion`/`IsImmersionAt` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion.lean`), `Bundle.RiemannianMetric`, `IsRiemannianManifold`, `CovariantDerivative` and Levi-Civita (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/{Basic,LeviCivita}.lean`), pullback bundles (`VectorBundle/Pullback.lean`). Missing: pullback connection along a map, normal bundle of an immersion.

## Tau Ceti code
- Flat ambient: `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`, normal part of D²i for a map into an inner product space) and `TauCeti.shapeOperator`, `inner_fderiv_shapeOperator_apply` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.lean`); on a manifold source, `TauCeti.manifoldSecondFundamentalForm` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`: values in `normalSubspace`, symmetry and chart independence for `C²` maps) and `TauCeti.manifoldShapeOperator` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/ShapeOperator.lean`: Weingarten identity, self-adjointness for the induced metric, reparametrization invariance, focal points and Milnor's index formula for squared distance).
- Riemannian ambient groundwork: smooth tangent and normal projections along immersions into a Riemannian manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Projection.lean`), the orthogonal normal bundle `ν_f` of a `C^n` immersion as a `C^m` subbundle of `f *ᵖ TN` (`TauCeti.orthogonalNormalSpace`, `TauCeti.orthogonalNormalContMDiffVectorBundle`, `Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/NormalBundle.lean`), covariant differentiation of sections along a map `CovariantDerivative.alongMap`, agreeing with pullback of ambient fields (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/AlongMap/Basic.lean`, R5a's pullback ambient connection prerequisite), the Gauss formula `CovariantDerivative.IsLeviCivitaConnection.inner_apply_eq_inner_mvfderiv`, embedding normal spaces (`Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/NormalSpace/`), the pullback metric along immersions (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Pullback/Basic.lean`).
- Missing: Riemannian-ambient B/S/∇⊥/H, principal curvatures as a named spectrum, convexity notions.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5a ("Immersion geometry and first variation"): pullback ambient connection, tangent/normal splitting, B, S, normal connection, Gauss/Codazzi/Ricci. Target, not implemented beyond the flat-ambient case and the projections.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: principal curvatures as eigenvalues of S_ν for a chosen unit normal, H = tr B / m, minimality H = 0; identification of R5a's intrinsic B,S with the Euclidean `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm`. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R7.9 ("Separating and two-sided hypersurfaces"): two-sidedness of a closed embedded hypersurface as existence of a unit normal field, the unit normal double cover, and separation. Not implemented.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md) Conventions: the scalar second fundamental form A of a hypersurface for a unit normal and `|A|² = |II|²`; MS10.1 (Reilly's formula) uses `H = tr A`, the sum of principal curvatures.
- Consumers: [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §5.5 (totally geodesic submanifolds, `B = 0` in the sense of R5a), GeometricCurrents G6/G7 (mean-convex barriers), [PositiveMass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PositiveMass/README.md) Layer 3 and [CalibratedGeometry](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/CalibratedGeometry/README.md) (both through R5a/R5b), PositiveScalarCurvature, AffineMorseLefschetz 2.1, [BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices/README.md) (#2425; its supplier contract, l.92, takes R5a's `B` with "totally geodesic" meaning `B = 0`, for §3.2 (l.347) totally geodesic complex submanifolds of the ball and §12.1 (l.910) totally geodesic subvarieties of ball quotients; it specifies no second fundamental form of its own).
- Convention clash to note: R5a.2 defines H = (tr B)/m (averaged, vector-valued); def #49 uses H = tr S (unnormalised scalar). k-convexity and two-convexity of 49.4(6) are not targets anywhere.
- [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary/README.md): §1.4 the scalar second fundamental form `II` of `∂M` (outward-normal convention, `II = h` on the unit ball), §1.5 the Weingarten map, principal curvatures as its eigenvalues and the averaged mean curvature `H = tr S / n`, §1.6 the conformal change law, §2.1 the convexity predicates `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, `IsStrictlyConvexBoundary` (strict convexity iff all principal curvatures are positive), §3.6 the identification of `II` with R5a's `B` of `∂M`. This is the boundary case of 49.1–49.3 with strict convexity, not k-convexity or two-convexity. Not implemented.
- [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md) §5.1 uses the second fundamental form `B(X, Y) = (∇_X Y)^⊥` and averaged mean curvature of `C²` submanifolds of Euclidean space (R5a's `B`, R5a.2's `H` for smooth ones), with the first variation `δ[Σ](X) = −k ∫ ⟨H_Σ, X⟩`.

## Prerequisite chain
Riemannian metric, Levi-Civita [Mathlib] -> immersion [Mathlib `IsImmersion`] -> pullback bundle [Mathlib] -> tangent and normal projections, normal bundle, covariant derivative along maps [Tau Ceti] -> pullback connection [RiemannianGeometry R5a, not implemented; R5a builds the pullback ambient Levi-Civita connection itself and uses SmoothBundlesConnections B2.1 only for pullback/Hom bundles, since B2 has no connection content] -> B, S, ∇^⊥ [R5a, not implemented; Euclidean case in Tau Ceti] -> principal curvatures, H [R5a.2, not implemented] -> strict convexity of a boundary [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §2.1, merged, not implemented] -> k-convexity / two-convexity [unplanned, trivial once eigenvalues exist].

## Library-scale gap
Covered by RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 once implemented; the only unplanned pieces are the convexity notions (k-convex, uniformly two-convex) used in mean curvature flow, a few lines on top of principal curvatures. A mean curvature flow roadmap (Mantegazza, *Lecture Notes on Mean Curvature Flow*; Ecker) would naturally sit beside RiemannianGeometry R5.
