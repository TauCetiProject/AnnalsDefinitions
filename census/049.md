## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 specify B, S, normal connection, principal curvatures and mean curvature for immersions into a Riemannian manifold, and R7.9 two-sidedness of closed embedded hypersurfaces; Tau Ceti code has the flat-ambient case (second fundamental form and shape operator of a map from a manifold into an inner product space) and the smooth tangent and normal projections along immersions into a Riemannian manifold, not B, S or H for a Riemannian ambient.

## Mathlib
No second fundamental form, shape operator or mean curvature. Prerequisites present: `IsImmersion`/`IsImmersionAt` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion.lean`), `Bundle.RiemannianMetric`, `IsRiemannianManifold`, `CovariantDerivative` and Levi-Civita (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CovariantDerivative/{Basic,LeviCivita}.lean`), pullback bundles (`VectorBundle/Pullback.lean`). Missing: pullback connection along a map, normal bundle of an immersion.

## Tau Ceti code
- Flat ambient: `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`, normal part of D²i for a map into an inner product space) and `TauCeti.shapeOperator`, `inner_fderiv_shapeOperator_apply` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/ShapeOperator.lean`); on a manifold source, `TauCeti.manifoldSecondFundamentalForm` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`: values in `normalSubspace`, symmetry and chart independence for `C²` maps) and `TauCeti.manifoldShapeOperator` (`Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/ShapeOperator.lean`: Weingarten identity, self-adjointness for the induced metric, reparametrization invariance, focal points and Milnor's index formula for squared distance).
- Riemannian ambient groundwork: smooth tangent and normal projections along immersions into a Riemannian manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Projection.lean`), the Gauss formula `CovariantDerivative.IsLeviCivitaConnection.inner_apply_eq_inner_mvfderiv`, embedding normal spaces (`Geometry/Manifold/SmoothEmbedding/NormalSpace/`), the pullback metric along immersions (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Pullback/Basic.lean`).
- Missing: Riemannian-ambient B/S/∇⊥/H, principal curvatures as a named spectrum, convexity notions.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry R5a ("Immersion geometry and first variation"): pullback ambient connection, tangent/normal splitting, B, S, normal connection, Gauss/Codazzi/Ricci. Target, not implemented beyond the flat-ambient case and the projections.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a.2: principal curvatures as eigenvalues of S_ν for a chosen unit normal, H = tr B / m, minimality H = 0; identification of R5a's intrinsic B,S with the Euclidean `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm`. Not implemented.
- RiemannianGeometry R7.9 ("Separating and two-sided hypersurfaces"): two-sidedness of a closed embedded hypersurface as existence of a unit normal field, the unit normal double cover, and separation. Not implemented.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md) Conventions: the scalar second fundamental form A of a hypersurface for a unit normal and `|A|² = |II|²`; MS10.1 (Reilly's formula) uses `H = tr A`, the sum of principal curvatures.
- Consumers: GeometricCurrents G6/G7 (mean-convex barriers), [PositiveMass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PositiveMass/README.md) Layer 3 and [CalibratedGeometry](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/CalibratedGeometry/README.md) (both through R5a/R5b), PositiveScalarCurvature, AffineMorseLefschetz 2.1.
- Convention clash to note: R5a.2 defines H = (tr B)/m (averaged, vector-valued); def #49 uses H = tr S (unnormalised scalar). Convexity/k-convexity of 49.4(6) is not a target anywhere.

## Open roadmap PRs
None specific. PR #2229 (RealSemisimpleLieGroups) and PR #2232 (LorentzianGeometry) consume R5a.

## Prerequisite chain
Riemannian metric, Levi-Civita [Mathlib] -> immersion [Mathlib `IsImmersion`] -> pullback bundle [Mathlib] -> tangent and normal projections [Tau Ceti] -> pullback connection, normal bundle [RiemannianGeometry R5a, not implemented; R5a builds the pullback ambient Levi-Civita connection itself and uses SmoothBundlesConnections B2.1 only for pullback/Hom bundles, since B2 has no connection content] -> B, S, ∇^⊥ [R5a, not implemented; Euclidean case in Tau Ceti] -> principal curvatures, H [R5a.2, not implemented] -> k-convexity / two-convexity [unplanned, trivial once eigenvalues exist].

## Library-scale gap
Covered by RiemannianGeometry R5a/R5a.2 once implemented; the only unplanned pieces are the convexity notions (k-convex, uniformly two-convex) used in mean curvature flow, a few lines on top of principal curvatures. A mean curvature flow roadmap (Mantegazza, *Lecture Notes on Mean Curvature Flow*; Ecker) would naturally sit beside RiemannianGeometry R5.
