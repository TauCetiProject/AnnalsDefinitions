# 11. Split classical groups Sp₂ₙ, SO_l and general spin groups GSpin_l

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has `Sp₂ₘ` as a smooth reductive group scheme over any commutative ring, with a standard pinning, so Definition 11.1(1) is formalized up to the choice of Gram matrix. It also has the orthogonal group scheme of an arbitrary constant matrix (`TauCeti.ConstantForm`) and `SOₙ` for the identity form `XXᵀ = 1`, which is split only when `−1` is a square; the split `SO_l` of 11.1(2) and `GSpin_l` are not in code. Merged [ReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ReductiveGroups/README.md) §§9B.5–9B.8 (merged PR #2530) specify the whole definition as group schemes over every commutative ring: §9B.5 transports the pinning of `Sp₂ₘ` to the Gram matrix `J_{Sp₂ₙ}` of 11.1(1) (diagonal torus, upper triangular Borel) and defines `GSp₂ₘ`; §9B.6 defines `O(q)`, `SO(q)` (through the Dickson invariant in even rank, so in every characteristic), `GO(q)`, `GSO(q)` of a nondegenerate quadratic module, proves smoothness and reductivity, and identifies `SO(q_l)` with `{g ∈ SL_l : ᵗg J_l g = J_l}` of 11.1(2) over `ℤ[1/2]`, with torus, Borel and the pinned models of types `Bₙ` and `Dₙ`; §9B.7 defines `GPin`, `GSpin`, `Spin` with the Clifford norm `ν` (Proposition 11.3(1)), the exact sequences `1 → 𝔾_m → GSpin → SO → 1` and `1 → μ₂ → Spin → SO → 1` (11.3(2)), `(𝔾_m × Spin)/μ₂ ≅ GSpin` (11.3(3)), surjectivity of `GSpin(F) → SO(F)` by Hilbert 90 (Remark 2), and the split torus with the based root datum of Definition 11.2 (`e₀∨` central, `ν = Υ`, `GSpin_l/e₀∨(𝔾_m) ≅ SO_l`, 11.3(4)), identifying `GSpin_l` with the pinned model of that datum; §9B.8 gives the low-rank isomorphisms of Remark 3 and the dualities of based root data behind Remark 1's table, whose dual groups and `r_G` are ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5. Not implemented. Open Tau Ceti PRs cover first parts of each section: §9B.5 `GSp₂ₘ` as a group scheme with multiplier (https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45420) and `Sp₂ₘ` for any Gram matrix congruent to `Jₘ` with the transported pinning (https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3702); §9B.6 the half-discriminant nondegeneracy convention over a ring (https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3723), the scheme `O(q)` of a quadratic form (https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45418), and the split `SO_l` of 11.1(2) as the group scheme `{g ∈ SL_l : g J_l gᵀ = J_l}` over every commutative ring with its diagonal torus (https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3710); §9B.7 `GPin(Q)`, `GSpin(Q)`, `Pin(Q)`, `Spin(Q)` with `ρ` and `ν` on points over a ring (https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45439), the rational points `GSpin(Q)` over a field with `e₀∨` and the exact sequence on points (https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2348), and the torus `c ∏ u_i(a_i)` in the Clifford algebra with Clifford norm `c² ∏ a_i` (https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3720); §§9B.5, 9B.7–9B.8 the based root data of `GSpin_l` (Definition 11.2, with `ε₀∨` central and `Υ`), `GSp₂ₙ` and `GSO₂ₙ` and their dualities (https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3716). None of these is a `GSpin` group scheme or proves smoothness or reductivity of the orthogonal and spin groups.

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: ReductiveGroups §§9B.5–9B.8 specify the classical split groups as group schemes over every commutative ring, beyond the characteristic-not-two field setting of the text, and RG2.5 the dual groups. What remains is implementation: open PRs start the definitions as closed subgroup schemes, the split torus in the Clifford algebra and the root data, while the Dickson invariant, the `GSpin` scheme, the exact sequences and the pinned comparisons wait on the pinned models of ReductiveGroups Layer 9 and the inputs listed in step 5 of the chain. Not targets of §§9B.5–9B.8: unitary groups, non-split quasi-split orthogonal groups and integral models of `GSpin` for non-unimodular lattices (README l.37–40). Reference: Knus–Merkurjev–Rost–Tignol, *The Book of Involutions*, §§12–13 and 23; Conrad, *Reductive Group Schemes*, Appendix C; Asgari, *Local L-functions for split spinor groups*.
