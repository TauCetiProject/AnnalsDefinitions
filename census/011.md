# 11. Split classical groups Sp₂ₙ, SO_l and general spin groups GSpin_l

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has `Sp₂ₘ` as a smooth reductive group scheme over any commutative ring, with a standard pinning, so Definition 11.1(1) is formalized up to the choice of Gram matrix, with the pinning transported to any Gram matrix congruent to `Jₘ` (`ConstantForm.groupSchemeCongr`, `constantFormPinning`). It also has the orthogonal group scheme of an arbitrary constant matrix (`TauCeti.ConstantForm`), the orthogonal group scheme `O(q)` of a quadratic form on `Rⁿ` over any commutative ring, correct in characteristic two (`TauCeti.QuadraticOrthogonal.groupScheme`), and `SOₙ` for the identity form `XXᵀ = 1`, which is split only when `−1` is a square; the split `SO_l` of 11.1(2) is in code as the group scheme `{g ∈ SL_l : g J_l gᵀ = J_l}` over every commutative ring with its diagonal torus, upper triangular subgroup and root subgroups (`TauCeti.AntidiagonalSpecialOrthogonal`), smooth and isomorphic to `SO_l` over rings containing `1/2` and `i`, and reductive over every field of characteristic not two, without Borel maximality or pinning, and `GSpin_l` is in code only as a group of points, over any commutative ring in every characteristic (`CliffordAlgebra.specialCliffordGroup`, with the Clifford group `cliffordGroup`, their norms and `Pin`, `Spin` as the kernels) and over a field (`CliffordAlgebra.gSpinGroup`, with the exact sequence on points), not as a group scheme. Merged [ReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ReductiveGroups/README.md) §§9B.5–9B.8 (merged PR #2530) specify the whole definition as group schemes over every commutative ring: §9B.5 transports the pinning of `Sp₂ₘ` to the Gram matrix `J_{Sp₂ₙ}` of 11.1(1) (diagonal torus, upper triangular Borel) and defines `GSp₂ₘ`; §9B.6 defines `O(q)`, `SO(q)` (through the Dickson invariant in even rank, so in every characteristic), `GO(q)`, `GSO(q)` of a nondegenerate quadratic module, proves smoothness and reductivity, and identifies `SO(q_l)` with `{g ∈ SL_l : ᵗg J_l g = J_l}` of 11.1(2) over `ℤ[1/2]`, with torus, Borel and the pinned models of types `Bₙ` and `Dₙ`; §9B.7 defines `GPin`, `GSpin`, `Spin` with the Clifford norm `ν` (Proposition 11.3(1)), the exact sequences `1 → 𝔾_m → GSpin → SO → 1` and `1 → μ₂ → Spin → SO → 1` (11.3(2)), `(𝔾_m × Spin)/μ₂ ≅ GSpin` (11.3(3)), surjectivity of `GSpin(F) → SO(F)` by Hilbert 90 (Remark 2), and the split torus with the based root datum of Definition 11.2 (`e₀∨` central, `ν = Υ`, `GSpin_l/e₀∨(𝔾_m) ≅ SO_l`, 11.3(4)), identifying `GSpin_l` with the pinned model of that datum; §9B.8 gives the low-rank isomorphisms of Remark 3 and the dualities of based root data behind Remark 1's table, whose dual groups and `r_G` are ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5. Of these sections the transported pinning of §9B.5, the split `SO_l` and the scheme `O(q)` of §9B.6, the torus `c ∏ u_i(a_i)` in the Clifford algebra with Clifford norm `c² ∏ a_i` of §9B.7 (`CliffordAlgebra.hyperbolicTorus`), the based root datum of Definition 11.2 with those of `GSp₂ₙ`, `GSO₂ₙ`, `SO_{2n+1}` and the dualities of §9B.8 (`gspinOddRootDatum`, `gspinEvenRootDatum`), the even Clifford algebra `C₀(q₃) ≅ M₂(R)` over any ring for `GSpin₃ ≅ GL₂` (`splitOddOneEvenEquivMatrix`) and the half-discriminant nondegeneracy convention over a ring (`QuadraticForm.IsDiscrNondegenerate`) are in code (above). Also in code: the explicit signed permutation for `J_{Sp₂ₙ}` with the transported pinning `Symplectic.antidiagPinning` and the polynomial presentation of `O(Sp₂ₘ)` (§9B.5); the isomorphism `SO_l^J ≅ SO_l` with the identity form over rings containing `1/2` and `i`, natural in the base ring, giving smoothness there, and reductivity of `SO_l^J` over fields of characteristic not two, and the upper triangular subgroup and root subgroups of `SO_l^J` (§9B.6); the rational points `GSpin(Q)` over a field with `e₀∨`, `ν` and the exact sequence on points (§9B.7, `CliffordAlgebra.gSpinGroup`); `C₀` of the split quaternary form as `M₂(R) × M₂(R)` (§9B.8). `GSp₂ₘ` is in code as a group scheme with multiplier over any commutative ring (§9B.5, `TauCeti.SymplecticSimilitude.groupScheme`), as are base change of the group scheme `O(q)` and naturality of its transport along isometries, and the similitude group scheme `GO(q)` of a quadratic form on `Rⁿ` with its multiplier, `O(q)` the kernel on points (§9B.6, `TauCeti.QuadraticSimilitude.groupScheme`). The first part of §9B.7 is in code: `GPin(Q)`, `GSpin(Q)`, `Pin(Q)`, `Spin(Q)` with `ρ` and `ν` on points over any commutative ring (`CliffordAlgebra.cliffordGroup`, `specialCliffordGroup`, `cliffordGroupNorm`, `specialCliffordGroupNorm`, `normOnePinGroup`, `normOneSpinGroup`, `cliffordGroupToOrthogonal`). So is the functoriality of `GPin`, `GSpin`, `Pin`, `Spin` along quadratic isometry equivalences (`QuadraticMap.IsometryEquiv.specialCliffordGroupEquiv`). So are, on points, the scalars and the hyperbolic torus in `GSpin(Q)` with `ν(c ∏ u_i(a_i)) = c² ∏ a_i` (`CliffordAlgebra.scalarSpecialCliffordGroup`, `specialHyperbolicTorus`) and `GSpin(Q)`, `Spin(Q)` in ranks zero to two. None of these is a `GSpin` group scheme or proves smoothness or reductivity of the orthogonal and spin groups in general.

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: ReductiveGroups §§9B.5–9B.8 specify the classical split groups as group schemes over every commutative ring, beyond the characteristic-not-two field setting of the text, and RG2.5 the dual groups. What remains is implementation: the split torus in the Clifford algebra, `GSpin` on points over a field, `SO_l^J` with its upper triangular and root subgroups and the root data are in code, `O(q)`, `GO(q)` and `GSp₂ₘ` are in code as closed subgroup schemes, while the Dickson invariant, the `GSpin` scheme, the exact sequences and the pinned comparisons wait on the pinned models of ReductiveGroups Layer 9 and the inputs listed in step 5 of the chain. Not targets of §§9B.5–9B.8: unitary groups, non-split quasi-split orthogonal groups and integral models of `GSpin` for non-unimodular lattices (README l.37–40). Reference: Knus–Merkurjev–Rost–Tignol, *The Book of Involutions*, §§12–13 and 23; Conrad, *Reductive Group Schemes*, Appendix C; Asgari, *Local L-functions for split spinor groups*.
