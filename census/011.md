# 11. Split classical groups Sp₂ₙ, SO_l and general spin groups GSpin_l

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has `Sp₂ₘ` as a smooth reductive group scheme over any commutative ring, with a standard pinning, so Definition 11.1(1) is formalized up to the choice of Gram matrix. It also has the orthogonal group scheme of an arbitrary constant matrix (`TauCeti.ConstantForm`) and `SOₙ` for the identity form `XXᵀ = 1`, which is split only when `−1` is a square; the split `SO_l` of 11.1(2) and `GSpin_l` are not in code. Merged [ReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ReductiveGroups/README.md) §§9B.5–9B.8 (merged PR #2530) specify the whole definition as group schemes over every commutative ring: §9B.5 transports the pinning of `Sp₂ₘ` to the Gram matrix `J_{Sp₂ₙ}` of 11.1(1) (diagonal torus, upper triangular Borel) and defines `GSp₂ₘ`; §9B.6 defines `O(q)`, `SO(q)` (through the Dickson invariant in even rank, so in every characteristic), `GO(q)`, `GSO(q)` of a nondegenerate quadratic module, proves smoothness and reductivity, and identifies `SO(q_l)` with `{g ∈ SL_l : ᵗg J_l g = J_l}` of 11.1(2) over `ℤ[1/2]`, with torus, Borel and the pinned models of types `Bₙ` and `Dₙ`; §9B.7 defines `GPin`, `GSpin`, `Spin` with the Clifford norm `ν` (Proposition 11.3(1)), the exact sequences `1 → 𝔾_m → GSpin → SO → 1` and `1 → μ₂ → Spin → SO → 1` (11.3(2)), `(𝔾_m × Spin)/μ₂ ≅ GSpin` (11.3(3)), surjectivity of `GSpin(F) → SO(F)` by Hilbert 90 (Remark 2), and the split torus with the based root datum of Definition 11.2 (`e₀∨` central, `ν = Υ`, `GSpin_l/e₀∨(𝔾_m) ≅ SO_l`, 11.3(4)), identifying `GSpin_l` with the pinned model of that datum; §9B.8 gives the low-rank isomorphisms of Remark 3 and the dualities of based root data behind Remark 1's table, whose dual groups and `r_G` are ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5. Not implemented. The rational points `GSpin(Q)` as even Lipschitz elements, with `e₀∨`, the Clifford norm and the exact sequence on points over a field, are in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2348.

## Mathlib
- Has: `Matrix.symplecticGroup` (`LinearAlgebra/SymplecticGroup.lean`), `Matrix.specialOrthogonalGroup`, `Matrix.orthogonalGroup` (`LinearAlgebra/UnitaryGroup.lean`; groups of matrices, not schemes), `CliffordAlgebra` with `involute`, `reverse`, even subalgebra, `lipschitzGroup`, `pinGroup`, `spinGroup` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/SpinGroup.lean`), `RootPairing`.
- Missing: affine group schemes, reductive groups, GSpin, dual groups.

## Tau Ceti code
- `Sp₂ₘ`: `TauCeti.Symplectic.definingHopfIdeal`, points `TauCeti.GLSymplecticFin`, smoothness, connectedness, reductivity, semisimplicity, centre, diagonal torus, root subgroups, `Symplectic.standardPinning` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Symplectic/*`).
- `SOₙ` (identity form): `Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/SpecialOrthogonal/*` (smooth, connected, `reductiveCommHopfAlgProperty_finiteTypeCoordinateHopfAlgebra`, semisimple, adjoint for odd `n`, centre, `SO₂` as a split torus when `√−1 ∈ R`). General constant forms: `TauCeti.ConstantForm` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/ConstantForm/Basic.lean`), so `O(J_l)` exists; the `det = 1` cut and split torus/pinning for `J_l` do not.
- Clifford side (points over a field): `CliffordAlgebra.mem_lipschitzGroup_iff_involute_act_ι_mem_range_ι` (Lipschitz = Clifford group, `LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/CliffordGroup.lean`), spinor norm and `Spin → SO` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinorNorm/*`), low-rank `Spin` comparisons (`Spin/LowRank/{Four,Five}.lean`). Pin groups and the normalized Clifford full cover of the orthogonal group (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Lipschitz/FullCover/*`); comparison of `O(Q)`, `SO(Q)` with the matrix groups along an isometry (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/Matrix.lean`). No GSpin group or scheme (grep `GSpin|gSpinGroup` empty). Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2348 (`CliffordAlgebra.gSpinGroup`, the even elements of Mathlib's `lipschitzGroup`, with the central cocharacter `gSpinScalarUnits`, the Clifford norm `gSpinNorm` with kernel Spin, and the vector action `gSpinToSpecialOrthogonal`, surjective over a field with kernel the image of `e₀∨`: the exact sequence `1 → Kˣ → GSpin(Q) → SO(Q) → 1` on rational points; group of points, not a group scheme).
- Root data, pinnings and their transport: `TauCeti.Pinning`, `Pinning.comapOfIso` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Pinning/*`); Chevalley data `Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Chevalley/*`, including the braid and Coxeter relations of the Weyl representatives (`Chevalley/Braid/{Basic,DoubleBond,TripleBond,Coxeter}.lean`).

2. Affine group schemes over rings, `GLₙ`, Hopf-ideal subgroups, `ConstantForm`, `SLₙ`: Tau Ceti.
3. `Sp₂ₙ` as a split reductive group with pinning: Tau Ceti (done).
4. Root data and split data with pinnings, pinned Chevalley–Demazure models and their comparisons: ReductiveGroups Layers 7, 8 and 9 (§9B, §§9B.2–9B.3) (merged, partial).
5. Inputs of §9B.6: ordinary quadratic forms with their étale-local normal form and the étale centre of the even Clifford algebra, LefschetzPencilsAndVanishingCycles §§2.1–2.2 (merged; not in code); the local fibral flatness criterion, DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebra §3.3 (merged). Inputs of §9B.7: fppf quotients by central diagonalizable subgroups, ReductiveGroups Layers 3–4 (Layer 3 partial); Hilbert 90, SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.11 (merged, partial).
6. `Sp₂ₙ` for `J_{Sp₂ₙ}`, `GSp₂ₙ`, `O(q)`, `SO(q)` with the Dickson invariant, `SO_l` of `J_l`, `GPin`, `GSpin`, `Spin`, `ν`, the exact sequences, the root datum of Definition 11.2 and the low-rank isomorphisms: ReductiveGroups §§9B.5–9B.8 (merged, not implemented). The points of `GSpin(Q)` with `e₀∨` and the exact sequence on points over a field: open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/2348.
7. Dual groups and `r_G` (Remark 1): ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5 (merged, partial), from the dualities of §9B.8.

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: ReductiveGroups §§9B.5–9B.8 specify the classical split groups as group schemes over every commutative ring, beyond the characteristic-not-two field setting of the text, and RG2.5 the dual groups. What remains is implementation, after the pinned models of ReductiveGroups Layer 9. Not targets of §§9B.5–9B.8: unitary groups, non-split quasi-split orthogonal groups and integral models of `GSpin` for non-unimodular lattices (README l.37–40). Reference: Knus–Merkurjev–Rost–Tignol, *The Book of Involutions*, §§12–13 and 23; Conrad, *Reductive Group Schemes*, Appendix C; Asgari, *Local L-functions for split spinor groups*.
