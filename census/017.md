## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Open PR #2489 "SchemeAndStackFoundations: cohomologically affine morphisms" adds §1.22a to SchemeAndStackFoundations, which specifies Definitions 17.2 and 17.3 at the stated generality: *Stacks.IsCohomologicallyAffine* `f` (quasi-compact, quasi-separated, and *QCoh.pushforward* `f` preserves epimorphisms of `Stacks.QCoh`), the absolute version through `Γ(X, −)`, and *Groups.IsLinearlyReductive* `G` (`BG → S` cohomologically affine) for flat finitely presented group schemes over a base, with *Groups.isLinearlyReductive_iff* identifying it over a field with Tau Ceti's `linearlyReductiveAffineGroupSchemeProperty`. It also states Proposition 17.1's flat base change (*QCoh.flatBaseChange*) and 17.4(a)–(b) (affine morphisms, Serre's criterion, descent and base change), and recasts §1.23's `IsTame` through it. 17.4(c) is left out (higher direct images on stacks are not constructed), and 17.4(d) is owned by ReductiveGroups. No merged roadmap specifies the definition: merged SSF §1.22 gives `Stacks.QCoh` and `QCoh.pushforward`, and §1.23 only the special case `IsTame`. Good moduli spaces and the Luna slice theorem are specified nowhere. Linear reductivity in comodule form, and exactness of invariants on arbitrary comodules, are in Tau Ceti.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Linearly reductive affine group (comodule form). In Tau Ceti.
2. Algebraic stacks; qc and qs morphisms of stacks. Target in merged SSF §1.19–1.20 (partial: `IsRepresentableBySpaces` of §1.19 is in Tau Ceti, `AlgebraicGeometry/Stack/Representable.lean`; algebraic stacks and the qc and qs morphisms of stacks are not implemented).
3. `Stacks.QCoh`, `QCoh.pushforward` for qcqs `f`, flat base change. Target in merged SSF §1.22 (unimplemented).
4. Cohomologically affine morphism (`f` qcqs and `QCoh.pushforward f` exact). Target of PR #2489 §1.22a (*Stacks.IsCohomologicallyAffine*); it generalizes merged SSF §1.23 `IsTame`.
5. "`G` linearly reductive ⇔ `BG → Spec k` cohomologically affine", via `QCoh(BG) ≃ Comod`. Target of PR #2489 §1.22a (*Groups.isLinearlyReductive_iff*); the comodule-side exactness is in Tau Ceti (`Coalgebra.isLinearlyReductive_iff_forall_fixedMap_surjective_of_surjective`, `Comodule.Hom.fixedMap_surjective_of_isLinearlyReductive`).
6. Good moduli spaces, Alper's properties, Luna slice (the paper's theorems). Unplanned; the slice theorem's local data (`N_x`, `G_x`, the tangent comparison) are targets of merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes Layer 7 (unimplemented).

## Library-scale gap
Cohomologically affine morphisms and linear reductivity over a base are specified in PR #2489 (SSF §1.22a). Unplanned: a "good moduli spaces" layer after it, with
- good and adequate moduli spaces, their universality and the uniqueness of closed points in fibres;
- `[Spec A/G] → Spec A^G`;
- Alper–Hall–Rydh local structure (Luna étale slice).
