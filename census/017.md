## Prerequisite chain
1. Linearly reductive affine group (comodule form). In Tau Ceti.
2. Algebraic stacks; qc and qs morphisms of stacks. Target in merged SSF §1.19–1.20 (unimplemented).
3. `Stacks.QCoh`, `QCoh.pushforward` for qcqs `f`, flat base change. Target in merged SSF §1.22 (unimplemented).
4. Cohomologically affine morphism (`f` qcqs and `QCoh.pushforward f` exact). Unplanned; it generalizes SSF §1.23 `IsTame`.
5. "`G` linearly reductive ⇔ `BG → Spec k` cohomologically affine", via `QCoh(BG) ≃ Comod`. Unplanned; the analogue `IsTame.classifying_iff` is in SSF §1.23.
6. Good moduli spaces, Alper's properties, Luna slice (the paper's theorems). Unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "good moduli spaces" layer would extend SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.22–1.23. Contents:
- cohomologically affine morphisms and their base-change and descent properties;
- Serre's criterion for algebraic spaces;
- good and adequate moduli spaces, their universality and the uniqueness of closed points in fibres;
- `[Spec A/G] → Spec A^G`;
- Alper–Hall–Rydh local structure (Luna étale slice).

References: Alper, *Good moduli spaces for Artin stacks*; Alper–Hall–Rydh (the Annals paper); Alper, *Stacks and Moduli* notes.
