# 82. The quasi-split unitary group of a self-dual hermitian lattice

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #966 "PELModuli: Siegel and PEL moduli problems" covers the general construction. Its M0.5 `IntegralPELDatum` is an order with involution and a lattice with an alternating `ℤ`-valued form, and M0.7 builds the Hopf algebra over `ℤ` of `O`-linear similitudes `G` and its isometry subgroup `G₁`. With `O = O_F`, complex conjugation and a self-dual `L`, `G₁` is exactly Def. 82.4. The specific quasi-split lattice `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` and Prop. 82.5(d)(e) (split-prime `GL_{2n}` isomorphism, hyperspecial at unramified `p`) are not stated targets. Tau Ceti already has the "closed subgroup of `GL_n` preserving a constant matrix" group scheme, which is the main prerequisite.

## Mathlib
- Has: CM fields (`NumberField.IsCMField`), `differentIdeal`, trace dual (`Submodule.traceDual`, `FractionalIdeal.dual`), bilinear/sesquilinear forms, `LinearMap.Nondegenerate`, `Submodule.IsLattice`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup` (over a star ring, not as a group scheme).
- Missing: hermitian lattices and their duals, unitary groups as affine group schemes, Weil restriction of group schemes.

## Tau Ceti code
- `ConstantForm` group scheme over any commutative ring: the closed subgroup of `GL n` with points `{M : M C Mᵀ = C}` for an arbitrary constant matrix `C` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/ConstantForm/Basic.lean`), specialised to `TauCeti.Orthogonal` and `TauCeti.Symplectic` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Symplectic/Scheme.lean`). Def. 82.4 is the intersection of such a group (`Φ` the Gram matrix of the trace form) with the centraliser of `O_F`; that intersection is not built.
- Weil restriction of Hopf algebras (`TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction`), point topology and compact-open integral points (`isOpenEmbedding_integralPoints`), used for 82.5(a)(c).
- Missing: hermitian lattices, unitary group schemes, the quasi-split lattice.

## Merged roadmaps
- ReductiveGroupsPartII: quasi-split `SU_3` for the antidiagonal hermitian form (the unitary-rank-one example), Weil restriction (RG2.0a, partial) and hyperspecial/parahoric group schemes (RG2.x, untouched). These cover the local field part, not the integral group of a global lattice.
- ReductiveGroups: reductive group schemes, closed subgroups of `GL`.
- No merged roadmap defines unitary groups of hermitian lattices over `ℤ`.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #966 "PELModuli: Siegel and PEL moduli problems": M0.5 `IntegralPELDatum`, M0.6 `p`-integral data with lattices "self-dual at p", M0.7 `IntegralPELDatum.coordinate`, the closed subgroup scheme `G ⊆ GL(L) × G_m` and `PELDatum.isometryGroup = G₁` with its own coordinate Hopf algebra. It records `G_ℚ = GU(V)`, `G₁ = U(V)` for hermitian data, and M5 `unitaryRationalDatum` for CM fields. This is the general Def. 82.4 (and the similitude group Caraiani–Scholze actually use).
- #851 "Geometry of numbers, quadratic forms and homogeneous arithmetic", §2.7: `IntegralHermitianLattice.ofCarrier`, `.dual`, self-duality and invariants (Def. 82.2 over a Dedekind domain with involution).
- #1756 "AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactorsPartII", §0.0: unitary and `GU` groups of hermitian forms over quadratic étale algebras (field level). #1404 "GanGrossPrasadUnitaryGroups" asks ReductiveGroupsPartII for a `gap:hermitian-unitary`.
- #871 "Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces" uses quasi-split `U(n,n)` with Siegel parabolic (ALS.4) but does not build the integral model.

## Prerequisite chain
1. CM fields, inverse different, trace dual, forms: Mathlib.
2. Group scheme preserving a constant form, Weil restriction, integral points as compact opens: Tau Ceti.
3. Hermitian lattices, duals, self-duality: target in PR #851 §2.7.
4. Integral group scheme of `O`-linear isometries of a lattice with alternating trace form: target in PR #966 M0.7 (`isometryGroup`).
5. The standard quasi-split self-dual lattice `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` and `G(ℝ) ≅ ∏ U(n,n)`: unplanned (a Check-sized addition to #966 M5).
6. 82.5(d) split-prime isomorphism `G(ℤ_p) ≅ ∏ GL_{2n}(O_{F_w})`, 82.5(e) reductive/hyperspecial at unramified `p`: unplanned. Hyperspecial subgroups are a target of ReductiveGroupsPartII (merged, not implemented).

## Library-scale gap
Small. Add to #966 (or a unitary section of ReductiveGroupsPartII) the standard quasi-split hermitian datum over a CM field with its self-dual lattice, the archimedean signature `(n,n)` computation, and the split/unramified local structure theorems: the isotropic decomposition `L_𝔭 ⊕ L_𝔭̄` giving `GL_{2n}`, and smoothness and reductivity of `G_{ℤ_p}` for `p` unramified. References: Kottwitz, *Points on some Shimura varieties over finite fields* §5; Lan, *Arithmetic compactifications of PEL-type Shimura varieties* Ch. 1.
