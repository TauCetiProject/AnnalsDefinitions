# 82. The quasi-split unitary group of a self-dual hermitian lattice

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration/README.md) (PR #1090) §0.3 "Quasi-split unitary similitude data" states the paper's datum verbatim: `V = F^{2n}` with the antidiagonal skew-hermitian form and its trace form (82.1), an `O_F`-lattice `L` self-dual for the trace form (82.2), with the Check that `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` is self-dual (82.3), the similitude group scheme `G` over `ℤ` with `G(R) = {(g, c) ∈ GL_{O_F}(L)(R) × 𝔾_m(R) : (gv, gw) = c(v, w)}` and the unitary group `G⁰ = ker c` (Def. 82.4, the paper's `G⁰`), the matrix description over `ℚ`-algebras (82.5(a)) and the PELModuli M0 datum; §0.4 gives the split-prime isomorphisms `G(ℚ_q) ≅ ∏_{w|𝔮} GL_{2n}(F_w) × ℚ_q^×`, `G(ℤ_q) ≅ ∏ GL_{2n}(O_{F_w}) × ℤ_q^×` with `G⁰(ℚ_q)` the product of the `GL_{2n}(F_w)` (82.5(d)) and the Hecke algebras the paper computes with them. Merged [PELModuli](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PELModuli/README.md) supplies the general construction: M0.4 `SymplecticOLattice.dual` (the trace-form dual, `IsSelfDualAt`), M0.7 the similitude group and `PELDatum.isometryGroup = G₁` over `ℤ`, M5.5 unitary data of prescribed signature, and M0.20(3) `G(ℚ_p)`-transitivity on self-dual lattices at good primes (the lattice independence in 82.5(e)). For the unitary group `G⁰`, merged [HermitianFormsAndLattices](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/HermitianFormsAndLattices/README.md) §4.6 gives the reductive integral model of the stabiliser of a lattice unimodular at a place of `F⁺` unramified or split in `F`, the unitary part of 82.5(e) at `p` unramified in `F`. Reductivity of the similitude group `G_{ℤ_p}` and hyperspeciality (82.5(e)) are not stated targets; IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration §0.4 assumes hyperspeciality outside `S` through the choice of `S`. Nothing in code beyond the prerequisites: Tau Ceti has the "closed subgroup of `GL_n` preserving a constant matrix" group scheme, not its intersection with the centraliser of `O_F`.

## Mathlib
- Has: CM fields (`NumberField.IsCMField`), `differentIdeal`, trace dual (`Submodule.traceDual`, `FractionalIdeal.dual`), bilinear/sesquilinear forms, `LinearMap.Nondegenerate`, `Submodule.IsLattice`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup` (over a star ring, not as a group scheme).
- Missing: hermitian lattices and their duals, unitary groups as affine group schemes, Weil restriction of group schemes.

## Tau Ceti code
- `ConstantForm` group scheme over any commutative ring: the closed subgroup of `GL n` with points `{M : M C Mᵀ = C}` for an arbitrary constant matrix `C` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/ConstantForm/Basic.lean`), specialised to `TauCeti.Orthogonal` and `TauCeti.Symplectic` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Symplectic/Scheme.lean`). Def. 82.4 is the intersection of such a group (`Φ` the Gram matrix of the trace form) with the centraliser of `O_F`; that intersection is not built.
- Weil restriction of Hopf algebras (`TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction`), point topology and compact-open integral points (`isOpenEmbedding_integralPoints`), used for 82.5(a)(c).
- `Matrix.specialIsometryGroup Q σ` (`LinearAlgebra/Matrix/SpecialLinearGroup/Isometry/Basic.lean`): the determinant-one matrices over a commutative ring preserving a `σ`-sesquilinear Gram matrix, a special unitary group `SU(Q)` for an involution `σ` and Hermitian `Q`, as a subgroup of `SpecialLinearGroup` (points only, no group scheme, no non-special `U`); its elementary subgroup `Matrix.elementaryUnitaryGroup` with transitivity on isotropic points over fields (`isPretransitive_elementaryUnitaryGroup`, `.../Isometry/Elementary.lean`), used for finite simple groups.
- Missing: hermitian lattices, unitary group schemes, the quasi-split lattice. (The hermitian vector bundles over `O_K` of ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights in Tau Ceti, `TauCeti.HermitianVectorBundle` (`NumberTheory/Arakelov/HermitianVectorBundle.lean`), are metrized `O_K`-modules, not the hermitian lattices of 82.2.)
- Open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45455 (PELModuli M0.1 the field case of `center_classification` and `fixedCenter_totallyReal`: a positive involution of a number field is complex conjugation of a CM field or the identity of a totally real field, `TauCeti.NumberField.isTotallyReal_and_eq_id_or_isCMField_of_trace_mul_pos`).
- ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.3 preamble, the base fields of its hyperspecial and parahoric group schemes: `TauCeti.ModelField`, `ValuativeRel.isDiscreteValuationRing_integer`, the instance `ModelField.isDiscreteValuationRing`, and `TauCeti.IsNonarchimedeanLocalField.modelField` (`RingTheory/Valuation/ValuativeRel/ModelField.lean`, `NumberTheory/LocalField/ModelField.lean`); the group schemes are not in code. Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4453 (RG2.3 preamble, `Breve E` is a model field, `TauCeti.MaxUnramifiedCompletion.breveModelField`, `NumberTheory/LocalField/Unramified/ModelField.lean`).

## Prerequisite chain
1. CM fields, inverse different, trace dual, forms: Mathlib.
2. Group scheme preserving a constant form, Weil restriction, integral points as compact opens: Tau Ceti.
3. Symplectic `O`-lattices with the trace-form dual and self-duality (82.2): target of merged PELModuli M0.4, on M0.1 (not started; for M0.1 the star orders `TauCeti.StarOrder` are in code and the number-field case of the classification of positive involutions is in open Tau Ceti PR https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45455).
4. The unitary similitude group scheme `G` over `ℤ` of a self-dual `O_F`-lattice and `G⁰ = ker c` (82.4): merged IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration §0.3, on PELModuli M0 (integral PEL datum M0.5 and the similitude and isometry groups M0.7); not started.
5. The unitary datum over a CM field from a hermitian space of given signature: merged PELModuli M5.5 (not started). The standard quasi-split self-dual lattice `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` (82.3) and the signature `(n,n)` symmetric space (82.5(b)): merged IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration §0.3 (not started).
6. 82.5(d) split-prime isomorphism `G(ℤ_p) ≅ ∏ GL_{2n}(O_{F_w})`: merged IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration §0.4 (not started). 82.5(e): lattice independence at good primes is merged PELModuli M0.20(3); for the unitary group `G⁰`, the reductive model of a unimodular lattice at a place unramified or split in `F` is merged HermitianFormsAndLattices §4.6 (not started); reductivity of the similitude group `G_{ℤ_p}` and hyperspeciality at unramified `p` are unplanned. Hyperspecial subgroups are a target of ReductiveGroupsPartII (merged; only the base fields `ModelField` of RG2.3 are in code, with `Breve E` as a model field in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/4453).

## Library-scale gap
Small. The datum, the group scheme, the quasi-split lattice and the split-prime structure are planned in IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration §§0.3–0.4. The unitary part of 82.5(e), a reductive model of `G⁰` at unimodular places unramified in `F`, is HermitianFormsAndLattices §4.6. Unplanned: smoothness and reductivity of the similitude group `G_{ℤ_p}` for `p` unramified (hyperspeciality, 82.5(e)), a short addition to HermitianFormsAndLattices §4.6, PELModuli M0.20 or ReductiveGroupsPartII's hyperspecial group schemes. References: Kottwitz, *Points on some Shimura varieties over finite fields* §5; Lan, *Arithmetic compactifications of PEL-type Shimura varieties* Ch. 1.
