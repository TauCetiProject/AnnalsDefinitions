# 82. The quasi-split unitary group of a self-dual hermitian lattice

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [PELModuli](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PELModuli/README.md) covers the general construction. Its M0.4 `SymplecticOLattice` is an `O`-lattice with an alternating `ℤ(1)`-valued pairing, `b` and `b*` adjoint, and `SymplecticOLattice.dual` is `L^# = {x ∈ L ⊗ ℚ : ⟨x, L⟩ ⊆ ℤ(1)}`, the trace-form dual of Def. 82.2 (self-duality is `L^# = L`; `IsSelfDualAt p` locally). M0.5 `IntegralPELDatum` adds to such a lattice over a star-order the complex structure `h` (adjoint and positive), and M0.7 builds the Hopf algebra over `ℤ` of `O`-linear similitudes `G` and its isometry subgroup `PELDatum.isometryGroup = G₁` with its own coordinate algebra. With `O = O_F`, complex conjugation, a self-dual `L` and a choice of `h` (which exists for the signature `(n,n)` datum), `G₁` is exactly Def. 82.4. M5.5 `unitaryRationalDatum` builds the datum `ψ = Tr_{F/ℚ}(δH)` of a hermitian space of given signature over a CM field, with an integral version on an `O_F`-lattice; M0.20(3) gives `G(ℚ_p)`-transitivity on self-dual lattices at good primes (the lattice independence in 82.5(e)). The specific quasi-split lattice `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` (82.3), the split-prime `GL_{2n}` isomorphism (82.5(d)), and reductivity of `G_{ℤ_p}` and hyperspeciality at unramified `p` (82.5(e)) are not stated targets. Tau Ceti has the "closed subgroup of `GL_n` preserving a constant matrix" group scheme, the main prerequisite of M0.7.

## Mathlib
- Has: CM fields (`NumberField.IsCMField`), `differentIdeal`, trace dual (`Submodule.traceDual`, `FractionalIdeal.dual`), bilinear/sesquilinear forms, `LinearMap.Nondegenerate`, `Submodule.IsLattice`, `Matrix.unitaryGroup` (over a star ring, not as a group scheme).
- Missing: hermitian lattices and their duals, unitary groups as affine group schemes, Weil restriction of group schemes.

## Tau Ceti code
- `ConstantForm` group scheme over any commutative ring: the closed subgroup of `GL n` with points `{M : M C Mᵀ = C}` for an arbitrary constant matrix `C` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/ConstantForm/Basic.lean`), specialised to `TauCeti.Orthogonal` and `TauCeti.Symplectic` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Symplectic/Scheme.lean`). Def. 82.4 is the intersection of such a group (`Φ` the Gram matrix of the trace form) with the centraliser of `O_F`; that intersection is not built.
- Weil restriction of Hopf algebras (`TauCeti.Algebra.WeilRestriction`), point topology and compact-open integral points (`isOpenEmbedding_integralPoints`), used for 82.5(a)(c).
- `Matrix.specialIsometryGroup Q σ` (`LinearAlgebra/Matrix/SpecialLinearGroup/Isometry/Basic.lean`): the determinant-one matrices over a commutative ring preserving a `σ`-sesquilinear Gram matrix, a special unitary group `SU(Q)` for an involution `σ` and Hermitian `Q`, as a subgroup of `SpecialLinearGroup` (points only, no group scheme, no non-special `U`); its elementary subgroup `Matrix.elementaryUnitaryGroup` with transitivity on isotropic points over fields (`isPretransitive_elementaryUnitaryGroup`, `.../Isometry/Elementary.lean`), used for finite simple groups.
- Missing: hermitian lattices, unitary group schemes, the quasi-split lattice.

## Prerequisite chain
1. CM fields, inverse different, trace dual, forms: Mathlib.
2. Group scheme preserving a constant form, Weil restriction, integral points as compact opens: Tau Ceti.
3. Symplectic `O`-lattices with the trace-form dual and self-duality (82.2): target of merged PELModuli M0.4, on M0.1 (not started).
4. Integral PEL datum (adds `h`) and the integral group scheme of `O`-linear isometries `G₁` (82.4): targets of merged PELModuli M0.5, M0.7 (not started).
5. The unitary datum over a CM field from a hermitian space of given signature: merged PELModuli M5.5 (not started). The standard quasi-split self-dual lattice `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` (82.3) and `G(ℝ) ≅ ∏ U(n,n)` (82.5(b)): unplanned (a Check-sized addition to M5.6).
6. 82.5(d) split-prime isomorphism `G(ℤ_p) ≅ ∏ GL_{2n}(O_{F_w})`: unplanned. 82.5(e): lattice independence at good primes is merged PELModuli M0.20(3); reductivity of `G_{ℤ_p}` and hyperspeciality at unramified `p` are unplanned. Hyperspecial subgroups are a target of ReductiveGroupsPartII (merged, not implemented).

## Library-scale gap
Small. Add to PELModuli M5 (or a unitary section of ReductiveGroupsPartII) the standard quasi-split hermitian datum over a CM field with its self-dual lattice, the archimedean signature `(n,n)` computation, and the split/unramified local structure theorems: the isotropic decomposition `L_𝔭 ⊕ L_𝔭̄` giving `GL_{2n}`, and smoothness and reductivity of `G_{ℤ_p}` for `p` unramified.
