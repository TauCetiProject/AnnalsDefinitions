# 82. The quasi-split unitary group of a self-dual hermitian lattice

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration/README.md) (PR #1090) §0.3 "Quasi-split unitary similitude data" states the paper's datum verbatim: `V = F^{2n}` with the antidiagonal skew-hermitian form and its trace form (82.1), an `O_F`-lattice `L` self-dual for the trace form (82.2), with the Check that `O_F^n ⊕ (𝔡⁻¹)^n` is self-dual (82.3), the similitude group scheme `G` over `ℤ` with `G(R) = {(g, c) ∈ GL_{O_F}(L)(R) × 𝔾_m(R) : (gv, gw) = c(v, w)}` and the unitary group `G⁰ = ker c` (Def. 82.4, the paper's `G⁰`), the matrix description over `ℚ`-algebras (82.5(a)) and the PELModuli M0 datum; §0.4 gives the split-prime isomorphisms `G(ℚ_q) ≅ ∏_{w|𝔮} GL_{2n}(F_w) × ℚ_q^×`, `G(ℤ_q) ≅ ∏ GL_{2n}(O_{F_w}) × ℤ_q^×` with `G⁰(ℚ_q)` the product of the `GL_{2n}(F_w)` (82.5(d)) and the Hecke algebras the paper computes with them. Merged [PELModuli](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PELModuli/README.md) supplies the general construction: M0.4 `SymplecticOLattice.dual` (the trace-form dual, `IsSelfDualAt`), M0.7 the similitude group and `PELDatum.isometryGroup = G₁` over `ℤ`, M5.5 unitary data of prescribed signature, and M0.20(3) `G(ℚ_p)`-transitivity on self-dual lattices at good primes (the lattice independence in 82.5(e)). For the unitary group `G⁰`, merged [HermitianFormsAndLattices](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannHypothesisForDirichletLFunctions/HermitianFormsAndLattices/README.md) §4.6 gives the reductive integral model of the stabiliser of a lattice unimodular at a place of `F⁺` unramified or split in `F`, the unitary part of 82.5(e) at `p` unramified in `F`. Reductivity of the similitude group `G_{ℤ_p}` and hyperspeciality (82.5(e)) are not stated targets; IgusaVarietiesAndTorsionConcentration §0.4 assumes hyperspeciality outside `S` through the choice of `S`. Nothing in code beyond the prerequisites: Tau Ceti has the "closed subgroup of `GL_n` preserving a constant matrix" group scheme, not its intersection with the centraliser of `O_F`, and signed hermitian spaces over a field with the trace forms of skew-hermitian forms (`TauCeti.SignedHermitianSpace`, `SignedHermitianSpace.traceForm`), not hermitian lattices.
