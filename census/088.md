# 88. Weak functorial transfer to GL_N

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED, for split classical groups and the standard representation. Merged [AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactorsPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactorsPartII/README.md) (PR #1756) §10.1 "weak functorial transfer" (README l.1604) is CFK Definition 2.2: for split `Sp_{2n}`, `SO_{2n}`, `SO_{2n+1}`, `GSpin_{2n}`, `GSpin_{2n+1}` and the standard `r_G`, a weak transfer is an irreducible automorphic `Π` on `GL_N` with matching Satake classes at almost all unramified places and the `r_G`-image infinitesimal character at every archimedean place. This is exactly the generality of the cited paper (Remark 4). The general notion for an arbitrary L-representation `r : ᴸG → GL_N` (88.1–88.3 as stated) is only partly specified: merged AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors AL.4.1.1 defines the unramified Euler factor `det(1 − T r(t))` of a Satake class `t` for an arbitrary finite-dimensional `r` of the unramified L-group, but not the transferred local representation `r_*(π_v)` or the map `dr` on infinitesimal characters, and merged ModularityAndLanglandsExtensions §1.6 (PR #1180) defines lifts and weak lifts along algebraic `R : GL_n → GL_N`, the case `G = GL_n`. Borderline: the merged target is the definition at the paper's generality, not at the generality of Kevin Buzzard's text. §0.0 of the same roadmap needs MetaplecticAutomorphicForms §3.2a, merged with PR #1063. The L-group itself is a merged target (ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5), not implemented. Nothing is implemented.
