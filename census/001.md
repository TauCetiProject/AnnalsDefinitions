# 1. Real rank of a semisimple Lie group

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti defines the real rank of a real Lie algebra, `LieAlgebra.realRank`, as the supremum of `finrank ℝ 𝔞` over Cartan involutions `θ` and maximal abelian `𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭_θ`, with invariance under isomorphism and `realRank = 0` exactly when the Killing form is negative definite, given a Cartan involution (`Algebra/Lie/CartanInvolution/RealRank.lean`). That every maximal abelian `𝔞` has this dimension (conjugacy), the unconditional compactness criterion, additivity over simple ideals and the group version `groupRealRank` are not in code, so this is a borderline PARTIAL-CODE: the number exists, its independence of choices does not. Merged [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) specifies the real rank as a target: `realRank` of a real semisimple or reductive Lie algebra with its independence of choices, additivity over simple ideals, the compactness criterion and the comparison with the complex rank (§1.3), and `groupRealRank`, almost-simple and compact factors and higher rank for Lie groups (§2.1). It also covers the equivalent descriptions of Remark (1): restricted root system rank (§1.3), Borel–Tits `ℝ`-rank as the dimension of a maximal `ℝ`-split torus, with the conjugacy of maximal `ℝ`-split tori and the reductive count of Remark (3) (§2.4), rank of `G/K` as maximal flat dimension (§5.5), and split forms (§1.4). Of its Layer 0, the Cartan-involution predicate of a real Lie algebra and the Cartan decomposition are in code (`LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution`, §0.1), as are the Cartan involution of a complex Lie algebra viewed as real, the preservation of simple ideals and the compact and noncompact ideals of §0.3, the Cartan involution `X ↦ -Xᴴ` of a conjugate-transpose-closed matrix Lie algebra with trivial radical (applied to `𝔰𝔬(p, q)` and `𝔰𝔭`), `𝔲(p, q)` and `𝔰𝔲(p, q)`, the Cartan involutions `X ↦ -Xᴴ` of `𝔰𝔲(p, q)`, `𝔰𝔭(p, q)` and `𝔰𝔩_n(ℂ)`, `𝔰𝔬_n(ℂ)`, `𝔰𝔭_{2n}(ℂ)` viewed as real, the Killing forms of `𝔰𝔩_n`, `𝔰𝔲(p, q)`, `𝔰𝔬(p, q)` and `𝔰𝔭`, `𝔰𝔭(p, q)` and `𝔰𝔩₂(ℝ) ≅ 𝔰𝔬(2, 1)` with the `𝔰𝔬(2, 1)` Check (§§0.3–0.4), of §1.1 the maximal abelian subspaces of `𝔭`, their existence and the restricted root space decomposition (`LieEquiv.IsMaximalAbelianInP`, `Submodule.restrictedRootSpace`, `LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.isInternal_restrictedRootSpace`), and the first steps of §1.2, `𝔞 ≃ 𝔞*` (`LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.killingEquivDual`) and the `𝔰𝔩₂` triple of a restricted root with integrality (`LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.isSl2Triple_restrictedCoroot`, `exists_int_apply_restrictedCoroot`), and of §1.3 the definition `LieAlgebra.realRank` with its choice-free properties; the restricted root system, conjugacy, `finrank_eq_realRank` and `groupRealRank` are not. Its Cartan-involution and Iwasawa inputs are merged targets in RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layers 7 and 9, implemented only for matrix groups (`GL n`, `SL n`, `Sp(2n, ℝ)`) and, for Layer 7, up to the conjugate-linear involution `τ` of a Serre presentation.

## Mathlib
- Has: `LieAlgebra.IsSemisimple`, `LieAlgebra.IsSimple` (`Algebra/Lie/Semisimple/Defs.lean`) with decomposition into simple ideals; `killingForm` (`Algebra/Lie/TraceForm.lean`), `LieAlgebra.IsKilling`, `rootSystem` of a split Cartan (`Algebra/Lie/Weights/RootSystem.lean`); `LieGroup` (`Geometry/Manifold/Algebra/LieGroup.lean`) and `LeftInvariantDerivation`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Real semisimple Lie algebra, Killing form, simple ideals: Mathlib.
2. Cartan-involution predicate and Cartan decomposition 𝔤 = 𝔨 ⊕ 𝔭: Tau Ceti (`LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution`, `isCompl_fixedSubalgebra_cartanP`; RealSemisimpleLieGroups §0.1). Reductive predicate and conjugacy of Cartan involutions: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §§0.1–0.2 (merged, not implemented; also ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.0.1 for algebraic groups, whose predicate is in code).
3. Existence of a Cartan involution θ of 𝔤₀ (via compact real form): target LieGroups Layer 7 (merged; the conjugate-linear `τ` is `TauCeti.serreCompactInvolution`, the rest not implemented); the passage from `τ` to θ and the group-level Cartan data for connected semisimple `G` with finite centre: NoncompactQuantumSymmetry Layer 3 (M0) item 2 (merged, not implemented). RealSemisimpleLieGroups Layers 0–1 can take existence as a hypothesis.
4. Global `CartanInvolution`, `IwasawaData` and `Ad(K)𝔞 = 𝔭`: LieGroups Layer 9 (merged, partial; matrix groups only).
5. Maximal abelian 𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭, restricted root system, conjugacy of maximal abelian subspaces (Theorem 1.2): RealSemisimpleLieGroups §§1.1–1.3 (merged; §1.1 is in Tau Ceti, `LieEquiv.IsMaximalAbelianInP`, `Submodule.restrictedRootSpace`, `LieEquiv.IsCartanInvolution.isInternal_restrictedRootSpace`, as are the first steps of §1.2, `killingEquivDual` and the `𝔰𝔩₂` triple `isSl2Triple_restrictedCoroot` with integrality; the rest is not implemented); under `K⁰` for disconnected groups and reductive Cartan data: LieGroupsPartII §2.2 (merged).
6. `realRank 𝔤₀`, well-definedness, additivity over simple ideals, `realRank = 0 ↔ compact`: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §1.3 (merged; the definition `LieAlgebra.realRank` and its choice-free properties are in Tau Ceti, well-definedness and additivity are not).
7. Group version `groupRealRank`, higher rank, almost-simple factors: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §2.1 (merged).
8. Borel–Tits comparison with maximal ℝ-split tori, their conjugacy under `G°` (Borel–Tits Thm. 4.21; Borel, *Linear Algebraic Groups*, Thm. 20.9(ii)) and the reductive count `rank_ℝ 𝐆 = groupRealRank (𝐆_der(ℝ)) + dim 𝐙_s`: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §2.4 (merged), consuming AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a's maximal ℝ-split torus (merged) and ReductiveGroups Layers 4 and 6.

## Library-scale gap
None. RealSemisimpleLieGroups is the roadmap for this theory (Knapp, *Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction*, Ch. VI §§2–5; Helgason Ch. V): it defines the θ-stable data purely algebraically in Layers 0–1, before any group, and identifies LieGroups Layer 9's group-level restricted roots of an `IwasawaData` with them. Its Layers 0–1 can proceed with the existence of Cartan involutions as a hypothesis (its "Ordering", l.1141), on the §0.1 code; §1.3's conjugacy and the group-level layers wait on LieGroups Layers 7 and 9.
