# 1. Real rank of a semisimple Lie group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) specifies the real rank as a target: `realRank` of a real semisimple or reductive Lie algebra with its independence of choices, additivity over simple ideals, the compactness criterion and the comparison with the complex rank (§1.3), and `groupRealRank`, almost-simple and compact factors and higher rank for Lie groups (§2.1). It also covers the equivalent descriptions of Remark (1): restricted root system rank (§1.3), Borel–Tits `ℝ`-rank as the dimension of a maximal `ℝ`-split torus, with the conjugacy of maximal `ℝ`-split tori and the reductive count of Remark (3) (§2.4), rank of `G/K` as maximal flat dimension (§5.5), and split forms (§1.4). Not implemented. Its Cartan-involution and Iwasawa inputs are merged targets in RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layers 7 and 9, implemented only for matrix groups (`GL n`, `SL n`, `Sp(2n, ℝ)`) and, for Layer 7, up to the conjugate-linear involution `τ` of a Serre presentation.

- Only special cases: `LieAlgebra.Symplectic.cartanInvolution` with `killingForm_cartanInvolution_self_neg` for `sp` (`Algebra/Lie/Symplectic/{Cartan,Killing}.lean`); smooth Iwasawa/Cartan decomposition for `GL n` (`TauCeti.iwasawaDiffeomorph`, `Geometry/Lie/Iwasawa.lean`), Cartan decompositions `K × 𝔭 ≃ₜ G` for transpose-stable matrix groups (`Subgroup.cartanHomeomorph`, `Analysis/SpecialFunctions/ContinuousFunctionalCalculus/Cartan.lean`) instantiated for `SL n`, `Sp(2n, ℝ)` and the realified `GL(n, ℂ)` (`Analysis/Matrix/Cartan.lean`, `Analysis/Matrix/Cartan/Realify.lean`).
- Compact real form input (LieGroups Layer 7, first step): `TauCeti.serreCompactInvolution`, the conjugate-linear involution `τ` of the Serre-presented Lie algebra `Matrix.ToLieAlgebra R CM` with `τ Hᵢ = −Hᵢ`, `τ Eᵢ = −Fᵢ`, `τ Fᵢ = −Eᵢ`, bracket-preserving and involutive (`Algebra/Lie/Presentation/Serre/CompactInvolution.lean`). The Hermitian form `−B(x, τy)`, its positivity, the real form `𝔲 = Fix τ` and θ are not in code.
- No `CartanInvolution`, `IwasawaData`, restricted roots or real rank (grep for `CartanInvolution|IwasawaData|restrictedRoot|realRank` empty).
- Compact Lie algebras (LieGroups Layer 5A): the Killing form is negative definite on the derived algebra of a compact Lie group, which splits off the centre (`TauCeti/Geometry/Lie/Adjoint/Compact.lean`, `Algebra/Lie/InvariantForm/Positive.lean`).
- Algebraic tori over a field: `TauCeti.HopfIdeal.IsMaximalTorus` with descent of maximality along any field extension (`isMaximalTorus_of_baseChange`, `Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Torus/Maximal.lean`) and `TauCeti.torusCommHopfAlgProperty.baseChange` (`Torus/BaseChange.lean`). No `k`-split subtori, maximal `k`-split tori or `k`-rank (grep `MaximalSplit|splitRank` empty).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Real semisimple Lie algebra, Killing form, simple ideals: Mathlib.
2. Existence of a Cartan involution θ of 𝔤₀ (via compact real form): target LieGroups Layer 7 (merged; the conjugate-linear `τ` is `TauCeti.serreCompactInvolution`, the rest not implemented); the passage from `τ` to θ and the group-level Cartan data for connected semisimple `G` with finite centre: NoncompactQuantumSymmetry Layer 3 (M0) item 2 (merged, not implemented). Cartan-involution predicate, Cartan decomposition 𝔤 = 𝔨 ⊕ 𝔭 and conjugacy of Cartan involutions: RealSemisimpleLieGroups Layer 0 (merged; also ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.0.1 for algebraic groups).
3. Global `CartanInvolution`, `IwasawaData` and `Ad(K)𝔞 = 𝔭`: LieGroups Layer 9 (merged, partial; `GL n` only).
4. Maximal abelian 𝔞 ⊆ 𝔭, restricted root system, conjugacy of maximal abelian subspaces (Theorem 1.2): RealSemisimpleLieGroups §§1.1–1.3 (merged); under `K⁰` for disconnected groups and reductive Cartan data: LieGroupsPartII §2.2 (merged).
5. `realRank 𝔤₀ := finrank ℝ 𝔞`, well-definedness, additivity over simple ideals, `realRank = 0 ↔ compact`: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §1.3 (merged).
6. Group version `groupRealRank`, higher rank, almost-simple factors: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §2.1 (merged).
7. Borel–Tits comparison with maximal ℝ-split tori, their conjugacy under `G°` (Borel–Tits Thm. 4.21; Borel, *Linear Algebraic Groups*, Thm. 20.9(ii)) and the reductive count `rank_ℝ 𝐆 = groupRealRank (𝐆_der(ℝ)) + dim 𝐙_s`: RealSemisimpleLieGroups §2.4 (merged), consuming AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3a's maximal ℝ-split torus (merged) and ReductiveGroups Layers 4 and 6.

## Library-scale gap
None. RealSemisimpleLieGroups is the roadmap for this theory (Knapp, *Lie Groups Beyond an Introduction*, Ch. VI §§2–5; Helgason Ch. V): it defines the θ-stable data purely algebraically in Layers 0–1, before any group, and identifies LieGroups Layer 9's group-level restricted roots of an `IwasawaData` with them. Its Layers 0–1 can start with the existence of Cartan involutions as a hypothesis (its "Ordering"); the group-level layers wait on LieGroups Layers 7 and 9.
