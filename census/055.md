## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Gaussian perimeter and Gaussian clusters are not targets anywhere; Euclidean perimeter, reduced boundary and De Giorgi structure are targets of GeometricMeasureTheory G5 (partly implemented), and the standard Gaussian measure is in Mathlib, so the weighted perimeter and the cluster definition are a modest extension.

## Mathlib
Has `ProbabilityTheory.stdGaussian` (`Probability/Distributions/Gaussian/Multivariate.lean`) and `multivariateGaussian`, `hausdorffMeasure`, `Measure.withDensity`. No perimeter, BV or reduced boundary.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.perimeter μ Ω s` (`Analysis/BoundedVariation/Perimeter.lean`): total variation of the indicator, `perimeter_eq_iSup` (sup of ∫_s div φ dμ), `perimeter_congr_ae`, `perimeter_compl` (Haar μ), `perimeter_le_liminf`. Note: taking μ = Gaussian here is not the Gaussian perimeter (55.1 needs the drift term div X − ⟨x, X⟩, i.e. weighting |Dχ| by e^{-W}).
- `TauCeti.reducedBoundary` (`Analysis/BoundedVariation/ReducedBoundary.lean`), blow-ups with the density of the perimeter measure at reduced-boundary points (`tendsto_perimeter_ball_div`, `Analysis/BoundedVariation/BlowUp.lean`), density estimates, coarea, slicing, isoperimetric inequality files in `Analysis/BoundedVariation/`.
- No Gaussian/weighted perimeter, clusters, interfaces Σ_ij, isoperimetric profile.

## Merged roadmaps
- GeometricMeasureTheory G5 (perimeter API, reduced boundary, De Giorgi structure theorem |Dχ_E| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E): partial: the structure theorem itself (rectifiability of ∂*E and `|Dχ_E| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E`) is not proved.
- Isoperimetry (sharp Euclidean isoperimetry) consumes GMT; no Gaussian or cluster content.
- No roadmap mentions Gaussian isoperimetry, Borell-Sudakov, Ehrhard or clusters (grep).

## Prerequisite chain
Lebesgue/Haar, stdGaussian [Mathlib] -> BV, perimeter [Tau Ceti `perimeter`; GMT G3/G5] -> reduced boundary, De Giorgi |Dχ| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E [Tau Ceti `reducedBoundary`; GMT G5, structure theorem unproved] -> weighted perimeter P_γ = ∫ e^{−W} d|Dχ| (55.1/55.2) [unplanned] -> q-cluster, interfaces Σ_ij, total perimeter (55.3/55.4) [unplanned; cf. Maggi Part IV] -> isoperimetric profile, simplicial clusters (55.5) [unplanned].

## Library-scale gap
A "weighted and multi-phase isoperimetry" roadmap extending GeometricMeasureTheory/Isoperimetry: perimeter with smooth positive densities, existence of minimizing clusters (Almgren; Maggi, *Sets of Finite Perimeter and Geometric Variational Problems*, Part IV), the Gaussian isoperimetric inequality (Borell, Sudakov-Tsirelson; Bakry-Ledoux), and the Gaussian multi-bubble statements of Milman-Neeman. Natural home: beside Isoperimetry, consuming GMT G5.
