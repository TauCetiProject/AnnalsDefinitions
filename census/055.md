## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters/README.md) specifies the definition in full: weighted variation and perimeter `P_μ` for any positive `C¹` density `e^{−W}`, with `gaussianPerimeter` the Gaussian case (§2.1, 55.1–55.2) and its identification with `e^{−W}μHE[n−1]` on the reduced boundary (§2.2); `q`-clusters with total perimeter `½ Σ P_μ(Ω_i)` (§3.1, 55.3), interfaces `Σ_ij = ∂*Ω_i ∩ ∂*Ω_j` and `P_μ(Ω) = Σ_{i<j} A_ij` (§3.2, 55.4), isoperimetric profiles (§3.4) and simplicial clusters (§§4.1–4.2, 55.5). Definition 55.1 is in Tau Ceti code (`TauCeti.gaussianPerimeter`, `Analysis/BoundedVariation/Weighted/Basic.lean`) with the elementary laws of Proposition 55.2 (null-set invariance, complement), its density-bounded form the identity `V_μ(u; Ω) = ∫_Ω e^{−W} d|Du|` (`weightedVariation_eq_lintegral_variationMeasure`) and, in dimension `n ≥ 2`, the reduced-boundary form `P_μ(U) = ∫_{∂*U} e^{−W} dHⁿ⁻¹` of 55.2 (`weightedPerimeter_eq_lintegral_reducedBoundary`, `Weighted/ReducedBoundary.lean`); the Lipschitz-domain and lower-semicontinuity clauses of 55.2, the clusters of 55.3, the interfaces of 55.4 and the profile and simplicial clusters of 55.5 are not. De Giorgi's structure theorem, the GeometricMeasureTheory G5 input of §§2.2 and 3.2, is in code. The roadmap proves the two-cell case of the Milman–Neeman theorem and leaves `3 ≤ q ≤ n+1` to a Part II.

## Merged roadmaps
- [GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters/README.md):
  - Layer 1 (l.50): `I = φ ∘ Φ⁻¹`, Gaussian tails, orthogonal splitting. In code apart from the §1.2 Checks.
  - Layer 2 (l.96): §2.1 l.100 `weightedVariation W Ω u`, `weightedPerimeter W Ω U` by duality with `div_μ X = div X − ⟨∇W, X⟩`, `gaussianPerimeter` (55.1, in code); §2.2 l.118 `weightedPerimeter_eq_lintegral_reducedBoundary` (`P_μ(U) = ∫_{∂*U} e^{−W} dμHE[n−1]`, 55.2; in code for `n ≥ 2` with its first step `V_μ(u; Ω) = ∫ e^{−W} d|Du|`); §§2.3–2.7 explicit perimeters, lower semicontinuity and compactness, weighted Gauss–Green, coarea, Gaussian invariance (§2.3's half-spaces, §2.5's compactly supported Gauss–Green formula for `n ≥ 2` and §2.7's isometry clause in code).
  - Layer 3 (l.188): §3.1 l.192 `Cluster W q` (pairwise disjoint Lebesgue-measurable cells covering up to a null set, finite perimeter) and `Cluster.perimeter` (55.3); §3.2 l.211 interfaces `Σ_ij`, `A_ij = μ^{n−1}(Σ_ij)`, `P_μ(Ω) = Σ_{i<j} A_ij` (55.4); §3.3 interface quadratic form; §3.4 l.231 isoperimetric profiles; §3.5 existence of minimizing clusters.
  - Layer 4 (l.266): §4.1 the canonical model cluster, §4.2 l.289 simplicial clusters in `E` (55.5), §§4.3–4.7 the measure map, model profile, its differential identities, and `I^{(q−1)} ≤ I_m^{(q−1)}`.
  - Layers 5–9: one-dimensional Gaussian isoperimetry, Ehrhard symmetrisation, the Gaussian isoperimetric theorem with equality and its two-cell cluster form (§§7.3–7.4), Borell–Sudakov–Tsirelson, Bobkov, concentration, Ehrhard's inequality.
  - Scope: the multi-bubble theorem for `3 ≤ q ≤ n + 1` (the paper's theorem) is "GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters, Part II", needing regularity of minimizing clusters (Almgren, Taylor, White, Colombo–Edelen–Spolaor) and the second-variation theory of Milman–Neeman §§5–10.
  - Suppliers: GeometricMeasureTheory G5 (structure theorem in code, density one-half, essential versus reduced boundary in code), G2 (weighted coarea, tangential area formula), G6 (Lipschitz domains); Isoperimetry I4 (finite-perimeter slicing, essential convexity).
- GeometricMeasureTheory G5 (perimeter API, reduced boundary, De Giorgi structure theorem `|Dχ_E| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E`): the structure theorem, the rectifiability of `∂*E` and the essential/reduced comparison are in code (see Tau Ceti code).
- Isoperimetry (sharp Euclidean isoperimetry): GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters consumes I4; no Gaussian or cluster content.
