# 7. Discrete series representations of GL₂(ℝ)

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) specifies `(𝔤,K)`-modules and admissibility (§2.1), infinitesimal characters (§2.2), normalized real induction and principal series (§2.3), `IsSquareIntegrable`/essentially square-integrable for real reductive groups with the discrete-series existence and parametrization (§2.4), and the explicit full-`O(2)` modules `D_k(μ)` of `GL₂(ℝ)` with K-types, central character, Casimir `k(k−2)/4` and the classification of irreducible admissible `(𝔤𝔩₂,O(2))`-modules (§2.6). Merged [GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer/README.md) R16.2.13 identifies them with principal-series subquotients and R16.3.6 gives their archimedean factors. Of §2.6, the modules `D_k(μ)` are in Tau Ceti as explicit `𝔤𝔩₂(ℂ)`-modules on `ℤ →₀ ℂ` with their weights, rays, central scalar, Casimir `k(k−2)/4` and the reflection swapping the rays (`TauCeti.CompactWeightModule.discreteSeries`, `RepresentationTheory/DiscreteSeries/GL2.lean`) and their irreducibility for the full group (`TauCeti.eq_bot_or_eq_discreteSeries`), without the `O(2)`-action that makes them `(𝔤𝔩₂, O(2))`-modules or the classification; the rest is not in code, and of its inputs §2.1's `K`-finite vectors, Banach isotypic projectors and the decomposition of the `G`-finite vectors as the direct sum of their ranges are. Uses #5.
