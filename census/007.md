# 7. Discrete series representations of GL₂(ℝ)

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1037 (Automorphic forms on reductive groups) specifies `(𝔤,K)`-modules, normalized principal series and their reducibility (§2.3), `IsSquareIntegrable`/essentially square-integrable for real reductive groups (§2.4), and the explicit full-`O(2)` modules `D_k(μ)` of `GL₂(ℝ)` with K-types, central character, Casimir `k(k−2)/4` and the classification of irreducible admissible `(𝔤𝔩₂,O(2))`-modules (§2.6). PR #931 R16.2.13 identifies them with principal-series subquotients. Nothing merged or in code. Uses #5.

## Mathlib
- Has: `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup`, Haar measure, `MeasureTheory.Lp`, unitary operators, `UniversalEnvelopingAlgebra`.
- Missing: unitary representations of locally compact groups as a theory, `(𝔤,K)`-modules, induced representations of Lie groups, matrix coefficients / square-integrability.

## Tau Ceti code
- None for real groups (grep `discreteSeries|HarishChandraModule|gKModule|squareIntegrable` finds only finite-field `GL₂` principal series in `RepresentationTheory/CharacterTable/GL2/*`). Related: `TauCeti.iwasawaDiffeomorph` for `GL n`; Harish-Chandra/Casimir algebra (see #5); compact-group Peter–Weyl (`TauCeti.stdPeterWeylBasis`).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `GL₂(ℝ)`, Haar measure on `G/Z`, `L²`: Mathlib.
2. Compact-group isotypic theory for `O(2)`: Tau Ceti (CompactGroups, complete).
3. Lie algebra action, `U(𝔤𝔩₂)`, Casimir: Tau Ceti (`sl₂` theory; `gl_n` trace-form Casimir `glCasimir`, `Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/Casimir.lean`, from LieHighestWeight Layer 9).
4. `(𝔤,K)`-modules, admissibility, infinitesimal character: PR #1037 §§2.1–2.2 (see #5).
5. Principal series `I(χ₁,χ₂)` and reducibility: PR #1037 §2.3, PR #931 R16.2.13.
6. Unitary representations, matrix coefficients, discrete series mod centre, essentially discrete series: PR #1037 §2.4.
7. `D_k`, weight, classification (Theorem 7.5): PR #1037 §2.6.
8. Infinitesimal equivalence between unitary representations and Harish-Chandra modules: PR #1037 §§2.1, 2.7.

## Library-scale gap
None beyond PRs #1037/#931, which are large (Knapp, *Representation Theory of Semisimple Groups*; Bump, *Automorphic Forms and Representations*, Ch. 2). Note that PR #1037's §2.4 targets the general real reductive case, far beyond `GL₂(ℝ)`; the `GL₂` statements of §2.6 could be landed first.
