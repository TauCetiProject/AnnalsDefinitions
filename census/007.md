# 7. Discrete series representations of GL₂(ℝ)

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) specifies `(𝔤,K)`-modules and admissibility (§2.1), infinitesimal characters (§2.2), normalized real induction and principal series (§2.3), `IsSquareIntegrable`/essentially square-integrable for real reductive groups with the discrete-series existence and parametrization (§2.4), and the explicit full-`O(2)` modules `D_k(μ)` of `GL₂(ℝ)` with K-types, central character, Casimir `k(k−2)/4` and the classification of irreducible admissible `(𝔤𝔩₂,O(2))`-modules (§2.6). ## Prerequisite chain
1. `GL₂(ℝ)`, Haar measure on `G/Z`, `L²`: Mathlib.
2. Compact-group isotypic theory for `O(2)`: Tau Ceti (CompactGroups); density of the `G`-finite vectors in a strongly continuous Banach representation of a compact group: Tau Ceti (`ContRepresentation.dense_gFiniteSubmodule`, `RepresentationTheory/Compact/GFiniteDensity.lean`).
3. Lie algebra action, `U(𝔤𝔩₂)`, Casimir: Tau Ceti (`sl₂` theory; `gl_n` trace-form Casimir `glCasimir`, `Algebra/Lie/GeneralLinear/Casimir.lean`, from LieHighestWeight Layer 9).
4. `(𝔤,K)`-modules, admissibility, infinitesimal character: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §§2.1–2.2 (merged, not implemented; §2.1 in open Tau Ceti PRs https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/45407, https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3763 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3825, the algebraic part of §2.2 in https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3728; see #5).
5. Principal series `I(χ₁,χ₂)` and reducibility: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.3 (merged); explicit `GL₂` cases in merged GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer R16.2.13.
6. Unitary representations, matrix coefficients, discrete series mod centre, essentially discrete series: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.4 (merged).
7. `D_k`, weight, classification (Theorem 7.5): AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.6 (merged; `D_k(μ)` as an explicit `𝔤𝔩₂(ℂ)`-module in open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3923).
8. Infinitesimal equivalence between unitary representations and Harish-Chandra modules: AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §§2.1, 2.7 (merged; the smooth vectors and derived action of §2.7 are in code; smoothness of `G`-finite vectors and the first part of Gårding smoothing in open Tau Ceti PRs https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3816 and https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3822).
