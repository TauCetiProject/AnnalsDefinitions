## Library-scale gap
Merged ComplexHessianEquations is the roadmap for this theory through Yau's theorem and the Kähler `L^∞` estimates of Guo–Phong–Tong, which is the cited paper's main theorem; what remains is implementation. The definition (Layers 1–3) needs only ComplexKahlerGeometry K1–K2 and the Levi form, in code, and §1.1 is in code; the global layers wait on ComplexKahlerGeometry K4–K6. The Hermitian `L^∞` estimate (Guo–Phong) needs a ComplexHessianEquations Part II, named in its Scope, with no roadmap PR. References: Guo–Phong–Tong (Annals 2023); Székelyhidi (JDG 2018); Caffarelli–Nirenberg–Spruck (Acta 1985); Guedj–Zeriahi, *Degenerate Complex Monge–Ampère Equations*.
