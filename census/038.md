## Prerequisite chain
1. Eigenvalues of Hermitian matrices/self-adjoint maps, `esymm`, symmetric cones: in Mathlib.
2. Compact complex manifold, `T^{1,0}`, real `(1,1)`-forms: ComplexKahlerGeometry K1 (merged, partial).
3. `i∂∂̄φ` for `φ ∈ C²(X)`: ComplexKahlerGeometry K2 (merged, partial); `C²` regularity version not explicitly specified.
4. Hermitian metric `ω_X`, Kähler metric: ComplexKahlerGeometry K5b/K6 (merged, untouched).
5. Endomorphism `h[α]` and eigenvalue vector relative to `ω_X` (38.1): unplanned (trivial given 1 and 4).
6. Admissible pair `(f,Γ)`, structural condition (38.2), the equation (38.3): unplanned, short.
7. For the theorem: pluripotential theory, complex Monge–Ampère on compact Kähler manifolds, Yau's estimates, entropy/energy: unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "Complex Monge–Ampère and Hessian equations" roadmap beside ComplexKahlerGeometry: plurisubharmonic and quasi-psh functions on compact Kähler manifolds, Bedford–Taylor Monge–Ampère operator, capacity, Kołodziej's `L^∞` estimates, Yau's solution of the Calabi conjecture, then Hessian-type equations (`σ_k`, quotients) with Caffarelli–Nirenberg–Spruck and Székelyhidi's `C²` estimates. References: Guedj–Zeriahi, *Degenerate Complex Monge–Ampère Equations*; Székelyhidi, *An Introduction to Extremal Kähler Metrics*, Ch. 3; Caffarelli–Nirenberg–Spruck (Acta 1985); Székelyhidi (JDG 2018).
