# 31. Totally geodesic subvariety of a ball quotient

## Status
UNPLANNED. Total geodesicity via the second fundamental form is a merged target (RiemannianGeometry), but the main prerequisite, the ball quotient `Γ\B^n` for an arbitrary (possibly non-arithmetic) lattice `Γ ⊂ PU(1,n)` with its quasi-projective algebraic structure (#15), is planned nowhere; open PRs reach only arithmetic ball quotients as Shimura varieties.

Depends on #15 (ball quotients) and #68 (arithmetic lattices). #15 is rated PREREQS-ROADMAPPED because the algebraic model of an arithmetic ball quotient is specified (PR #933); #31 is UNPLANNED because complex hyperbolic geometry (`B^n`, `PU(1,n)`, its metric, 31.3) and non-arithmetic `S_Γ` are in no roadmap.

## Mathlib
- `Matrix.unitaryGroup`, inner product spaces, Riemannian metrics (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/`, `PathELength`). No geodesics of Riemannian manifolds, no second fundamental form, no `PU(1,n)`, no complex hyperbolic space, no lattices in Lie groups.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm` for maps into an inner product space, with the holomorphic identities (`TauCeti/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SecondFundamentalForm.lean`, `ShapeOperator.lean`): Euclidean ambient only.
- Riemannian geodesics: `TauCeti.Manifold.IsGeodesicCurveOn` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Geodesic/`).
- `n = 1` only: Fuchsian groups and quotients of the disc (`TauCeti/Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/`). No complex ball `B^n`, no `PU(1,n)`, no ball quotients. Definition absent.

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry: second fundamental form `B` of a submanifold, "B=0 is equivalent to total geodesicity" (31.2), shape operators, Gauss–Codazzi. Not checked as implemented beyond the Euclidean version.
- FuchsianOrbifolds: lattices (finite covolume) in `PSL₂(ℝ)`, i.e. `n = 1` (where 31.4 is vacuous).
- ComplexKahlerGeometry: Kähler manifolds, Fubini–Study / `ℂP^n` (mentions `PU(1)` only for `n = 0`). No ball, no Bergman metric.
- Nothing on `PU(1,n)`, complex hyperbolic geometry or ball quotients.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #933 "Roadmap: complex Shimura varieties and canonical models": Hermitian symmetric domains and Baily–Borel quasi-projectivity for arithmetic `Γ`; ball quotients appear only as rank-one examples (Picard modular surfaces). Covers arithmetic `S_Γ` but not non-arithmetic lattices, which is the case of the paper.
- #792 AdelicAlgebraicGroups (lattices in real Lie groups, arithmetic lattices; see #68), #1038 ShimuraData (Hermitian domains), #871 "Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces and their cohomology" (arithmetic `Γ`), #2026 ArithmeticFuchsianGroups (`n = 1`).
- No PR mentions totally geodesic subvarieties or complex hyperbolic geometry.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Riemannian/Kähler manifolds, geodesics: Mathlib + Tau Ceti `IsGeodesicCurveOn`.
2. Totally geodesic submanifold (31.2): RiemannianGeometry (merged; Tau Ceti has only the Euclidean second fundamental form).
3. Complex hyperbolic space `X = B^n ⊂ ℙ^n`, `PU(1,n)` action, invariant Kähler (Bergman) metric: unplanned.
4. Classification of complex totally geodesic submanifolds as `X ∩ ℙ(W')` (31.3): unplanned.
5. Lattices in a real Lie group and arithmetic lattices (#68): open PR #792 (AdelicAlgebraicGroups, Layer 3); Selberg's lemma for `PU(1,n)`: not checked.
6. Ball quotient `S_Γ` as normal quasi-projective variety (#15): arithmetic case via Baily–Borel in PR #933; non-arithmetic case (Siu–Yau, Mok) unplanned.
7. Totally geodesic subvariety, strict, maximal (31.4), group-theoretic form (31.5(b)): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "complex hyperbolic geometry and ball quotients" roadmap: the projective and ball models of `ℍ^n_ℂ`, `PU(1,n)` and its Bergman metric, geodesics and the classification of totally geodesic complex submanifolds, lattices in `PU(1,n)` (Selberg, finite volume, cusps, arithmetic vs non-arithmetic, Deligne–Mostow examples), and the analytic-then-algebraic structure of `Γ\B^n` (Satake–Baily–Borel for arithmetic `Γ`, Siu–Yau/Mok compactification in general), ending with `Γ`-special/totally geodesic subvarieties. It would sit beside RiemannianGeometry and ComplexKahlerGeometry and feed PR #933 ShimuraVarieties. References: Goldman, *Complex hyperbolic geometry*; Mok, *Projective algebraicity of minimal compactifications of complex-hyperbolic space forms of finite volume*; Borel–Ji.
