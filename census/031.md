# 31. Totally geodesic subvariety of a ball quotient

## Status
PREREQS-ROADMAPPED. Definition 31.4 (and 31.5) is not a target anywhere, but its main prerequisites are targets. Total geodesicity via the second fundamental form (31.2) is merged RiemannianGeometry R5a, and analytic subsets with their singular loci and irreducible components are merged ComplexKahlerGeometry §3.4. Open PR #2229 (RealSemisimpleLieGroups) §5.7 specifies Setting 31.1 and Proposition 31.3: `PU(1,n)` acting on the ball `𝔹ⁿ ⊂ ℙ(ℂ^{n+1})`, the invariant Kähler metric of `ω_X = −i∂∂̄ log(1 − |w|²)`, the complex totally geodesic submanifolds `X_{W'}` with their stabilizers, and the converse through §5.5's local Lie-triple-system statement. Lattices and arithmetic lattices (#68) are a target of merged [AdelicAlgebraicGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md) AA.3.4 (not implemented). The ball quotient `S_Γ` (#15) is rated PREREQS-ROADMAPPED in its algebraic-model form: arithmetic quotients are specified (PR #933), the Siu–Yau–Mok existence for non-arithmetic `Γ`, which Baldi–Ullmo need, is in no roadmap.

Depends on #15 (ball quotients) and #68 (arithmetic lattices).

## Merged roadmaps
- RiemannianGeometry: second fundamental form `B` of a submanifold, "B=0 is equivalent to total geodesicity" (31.2), shape operators, Gauss–Codazzi. Not checked as implemented beyond the Euclidean version.
- FuchsianOrbifolds: lattices (finite covolume) in `PSL₂(ℝ)`, i.e. `n = 1` (where 31.4 is vacuous).
- ComplexKahlerGeometry: Kähler manifolds, Fubini–Study / `ℂP^n` (mentions `PU(1)` only for `n = 0`). No ball, no Bergman metric.
- ComplexKahlerGeometry §3.4 also has reduced analytic subsets, their regular and singular loci and locally finite irreducible components, with `Reg(V)` a complex manifold, which 31.4's `Z^reg` and components of `π⁻¹(W)` use.
- [AdelicAlgebraicGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md) AA.3.4 "Arithmetic lattices in a real Lie group": lattices in a real Lie group and arithmetic lattices (#68). Not implemented.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #933 "Roadmap: complex Shimura varieties and canonical models": Hermitian symmetric domains and Baily–Borel quasi-projectivity for arithmetic `Γ`; ball quotients appear only as rank-one examples (Picard modular surfaces). Covers arithmetic `S_Γ` but not non-arithmetic lattices, which is the case of the paper.
- #1038 ShimuraData (Hermitian domains), #871 "Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces and their cohomology" (arithmetic `Γ`), #2026 ArithmeticFuchsianGroups (`n = 1`).
- #2229 "feat: add the RealSemisimpleLieGroups roadmap" §5.7 "Complex hyperbolic space": for the form `z₀w̄₀ − Σ z_i w̄_i` of signature `(1, n)`, `U(1, n)`, `PU(1, n)` connected and centre-free with `Ad : PU(1, n) ≅ Int(𝔰𝔲(1, n))`, `K ≅ U(n)`; the ball model `𝔹ⁿ = {[z] | ⟨z, z⟩ > 0}` (Mathlib `Projectivization`) with chart onto the unit ball of `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin n)`, a faithful transitive holomorphic action with stabilizer `K` and `PU(1, n)/K ≅ 𝔹ⁿ`; the metric `g_𝔹` of the Kähler form `ω_X = −i∂∂̄ log(1 − |w|²)` (Kähler forms from ComplexKahlerGeometry), `PU(1, n)`-invariant, the Killing metric being `2(n + 1)·g_𝔹`, and every invariant metric a multiple of it (Setting 31.1); the Lie triple systems in `𝔭 ≅ ℂⁿ` are exactly the complex and the totally real subspaces; for `W'` of signature `(1, m)`, `X_{W'} = 𝔹ⁿ ∩ ℙ(W')` is closed, connected, totally geodesic, a ball in a complex affine subspace, biholomorphic to `𝔹^m`, with stabilizer the image of `U(W') × U(W'^⊥)` acting through `PU(1, m)`; conversely every connected totally geodesic submanifold of real dimension `2m ≥ 2` with complex tangent spaces is an open subset of a unique `X_{W'}` (Proposition 31.3, Goldman Ch. 3). §5.5 includes connected totally geodesic submanifolds that are neither closed nor complete (31.2's "not necessarily closed"), and §5.6 quotients `Λ\X` by discrete subgroups. It names ShimuraVarieties (PR #933) for the complex structure and Baily–Borel algebraicity of arithmetic ball quotients, ComplexKahlerGeometry for Kähler forms, AdelicAlgebraicGroups (merged) for lattices and arithmetic lattices, and leaves outside Selberg's lemma, non-arithmetic ball quotients and their Siu–Yau–Mok compactification, and the full biholomorphism group of the ball. Not stated: 31.3's final clause, that an irreducible closed analytic subset with totally geodesic regular locus is an `X_{W'}`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Riemannian/Kähler manifolds, geodesics: Mathlib + Tau Ceti `IsGeodesicCurveOn`.
2. Totally geodesic submanifold (31.2): RiemannianGeometry R5a (merged; Tau Ceti has only the Euclidean second fundamental form).
3. Complex hyperbolic space `X = 𝔹ⁿ ⊂ ℙⁿ`, `PU(1,n)` action, invariant Kähler metric (31.1): PR #2229 §5.7.
4. Complex totally geodesic submanifolds `X_{W'}` and the converse (31.3): PR #2229 §5.7, on §5.5; the analytic-subset clause of 31.3: not stated, from ComplexKahlerGeometry §3.4's analytic subsets plus §5.7.
5. Lattices in a real Lie group and arithmetic lattices (#68): merged AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.3.4 (on AA.2.2–AA.2.3; not implemented). Selberg's lemma (31.5(a)): outside PR #2229, in no roadmap.
6. Ball quotient `S_Γ` as normal quasi-projective variety (#15): arithmetic case via Baily–Borel in PR #933; non-arithmetic existence (Siu–Yau, Mok) unplanned.
7. Totally geodesic subvariety, strict, maximal (31.4), group-theoretic form (31.5(b)): unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A "complex hyperbolic geometry and ball quotients" roadmap on top of PR #2229 §5.7, which supplies the ball model, `PU(1,n)`, its Kähler metric and the classification of complex totally geodesic submanifolds: lattices in `PU(1,n)` (Selberg, finite volume, cusps, arithmetic vs non-arithmetic, Deligne–Mostow examples), and the analytic-then-algebraic structure of `Γ\B^n` (Satake–Baily–Borel for arithmetic `Γ` in PR #933, Siu–Yau/Mok compactification in general), ending with `Γ`-special/totally geodesic subvarieties (31.4, 31.5). It would sit beside RiemannianGeometry and ComplexKahlerGeometry and feed PR #933 ShimuraVarieties. References: Goldman, *Complex hyperbolic geometry*; Mok, *Projective algebraicity of minimal compactifications of complex-hyperbolic space forms of finite volume*; Borel–Ji.
