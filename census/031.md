# 31. Totally geodesic subvariety of a ball quotient

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices/README.md) (PR #2425) §12.1 (README l.798) defines totally geodesic subvarieties of `S_Γ` (Definition 31.4, with "every irreducible component of `π⁻¹(Z^an)`", equivalent to "some" by transitivity) and Γ-special sub-balls and subvarieties (31.5(b)); §12.2 (l.812) proves Γ-special subvarieties are algebraic and totally geodesic, §12.3 (l.822) the converse, and §3.3 (l.246) the analytic-subset clause of Proposition 31.3. Only its §1.1 (`U(1,n)`) is in code. Flag: "strict" and "maximal" are not defined there (the §12.3 Checks mention maximality only in quoting Baldi–Ullmo's theorem, l.829); they are one-line order-theoretic definitions on top of §12.1. Setting 31.1 and the rest of Proposition 31.3 are merged targets in [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §5.7 (on §5.5's local Lie-triple-system statement), total geodesicity via the second fundamental form (31.2) is merged RiemannianGeometry R5a, and analytic subsets with their singular loci and irreducible components are merged ComplexKahlerGeometry §3.4. The ball quotient `S_Γ` (#15) is a target of the same merged roadmap (§10.4).

Depends on #15 (ball quotients) and #68 (arithmetic lattices, needed only for the papers' theorems).

## Merged roadmaps
- [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §5.7 l.1077 "Complex hyperbolic space": for the form `z₀w̄₀ − Σ z_i w̄_i` of signature `(1, n)`, `U(1, n)`, `PU(1, n)` connected and centre-free with `Ad : PU(1, n) ≅ Int(𝔰𝔲(1, n))`, `K ≅ U(n)`; the ball model `𝔹ⁿ = {[z] | ⟨z, z⟩ > 0}` (Mathlib `Projectivization`) with chart onto the unit ball of `EuclideanSpace ℂ (Fin n)`, a faithful transitive holomorphic action with stabilizer `K` and `PU(1, n)/K ≅ 𝔹ⁿ`; the metric `g_𝔹` of the Kähler form `ω_X = −i∂∂̄ log(1 − |w|²)`, `PU(1, n)`-invariant, the Killing metric being `2(n + 1)·g_𝔹`, and every invariant metric a multiple of it (Setting 31.1); the Lie triple systems in `𝔭 ≅ ℂⁿ` are exactly the complex and the totally real subspaces; for `W'` of signature `(1, m)`, `X_{W'} = 𝔹ⁿ ∩ ℙ(W')` is closed, connected, totally geodesic, a ball in a complex affine subspace, biholomorphic to `𝔹^m`, with stabilizer the image of `U(W') × U(W'^⊥)` acting through `PU(1, m)`; conversely every connected totally geodesic submanifold of real dimension `2m ≥ 2` with complex tangent spaces is an open subset of a unique `X_{W'}` (Proposition 31.3 except its final clause, Goldman Ch. 3). §5.5 l.1011 includes connected totally geodesic submanifolds that are neither closed nor complete (31.2's "not necessarily closed"), and §5.6 quotients `Λ\X` by discrete subgroups. ## Prerequisite chain
1. Riemannian/Kähler manifolds, geodesics: Mathlib + Tau Ceti `IsGeodesicCurveOn`.
2. Totally geodesic submanifold (31.2): RiemannianGeometry R5a (merged; Tau Ceti has the Euclidean second fundamental form, and for a Riemannian ambient the normal bundle and covariant derivatives along maps, not `B`).
3. Complex hyperbolic space `X = 𝔹ⁿ ⊂ ℙⁿ`, `PU(1,n)` action, invariant Kähler metric (31.1): RealSemisimpleLieGroups §5.7 (merged, not implemented); `U(1,n)` itself is in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.Matrix.indefiniteUnitaryGroup`, BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §1.1).
4. Complex totally geodesic submanifolds `X_{W'}` and the converse (31.3): RealSemisimpleLieGroups §5.7, on §5.5 (merged); the analytic-subset clause: merged BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §3.3, on ComplexKahlerGeometry §3.4.
5. Lattices in `PU(1,n)` and arithmetic lattices (#68): merged VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 9 and AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.3.4, read on `PU(1,n)` in merged BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §§5.1, 11.1. Selberg's lemma (31.5(a)): its §6.3.
6. Ball quotient `S_Γ` as normal quasi-projective variety (#15): merged BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §§9.5, 10.4 (not implemented).
7. Totally geodesic subvariety (31.4) and the group-theoretic form (31.5(b)): merged BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §§12.1–12.3 (not implemented). Strict and maximal: not stated, immediate from §12.1.
