## Prerequisite chain
1. Lie groups and Haar measure. In Mathlib.
2. `U(1,n)`, `PU(1,n)` and the ball `𝔹^n ⊂ ℙ^n` with its transitive holomorphic action and invariant Kähler form `ω_X`: PR #2229 §5.7. Full biholomorphism group (H. Cartan): outside PR #2229, unplanned. Hermitian domains in general are in PR #1038 and PR #933 §P.
3. Lattice in a locally compact group (discrete, finite-covolume fundamental domain; 15.2): PR #792 AA.2.2–AA.2.3 (see #68); Tau Ceti has the `n = 1` case (`IsCofinite`). Arithmeticity is definition #68, PR #792 §3.4. Finite generation and Selberg's lemma (15.3): outside PR #2229, unplanned.
4. Normal complex-space quotient `Γ\𝔹^n` (Cartan). Unplanned in general; PR #933 Layer 1–2 does it for arithmetic quotients.
5. Analytification of quasi-projective varieties and GAGA. PR #848 C-1.1, C1.9.
6. Algebraic model `(S, φ: S^an ≅ Γ\X)`, the definition in the data-carrying form. Unplanned; a small step once items 2–5 exist.
7. Existence: arithmetic case in PR #933 §2.7; non-arithmetic case (Siu–Yau, Mok) unplanned. Uniqueness: Borel extension in PR #933 §3.1; Deng's big Picard theorem unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
A roadmap "complex hyperbolic lattices and ball quotients" would sit beside #933 and #1038. Contents:
- lattices in `PU(1,n)` and Selberg's lemma, on PR #2229 §5.7's ball model and PR #792's lattices;
- Cartan quotients of complex spaces by properly discontinuous groups;
- finite-volume ends and cusp neighbourhoods;
- the Siu–Yau compactification and Mok's projectivity;
- extension theorems (Borel, Deng) for uniqueness of the algebraic structure;
- arithmeticity and Deligne–Mostow examples.

References: Goldman, *Complex hyperbolic geometry*; Mok, *Projective algebraicity of minimal compactifications of complex-hyperbolic space forms of finite volume*; Siu–Yau; Borel, *Introduction aux groupes arithmétiques*.
