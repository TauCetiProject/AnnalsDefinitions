## Tau Ceti code
- Case `n = 1`:
  - `TauCeti...Fuchsian.IsCofinite` (discrete with finite covolume in `PSL₂(ℝ)`, `Analysis/Complex/Fuchsian/Covolume.lean`);
  - Poincaré polygon theorem;
  - quotients `Γ\ℍ` (FuchsianOrbifolds, layers done).
- Native holomorphic structure sheaf on complex manifolds and quotients (`Geometry/Manifold/Complex/StructureSheaf/Quotient.lean`).
- `PU(p, q)` (BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §1.2): `TauCeti.Matrix.ProjectiveIndefiniteUnitaryGroup p q K` with the injective conjugation representation `ProjectiveIndefiniteUnitaryGroup.innerAut` (`innerAut_injective`, `LinearAlgebra/Matrix/IndefiniteUnitaryGroup/Projective.lean`), a closed embedding over `ℝ` and `ℂ` (`isClosedEmbedding_innerAut`, `instT2Space`, `Analysis/Matrix/IndefiniteUnitaryGroup/Projective.lean`).
- The Siegel model of the ball (BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices §1.3): the Siegel domain `TauCeti.siegelDomain` and the Cayley transform of the unit ball `TauCeti.siegelCayley`, an analytic open partial homeomorphism (`analyticOnNhd_siegelCayley`), with `ballHomeomorphSiegelDomain` and `closedBallHomeomorphClosureSiegelDomain` (`Analysis/Complex/SiegelDomain.lean`). Open Tau Ceti PR https://git.taucetiproject.org/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pulls/3494 (§2.1, H. Cartan's uniqueness and linearity theorems).
- Missing: the ball `𝔹ⁿ` in `ℙ(ℂ^{n+1})` with the action of `PU(1,n)`, its metric and Kähler form, lattices in general Lie groups, normal complex-space quotients, scheme analytification, minimal compactification.
- Connected immersed subgroups of the positive triangular group (RepresentationTheory/LieGroups Layer 9): for every connected immersed Lie subgroup `C` of `A N`, the intrinsic exponential is a diffeomorphism and `C` is closed with a closed-embedding presentation and contractible (`TauCeti.Lie.ImmersedLieSubgroupData.lieExp_bijective_of_posUpperTriangular`, `posUpperTriangularLieExpDiffeomorph`, `isClosed_of_posUpperTriangular`, `isClosedEmbedding_toHom_of_posUpperTriangular`, `contractibleSpace_of_posUpperTriangular`), and for a Lie subalgebra `S` the subgroup `S.integralLeafSubgroup` is `lieExp '' S` (`LieSubalgebra.coe_integralLeafSubgroup_posUpperTriangular`) (`Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/PosUpperTriangular/Immersed.lean`, from the general step for groups with diffeomorphic `lieExp` in `Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/Immersed/Exponential.lean`). For an injective immersive homomorphism `f : C →* GL_n(𝕜)` (`𝕜 = ℝ` or `ℂ`) of a connected real Lie group with values in `A N`, the exponential of `C` is a diffeomorphism and `f` is a smooth embedding with closed image (`TauCeti.Lie.lieExp_bijective_of_immersed_posUpperTriangular`, `lieExpDiffeomorphOfImmersedPosUpperTriangular`, `isSmoothEmbedding_of_immersed_posUpperTriangular`, `isClosedEmbedding_of_immersed_posUpperTriangular`, `Geometry/Lie/Subgroup/PosUpperTriangular/GeneralLinear.lean`); `K ∩ C = {1}` for immersed `C` is not stated separately (it follows from `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.inf_posUpperTriangularGroup_eq_bot_of_isCompact`).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lie groups and Haar measure. In Mathlib.
2. `U(1,n)`, `PU(1,n)` and the ball `𝔹^n ⊂ ℙ^n` with its transitive holomorphic action and invariant Kähler form `ω_X`: merged RealSemisimpleLieGroups §5.7 (not implemented; `PU(p, q)` and the Siegel model of the unit ball in Tau Ceti, BallQuotients §§1.2–1.3). Full biholomorphism group (H. Cartan): merged BallQuotients §2.1.
3. Lattice in a real Lie group (discrete, finite covolume; 15.2): merged VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 9 and AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.2.3, AA.3.4 (not implemented), specialised to `PU(1,n)` in merged BallQuotients §5.1; Tau Ceti has the `n = 1` case (`IsCofinite`). Finite generation and Selberg's lemma (15.3): BallQuotients §§6.2–6.4.
4. Normal complex-space quotient `Γ\𝔹^n` (Cartan): BallQuotients §7.1, on merged ComplexComparisonPartII C-1.1–C-1.2.
5. Analytification of quasi-projective varieties and GAGA: merged ComplexComparisonPartII C-1.1, C1.9, C4.1–C4.4.
6. Existence of `S*_Γ` (Theorem 15.4) for every lattice: BallQuotients §§8.2, 9.3–9.5 (Mok); arithmetic case also through merged ShimuraVarieties §2.7, compared in BallQuotients §11.4.
7. Uniqueness (Proposition 15.5) and the ball quotient `(S_Γ, φ)` (Definition 15.6): BallQuotients §§10.2–10.4.

## Library-scale gap
None. Merged BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices is the "complex hyperbolic lattices and ball quotients" roadmap (Goldman, *Complex hyperbolic geometry*; Mok, *Projective algebraicity of minimal compactifications of complex-hyperbolic space forms of finite volume*; Siu–Yau; Kiernan; Witte Morris, *Introduction to Arithmetic Groups*). What remains is implementation; it consumes merged ShimuraVarieties only for the arithmetic comparison of Layer 11, and its long poles are complex analytic spaces and analytification (ComplexComparisonPartII §§C-1.1–C-1.2).
